# Decisive post-incumbent test

The following were **not** observed:

1. a repeat buyer with a good broker still failing because that broker is unavailable;
2. a recurring need for multi-broker replacement;
3. an unresolved independent-acceptance gap after current grading standardization;
4. a settlement/dispute failure requiring another layer;
5. willingness to pay an additional Rooter fee after the buyer already has a trusted broker.

Therefore:

# fail closed.
