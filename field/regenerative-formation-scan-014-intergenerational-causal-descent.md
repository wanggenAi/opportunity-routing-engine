# Regenerative Formation Scan 014 — Intergenerational Co-Residence Causal Descent

Date: **2026-09-19**

Status: **CAUSAL FRONTIER ACCEPTED / INSTITUTION-BASED FORMATION DEMOTED AS PRIVATE PRIMARY / ZERO COMMERCIAL CANDIDATES**

Parent: REGENERATIVE_FORMATION_SCAN_013_FRESH_LIFE_WORLD

## 1. Question

Scan 013 retained one research formation: INTERGENERATIONAL HOUSING × COMPANIONSHIP EXCHANGE.

Retention was not promotion. This scan asks what actually causes the exchange to exist, and who naturally owns the control plane.

Competing explanations:
1. youth housing-price disequilibrium;
2. underused institutional residential capacity;
3. older-adult social-contact deficit;
4. trust / responsibility / operating-boundary friction;
5. public/institutional program economics.

The goal is not to find a product. The goal is to decide whether a portable, unowned commercial river survives causal descent.

## 2. First correction: the structure is triadic, not bilateral

Naive representation: YOUTH ↔ OLDER ADULT.

Current Chinese execution evidence instead supports:

YOUTH ↔ ELDERCARE INSTITUTION / PUBLIC PROGRAM ↔ OLDER ADULT.

The institution is not a passive room supplier. It contributes accommodation, access to vulnerable residents, eligibility screening, health/suitability checks, training, service boundaries, contracts, house rules, service-time accounting, satisfaction monitoring, conflict handling, exit, safety and regulatory responsibility.

That changes the business interpretation materially.

## 3. Youth latent outcome: affordable, stable urban housing

A long-running Suzhou participant described a comparable room at roughly RMB 2,000/month while her eldercare-institution room cost roughly RMB 200/month. Rent pressure was an explicit reason for joining. The program reportedly received roughly 40–50 applicants for five initial places, and the highlighted participant had remained for more than two years.

Source: https://health.ycwb.com/2026-02/21/content_53973559.htm

Shanghai's 2026 policy response independently names youth rent cost and commute pressure as part of the intergenerational-co-living problem.

Source: https://mzj.sh.gov.cn/MZ_zhuzhan2595_0-2-8-2593/20260608/f8e3a1fa11094a2483d149feb4fb9747.html

Verdict: YOUTH PRIMARY LATENT OUTCOME = AFFORDABLE / STABLE URBAN HOUSING. Confidence: HIGH.

A secondary youth outcome is belonging / relational support. The Suzhou participant also described loneliness after arriving in the city and a family-like sense of warmth. This is plausible but current evidence does not show that it independently produces the exchange without the housing-price wedge.

## 4. Older-adult latent outcome: ordinary intergenerational social contact

Shanghai frames the contribution as bounded non-professional companionship intended to enrich older residents' spiritual/social life.

Source: https://mzj.sh.gov.cn/MZ_zhuzhan2595_0-2-8-2593/20260608/f8e3a1fa11094a2483d149feb4fb9747.html

Changzhou's operating model includes teaching, reading companionship and activity assistance rather than professional care replacement.

Source: https://www.wj.gov.cn/html/czwj/2026/FAOOPMFJ_0916/562106.html

Verdict: OLDER-ADULT PRIMARY OUTCOME = ORDINARY HUMAN CONTACT + INTERGENERATIONAL SOCIAL VITALITY. Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH.

Social value is supported. Private payer willingness is not.

## 5. Institution outcome: utilization pressure is real, convertible capacity is unresolved

Shanghai reported eldercare bed utilization at 62% in 2025.

Source: https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/gwk/search/content/ca90401827b44f3e805468ee0de7a870

In August 2026 Shanghai made annual bed utilization, occupancy stability and empty-capacity remediation explicit operating-performance indicators.

Source: https://mzj.sh.gov.cn/MZ_zhuzhan2595_0-2-8-2593/20260819/c99c92e003bf45cdb04d8bd49d2bb3fc.html

But an empty eldercare bed is not automatically a legal or suitable youth room. Some programs use employee dormitories or separately designated rooms.

Verdict: UNDERUSED INSTITUTIONAL CAPACITY = SUPPORTED AT SYSTEM LEVEL; DENSE CONVERTIBLE YOUTH CAPACITY = NOT PROVEN.

## 6. Competing-cause verdicts

### H1 — Housing-price disequilibrium

Strongly supported as the youth-side demand pump. Large accommodation discounts, applicant surplus and explicit rent-pressure narratives all support it. It is not a complete formation explanation because it does not explain why the institution supplies rooms or absorbs governance burden.

### H2 — Underused institutional capacity

Supported at system level, but unit-level suitability and legal convertibility remain unresolved. Do not estimate supply from total empty eldercare beds.

### H3 — Older-adult social-contact deficit

Supported as a repeatable social outcome. Monetization remains unknown. No current evidence establishes a private payer for this outcome independent of the housing exchange.

### H4 — Trust / responsibility / operating boundary

Strongly supported as a governance constraint.

Beijing's formal trial requires eligibility screening, medical examination, psychological testing, signed agreements, training, monthly service thresholds, satisfaction standards, privacy rules and explicit exit conditions.

Source: https://www.bjft.gov.cn/ftq/zfgg/202408/efd8d76c03e448188eb9a85e8892945a.shtml

The Jinyun case study independently records background investigation, staff coordination, schedule conflict, lifestyle conflict, language barriers and retention/participation difficulty.

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392686534_Research_on_the_Intergenerational_Co-Residence_Endowment_Model_in_Jinyun_County

National 2026 eldercare safety policy strengthens whole-process safety responsibility and accountability for eldercare institutions.

Source: https://nyj.nc.gov.cn/ncsmzj/mzyw1/202602/8ab28c77483d4487bdf3739a896f5d6c.shtml

Verdict: GOVERNANCE / RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARY = REAL. But a real governance function is not automatically an open missing edge.

### H5 — Public / institutional program economics

Material and possibly dominant. Current China cases are commonly government-guided, institution-operated, volunteer-framed and deeply discounted, while the institution absorbs ongoing coordination and risk.

Shanghai describes the intended structure as government-guided + institution-led + social participation and is considering a municipal digital platform.

Source: https://mzj.sh.gov.cn/MZ_zhuzhan2595_0-2-8-2593/20260608/f8e3a1fa11094a2483d149feb4fb9747.html

Beijing routes recruitment through a public volunteer platform while each institution performs final selection, training, contracting and supervision.

Source: https://www.beijing.gov.cn/ywdt/gzdt/202408/t20240815_3774505.html

## 7. Key causal result: the institution already owns the local control plane

The institution controls the building, resident access, resident safety, schedules, staff, emergency handling, house rules, volunteer activity and regulatory accountability. It bears the consequences when a match fails.

Therefore the current Chinese institution-based architecture is:

PUBLIC / POLICY LAYER → ELDERCARE INSTITUTION → SCREEN / CONTRACT / TRAIN / GOVERN / EXIT → YOUTH + OLDER ADULT.

The institution is not one node waiting for a neutral router. It is already part of the transaction mechanism and the natural local governance owner.

This materially weakens a standalone Rooter thesis.

## 8. International analogue: governance can be charged, but that does not prove a China private gap

Homeshare UK reports recurring program fees paid by both householders and homesharers to cover finding and supporting good matches, with no rent paid between the two parties.

Source: https://homeshareuk.org/frequently-asked-questions/

Homeshare International reports that professional brokerage is common, but most surveyed programs were charities/not-for-profits, only a few were businesses, and some charge fees to cover administration.

Source: https://homeshare.org/network/how-homeshare-programmes-work/

UK good-practice guidance describes participant fees funding recruitment, vetting, background checks, matching, ongoing monitoring and support.

Source: https://homeshareuk.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/3.6-Homeshare-fees-and-financial-processes.pdf

This proves SAFE CO-RESIDENCE ORCHESTRATION CAN HAVE AN ECONOMIC UNIT.

It does not prove CURRENT MAINLAND CHINA HAS THE SAME OPEN PRIVATE EDGE.

Many mature analogues look more like cost-recovery social infrastructure than high-margin control-plane businesses.

## 9. Household homeshare is a different formation

Institution model: YOUTH ↔ ELDERCARE INSTITUTION ↔ OLDER RESIDENT.

Household model: YOUTH ↔ PROFESSIONAL BROKER / GOVERNANCE LAYER ↔ OLDER HOMEOWNER WITH SPARE ROOM.

The household model is structurally more favorable to an independent router because no eldercare institution already owns the control plane.

A 2026 China Cities Daily / People's Daily-family article recommends exploring older adults with idle housing offering low-cost rooms to young people in exchange for nearby companionship/help, with stricter screening and management.

Source: https://paper.people.com.cn/zgcsb/pc/content/202604/20/content_30152142.html

But this is a recommendation, not an executed domestic household-homeshare flow.

This scan did not establish strong current mainland-China evidence of repeated household homeshare.

Verdict: PRIVATE-HOUSEHOLD HOMESHARE = GEOGRAPHIC-TRANSFER / WATCHLIST HYPOTHESIS, NOT ACTIVE FORMATION.

## 10. Accepted causal frontier

Youth: large housing-price wedge → strong participation pull.

Older adult: ordinary companionship / social vitality → real qualitative value.

Institution: suitable-space utilization + resident experience + policy/social value.

Constraint: co-residence with vulnerable residents requires high-touch local governance.

Natural control-plane owner in current China institution cases: the eldercare institution / public program.

This is the causal stop. The system does not descend into app features, identity verification, insurance products, contract templates, service ledgers, scheduling or matching algorithms. Those can be components of governance, but they are not evidenced missing edges.

## 11. Commercial verdict

INSTITUTION-BASED INTERGENERATIONAL HOUSING / COMPANIONSHIP is DEMOTED FROM PRIVATE PRIMARY FORMATION.

Preserve it as:
- a triadic value-exchange archetype;
- evidence that a large housing-price wedge can mobilize youth into social-contribution flows;
- evidence that governed co-residence requires screening, rules, monitoring and exit;
- a sensor for future household-homeshare leakage.

Do not currently build:
- a cross-generation matching app;
- a youth screening platform;
- a service-hours ledger;
- an outsourced institution program;
- an insurance wrapper;
- a local agency placing young people in nursing homes.

The missing commercial proof would be:

INSTITUTION REPEATEDLY FAILS AT A PORTABLE GOVERNANCE FUNCTION + WANTS AN EXTERNAL PARTY TO OWN IT + EXTERNAL OWNERSHIP LOWERS COST/RISK + NORMALIZED PRIVATE MARGIN SURVIVES.

That chain is not evidenced.
