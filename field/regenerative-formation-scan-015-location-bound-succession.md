## 2. Wide-scan demotions

### Pet home-entry care — demoted

Pet owners have a repeated need during travel/overtime and handing keys/passwords to strangers creates genuine privacy, property and animal-risk concerns.

But in 2026 specialized platforms are already standardizing this boundary. A Wuhan report describes a platform covering 12 cities with more than 1,500 signed pet sitters, continuous livestreaming, customer-service supervision and reported repeat purchase of 80–90%.

Source: https://news.cjn.cn/whpd/yw_19947/202607/t5316771.htm

Other 2026 reporting explicitly identifies contracts, insurance, platform discipline and service standards as the industry's normalization path.

Source: https://sh.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0224/c176738-41507326.html

Verdict: the trust boundary is real, but the private control plane is already forming. Do not rediscover pet care as an open edge.

### Shared-grandparent child pickup — demoted

Dual-income parents face a predictable school-dismissal/work-hours mismatch while healthy retired residents have time, proximity and childcare experience.

The exchange is real and repeated across cities, but communities coordinate it as volunteer mutual aid.

Sources:
- https://www.zyshgzb.gov.cn/n1/2025/0403/c460343-40453028.html
- https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-03-26/doc-inhshaik3142035.shtml
- https://paper.people.com.cn/jksb/pad/content/202603/20/content_30148690.html

A 2026 Zhejiang program set service time at 17:30–19:00 around the exact school/work timing gap and filled 35 child places across two communities in less than half a day.

Verdict: strong bilateral timing complementarity, but child safety and volunteer orientation make the community the natural coordinator. No private control plane is promoted.

### Shared household tools — demoted

Households repeatedly buy low-frequency tools while nearby households hold idle copies.

People's Daily proposed community shared toolboxes in August 2026 because buying rarely used tools is uneconomic and calling a repair worker can cost more than the small task itself.

Source: https://society.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0817/c1008-40780525.html

Operating community tool corners already centralize donated/purchased inventory, use simple registration, and often charge neither deposit nor rent.

Source: https://www.peopleweekly.cn/html/2026/chengshi_0701/272621.html

Verdict: community-owned pooling collapses the peer-to-peer routing problem and leaves weak private economics.

### Youth night-space / idle community-space reuse — demoted

The time mismatch is real: public/community rooms can sit underused while young adults need nearby learning, work and social space.

A 2026 night-school case raised evening space utilization from below 20% to 85% by shifting existing rooms across time slots.

Source: https://www.bhxf.gov.cn/DocHtml/1/26/07/00078559.html

Yantai Laishan already reports tiered public-space operating models with qualified third-party operators and more than 330 projects.

Source: https://ytdangjian.yantai.gov.cn/col/col101296/art/2026/art_8c991e5fbd0842acb2cac5168b9a4fd9.html

Tianfu New Area is actively looking for uses/operators for more than 500,000 square meters of idle community carriers.

Source: https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-07-29/4525617.html

Verdict: the asset owner/community already controls access and recruits operators. A generic space-routing platform is not retained.

## 3. Retained formation: location-bound lease-succession assets

Working name: LOCATION-BOUND SUCCESSION ASSET FLOW.

The observed structure is not ordinary secondhand resale.

It is a transition event in which:
- an outgoing renter has furniture/appliances/fit-specific household assets that are costly or impractical to move;
- an incoming renter may need those exact categories immediately;
- the landlord/property actor wants low-friction occupancy succession;
- the exact dwelling/address may make those assets more valuable to the next occupant than to a generic secondhand buyer.

### 3.1 Current executed leakage

A current 2026 Shenzhen transfer listing explicitly offers furniture together with the room: '转租可送家具'.

Source: https://www.wellcee.com/tw/rent-apartment/1778680906006641

A separate 2026 Shenzhen renter profile reports a tenant spending more than RMB 10,000 furnishing a sparse rental, then being relocated for work after only ten months. Taking the furniture was impractical, so she transferred it to the next tenant at roughly 70% of cost and personally helped the landlord find that successor.

Source: https://global.hk01.com/%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD%E8%A7%82%E5%AF%9F/60344465/%E6%B7%B1%E5%9C%B3%E7%A7%9F%E6%A5%BC%E5%A6%82%E8%A7%81%E5%B7%A5-%E4%B8%9A%E4%B8%BB%E5%8C%96%E8%BA%ABhr%E6%9F%A5%E8%83%8C%E6%99%AF-%E6%B7%B1%E6%BC%82%E5%8F%B9-%E6%9C%89%E9%92%B1%E4%BA%A4%E7%A7%9F%E9%83%BD%E6%9C%AA%E5%BF%85%E4%BD%8F%E5%BE%97

This is a real triadic exchange, not a hypothetical complementarity.

### 3.2 Transition pressure is regenerative

Graduation markets in 2026 repeatedly describe students needing to 'lighten' what they carry and solve the disposal/movement of goods that cannot follow them.

Source: https://sjxy.nwsuaf.edu.cn/xsgzB/xsgzxB/3356d13b7d7c411fba33390900662bc6.htm

Campus turnover is not identical to urban rental succession, but it supports the wider fact that major life transitions generate recurring residual-asset pressure.

## 4. Structural hypothesis

The working hypothesis is:

DWELLING / ADDRESS + LEASE-TURNOVER EVENT may be a stronger routing key than ITEM CATEGORY.

For some bulky or fitted goods, the economically best successor may be the next occupant of the exact same dwelling because this avoids dismantling, transport, storage, disposal and re-installation.

Candidate latent outcomes:

Outgoing renter:
- recover residual value;
- avoid dismantling / moving / disposal cost;
- clear the unit on time;
- reduce transition workload.

Incoming renter:
- reduce setup cost;
- reduce procurement time;
- receive items already fitted to the room/home;
- move in with less coordination.

Landlord/property actor:
- reduce vacancy and turnover friction;
- preserve move-in readiness;
- avoid abandoned-item disputes.

These remain hypotheses. They are not yet selected causal outcomes.

## 5. Counterevidence

The current Shenzhen rental listing already carries furniture-transfer information inside the housing listing itself.

That means an existing rental marketplace can bundle this flow without a new platform.

Source: https://www.wellcee.com/tw/rent-apartment/1778680906006641

An older but useful Shenzhen calibration case showed a renter discounting furniture from 80% of cost toward effectively free, yet still failing to secure a successor when the underlying housing demand did not match.

Source: https://m.huxiu.com/article/2363437.html

This is important:

ASSET VALUE CANNOT RESCUE A BAD HOUSING MATCH.

Large rental platforms also already bundle transition services such as moving and cleaning around graduate-rental events.

Source: https://www.news.cn/house/20260706/31ddb867a4734d2ba337f9a05c469bbf/c.html

So incumbents can expand around the same move event.

## 6. What is still unknown

The formation cannot promote until the system establishes:

- frequency and density of same-dwelling asset succession in mainstream mainland rentals;
- net residual value after depreciation, condition risk and landlord constraints;
- whether outgoing and incoming renters overlap in time often enough;
- whether the landlord, property manager or rental platform is already the natural control-plane owner;
- whether any missing edge exists beyond adding furniture notes to the transfer listing;
- whether repeated failure occurs in inventory, condition verification, consent, timing, settlement or discovery;
- who pays for orchestration;
- whether enough margin survives outside high-churn rental districts.

## 8. Meta-learning

Scan 015 strengthens a search-space correction:

COMMUNITY/PUBLIC-RESOURCE FORMATIONS OFTEN COLLAPSE INTO NATURAL INSTITUTIONAL CONTROL PLANES.

Therefore future sensing should preferentially inspect transition boundaries where neither side naturally owns the handoff.

## 9. Next action

Run causal descent on the lease-turnover event.

Specifically test:
1. Is the outgoing renter primarily optimizing residual-value recovery or simply fast unit clearance?
2. Is the incoming renter actually willing to pay for inherited items, or are free items merely a weak incentive attached to the housing decision?
3. Does the landlord benefit enough to participate, or does landlord control destroy the transfer?
4. Is the governing friction disposal/transport economics, timing overlap, inventory/condition trust, landlord consent, or fragmented discovery?
5. Who already owns the event: rental platform, broker, landlord, property manager, or nobody?
6. Does exact-address succession create enough incremental value to support a repeatable economic unit?

Kill the formation if current rental/property actors already solve the useful boundary at low marginal cost.
