## 5. Xuzhou exporter -> overseas local evidence / field-service unit — RETAIN, NOT P0

Xuzhou manufacturers are moving deeper overseas. Current local reporting describes a transition from simply exporting products toward localized service and complete solutions, and says overseas customers increasingly care about response speed and service capability. Jiangsu is simultaneously building overseas service points because firms lack destination-country local channels and need first-hand market information and localized operating support.

The possible minimum-service-unit mismatch is not “overseas consulting” and not “build an overseas office”. It is one small, bounded local unit:

1. one named Xuzhou/Jiangsu exporter with a recent repeated cost at this micro-task granularity;
