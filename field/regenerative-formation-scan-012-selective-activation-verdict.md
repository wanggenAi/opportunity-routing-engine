## 1. Question

Scan 011 weakened blanket supply-first discovery.

This scan asks whether three cheaper mechanisms can rescue the formation:

1. retirement-transition capture;
2. use-case-informed discovery;
3. social referral.

The formation survives only if these mechanisms can surface **ordinary/tacit older-adult assets** cheaply enough to create repeated downstream value.

---

## 2. Retirement-transition capture is real — but biased

Current public practice already captures people at the point of retirement.

Examples:
- one-person-one-file records;
- professional specialties;
- interests/hobbies;
- willingness to participate;
- immediate inclusion in silver-talent pools;
- “退休第一课” / 荣退 procedures used as an entry point.

Sources:
- https://www.hnlgb.gov.cn/2025/07-08/425296.html
- https://www.zjlgbj.gov.cn/yyga/art/2026/art_080bd85fd19042d6b87f735e762eba44.html
- https://www.sclgb.gov.cn/fwbz-1/1421930754035679232.shtml

This is clearly cheaper than rediscovering someone years later.

It preserves:

```text
FORMER EMPLOYER
+ ROLE
+ TITLE
+ PROFESSIONAL SPECIALTY
+ INTEREST
+ WILLINGNESS
```

before context decays.

But the current examples mostly involve:
- cadres;
- teachers;
- doctors;
- engineers;
- institutionally known retirees.

So:

`RETIREMENT CAPTURE = REAL COST REDUCTION`

but:

`RETIREMENT CAPTURE != PROOF OF LOW-COST DISCOVERY OF ORDINARY TACIT ASSETS`

It mostly prevents **loss of already-legible identity**.

---

## 3. Use-case-informed discovery is highly effective — but converges toward existing routing

Minqing provides a strong current example.

The system collected:
- 43 effective needs;
- matched 39 service projects;

using:

`群众点单 → 平台派单 → 人才接单 → 群众评单`

Source:
- https://fz.fjsen.com/2026-01/14/content_32115915.htm

A Hunan community combines:
- silver-resource mapping;
- resident questionnaires;
- grid visits;
- service-demand lists;

and reports a 98% demand-response rate.

Source:
- https://www.hnlgbj.gov.cn/lgbj/20/22/content_26656.html

Xinghua similarly uses:
- 2,819 pooled retirees;
- 12 designed service projects;
- six broad service domains;
- resident-order / community-dispatch / volunteer-acceptance.

Source:
- https://www.xinghua.gov.cn/xwzx/xhxc/art/2025/art_bf24f2199afb4ebdbdac86009f647148.html

This mechanism is efficient because it asks:

> What recurring problem exists?

Then:

> Who in the pool can solve it?

That is much better than mapping everyone blindly.

But it creates a new contradiction.

Once the need and required capability are already explicit, the mechanism becomes ordinary:

`DEMAND ROUTING / SERVICE MATCHING`

The hidden-asset-specific edge becomes smaller.

So:

`USE-CASE-INFORMED DISCOVERY = EFFECTIVE`

but:

`UNIQUE HIDDEN-ASSET INFRASTRUCTURE = NOT PROVEN`

## 4. Social referral exists — but the regenerative referral loop does not

Current talent discovery uses:
- organization recommendation;
- unit recommendation;
- resident nomination;
- party-member recommendation;
- personal self-nomination.

Sources:
- https://www.hnlgbj.gov.cn/lgbj/25/26/content_26629.html
- https://www.hnjjbs.com/article/2026-09/178840547769131.html

This proves that local social graphs matter.

Adjacent community work also shows that:

`CORE PARTICIPANT → RESIDENT → NEW CORE PARTICIPANT`

can happen in governance settings.

Source:
- https://www.zyshgzb.gov.cn/n1/2026/0420/c462099-40705020.html

But the missing proof remains:

```text
SUCCESSFULLY ACTIVATE ONE HIDDEN ELDER ASSET
→ THAT PERSON REFERS ANOTHER HIGH-VALUE ASSET
→ QUALITY REMAINS HIGH
→ DISCOVERY COST FALLS
→ LOOP REPEATS
```

Current state:

`REFERRAL CHANNEL = REAL`

`SUCCESS-GENERATED DISCOVERY LOOP = NOT EVIDENCED`

## 5. Ordinary hidden assets survive only in focused structures

The strongest counterexample to a full rejection is Fengtai.

The Silver Workshop explicitly discovers:
- scattered older residents;
- crafts;
- retired-engineer knowledge;
- local hidden capability.

It now spans 38 community pilots.

Source:
- https://www.bjft.gov.cn/fengtaishibao/html/2026-06/29/content_9500_19659829.htm

The youth/elder Silver Artisan project also targets overlooked community craft capability and moves it through:
- discovery;
- youth/social-worker activation;
- products;
- classes;
- sales channels.

Source:
- https://www.bjyouth.gov.cn/news/mediaFocus/826558700261445.html

So ordinary hidden assets are real.

But these systems are:

`CATEGORY-FOCUSED + COMMUNITY-ACCESS-DEPENDENT`

They do not demonstrate a generic discovery engine for arbitrary older-adult value.

## 6. New visible roles can cause supply to self-select

Current reporting shows low-age retirees moving into newly visible roles such as:

- short-drama acting;
- livestreaming;
- dance teaching and commercial performance;
- silver-age broadcasting;
- interest-community operations;
- travel / photography / lifestyle-related roles.

Source:
- https://sh.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0417/c176737-41555782.html

This is important counterevidence.

When society makes a role visible and accessible:

`ROLE BECOMES LEGIBLE → SOME RETIREES SELF-SELECT`

A universal hidden-asset discovery intermediary becomes less necessary.

This suggests the deeper bottleneck can often be:

> **lack of a visible useful role / value form**

rather than:

> **lack of a database that knows the person exists.**

## 7. Selective activation does not rescue the broad formation

The three cost reducers each work partially.

### Retirement capture
Works well for preserving known institutional identity.

### Use-case-informed discovery
Works well when recurring need/capability is already describable.

### Social referral
Helps local discovery, but no self-replenishing high-value loop is proven.

None currently establishes:

> a cheap, general, privately replicable way to discover ordinary older people whose valuable assets are invisible even to themselves.

Therefore the broad thesis:

`LATENT HUMAN-ASSET LEGIBILITY / ACTIVATION`

is no longer strong enough to remain the primary formation direction.

## 8. What is being demoted — and what is not

### Demoted

> **Generic older-adult hidden-asset discovery as a broad private regenerative river.**

Why:
- high-touch discovery;
- uneven value density;
- community-access dependence;
- strongest economics in already-legible roles;
- selective mechanisms mostly preserve existing legibility or converge toward ordinary demand routing.

### Preserved

The factual insight remains:

> Retirement can make capability lose its institutional address, and active elicitation can recover value.

This remains useful as:
- a sensor;
- a subsystem;
- a category-specific operating function;
- a clue inside another formation.

It is not currently a standalone opportunity archetype.
