# Regenerative Formation Scan 016 — Lease-Succession Causal Descent

Date: **2026-09-19**

Status: **CAUSAL DESCENT COMPLETE / FORMATION DEMOTED FROM PRIMARY COMMERCIAL SEARCH / ZERO COMMERCIAL CANDIDATES**

Parent: REGENERATIVE_FORMATION_SCAN_015_LOCATION_BOUND_SUCCESSION

## 1. Question

Test whether exact-address lease succession creates a repeatable independent commercial boundary for outgoing-renter residual assets, incoming-renter setup needs and landlord turnover incentives.

The key distinction is:

```text
REAL SAME-ADDRESS VALUE
!=
OPEN INDEPENDENT CONTROL PLANE
```

## 2. Evidence update

### Housing decision dominates asset decision

A July 2026 Beike Research Institute renter survey reported that renters place the greatest emphasis on total housing cost, house condition/furniture-appliance configuration and commute. Work/commute change was the leading reported move trigger.

Source:
https://house.cnr.cn/20260723/t20260723_527725941.shtml

A 2026 China Index Academy renter survey similarly shows that renters care about room quality and furniture, but treats these as attributes of the rental product. For centralized apartments, furniture is one component of the room bundle rather than a separate predecessor-asset market.

Source:
https://www.cih-index.com/news/2026-07-16/54903324.html

Causal implication:

```text
HOUSING MATCH
precedes
PREDECESSOR-ASSET VALUE
```

Furniture can improve a matched dwelling. It usually cannot create the underlying housing match.

### Same-address transfer is real, but already expressible inside rental listings

Current Shenzhen transfer listings show several native patterns:

- furniture/appliances are simply described as part of a move-in-ready room;
- the outgoing tenant may leave a self-purchased item for the successor;
- the outgoing tenant may separately ask the successor to pay for appliances.

Examples:
- a current Shenzhen Luohu transfer listing states that landlord-provided furniture stays and the outgoing renter's lift desk can remain for the next tenant:
  https://www.wellcee.com/en/rent-apartment/1770449672961855
- a Shenzhen Longhua transfer listing asks for the deposit plus about RMB 2,300 for appliances:
  https://www.wellcee.com/tw/rent-apartment/1779448470155914
- other current Shenzhen transfer listings describe complete furniture/appliances and direct re-signing with the landlord:
  https://www.wellcee.com/en/rent-apartment/1788834263203790
  https://www.wellcee.com/en/rent-apartment/1772804713050398

This proves the flow exists, but it is strong counterevidence to an unowned discovery edge: the rental listing itself can already carry the information.

### Outgoing-renter pressure is dominated by clearance economics

Large low-residual-value furniture can be expensive to dismantle and transport relative to resale value.

A 2025 Beijing report describes used furniture where transport/dismantling cost materially eroded resale proceeds; one example produced RMB 700 sale proceeds but RMB 440 moving cost, leaving only RMB 260 net. Merchants also cited roughly RMB 300 dismantling/transport cost for certain board furniture.

Source:
https://finance.sina.cn/2025-09-28/detail-infsamff4027055.d.html

An additional consumer-side case describes a sofa that could not be given away easily because of removal/transport friction and ultimately required paid removal.

Source:
https://post.smzdm.com/p/a2q8r787/

The stronger outgoing outcome is therefore usually:

```text
CLEAR UNIT ON TIME
+
AVOID MOVING / DISPOSAL COST
```

Residual-value recovery is secondary and highly conditional.

### Landlord/property control is structurally unavoidable

The Civil Code requires the lessee to return the leased property at expiry in the agreed or normally used condition. Subletting also generally requires the lessor's consent.

Sources:
https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250312_1958939.html
https://gdca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2020/art_573d6ef5018b46b6a4e1f31ca085a710.html

Court guidance also recommends that furniture/appliance inventories be explicitly recorded with the house handover.

Source:
https://www.a-court.gov.cn/xxfb/no1court_412/docs/202308/d_3967006.html

A Ministry of Justice case note further distinguishes removable tenant-owned furniture from fixtures and emphasizes negotiated handling at surrender.

Source:
https://legalinfo.moj.gov.cn/zxxfyasf/202407/t20240704_501781.html

This means the landlord/property actor is not a peripheral participant. It controls the transition boundary whenever predecessor-owned assets remain in the dwelling.

## 3. Competing latent outcomes

### Outgoing renter

Hypothesis A — maximize resale recovery:
- supported only in some cases;
- weakened by low resale value, short deadlines and transport cost.

Hypothesis B — clear the unit on time with minimum effort/cost:
- strongly supported;
- explains gifting, deep discounting and same-address transfer.

Selected primary outcome:
**FAST COMPLIANT CLEARANCE WITH MINIMUM NET LOSS.**

### Incoming renter

Hypothesis A — buy predecessor assets:
- sometimes true;
- direct paid appliance transfer is observed.

Hypothesis B — obtain a suitable, move-in-ready dwelling:
- stronger and more general;
- predecessor assets matter only after the dwelling already fits.

Selected primary outcome:
**MOVE-IN READINESS AT ACCEPTABLE TOTAL HOUSING COST.**

### Landlord/property actor

Primary outcome:
**FAST, LOW-RISK TURNOVER INTO A RENTABLE/HANDOVER-COMPLIANT STATE.**

## 4. Competing structural constraints

### C1 — transport/disassembly economics

Status: **EVIDENCED / PRIMARY**

For bulky low-value furniture, moving and dismantling cost can consume most or all residual value.

### C2 — housing-match precedence

Status: **EVIDENCED / PRIMARY**

A successor who does not want the dwelling is not made into a viable tenant by furniture value alone.

### C3 — timing overlap

Status: **PLAUSIBLE BUT NOT SHOWN TO BE THE GOVERNING FAILURE**

Same-address handoff benefits from overlap, but current transfer listings already coordinate dates and direct landlord re-signing. Repeated costly failure caused specifically by timing is not established.

### C4 — inventory / condition trust

Status: **REAL BUT NATURALLY EMBEDDED IN THE HOUSE HANDOVER**

Inventory/condition recording is already part of rental contracting and handover practice. No independent missing edge is evidenced.

### C5 — landlord consent / boundary ownership

Status: **EVIDENCED / PRIMARY**

The landlord/property actor controls lease succession and the acceptable returned state. Any predecessor-asset handoff that remains inside the dwelling passes through this boundary.

### C6 — fragmented discovery

Status: **NOT PROVEN AS A DECISIVE STRUCTURAL FRICTION**

Existing rental listings can already express:
- furnished state;
- free successor items;
- paid appliance transfer;
- direct landlord re-signing.

No evidence establishes that a separate routing layer materially improves the outcome beyond the housing listing.

## 5. Causal frontier

The causal chain resolves to:

```text
WORK / LIFE MOVE
→ HARD DEADLINE TO SURRENDER DWELLING
→ BULKY LOW-RESIDUAL-VALUE ASSETS
→ DISASSEMBLY / TRANSPORT / DISPOSAL COST
→ STRONG CLEARANCE PRESSURE

INCOMING HOUSING SEARCH
→ LOCATION / PRICE / COMMUTE / ROOM QUALITY FIRST
→ FURNITURE CONFIGURATION SECOND
→ PREDECESSOR ASSETS ONLY MATTER AFTER HOUSING MATCH

LEASE TURNOVER
→ LANDLORD / PROPERTY CONSENT + HANDOVER CONTROL
→ LISTING / HANDOVER ALREADY CARRIES FURNITURE INFORMATION
```

The exact address is genuinely valuable because it can eliminate transport.

But:

```text
ROUTING KEY VALUE
!=
SEPARATE COMMERCIAL CONTROL PLANE
```

## 6. Missing-edge test

No repeated costly missing edge is established beyond capabilities already available to:
- the outgoing renter's transfer listing;
- the landlord/broker/property manager;
- the rental platform;
- ordinary local removal/resale channels.

The current evidence supports a workflow attribute:

**SAME-ADDRESS SUCCESSION OPTION**

rather than an independent formation requiring a new orchestrator.

## 8. Meta-learning

Scan 016 adds a durable discovery rule:

```text
A NEW ROUTING KEY CAN IMPROVE AN EXISTING TRANSACTION
WITHOUT CREATING A NEW BUSINESS CONTROL PLANE.
```

Location/time coupling is valuable only when it exposes a repeated failure that existing transaction owners do not already absorb.

Future wide sensing should reject formations where:
1. the new signal is merely an attribute inside an incumbent transaction;
2. the incumbent already owns permission, settlement and handover;
3. the added value cannot independently support normalized orchestration economics.

## 9. Next action

Prefer transition boundaries where:
- value is stranded before an incumbent transaction exists;

- repeated workaround/failure is visible before product design.

Do not reopen lease-succession assets without new evidence of a repeated, costly, unowned failure.
