## 1. Question

## 2. The rail itself is now publicly proven

The governing 2026 Jiangsu rule is explicit.

Article 9 says technical-manager offices may:

- recruit technical managers from society;
- establish affiliation through **project cooperation and similar arrangements**;
- sign a standardized agreement;
- settle technology-transfer service commission with affiliated technical managers **according to the agreement**;
- provide transaction-service contract signing;
- provide invoicing;
- provide settlement;
- organize/coordinate enterprise demand diagnosis and research-team/result matching;
- lawfully obtain service income.

Source:

- https://www.jstec.com.cn/news/202602628049695003

This changes the truth state.

```

PRIVATE_OFFICE_TERMS_UNKNOWN
```

The remaining unknown is not whether this institutional mechanism exists.

It exists.

## 3. Individual entry is deliberately flexible at policy level

The 2026 management rule does **not** publish one universal certificate as a prerequisite for every externally affiliated/project-cooperation technical manager.

What it does say is:

- the office may recruit excellent technical managers from society;
- project cooperation may establish the affiliation relationship;
- a standardized agreement is required.

Current 2026 training materials also explicitly send participants into:

```text
双选
+
挂靠
+
事务所合作
+
真实技术需求挖掘 / 诊断 / 交易对接
```

Source:

- https://taizhou.jstec.com.cn/news/202619528051061976

The latest directly indexed official registration guide found in public search is from 2024. It describes:

```text
account registration
→ real-name verification
→ technical-manager identity application
→ voluntary office affiliation
```

and states that affiliation is a bilateral choice: first contact the office and sign an affiliation agreement, then complete platform affiliation.

Source:

- https://suzhou.jstec.com.cn/news/202420421398052624

Because that guide is from 2024, Scan 028 treats it as **continuity evidence**, not as proof of every 2026 office's current intake rule.

## 4. Training / certification is useful but not yet proven as a universal gate

2026 Jiangsu training programs accept:

- enterprise/university/government technology-transfer staff;
- staff of IP, technology-transfer and finance service institutions;
- other people engaged in or interested in technology transfer;
- already registered technical managers.

Current programs provide 24-hour core training plus practical activity and office-affiliation routes.

Source:

- https://taizhou.jstec.com.cn/news/202619528051061976

The public rule therefore supports:

```text
TRAINING / REGISTRATION = PROFESSIONALIZATION PATH
```

but does not yet support:

```text
NO CERTIFICATE = NO PROJECT COOPERATION
```

That exact office-specific gate remains UNKNOWN.

## 5. Money flow — what is proven

Publicly proven:

```text
OFFICE
→ may obtain lawful service income
→ may sign transaction-service contracts
→ may issue invoices
→ may receive / settle money
→ may settle commission with affiliated technical manager by agreement
```

Source:

- https://www.jstec.com.cn/news/202602628049695003

This is materially stronger than an informal referral market.

The office is explicitly designed as a legal/organizational settlement layer.

## 6. Money flow — what is NOT proven

Scan 028 did **not** find a current 2026 public rule that fixes:

- one technical-manager commission percentage;
- one office service-fee percentage;
- one universal multi-manager split;
- one universal settlement trigger;
- one mandatory attribution method for self-sourced projects;
- one universal refund / dispute / IP / failure-liability allocation.

The 2026 governing rule deliberately says commission settlement is **by agreement**.

Therefore:

```text
COMMISSION ALLOWED
!=
COMMISSION RATE KNOWN
```

Older historical fee/split conventions are not promoted into current truth.

## 7. Contract infrastructure is real

The national 2026 `Technology Contract Recognition and Registration Measures` explicitly includes **technology intermediary contracts** as a type of technology service contract.

Technology-contract registration is voluntary, and qualifying registered contracts can support relevant policy treatment.

Source:

- https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/kjs/wjfb/art/2026/art_ccf28613115d4960810b3b68253b0a25.html

This does not mean every technical-manager service automatically qualifies for registration.

It means the national contract framework explicitly recognizes the kind of intermediary service this rail is designed to support.

## 8. OPC / partnership route is real — but not our first move

Article 6 of the 2026 Jiangsu rule is also important.

Professional/social technical managers are encouraged to:

- establish a partnership;
- or establish an OPC operating entity;
- and may apply to build a market-oriented technical-manager office without the normal Article 5 scale/history conditions.

Source:

- https://www.jstec.com.cn/news/202602628049695003

This is strategically useful.

It means that if the model later proves repeatable, the long-term path does **not** have to remain permanently dependent on another office.

But:

```text
CAN CREATE OWN OFFICE
!=
SHOULD CREATE OWN OFFICE BEFORE FIRST TRANSACTION
```

For first external value flow, using an existing office remains lower friction.
