## 4. Live Track A — Existing technical-manager transaction rail

### What reality shows

Jiangsu's 2026 technical-manager-office rules are unusually concrete.

A technical-manager office may:

- recruit/attach technical managers through project cooperation;
- sign technical-transfer service contracts;
- invoice and settle;
- settle technical-transfer commissions with attached technical managers by agreement;
- earn lawful service revenue.

Most importantly, the rules explicitly encourage professional/social technical managers to establish **partnership or OPC operating entities**, and allow such applicants to bypass the normal scale/history conditions that ordinary marketized office applicants face.

Primary rule:

- https://www.jstec.com.cn/news/202602628049695003

Jiangsu also allows eligible social technology-transfer institutions that facilitate major transfers to receive policy support calculated at 3% of technology transaction value, capped at 1 million yuan per institution. That is an institutional subsidy rule, **not** a promised personal commission rate.

Source:

- https://kxjst.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/14/art_82571_11816969.html

### This is not a paper market

The August 2026 J-TOP advanced-materials session reports:

- 200+ real technical demands collected;
- 20 signed projects;
- more than 11 million yuan of facilitated technology transactions.

Source:

- https://kxjst.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/25/art_82539_11820282.html

Xuzhou itself already has a live rail:

- nearly 1000 certified technical managers are reported as active locally;
- one university-science-park technical-manager office facilitated 8 technical contracts worth nearly 2 million yuan in 2024;
- the Jiangsu Huaihai Technology Property Rights Trading Center and JSTEC Xuzhou branch already operate locally.

Sources:

- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202607/t20260713_8583095.shtml
- https://www.cumtusp.com/
- https://xuzhou.jstec.com.cn/

### Why this survives the first cut

This is **not** being retained as “invent a technology marketplace.”

The market and transaction rail already exist.

The interesting structure is:

```text
our discovery/qualification cognition
+
existing technical-demand/result/expert infrastructure
+
existing contract / invoice / commission rail
→ possible first external value flow
```

The commercial insight is therefore:

> The fastest route to value may be to enter an existing transaction rail with better discovery and qualification, rather than building another marketplace.

### Counterevidence

This is not empty whitespace.

Xuzhou already has:

- large institutional players;
- mature university/science-park channels;
- close to 1000 certified managers;
- active provincial platforms.

Therefore:

```text
FORMAL TRANSACTION RAIL EXISTS
!=
NEW ENTRANT HAS ACCESS
```

### Current truth state

```text
ROUTE_TESTABLE_EXISTING_RAIL
NOT_YET_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW
```

### Decisive unknown

Can an external/project-based operator bring self-sourced qualified projects into an existing Xuzhou/Jiangsu technical-manager office and participate in the commission-settlement rail?

We still need exact public or human-confirmed answers on:

- entry/attachment requirements;
- qualification/training expectations;
- commission structure;
- who signs with the enterprise/resource owner;
- what the office accepts as a qualified sourced project;
- what evidence triggers settlement.

## 5. Live Track B — Resource-first application discovery

### What reality shows

Xuzhou Guoji Atomic Manufacturing has already produced special-metal atomic-cluster powder, but its own engineer publicly states that because the material is new, **how to apply it remains an open question**, and the company is seeking downstream application partners.

Sources:

- https://kxjst.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/6/art_82539_11799463.html
- https://kxjst.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/6/29/art_82538_11796235.html

This is a clean example of:

```text
RESOURCE EXISTS
+
OWNER KNOWS RESOURCE
+
HIGH-VALUE APPLICATION NOT YET FORMED
```

That is materially different from:

```text
buyer posts explicit need
→ find supplier
```

Jiangsu's July scene-opening policy explicitly calls for market-oriented scene supply-demand matching and professional organizations able to coordinate scene resources and commercialize new technologies/products.

Source:

- https://fzggw.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/13/art_84097_11803331.html

### Connection pressure

There is real directional pressure:

- a named technology owner is actively seeking application partners;
- Jiangsu is opening real validation scenes;
- Xuzhou manufacturers publish hard technical problems and participate in expert-enterprise matching.

A Xuzhou case even shows a technical manager taking a manufacturer's vague statement that equipment “doesn't work well,” translating it into a precise technical-demand list, and finding a university expert within a week.

Source:

- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202607/13/content_52654.html

### But the missing edge is not proven

The contradiction search matters.

Jiangsu concept-validation centers already provide:

- product/scene-system verification;
- commercial-prospect validation;
- key application-scene screening;
- market validation;
- technical and commercialization pathway design.

Sources:

- https://m.jsde.org.cn/nd.jsp?id=1109
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2024/8/5/art_33718_11320461.html

The provincial technical-transfer market also already aggregates technology, demand, experts and service institutions.

Therefore:

```text
NEW RESOURCE SEEKS APPLICATION
!=
OPEN APPLICATION-DISCOVERY MISSING EDGE
```

### Current truth state

```text
LATENT_VALUE_HYPOTHESIS_WITH_CONNECTION_PRESSURE
MISSING_EDGE_NOT_EVIDENCED
```

### Decisive unknown

Find a **named novel-resource owner** that has already used or attempted existing technical-transfer / concept-validation routes and still cannot get the specific application-discovery/validation responsibility owned.

Only then can we say a new control layer exists.
