## 1. Question

Scan 003 found that housing, shared-grandma childcare and reciprocal skills share a recurring relationship-operating edge.

This scan asks the next question:

> **Can that relationship infrastructure travel across time, communities and organizers — or is the current flow only possible while one trusted local institution keeps pushing it?**

That distinction decides whether we are looking at a regenerative field or a collection of good community projects.

---

# 2. Portability is currently broken

A current 2026 Beijing report captures the problem directly.

Time-bank participants ask:

- if I save service time now, will it still be redeemable when I am old?
- if I move to another community, what happens to the stored value?
- will there still be enough future providers?
- will the services available later actually be useful?

Source:

- https://sc.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0826/c345167-41678024.html

This is not a user-interface problem.

It is a **claim credibility** problem.

A person contributes real time today.

What they receive in return is only valuable if the future network can honor the claim.

Current 2026 research based on Nanjing/Shanghai practice identifies the same mechanism:
- universal deposit/redemption barriers;
- liquidity blockage;
- weak credit guarantees;
- service-quality inconsistency;
- participant-structure imbalance.

Source:

- https://doi.org/10.12677/ar.2026.138543

A public Zhangjiagang time-bank rule provides a concrete locality boundary.

If a participant moves household registration out of the city, the account is closed and the stored time is converted into a one-off cash subsidy.

Source:

- https://www.zjg.gov.cn/zjg/glzd/system.shtml

So:

```text
RECIPROCAL VALUE EXISTS
but
RECIPROCAL CLAIM DOES NOT NECESSARILY TRAVEL
```

Current truth:

`PORTABILITY GAP = EVIDENCED`

# 3. A ledger is not liquidity

The naive solution would be:

> “Build an app and record points.”

Current evidence directly contradicts that.

Cities already have:
- accounts;
- points/time credits;
- service records;
- matching;
- evaluations;
- redemption rules.

Yet participants still worry about:
- future redemption;
- geographic movement;
- service availability;
- quality;
- institutional continuity.

So:

```text
RECORDING A CLAIM
!=
MAKING THE CLAIM CREDIBLE
```

A reciprocal claim becomes valuable only if there is enough future capacity, accepted valuation, quality control and a durable institution/network that will honor it.

A better structural phrase is:

> **portable reciprocity + credible future liquidity**

This is still a formation interpretation, not a product design.

# 4. Self-propulsion is not yet proven

A regenerative field should eventually create more flow because the flow itself works.

Current evidence does not show that clearly.

2026 time-bank research reports weak internal momentum even where systems already exist.

Source:

- https://doi.org/10.12677/ar.2026.138543

A 2026 academic review of mutual-aid eldercare notes that pilots have existed for more than a decade, but large-scale sustainable expansion remains difficult and many projects remain demonstration-style.

Source:

- https://opaj.napstic.cn/periodicalArticle/0120260802406759

Historical field research shows a revealing mechanism:
when a professional social-work team exited after its contracted project period, some community time-bank activity degraded and communication channels became inactive.

Source:

- https://html.rhhz.net/shgzygl/html/1586502160548-1305891357.htm

This older evidence is used only for mechanism illustration.

It does not prove current prevalence.

But together with current low-internal-momentum evidence, it supports:

`SELF_PROPULSION = NOT EVIDENCED`

# 5. The organizer may be part of the production function

Earlier we implicitly treated the organizer as:

> something that helps the relationship start.

That may be wrong.

In some life domains, the organizer may continuously produce:
- trust;
- screening;
- scheduling;
- rules;
- conflict handling;
- insurance;
- continuity;
- service quality;
- future redemption credibility.

If those functions disappear, the flow may disappear.

That means:

```text
ORGANIZER
!=
TEMPORARY FRICTION-REDUCTION ONLY
```

It may be a structural node.

This is especially important for:
- housing;
- childcare.

# 6. LWA-RELATIONSHIP now splits by risk class

The common substrate survives.

But execution conditions diverge.

## LWA-R1 — high-trust / high-liability reciprocal life support

Includes:
- intergenerational housing;
- shared-grandma childcare.

These involve:
- private living space;
- child safety;
- responsibility;
- emergency authority;
- liability;
- long-duration trust.

It is increasingly plausible that a persistent institution or regulated trust anchor is **structurally required**.

Full peer-to-peer self-propulsion may not be desirable.

Current state:

`COMMON SUBSTRATE PRESENT / HEAVY DOMAIN GOVERNANCE REQUIRED`

## LWA-R2 — lower-risk reciprocal capability exchange

Includes:
- local-life knowledge;
- language/dialect;
- cooking / market skills;
- woodwork / hobbies;
- smartphone / video / digital help.

This domain still needs:
- identity;
- matching;
- reputation;
- safe meeting context;
- reciprocity;
- continuity.

But it does not automatically carry the same housing/child-safety liability.

That makes it the cleaner environment for testing:

> **Can intergenerational reciprocity become repeatable and self-propelling outside heavy institutional protection?**

Current state:

`PRIMARY PORTABILITY / SELF-PROPULSION FORMATION TEST`

# 7. A layered missing-edge hypothesis

The evidence now supports a more nuanced hypothesis.

## Common substrate

```text
portable identity / reputation
+
legible offer / want
+
bounded reciprocal obligation
+
service / contribution history
+
credible future reciprocity claim
+
exception / dispute history
+
continuity across time and context
```

## Domain governance

Above that substrate, each domain has additional rules.

Housing:
- tenancy;
- privacy;
- residence rules.

Childcare:
- safeguarding;
- pickup authority;
- insurance;
- liability.

Skills:
- skill quality;
- supervision;
- safe meeting context.

This is a **structural hypothesis**.

It is not permission to design a software architecture.

# 8. Why existing time banks matter

Time banks are not just competitors.

They are a long-running natural experiment.

They show that:
- people will record reciprocal contribution;
- communities can activate dormant capacity;
- some exchange can repeat.

But they also show where the flow breaks:
- low liquidity;
- weak portability;
- valuation mismatch;
- quality uncertainty;
- institutional dependence;
- weak younger participation;
- doubt about future redemption.

A 2026 report calls the core fear very plainly:

> people do not know whether the goodwill they bank today will still be redeemable in the future.

Source:

- https://sc.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0826/c345167-41678024.html

Therefore the deeper edge may not be:

> “matching strangers.”

It may be:

> **turning today's contribution and reputation into a future reciprocal claim that remains credible after the original person, community or organizer changes.**

That is a much harder problem.

# 11. What we learned

The strongest result of this scan is negative but useful:

> **The current relationship flows are not yet demonstrably self-propelling.**

They often survive because:
- community staff recruit;
- social workers coordinate;
- public institutions back the promise;
- rules are local;
- credits are local;
- trust is local.

So the next layer is not:

> “how do we commercialize this?”

It is:

> **where does low-risk intergenerational exchange already continue by itself after the first connection?**

That is the cleanest place to find a genuine regenerative loop.

# 12. Next action

Focus on `LWA-R2`.

Search for real cases where:
- unrelated younger and retired people meet through one initial activity;
- exchange again later;
- refer each other to others;
- form repeat pairings;
- carry reputation/history forward;

The decisive question:

> **Does a successful low-risk exchange naturally produce the next exchange?**

If yes, we may finally have a self-propelling seed.
