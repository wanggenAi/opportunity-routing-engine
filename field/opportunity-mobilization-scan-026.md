## 1. 江苏中伦钢结构有限公司 — strongest direct residual

Current recruitment for an international / oversized-logistics specialist in 徐州沛县 says the company already works across:

- freight forwarders;
- shipping lines;
- truck fleets;
- terminals.

Yet it still needs an internal person to:

- compare and negotiate prices;
- sign contracts;
- coordinate booking and over-limit road transport;
- monitor cargo in transit;
- handle container detention, port detention and transport abnormalities;
- maintain logistics-supplier records;
- optimize shipment workflow.

Source:

- https://www.zhaopin.com/jobdetail/CC829256030J40923227008.htm

This is direct current evidence of:

`EXTERNAL EXECUTION + INTERNAL CONTROL LAYER`

The external providers have not eliminated:

- cross-provider coordination;
- exception ownership;
- cost control;
- supplier governance.

That is a **real post-provider residual**.

# Decisive evidence boundary

The residual is now directly observed.

What is **not** observed is equally important.

For the named buyers, current public evidence does not show:

1. that they tried a 4PL / Control Tower / integrated-logistics provider;
2. that such a provider failed;
3. which exact responsibility still could not be externalized;
4. whether internal authority, technical judgment, customer ownership or another constraint makes that role structurally internal;
5. whether a new entrant can outperform mature control-tower providers economically.

Therefore:

# `REAL POST-PROVIDER RESIDUAL != OPEN CATEGORY`

and:

# `MANAGING MULTIPLE 3PLs IS ALREADY A 4PL / CONTROL-TOWER FUNCTION`

The current evidence proves a **problem shape**, not a new business opening.
