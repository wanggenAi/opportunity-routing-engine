## 1. Question

Scan 019 established that former-OEM / project-specific knowledge can be valuable around unsupported legacy assets. Scan 020 tests whether that knowledge forms an independent routing control plane or is normally absorbed by accountable expert/service institutions.

## 2. Technical lineage remains a valid relevance signal

Prior evidence survives: current legacy-controls cases describe retired original engineers and lost source/documentation; a 2026 PLC forum request sought a retired Rockwell/Allen-Bradley engineer for paid consulting; a 2026 Motion case reported former employees of original spindle manufacturers participating in a 3–4 week repair path versus more than 20 weeks for replacement.

The decisive question is not whether lineage has value. It is who owns the customer outcome.

## 3. Operational markets already package former-OEM knowledge inside service firms

Arjavin Power explicitly describes a global pool of former OEM engineers, remote problem-solving, obsolete-system support, expert engagement, safety/quality review and outcome-oriented delivery.

Source: https://arjavinpower.com/about/

Concept Part Solutions states that it is an authorized/licensed partner for maturing semiconductor-equipment product lines and staffs each expertise area with former OEM employees.

Source: https://conceptpartsolutions.com/about

Evergreen Crane & Equipment Service states that former OEM employees are on staff and uses them to repair, maintain and upgrade old and current equipment.

Source: https://www.evergreencrane.com/

These organizations do not sell expert identity as the whole product. They sell problem resolution, repair, maintenance, upgrades, parts and accountable service.

## 4. Generic expert discovery and compliance are already mature

GLG, Third Bridge and Guidepoint already support custom expert recruitment, conflict screening, former-employer confidentiality restrictions and project-specific compliance.

Sources:
- https://glg.com/compliance
- https://www.thirdbridge.com/en-us/about-us/compliance/policies/expert-terms-and-conditions
- https://www.thirdbridge.com/en-us/services/expert-calls
- https://www.guidepoint.com/services/compliance/compliance-faqs/

Therefore former-employee discovery plus a bounded, non-confidential advisory call is already an existing control plane.

## 5. Confidentiality limits what historical knowledge can be monetized

A 2025 US federal case involving former 3M employees discusses allegations around use of a confidential spindle drawing after employment.

Source: https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/indiana/inndce/1%3A2021cv00274/107773/163/

This is one jurisdiction and one dispute, not universal legal guidance. Structurally, however, it shows that a model cannot depend on extracting former-employer trade secrets.

Legitimate value must come from public experience, general skill, customer-owned data/equipment, authorized documentation and non-confidential technical judgment.

## 6. Reverse engineering remains a substitute

Specialist firms can recover PLC logic, reverse engineer undocumented controls, recreate obsolete parts and retrofit systems when original human history is unavailable.

Sources:
- https://atgcontrols.com/system-migration-and-revamp/
- https://powerdrivecle.com/reverse-engineering
- https://speed3dmold.com/replacement-casting

This reduces standalone pricing power for an expert-identity router.

## 7. Competing hypotheses

### H1 — The exact former person is the product

REJECTED AS A GENERAL PRODUCT. Useful in some cases, but expert networks and specialist service firms can already custom-source relevant people.

### H2 — Technical lineage is a strong ranking signal

SUPPORTED. Former OEM/project experience can improve relevance, diagnosis speed and confidence.

### H3 — A neutral technical-lineage marketplace can own the transaction

NOT EVIDENCED. Customers normally need an accountable repaired, migrated, tested or explained outcome, not merely an introduction.

### H4 — Specialist service firms are the natural institutional owner

SUPPORTED / GOVERNING FORM. They can combine lineage with liability, tools, parts, authorization, documentation, testing and execution.

### H5 — Advisory-only calls create a separate opportunity

INCUMBENT CONTROL PLANE EXISTS. Mature expert networks already own compliance-screened expert calls.

## 8. Founder-dependence test

A standalone lineage router would repeatedly need to reconstruct employment/project history, verify model-level experience, screen conflicts and confidentiality obligations, define liability scope, negotiate availability and hand off to another accountable executor.

That is structurally close to specialist headhunting or technical concierge work.

Without proprietary demand density or an existing service contract, this fails the intended scalable-orchestration model.

## 9. Missing-edge verdict

No standalone missing edge is established.

TECHNICAL LINEAGE IS A MATCHING FEATURE, NOT A COMPLETE TRANSACTION UNIT.

It can improve future service/vendor/expert routing, but it does not independently own responsibility, safety, execution, parts, warranty or compliance.
