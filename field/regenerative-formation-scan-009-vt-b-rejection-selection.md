Date: **2026-09-19**

## 1. Question

Scan 008 established that successful VT-B operators do more than digitize or market older-adult capability.

They repeatedly perform:

```text
DISCOVER
→ SCREEN
→ CHOOSE VALUE FORM
→ TEST
→ OBSERVE
→ REROUTE
```

The unresolved question was:

> Is this mainly irreducible curator taste, or do operators use recurring rejection and selection rules that can be learned across cases?

This scan prioritizes negative evidence.

---

## 2. Negative evidence — a real capability can still deserve rejection in its current form

The clearest current example remains the Shaoxing West-Lake basket.

Before redesign:
- older residents had genuine weaving skill;
- the original products were highly homogeneous;
- shape, color and use were dated;
- finished baskets accumulated unsold;
- even a roughly RMB6 price did not create meaningful demand;
- stalls and second-hand-platform attempts reportedly produced little response.

Source:
- https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5338388320487238.html

So:

`REAL CAPABILITY != VIABLE CURRENT VALUE FORM`

A 2026 craft-market discussion makes the same economic point from another angle:

> heavy labor time alone does not justify a high price when the product lacks distinctive design/artistic value.

Source:
- https://epaper.tianjinwe.com/tjrb/html/2026-03/03/content_143096_3185862.htm

This gives a genuine kill signal:

`HIGH EFFORT + LOW DIFFERENTIATION = POSSIBLE REJECTION`

---

## 3. A previously successful form can fail in a new channel

Tǎmadrama gives direct evidence that form/channel fit must be revalidated.

The operator first succeeded in:
- street/pop-up context;
- product retail.

After entering a core shopping mall, the customer mix changed.

Observed result:

> product-only retail did not attract the surrounding resident customer base sufficiently.

The operator then added:
- pottery classes;
- experiential activity;
- livestream selling.

Source:
- https://www.jingan.gov.cn/rmtzx/003008/003008004/20250916/dde899c5-7220-4a10-ab75-f77a2c1f3c25.html

Therefore:

```text
FORM WORKS IN CONTEXT A
!=
FORM WORKS IN CONTEXT B
```

A routing system needs switch/kill rules, not one-time translation.

---

## 4. Traditional form can fail when moved unchanged to a new market

A 2026 Anhui/Funan willow-weaving case provides strong negative evidence.

Operators reported that taking traditional local styles directly to overseas markets produced mediocre results.

They then:
- studied regional consumer habits;
- changed color;
- changed size/specification;
- changed product use;
- created storage, garden, pet and other categories;
- used **small-batch trial sales**;
- optimized from market response.

Source:
- https://fbh.anhuinews.com/project/202608/t20260827_9533549.html

This is important because it shows:

`CULTURAL AUTHENTICITY ALONE != MARKET FIT`

and:

`SMALL-BATCH TEST → FEEDBACK → ITERATE`

is already an observed commercial practice.

## 6. Cheap selection evidence is already visible

### 6.1 Shaoxing — peer scoring before production

A very important detail appears in the current reporting.

Student designers first make concept images.

Other students:
- review them;
- rate them;
- score them.

Only higher-scoring ideas are incorporated into later product designs.

Source:
- https://www.yzwb.net/news/yw/202609/t20260914_392413.html

This is not perfect market research.

But it is a real low-cost filter:

`CONCEPT → TARGET-COHORT SCORE → ONLY THEN PRODUCE`

### 6.2 Funan — small-batch trial sales

Observed process:

`ADAPT FORM → SMALL BATCH → REAL PURCHASE RESPONSE → OPTIMIZE`

Source:
- https://fbh.anhuinews.com/project/202608/t20260827_9533549.html

### 6.3 Shanghai — real-channel behavior

Observed process:

`SELL IN CONTEXT → OBSERVE WEAKNESS → SWITCH TO EXPERIENCE / LIVESTREAM`

Source:
- https://www.jingan.gov.cn/rmtzx/003008/003008004/20250916/dde899c5-7220-4a10-ab75-f77a2c1f3c25.html

## 7. Cross-operator selection criteria begin to converge

The evidence does not support one universal scoring formula.

But multiple operators and current industry analysis independently converge on several questions.

### A. Who is this for?

Selection must identify:
- target user;
- age/cohort;
- consumption context;
- geography/channel.

The Funan case changed products after studying overseas users.

### B. Where does it fit into actual life?

Current successful translations repeatedly move from:
- display-only;
- old household form;
- heritage-only symbolism

toward:
- commuting;
- home use;
- gifts;
- classes;
- experience;
- content;
- modern cultural consumption.

A 2026 national discussion of traditional crafts similarly argues that products need to re-enter contemporary daily life rather than remain museum/display objects.

Source:
- https://www.china.com.cn/opinion/think/2026-08/27/content_118666684.shtml

### C. Is it differentiated enough?

High labor does not rescue:
- old-fashioned style;
- generic form;
- low design value.

The selection question becomes:

> Is there a contemporary reason to choose this output?

### D. Can the economics work?

Current craft commercialization practice explicitly treats:
- material cost;
- labor time;
- production method;
- terminal price

as viability constraints.

Source:
- https://epaper.tianjinwe.com/tjrb/html/2026-03/03/content_143096_3185862.htm

### E. Can it be produced reliably?

Current craft research repeatedly identifies:
- production efficiency;
- consistency;
- standardization;
- material constraints

as market limits.

Source:
- https://www.cnacs.net.cn/14/202603/8034.html

### F. Does the channel fit?

The same capability can behave differently in:
- pop-up;
- mall;
- livestream;
- class;
- tourism;
- overseas retail.

### G. Can we cheaply test before deep investment?

Observed cheap signals now include:
- peer scoring;
- prototype concepts;
- small-batch sales;
- real-channel behavior;
- sell-through / delisting.

Current state:

`CROSS-OPERATOR SELECTION KERNEL = PARTIALLY CONVERGENT`

## 8. What is standardizable and what is not

This scan changes the curator-dependence conclusion.

### More standardizable than previously thought

The **front-end rejection layer** can increasingly use observable evidence:

```text
TARGET USER
+ USE SCENARIO
+ DIFFERENTIATION
+ COST / PRICE
+ PRODUCTION FEASIBILITY
+ QUALITY / SAFETY / IP
+ CHEAP TEST SIGNAL
+ CHANNEL RESPONSE
```

This can eliminate weak routes before expensive translation.

### Still strongly human

The harder creative decision remains:

> What *should* this old capability become?

For one source asset, valid forms may include:
- product;
- class;
- experience;
- content;
- license;
- cultural service;
- knowledge asset;
- nothing.

Evidence can reject bad directions.

It does not automatically invent the good one.

So the updated state is:

`FRONT-END SELECTION PARTIALLY STANDARDIZABLE / CREATIVE VALUE-FORM ROUTING HUMAN-HEAVY`

## 9. Rights and revenue sharing — structure repeats, percentages do not

### Actual 80/20 rule

The Shaoxing basket project publicly uses:

- 80% of sales proceeds → participating makers;
- 20% → community public-benefit fund.

Source:
- https://app.xinhuanet.com/news/article.html?articleId=2026090370923013799d45f3addd371a1aed6a7f

### Copyright licensing creates repeated income

Current National Copyright Administration reporting shows folk artists moving from:
- one-time sale

toward:
- copyright registration;
- derivative licensing;
- ongoing sales-linked income.

Source:
- https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/yjdt/202607/t20260720_999619.html

Another 2026 copyright-pilot report documents repeated licensing as a material revenue source rather than one-off sale.

Source:
- https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/yjdt/202608/t20260811_1002953.html

### But revenue allocation is not naturally settled

Current craft-design research documents recurring disputes around:
- who created which value;
- one-time licensing vs ongoing share;
- design contribution vs tradition-holder contribution;
- IP ownership.

Source:
- https://www.cnacs.net.cn/14/202603/8034.html

Fengtai itself is still exploring:
- IP protection;
- artisan certification.

Source:
- https://www.bjft.gov.cn/fengtaishibao/html/2026-02/04/content_9501_19279615.htm

Therefore:

```text
CONSENT / PROVENANCE / LICENSE / ATTRIBUTION / REVENUE-SHARING
= REUSABLE STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENT
```

but:

`UNIVERSAL SPLIT PERCENTAGE = NOT SUPPORTED`

## 11. Scan verdict

The strongest reusable primitive is no longer:

> “automatically recommend what an elder's capability should become.”

Current evidence does not support that.

A more defensible observed primitive is:

> **evidence-guided portfolio formation**

Meaning:

```text
discover many hidden assets
→ reject weak source/form/channel combinations cheaply
→ deepen only assets that survive user/use/cost/quality/context tests
→ let human creative routing work on a smaller qualified set
→ test again
→ kill / reroute / scale
```

Current state:

`EVIDENCE-GUIDED SELECTION KERNEL PARTIALLY OBSERVED`

`CREATIVE ROUTING HUMAN-HEAVY`

`GENERALIZED OPEN MARKET EDGE UNKNOWN`

---

## 12. Important contradiction

None of this is unique to older-adult assets.

Product managers, designers, retailers and cultural operators already use:
- targeting;
- prototypes;
- testing;
- pruning;
- channel feedback.

Therefore:

> **A reusable selection kernel does not itself prove our hidden missing edge.**

The next question must be elder-specific.

What friction remains because the source asset is:
- embodied in an older person;
- hard to describe;
- trust-sensitive;
- rights-sensitive;
- provenance-sensitive;
- potentially fragile/lost with time?

If nothing specifically elder-source-related remains, then the broad VT-B routing thesis collapses into ordinary product management + creative direction.
