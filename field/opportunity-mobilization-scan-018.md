# Opportunity Mobilization Scan 018 — platform bypass often means users want less intermediation

Date: 2026-09-19

## Result

Scan 018 tested a tempting inference:

> if users keep leaving mature platforms for WeChat/direct deals, maybe the platform still misses an important edge.

Result:

# `RETAINED_CANDIDATES = 0`

The dominant evidence points somewhere else:

# `PLATFORM BYPASS != PLATFORM CAPABILITY GAP`

Often the user is not asking for a better intermediary.

They are trying to remove the intermediary.

---

## 1. Freight off-platform contact — fee story, protection loss

A 2026 freight case describes a shipper who posted a load on a mature platform.

A driver proposed:

- adding WeChat / changing phone contact;
- settling outside the platform;
- negotiating a lower price;

under the stated rationale that both sides could save platform cost.

The shipper agreed.

The eventual case exposed serious off-platform cargo risk.

Sources:

- https://news.sina.com.cn/c/2026-07-27/doc-inikfsve9826089.shtml
- https://xxgk.mot.gov.cn/2020/xzgfxwj/202601/t20260126_4199002.html

The important commercial lesson is not the specific fraud mechanism.

It is:

```text
PLATFORM PROTECTION EXISTS
+
USERS STILL LEAVE
BECAUSE
DIRECT DEAL LOOKS CHEAPER
```

The bypass does not prove a missing trust layer.

The incumbent platform already owns:

- transaction records;
- carrier rules;
- responsibility;
- complaints/dispute process;
- monitoring.

Going off-platform removes some of that protection.

Judgment:

`FEE_AVOIDANCE / RISK_INCREASES_OFF_PLATFORM / NO_NEW_ROUTE`

## 2. “飞单” is common enough for platforms to govern explicitly

Current service-retail rules explicitly prohibit merchants from:

- persuading a user to cancel a platform order;
- switching to cash;
- Alipay/WeChat payment;
- another platform;
- store/private membership payment.

Sources:

- https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/1173?activeRule=1
- https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/416017

This proves bypass behavior is real.

It does **not** prove an unmet routing need.

Judgment:

`BYPASS_REAL / MOTIVE_NOT_A_NEW_GAP`

## 3. Restaurant private-domain migration — bypass is explicitly a margin strategy

Current merchant software directly markets:

```text
PLATFORM CUSTOMER
→ WECHAT / PRIVATE DOMAIN
→ SELF-OPERATED ORDERING
→ MEMBERSHIP / REPEAT PURCHASE
```

The stated business reasons include:

- reduce platform commission;
- reduce promotion cost;
- own the repeat relationship;
- operate stored value / coupons / membership directly.

Source:

- https://www.youzan.com/chanpin/waimaiyouli

This is the opposite of a Rooter signal.

The merchant wants:

# fewer intermediaries.

Judgment:

`COMMISSION_AVOIDANCE_AND_DIRECT_REPEAT / NO_ROOTER_GAP`

## 4. Home-service platform pressure — pricing competition, not missing control-plane functionality

Current reporting on appliance repair shows that some platform economics can be severe.

One technician described a platform arrangement where his share was 43%, implying 57% stayed at the platform layer for that component.

High platform take can:

- compress technician income;
- encourage add-on charges;
- create pressure for independent/off-platform work.

Sources:

- https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2026/0402/589588.shtml
- https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2026/0402/589622.shtml

But the market already reacts competitively.

Current alternatives include platforms advertising:

- zero/no commission;
- technician commission exemption;
- free job publishing;
- direct/self-selected quotes.

Sources:

- https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E6%AD%A3%E4%BA%8B%E5%A4%9A%E6%8E%A5%E5%8D%95-%E9%99%84%E8%BF%91%E5%B8%88%E5%82%85%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%85%E7%BB%B4%E4%BF%AE%E5%B9%B3%E5%8F%B0/id1143453476
- https://www.jdd51.com/index/official/masterInResidence
- https://www.zysaz.com/

So:

```text
HIGH COMMISSION
!=
MISSING MARKET FUNCTION
```

It is a platform-pricing/competition problem.

Judgment:

`PLATFORM_PRICING_COMPETITION / NO_DISTINCT_ROUTE`

## 5. Machining platforms already demonstrate zero-commission matching

Current machining platforms advertise:

- buyers publish requests free;
- no transaction commission;
- tens of thousands of factories;
- rapid matching/quotation.

Source:

- https://www.xtwaixie.com/

Machining communities also prefer direct buyer-factory contact.

Source:

- https://www.gejigeji.cn/

Therefore:

```text
DIRECT DEAL
```

is not evidence that a new escrow/settlement layer is missing.

It may be the participants' preferred endpoint.

Judgment:

`LOW_COMMISSION_ALREADY_AVAILABLE / HIGH_BYPASS / NO_NEW_CONTROL_PLANE`

# Rooter implication

Rooter needs to earn money from a control-plane function that users do not immediately want to remove.

If:

```text
buyer knows supplier
+
task repeats
+
transaction can safely go direct
```

then the middle layer has weak durability.

The strongest current evidence says many platform bypasses are driven by:

- fee avoidance;
- private customer ownership;
- repeat direct relationship;
- less platform process.

These are structurally hostile to a transaction-commission Rooter model.

# Next search zone

Fresh Scan 019 should invert the evidence test.

Search markets where:

> buyer and supplier already know each other, but both still voluntarily keep paying a middle layer on repeated transactions.

That is evidence of **non-bypassable control-plane value**.

The persistent intermediary may own:

- live multi-provider availability;
- replacement;
- liability;
- acceptance;
- financing;
- compliance;
- aggregation;
- settlement;
- geographic coverage.

Then search for a smaller Xuzhou/Jiangsu market where the same function is currently handled informally but not yet maturely productized.

This is a stronger starting point than asking where people bypass platforms.
