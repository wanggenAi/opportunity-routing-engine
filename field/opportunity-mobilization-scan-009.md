## Why this scan matters

OMS-017 showed:

```text
REAL SERVICE DEMAND
!=
SELF-PROPELLED ROOTER CHANNEL
```

Scan 009 therefore started one step later:

```text
BUYER ALREADY SEARCHING / RENTING / REPAIRING / BUYING
+
SUPPLIER ALREADY SELLING / SEEKING UTILIZATION
```

The question was only:

> after that natural flow already exists, is there still a narrow missing edge that incumbent routes fail to handle?

Across the tested categories, the answer was no.

# Main learning

The scan strengthens an important rule:

```text
NATURAL DEMAND
+
NATURAL SUPPLY
!=
OPEN ROOTER GAP
```

In fact, high-frequency urgent events are often where incumbents are strongest.

Rental, MRO, emergency quality, maintenance and urgent machining already have:

- direct providers;
- inventory;
- framework contracts;
- marketplaces;
- dispatch;
- acceptance;
- settlement.

So the next scan should stop enumerating standard industrial service categories.
