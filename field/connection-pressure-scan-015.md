# Connection Pressure Scan 015 — Risk Allocation Fit

Observed: 2026-09-18

## Why this scan exists

Scans 013–014 established that value can remain blocked because a counterparty cannot verify condition, capability, rights or quality. This scan asks the adjacent question:

> even when capability/value is real, is the exchange blocked because the wrong actor is forced to bear a downside it cannot control?

This is **not** a new ontology and not a search for rental/insurance businesses. It deepens the existing `RISK_BEARING_ORCHESTRATOR` and `ACCESS_THRESHOLD_COLLAPSE` mechanisms.

## Cross-domain reality evidence

### Industrial equipment

XCMG's current joint-leasing structure separates temporary use from full ownership. The lessor purchases the asset, the customer rents it, and a professional O&M party can maintain it. The customer may renew, return or buy at lease end. XCMG explicitly positions this for short, temporary and seasonal needs and highlights avoidance of repair/maintenance burden and technology-obsolescence risk.

Source: https://www.xcmg.com/financial/financial-programmes.htm

**Reading:** this is not merely financing. The user obtains the productive use of a high-fixed-cost asset without accepting the entire ownership/residual/maintenance risk bundle.

### Rehabilitation / assistive devices

Wuxi already has real rental usage rather than only policy intent: public material reported nearly 1,500 assistive-device lending/rental uses and eight smart rental points before the 2026 expansion. In 2026 the city organized 50 community rental points and a 57-product catalogue. Product-entry rules require repeat cleaning/disinfection durability, supplier maintenance training, local after-sales capability and warranty support.

Sources:
- https://cl.wuxi.gov.cn/doc/2026/01/09/4715456.shtml
- https://www.wuxi.gov.cn/doc/2026/05/22/4779809.shtml
- https://mzj.wuxi.gov.cn/doc/2026/01/16/4719282.shtml

**Reading:** a person with a two- or three-month rehabilitation need does not need to own an asset that may soon become idle. The specialist system can own, clean, maintain and recirculate it.

### Technology transfer

A Jiangsu enterprise used a university technology under a first-use-then-pay arrangement. After the technology reached the expected effect, the enterprise paid RMB 700,000 and expanded subsequent cooperation to RMB 3 million. The enterprise publicly described the arrangement as reducing uncertainty around investing in new technology. Current Jiangsu rules continue supporting first-use-then-pay and guarantee/insurance support.

Sources:
- https://jszwb.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2025/2/18/art_71797_11494090.html
- https://kxjst.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2025/12/11/art_82571_11689218.html
- https://www.js.gov.cn/art/2026/8/14/art_84418_11816317.html

**Reading:** the technology can be valuable while the buyer rationally refuses a large irreversible commitment before seeing evidence. Changing the payment/risk sequence can create the flow.

### Consumer fulfillment

Nantong's 2026 `通畅退` system reported 25,000+ participating offline no-reason-return merchants and uses an advance-payment fund pool for difficult refund cases, followed by responsibility tracing/recovery.

Source: https://scjgj.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/30/art_70155_11810738.html

**Reading:** a credible node can temporarily absorb a fulfillment/refund downside and recover later from the responsible party.

## Refined search rule

### `RISK_ALLOCATION_FIT`

Do not ask only:

`Who has capability?` + `Who has need?`

Also ask:

1. What exact downside blocks commitment?
2. Who is currently forced to bear it?
3. Can that actor actually control/diversify/recover that risk?
4. Which other actor is structurally better able to own, maintain, price, insure, reuse, recover or absorb it?
5. Has reality already begun reallocating the risk through rental, trial-before-payment, repurchase, guarantee, insurance, return rights or another partial flow?

A connection is stronger when reality already shows a risk-allocation workaround.

## Compound gate with verifiability

Risk reassignment is usually unstable without trusted state/outcome evidence.

Examples:

- first-use-then-pay needs an agreed observable outcome before payment is triggered;
- equipment leasing needs asset/use/maintenance/return state;
- assistive-device reuse needs qualification, cleaning and maintenance accountability;
- advance refund needs transaction identity and a recoverable responsibility chain.

Therefore:

`RISK_REALLOCATION_REQUIRES_VERIFIABILITY`

is a cross-scan compound gate, not a new business category.

## AI / digital transformation boundary

Jiangsu's 2026 AI+manufacturing and scene-opening policies explicitly discuss risk compensation, insurance around pilot/intermediate-test services and scene-application risk protection. This proves institutional recognition of adoption risk, **not** a Xuzhou paid missing edge.

Do not create an `AI outcome insurance` or `digital transformation guarantee` formation unless a named enterprise shows:

- the technology/provider is otherwise acceptable;
- existing pilot/subsidy/verification routes have been considered;
- the project is still blocked specifically by outcome/downside allocation;
- a bounded risk-sharing structure would change a real decision.
