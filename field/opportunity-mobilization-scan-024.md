# Opportunity Mobilization Scan 024 — buyer cost first

Date: 2026-09-19

## Result

Fresh Scan 024 stopped abstract mechanism-first scanning.

The rule was:

# `BUYER COST FIRST`

Do not begin with a marketplace idea, resource category, coordination mechanism or service format.

First bind a **named current private actor** to an observed economic cost:

- paying extra;
- waiting;
- rerouting;
- losing output/orders;
- or repeatedly using a costly workaround.

Only then inspect incumbents and ask whether a post-incumbent missing edge remains.

## Strongest current event — 徐州科亚机电有限公司

A current June 2026 report identifies **徐州科亚机电有限公司** in Fengxian and its general manager 王在峰.

The company reported that its self-developed wireless controller moved production from roughly 200 units/hour for the prior wired design to roughly 800–900 units/hour, yet overseas demand still exceeded fulfillment capacity. To preserve on-time delivery, it was **giving up part of the overseas orders**.

Source:
- https://www.xhby.net/content/s6a3b6e52e4b010b7fda2187a.html

This is materially stronger than the patterns in recent scans because the economic pressure is explicit:

`NAMED ACTOR + CURRENT EVENT + FOREGONE ORDERS`

The lost revenue amount is not public, so it remains unknown.

## Why this is not promoted

The same local reality creates strong counterevidence.

A September 2026 report describes Fengxian as having:

- 1,400+ complete-vehicle and parts manufacturers;
- annual complete-vehicle capacity above 5 million;
- motor capacity above 15 million;
- a roughly 30-minute procurement circle for almost every vehicle part except chips.

Source:
- https://zgjssw.jschina.com.cn/yaowen/202609/t20260913_8593060.shtml

Public factory listings and manufacturer sites also show local control-unit / electric-drive production and customization capacity, including private-label and sample-based processing.

Examples:
- https://www.aichangjia.com/factory/52123.html
- https://www.jumboot.com.cn/

Therefore the unresolved question is not:

> Can another factory theoretically make something?

It is:

> **Why is Keya still giving up orders instead of routing overflow production through existing nearby capacity?**

Possible reasons include proprietary process/IP, quality consistency, certification, test/acceptance requirements, component sourcing or unwillingness to outsource.

Those are **hypotheses only**. No current public evidence found in this scan proves any of them.

So:

`LOST ORDERS != ROUTABLE OVERFLOW`

and:

`NAMED COST EVENT != OPEN ROUTING GAP`

Judgment:

`BOUND_COST_EVENT / OBSERVATION_ONLY / NO_PROMOTION`

No founder outreach is released yet.

## Residual audit — closed

A targeted follow-up searched for attributable evidence of:

- outsourcing / subcontracting / OEM / second-source production;
- supplier qualification or refusal;
- IP/process-transfer constraints;
- quality, certification or test constraints;
- component bottlenecks;
- current capacity expansion behavior.

Public materials confirm that Keya has a deep in-house manufacturing and quality stack, including placement/SMT equipment, wave-soldering equipment, automated testing, patents and automotive quality-system claims. They also confirm abundant nearby Fengxian manufacturing capacity.

Those facts still do **not** prove why overflow production is not externalized.

The audit found no attributable public statement that Keya tried an external-capacity route and failed, rejected such a route for a stated reason, or is blocked by a named IP/quality/certification/component constraint.

Additional audit source:
- https://www.coatingol.com/xinxi/article-iewy-68609.html

Therefore the residual closes fail-closed:

`REAL BUYER COST + NO OBSERVED POST-INCUMBENT MISSING EDGE = NO CANDIDATE`

Final Keya judgment:

`CLOSED_NO_OBSERVED_MISSING_EDGE`

## Named cost events that are already closed

### 江苏本优机械有限公司 — verification downtime

A March 2026 report says a large-diameter flowmeter previously had to be removed and shipped to Nanjing or farther for verification, taking roughly half a month and causing production-line shutdown and order delay.

The current Xuzhou Inspection and Testing Center can now perform the calibration locally and on-site without shutdown/removal.

Source:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202603/t20260316_8561864.shtml

Judgment:

`CLOSED_BY_CURRENT_LOCAL_INSTITUTIONAL_ROUTE`

Current reporting states that the rail route to Ningbo Port cut per-container freight from about RMB 4,100 to RMB 2,700 for roughly 100 containers per month, saving more than RMB 100,000 monthly.

Source:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/nantong/202604/t20260427_8570309.shtml

Judgment:

`CLOSED_BY_CURRENT_MULTIMODAL_ROUTE`

## What was rejected before candidate formation

This scan discarded:

- anonymous buyer stories embedded in service-provider marketing copy;
- provider capability/price pages without a named buyer cost event;
- named cost events whose friction is already eliminated by a current route.

These are not retained merely because the underlying pain was real.

But the evidence boundary still matters:

# `NAMED COST EVENT != OPEN ROUTING GAP`
