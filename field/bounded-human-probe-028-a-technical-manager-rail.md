## Purpose

This is not a sales call and not a generic interview.

It resolves exactly one commercial unknown:

> Can an external project-based technical manager with a self-sourced qualified enterprise technology demand/project enter an existing Xuzhou technical-manager office's contract / invoice / commission rail?

## What is already known

Do not waste the call asking whether technical-manager offices exist or whether commissions are theoretically possible.

The 2026 Jiangsu rules already establish that offices may:

- recruit technical managers from society;
- use project cooperation to form affiliation;
- sign standardized affiliation/cooperation agreements;
- settle technology-transfer service commissions by agreement;
- sign technology-transaction service contracts;
- issue invoices;
- conduct settlement;
- obtain lawful service income.

Source:

## Questions

Ask only these, in order:

1. **External cooperation**  
   你们目前是否接受社会化/外部技术经理人，以“项目合作”或挂靠方式一起做技术转移项目？

2. **Self-sourced project**  
   如果我自己找到一个真实企业技术需求，或者已经拿到一个比较明确的技术转移项目，能不能先把项目给你们审核，再决定是否建立挂靠/项目合作关系？

3. **Mandatory qualification**  
   外部技术经理人目前必须具备哪些硬性条件？例如省技术市场备案、初级技术经理人培训证书、职称、既往成交记录，哪些是必须的，哪些只是加分项？

4. **Order of entry**  
   是先完成江苏省技术经理人备案，再签挂靠/项目合作协议，还是你们审核通过后协助办理？

5. **Contract controller**  
   项目成立后，一般是谁和企业/成果方签技术转移服务合同？是事务所作为合同主体吗？

6. **Invoice / payment controller**  
   服务费一般由谁开票、谁收款？

7. **Commission basis**  
   外部技术经理人的佣金怎么确定？有固定比例、事务所统一标准，还是每个项目单独约定？

8. **Settlement trigger**  
   什么节点算可以给技术经理人结算？企业付服务费以后、技术合同登记以后、技术交易款到账以后、项目验收以后，还是另有标准？

9. **Self-sourced attribution**  
   如果企业需求和技术解决方都是我自己找到的，这种项目你们接不接？项目归属和佣金怎么认定？

10. **Restrictions**  
    有没有行业、地区、金额、排他、保密、知识产权、项目失败责任方面的硬限制？

## Evidence capture

Persist:

- call date/time;
- department/person role if voluntarily provided;
- exact answer to each question;
- exact unknown/refusal;
- whether they ask for a project brief;
- whether they provide an agreement/template;
- whether they require training/registration first;
- any stated fee/commission formula;
- settlement trigger;
- next legitimate step.

Do not infer silence as acceptance.

## Promotion rule

Promote the rail to `ACCESS_CONFIRMED` only if the response establishes:

```text
external/project-based intake = YES
+
mandatory qualification is known
+
contract/invoice controller is known
+
commission basis is known
+
settlement trigger is known
```

Anything less remains `PARTIAL / UNKNOWN`.

## Kill rule

Kill this target route if:

- it only serves internal staff/current institutional partners;
- external managers cannot bring self-sourced projects;
- no lawful commission/service-income path is available to the external manager;
- the office requires a condition that makes the current operator route non-executable.

A target-level kill does not kill the Jiangsu technical-manager rail as a whole.
