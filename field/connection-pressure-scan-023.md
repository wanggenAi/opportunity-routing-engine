# Connection Pressure Scan 023 — Spatial Density and Effective Service Radius

Observed: 2026-09-18

## Why this scan exists

Scan 022 tightened the idea of idle capacity into callable capacity: time, equipment, labor or storage is not a usable resource merely because it is theoretically idle.

This scan asks the spatial version of the same question:

> can a real resource/capability and a real need remain disconnected because the exchange is outside its economic service radius or below the density needed to amortize coordination cost?

This is not a new ontology and not a thesis for a geographic marketplace. It deepens existing ACCESS_THRESHOLD_COLLAPSE, TIME_TO_VALUE_GATE and FLEXIBILITY_CALLABILITY_GATE.

## Cross-domain evidence

### 1. Xuzhou large-bore flowmeter verification

A manufacturer previously had to dismantle a large-bore flowmeter and send it to Nanjing or farther. The trip took roughly half a month and could stop the production line.

After Xuzhou obtained local full-range liquid-flowmeter verification capability, technicians could bring equipment into the factory and calibrate online.

Source:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202603/t20260316_8561864.shtml

Reading: the capability existed in the broader region before it became functionally available to this production system. Localization changed downtime, travel and handling economics.

### 2. Xuzhou customized commuting

Xuzhou now operates more than 140 customized bus routes and provides recurring commuter service to more than 85 enterprises/institutions. Public reporting describes the operator first mapping where employees live, when they travel and which routes they prefer; enterprise commuting cost is reported to fall by about 20%.

Source:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202602/t20260210_8556821.shtml

Reading: one person's trip is not a route. A route becomes an economic resource after origin, destination and time demand become sufficiently dense and repeatable.

### 3. Shared food-testing laboratory

Seven food producers concentrated in one Nanjing market faced repeated testing limits. A local shared laboratory reduced reported testing cost by about 80% and reduced report time from about three days to one day.

Sources:
- https://finance.jschina.com.cn/tj/scjd/202412/t20241211_s67b307cbe4b06b14f8cc89eb.shtml
- https://gjzx.jschina.com.cn/TodayJiangsu/202405/t20240527_8274870.shtml

Reading: specialist equipment/expert time becomes economically shareable when a sufficiently dense cluster of repeated bounded tasks exists nearby.

### 4. Small hazardous-waste collection

Jiangsu has explicitly identified small hazardous-waste generators as facing high disposal cost and difficult access. By the end of 2024, 95 centralized collection entities served more than 59,000 small-quantity waste-generating enterprises.

Source:
- https://gjzx.jschina.com.cn/PressConference/202506/t20250630_8500005.shtml

Reading: compliant downstream capability can exist while a small generator remains economically excluded. Aggregation converts dispersed sub-threshold batches into a callable route.

### 5. Waste heat / steam

Yangzhou connected residual heat from waste-incineration power generation to nearby enterprises through a heating network and developed five heat users.

In Lianyungang, an industrial-waste-heat project used about 4 km of steam pipeline and additional hot/cold-water pipelines to supply nearby breeding, hospitality and greenhouse users.

Sources:
- https://jsnews.jschina.com.cn/yz/a/202303/t20230306_3175396.shtml
- https://jsnews.jschina.com.cn/lyg/a/202111/t20211126_2900470.shtml

Reading: heat may have positive technical value but no economic value outside a practical transmission radius. Infrastructure and nearby demand create the reachable market.

### 6. Brewery wastewater -> wastewater-treatment carbon source

A Liyang wastewater operator identified brewery wastewater as a usable supplemental carbon source while the treatment plant was purchasing external carbon source. The parties evaluated wastewater quality and the geographic relationship between the two facilities, performed tests, obtained environmental approval and signed a raw-water agreement.

Reported external carbon-source dosage fell from roughly 175 mg/L to 35 mg/L during the trial.

Source:
- https://gongyi.jschina.com.cn/gdxw/202506/t20250623_3565638.shtml

Reading: material complementarity does not automatically create exchange. Quality, safety, distance, testability, regulatory acceptance and a contract all had to fit.

## Search-rule refinement

### SPATIAL_ECONOMIC_RADIUS

For each candidate connection, ask:

1. At what distance or travel/handling time does logistics, downtime, spoilage, response delay or infrastructure cost exceed the obtainable surplus?
2. Must the resource move, or can the capability move to the actor?
3. Can localization, a mobile service, aggregation hub, pipeline, cold chain or remote standardization expand the effective radius?
4. Does expanding the radius actually produce an accepted exchange?

### DENSITY_THRESHOLD_BEFORE_ORCHESTRATION

Ask:

1. How many similar bounded needs must coexist in a geographic/time window before a shared route/node becomes economic?
2. Is demand recurring enough to amortize fixed coordination cost?
3. Does aggregation preserve permission, compatibility and acceptance?
4. Is there an anchor actor whose stable demand makes the shared node viable?

### RADIUS_EXPANSION

Observed mechanisms include:

- localize the capability;
- move equipment/specialist to the actor rather than moving the actor/object;
- aggregate dispersed small batches;
- build a hub/shared node;
- build physical network infrastructure;
- standardize the task enough for remote or pooled execution.

These are not opportunities by themselves. They are ways reality changes whether a connection is callable.

## Hard boundaries
