# Opportunity Mobilization Scan 013 — handoff pain usually collapses into accountable scope

Date: 2026-09-18

## Result

Fresh Scan 013 tested a narrower proposition:

> Provider A and Provider B both exist, but the job loses time/money at the interface between them.

Result:

# `RETAINED_CANDIDATES = 0`

The cross-industry finding is:

# `HANDOFF PAIN != OPEN ROUTING GAP`

When the handoff is economically important, the market often responds by assigning the complete outcome to one accountable contractor or by expanding a vertical provider's service boundary.

---

## 1. Automation equipment — prime contractor absorbs the handoff

A current Jiangsu procurement package assigns one contractor responsibility for:

- equipment supply;
- electrical-cabinet integration;
- field assembly/wiring;
- system commissioning;
- documentation/training;
- final acceptance.

Most payment is tied to final acceptance and warranty money is retained.

The buyer is not asking a neutral router to coordinate several vendors.

The buyer contracts for:

```text
ONE ACCOUNTABLE OUTCOME OWNER
```

Judgment:

`CLOSED_PRIME_CONTRACTOR_ROUTE`

## 2. Multi-system installation — contract owns the interface

A current audio-system procurement includes:

Again, the handoff problem is real.

# Cross-industry lesson

The market response to painful handoffs is often:

```text
HANDOFF COST BECOMES LARGE
↓
BUYER DEMANDS ONE OUTCOME OWNER
↓
PRIME / TURNKEY / FULL-LIFECYCLE PROVIDER EXPANDS SCOPE
↓
ACCEPTANCE + WARRANTY ATTACH TO THAT PROVIDER
```

That is different from:

```text
TWO PROVIDERS
+
NEUTRAL ROUTER
```

A neutral router inserted into the first structure would inherit:

- scope disputes;
- schedule coordination;
- access planning;
- evidence handover;
- acceptance ownership;
- liability.

Rooter would become the project manager/general contractor.

That violates operator exit.

---

# What was not found

No named Xuzhou/Jiangsu transaction was bound where:

1. two mature adjacent providers both exist;
2. prime/turnkey/full-lifecycle options were considered;
3. the handoff still repeatedly causes measurable loss;
4. neither incumbent owns the interface;
5. the interface is small and standardizable;
6. Rooter can route it without becoming project manager.

Therefore there is no current promotion.
