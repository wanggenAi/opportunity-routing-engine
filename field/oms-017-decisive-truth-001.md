## 1. Demand is now proven directionally

The 2026-09-08 **徐州日报** report on 徐州多帮三维科技 is unusually valuable because it records both market research and operating history.

Before launch, the team spent more than a month visiting nearly ten local manufacturers and concluded:

- local enterprises had reverse-engineering demand;
- local providers were hard to find at the time.

The same company now reports real customer work including:

- large-tonnage engineering-machinery deformation-repair scanning/modeling;
- Mercedes-AMG body-part scanning/modeling and 3D-print trials;
- a 17-meter large-component project.

Source:

- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202609/08/content_56400.html

So this is not an invented problem.

---

## 2. But the same source kills the self-propulsion assumption

The company first tried online technical-service platforms.

Its own account says:

- it worked intensively on online acquisition;
- roughly six months produced only about **RMB 7,000** revenue;
- revenue was largely consumed by acquisition;
- the founder concluded that the online model did not fit the current stage.

The company then shifted toward:

- local industry connections;
- industrial-park targeted visits;
- Douyin / Kuaishou / Xiaohongshu content.

This is direct evidence about market motion.

```text
REAL DEMAND
+
REAL SERVICE
!=
SELF-PROPELLED ROUTING FLOW
```

The current buyer often has to be found and educated.

That is exactly the commercial burden 根哥 does not want to inherit.

- 徐州 逆向建模 服务采购;
- engineering-machinery on-site scan service demand;
- the distinctive projects reported by 多帮三维.

No clean named Xuzhou manufacturing buyer was publicly bound to:

- one recent paid outsourced on-site event;
- how the buyer searched;
- current time/cost;
- a fragmented procurement path that a neutral router uniquely fixes.

The visible public evidence is still mostly:

- provider-reported cases;
- provider marketing;

Absence of a procurement notice alone does not prove no demand.

But together with the provider's direct acquisition history, it does not support a self-propelled Rooter channel.
