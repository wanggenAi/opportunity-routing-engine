## 1. 南京六城食品 — strong named precedent, already solved

南京六城食品 previously:
- maintained its own laboratory;
- paid equipment / reagent / personnel operating costs;
- still had tests it could not do internally;
- had to send those tests to qualified third-party institutions.

A shared laboratory changed the transaction unit.

Current reporting says:
- per-variety / per-batch testing is below RMB 30;
- testing cost fell about 80%;
- report turnaround dropped from roughly three days to one day.

Source:
- https://njsj.nanjing.gov.cn/njszwfwglbgs/202606/t20260617_5861941.html

Judgment: `STRONG_MECHANISM_PRECEDENT / ALREADY_SOLVED`

## 2. 兴化 small food producers — cluster sharing compresses annual cost

Current 2026 reporting says small food enterprises previously faced missing equipment, missing inspection staff, high testing spend, and annual full-testing cost around RMB 140,000.

Shared laboratories reduced average annual testing cost to about RMB 20,000.

Source:
- https://www.xinghua.gov.cn/ztzl/yhyshj/yhyshj/art_212230903.html

This confirms: `LARGE PROFESSIONAL UNIT -> SHARED CAPACITY -> SMALL RECURRING UNIT`.

## 3. Commercial inspection is already sold in bounded small units

Current inspection platforms advertise basic inspection around RMB 500–550 per order/visit, standardized checklists, photos, reports, short turnaround and explicit fit for small-batch orders.

Sources:
- https://www.inspector.ltd/
- https://www.globalinsp.com/

## 4. Parcel-level evidence reaches RMB 2–6 per ticket

When the task is highly standardized, responsibility can be compressed much further.

SF Express currently sells return inspection from RMB 2 per ticket up to RMB 6 per ticket depending on inspection/photo combination.

Sources:
- https://www.sf-express.com/chn/sc/express/add-service/inspection
- https://www.sf-express.com/chn/sc/express/add-service/photo-return-proof

Cross-border parcel operators also advertise simple inspection around RMB 2–3 per order.

Source:
- https://www.kuajing84.com/index/index/help_details/help_id/MDAwMDAwMDAwMH7QtWM.html

## 5. Small-batch fulfillment also compresses toward one unit

Current overseas-warehouse / fulfillment providers explicitly advertise no minimum inbound quantity, a few pieces accepted, one-piece fulfillment and no small-order surcharge in some offerings.

# Main learning

`MINIMUM SERVICE UNIT MISMATCH` can destroy value.

But it is also one of the first mismatches mature markets attack through shared laboratories, pooled demand, standard checklists, per-order inspection, per-ticket evidence, one-piece fulfillment and group-buy pricing.

Therefore the next candidate cannot come from a price list alone. It must come from an observed actor still left out.
