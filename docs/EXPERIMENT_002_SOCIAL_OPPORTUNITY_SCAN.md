# EXPERIMENT 002 — Xuzhou Actor-First Social Opportunity Scan

Status: `ACTIVE — SCAN V2 COMPLETE / FIELD VALIDATION ACTIVE`

Date opened: 2026-09-08
Last updated: 2026-09-10

## Objective

Test whether the Opportunity Routing Engine can convert current Xuzhou social/market change into a ranked set of **transaction-worthy opportunities across individuals, households, groups, businesses and institutions**, rather than collapsing into enterprise-only ideas.

Xuzhou is the first field laboratory because online hypotheses can be converted into local observation, payer commitments and small transactions.

## Core question

> Can a structured actor-first scan identify opportunities with real need actors, identifiable payers, observable payment/workaround behavior, accessible capabilities/resources, bounded transactions and a realistic path to first cash — and can field evidence then eliminate the attractive-but-false ones?

## Mandatory actor coverage

The scan must deliberately cover at least:
1. university students / graduates;
2. young workers / flexible workers / job seekers;
3. single or renting young adults;
4. parents / households / children;
5. elderly people / caregivers / adult children;
6. pet owners;
7. value-conscious consumers;
8. skilled workers / farmers / local service workers;
9. merchants / self-employed operators;
10. SMEs / manufacturers;
11. institutions / communities / schools;
12. holders of idle personal or organizational resources: skill, time, vehicles, equipment, inventory, space, data, etc.

## Anti-bias requirement

Do not assume enterprises are the payer.

Every serious candidate must map:

```text
NEED_ACTOR
BENEFICIARY
PAYER
CAPABILITY_PROVIDER
RESOURCE_OWNER
SPONSOR
ORCHESTRATOR
TRANSACTION_TYPE
```

Compare B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B, sponsored and multi-sided structures under the same scorecard.

If more than half of promoted opportunities become enterprise-paid, comparative evidence must explain why. Ease of finding enterprise budgets is not evidence.

## Required evidence per serious candidate

Collect where available:
- measurable change signal;
- exact actor/group;
- observed behavior;
- repeated friction / desired outcome;
- current workaround;
- beneficiary;
- payer candidates;
- evidence of money/time/risk/headcount spent;
- existing purchase/service/transaction evidence;
- candidate capabilities/resources;
- incumbent/substitute strength;
- trust/safety/legal constraints;
- acquisition feasibility;
- bounded transaction design;
- shortest decisive field test;
- stop rule.

Public/free provision is both need evidence and an incumbent. It is not private willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Current ranking rule

Use `docs/OPPORTUNITY_SCORECARD.md` exactly.

Do not promote to `A: TEST NOW` if:
- actor roles are vague;
- payer gate is unknown;
- transactionability is unknown;
- legal/trust/safety fails;
- payment evidence is only narrative;
- first validation requires material fixed cost.

A high raw score does not override a hard gate.

## Scan-stage success criteria

The research scan stage required:
1. >=12 evidence-backed hypotheses across multiple actor groups;
2. meaningful personal/household and organizational representation;
3. >=3 candidates scoring >=65 with no failed hard gate;
4. >=1 plausible paid micro-test within 30 days;
5. >=1 serious non-enterprise-payer candidate unless evidence rejects all;
6. clear rejection reasons;
7. evidence-based ranking.

## Current falsification phase

EXP-002 now succeeds or fails based on whether the promoted candidates survive real field evidence.

The next evidence threshold is not another online scan. It is:
- Level 3: deposit / sponsor / buyer commitment;
- Level 4: completed paid transaction + accepted delivery;
- Level 5: repeat / referral.

If the top candidates repeatedly fail to convert despite credible public evidence, update the method rather than rationalizing the failures.

```text
3. EXP-003 — run bounded SME price/project conversations in parallel
4. EXP-004 — resolve trust/home-access economics before paid home-entry work
```

Use `docs/FIELD_VALIDATION_PLAYBOOK_XUZHOU.md`.

## Failure criteria

Revise the method if:
- scans repeatedly revert to companies/SMEs without comparative evidence;
- personal candidates rely on complaints without payment/workaround behavior;
- need actor and payer are conflated;
- free/public supply is incorrectly counted as private payment evidence;
- scores ignore canonical penalties;
- top candidates cannot convert Level 1–2 evidence into Level 3–4 commitments;
- orchestration adds no value beyond direct introductions;
- acquisition/support/trust cost destroys low-ticket economics;
- field failures are explained away instead of recorded as rejection evidence.

## Build rule

No marketplace/platform build is authorized by EXP-002.

Only repeated paid transactions and observed recurring bottlenecks can justify automation.
