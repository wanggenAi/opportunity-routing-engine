# Resource Activation Thesis

Status: `CANONICAL / LOCKED PRINCIPLE`

Constitutional parent: `docs/LATENT_VALUE_DOCTRINE.md`.

Causal-depth constraint: `docs/STRUCTURAL_FRICTION_DISCOVERY_PRINCIPLE.md`.

## Purpose

The system does not exist merely to discover a profitable product category, an explicit demand list or a supplier list.

Its deeper objective is to discover **latent value**: value that is underused, overlooked, fragmented, stranded, mispriced, misallocated, uncombined or not yet recognized by the actors who hold or experience it.

The system begins from actors and reality, not from a permanent supply/demand split.

```text
ACTOR A: endowments + constraints + behavior + hidden/unused value + deficits
                         ×
ACTOR B: endowments + constraints + behavior + hidden/unused value + deficits
                         ↓
             COMPLEMENTARY STRUCTURE
                         ↓
               EXCHANGE HYPOTHESIS
                         ↓
         EVIDENCE + BLOCKER DISCOVERY
                         ↓
            RESOURCE ACTIVATION DESIGN
                         ↓
CAPABILITY UNITS + RULES + INCENTIVES + ACCEPTANCE
                         ↓
             VALUE CREATED FOR ACTORS
                         ↓
       REPEAT / LEARNING / BETTER ALLOCATION
```

This is a resource-allocation and value-discovery thesis, not a promise that every idle resource has commercial value.

## Actors are not fixed sides

Do not treat one actor as permanently "demand" and another as permanently "supply".

The same actor can simultaneously be:
- a resource owner;
- a beneficiary;
- a payer;
- a capability provider;
- a demand source;
- a trusted channel;
- a source of information;
- a source of recurring transaction flow;
- an owner of latent value;
- an actor experiencing a deficit or friction.

`NEED_ACTOR` and `RESOURCE_OWNER` are roles, not identities.

The system's job is to discover the **exchange structure** that reveals how value can move between actors.

## What counts as a resource

A resource may be:
- human skill or time;
- professional capability;
- trusted relationship/channel access;
- physical space/equipment/vehicle;
- inventory or idle capacity;
- local knowledge;
- data/information;
- software/AI capability;
- institutional access;
- logistics capacity;
- reputation/trust;
- demand aggregation;
- recurring access to a workflow or actor group;
- installed base;
- a valuable problem stream;
- capital, where appropriate;
- a reusable process or acceptance standard.

The atomic unit is not necessarily a person or company. It is a **bounded capability or resource unit that can be invoked under explicit conditions**.

## Latent value

A resource does not need to be advertised as a resource before the engine can notice it.

The engine should search for observable evidence such as:
- idle time;
- low utilization;
- project-cycle gaps;
- repeated manual work;
- excess inventory;
- repeated repricing;
- unused relationship/channel capacity;
- fragmented capabilities;
- trusted communities without a transaction structure;
- recurring complaints/workarounds;
- recurring demand access that has not been packaged;
- combinations of assets whose value emerges only together.

But:

```text
POTENTIAL VALUE != PROVEN VALUE
LATENT_VALUE_HYPOTHESIS != VERIFIED RESOURCE
OBSERVED FRICTION != PAID NEED
COMPLEMENTARITY != TRANSACTIONABILITY
```

The engine may hypothesize broadly but must promote conservatively.

## Resource states

Every discovered resource must be labelled honestly:

```text
OWNED       — controlled now
OPTIONED    — provider has explicitly agreed to provide it under stated conditions
DISCOVERED  — existence is verified, but control/use permission is not established
HYPOTHETICAL— inferred only; not verified
```

`DISCOVERED != OPTIONED`.

`HYPOTHETICAL != AVAILABLE`.

Underuse is a separate axis. A resource may exist without being spare, and may be spare without being available for the proposed exchange.

## The core mismatch

The system searches for structures such as:

```text
actor A has surplus capability but weak demand access
actor B has costly recurring work but has not framed it as demand
actor C has trust/distribution but incomplete service capacity
actor D has idle capacity but no packaging/acceptance standard
actor E owns recurring demand access that itself can become a routing resource
actor F has a relationship/data/location advantage whose value exists only in combination
```

The orchestration opportunity exists only when a new arrangement creates enough incremental value to pay all necessary participants and preserve a defensible orchestration margin.

## Human-capital interpretation

Large labor supply alone is not the thesis.

The relevant opportunity is **unused or misallocated human capital**:
- graduates whose formal job title does not reveal their usable capability;
- workers with spare capacity between full-time opportunities;
- experienced people whose skills can be decomposed into smaller service units;
- people excluded by geography, schedule, credential mismatch or information asymmetry;
- people capable of useful work whose capability is not packaged into an observable, trusted, payable result.

The system should therefore prefer:

```text
JOB TITLE → CAPABILITY UNIT
CV CLAIM   → PROOF / ACCEPTANCE
IDLE TIME  → CONDITIONAL CAPACITY
NO ACCESS  → TRUSTED DEMAND ROUTE
```

It must not exploit desperation by hiding economics, shifting unreasonable risk, or suppressing fair compensation.

## Value objective

A good route should increase total realizable value rather than merely move margin from a weaker actor to a stronger actor.

A transaction is structurally stronger when:
- an actor gets a better/cheaper/faster/more certain outcome;
- a capability/resource owner gains paid utilization or access they lacked;
- an existing channel/resource owner gains justified value;
- previously hidden capability or demand becomes legible and executable;
- coordination/trust/acceptance costs are reduced;
- the orchestrator is paid for real incremental discovery/coordination/guarantee/routing value;
- the loop can repeat without recreating every relationship from zero.

## Discovery implication

The Discovery Engine must search beyond explicit `DEMAND` and explicit `SUPPLY`.

For every candidate actor/market, ask:
1. What does this actor have that may be underused, hidden, fragmented or misallocated?
2. What does this actor repeatedly struggle with, overpay for, delay, improvise or leave unresolved?
3. What changed in its environment, incentives, psychology, workflow or utilization?
4. Which parts are directly observed surface phenomena rather than explanations?
5. What product-agnostic state is the actor actually trying to reach or avoid?
6. What structural-friction hypothesis explains why that state transition is blocked?
7. What alternative explanations could fit the same behavior, and what evidence distinguishes them?
8. Only after the structural layer is evidenced: which other actor/node has a complementary state, resource, deficit, access or flow?
9. What connection-pressure evidence shows reality is already pushing those nodes together?
10. What inter-node missing edge prevents normal flow?
11. Can the blocker be reduced without large irreversible capital?
12. Can a small Hook convert a discovered resource into an optioned resource or a latent deficit into a real commitment?
13. Does the resulting exchange improve value for participants, not only the orchestrator?
14. Can the loop regenerate?

## Orchestrator role

The operator is primarily responsible for:
- seeing hidden structure;
- discovering latent value and deficits;
- identifying complementary actors;
- turning vague resources into callable CapabilityUnits;
- designing the first credible Hook;
- defining interfaces and acceptance;
- aligning incentives;
- routing and replacing resources;
- protecting trust boundaries;
- learning from outcomes.

Routine sales, sourcing, delivery, QA, logistics and support should themselves become routable capabilities whenever feasible.

> **发现未被看见、甚至尚未被拥有者理解的价值；观察每个 Actor 的所余、所缺与变化，寻找互补结构，证明真实价值与真实阻塞，再用规则、信任、激励和可验收能力使原本无法流动的价值进入交换与持续循环。**

The commercial system must remain empirical: noble intent cannot substitute for payer evidence, unit economics, legality, safety or repeatability.
