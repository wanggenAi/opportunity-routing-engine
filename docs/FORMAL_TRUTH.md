# Formal Truth

Last updated: 2026-09-05

This document separates **observed facts**, **working hypotheses**, and **unknowns**. It is the project's current commercial source of truth and should be updated whenever evidence changes.

## 1. Mission

Help Xuzhou manufacturers connect with real overseas demand by reducing search, verification, matching, and coordination costs.

## 2. What we are NOT building

- Generic export consulting
- Alibaba / Google / TikTok agency services
- A market-report business as the core product
- A raw buyer-list resale business
- A system that merely republishes public RFQs
- A system that pretends AI can replace experienced export salespeople end-to-end

## 3. Current business hypothesis

An individual operator using AI, software, public/licensed trade information, and local Xuzhou supplier access may be able to outperform a basic single-channel manual process in the **front end of overseas business development**:

1. discover more relevant demand signals;
2. filter noise faster;
3. verify real buyers more systematically;
4. identify valid written contact channels;
5. match opportunities to real local factory capability;
6. hand qualified opportunities to factories and facilitate progression.

## 4. Evidence-supported observations

### Supply side

- Xuzhou has substantial manufacturing and export activity.
- Glass packaging is a recognizable Xuzhou industrial cluster and is suitable as the first narrow test vertical.
- Manufacturers continue to spend money on overseas sales/customer-development labor even when they already use B2B platforms, search engines, social media, exhibitions, or trade-data tools.

### Demand side

- Public B2B sourcing platforms contain current overseas buying requests for glass packaging.
- Historical import/shipment datasets can reveal firms that actually imported relevant products.
- Public business channels can sometimes identify companies and purchasing/supply-chain contacts.

### Competition

- Generic export consulting, platform operation, digital marketing, buyer-list services, AI lead generation, sourcing agents, SDR outsourcing, and trade intermediaries already exist.
- Therefore the project cannot rely on novelty alone.

## 5. Current competitive thesis

The potential wedge is not "we have information nobody else can access."

The wedge is a lower-cost, more systematic process combining:

- multi-source opportunity discovery;
- cross-source verification;
- contactability gating;
- local supplier intelligence;
- human judgment;
- on-the-ground Xuzhou execution.

The future defensible asset, if the model works, is the accumulated network and verified operational knowledge of:

- which buyers are real and responsive;
- which demand patterns convert;
- which Xuzhou factories can actually produce what;
- MOQ, process, lead time, export experience, and reliability;
- which buyer-supplier combinations progress to transactions.

## 6. Hard unknowns

The following are NOT yet proven:

1. We can consistently produce qualified buyers more efficiently than a normal export salesperson.
2. Qualified buyers found by this process will respond at commercially useful rates.
3. Xuzhou factories will authorize or cooperate with an unknown intermediary.
4. Buyers will accept this intermediary role.
5. The economics after tools, data, travel, communication, and human time will remain attractive.
6. A repeatable fee/commission model can be established.
7. The process can scale beyond one operator without quality collapse.

## 7. Current decision

**Status: GO — controlled validation only.**

Do not make heavy capital commitments. Do not claim product-market fit. Do not build broad automation before benchmark evidence exists.

## 8. First vertical

Glass packaging, with initial emphasis on perfume/cosmetic glass bottles.

Reason: strong local supplier access + observable overseas demand + manageable product scope for testing.

## 9. Buyer qualification principle

A qualified buyer opportunity is not just a company name or RFQ. At minimum it should have evidence across several of these dimensions:

- recent or recurring purchase signal;
- clear product relevance;
- credible company identity;
- buying/import history where available;
- identifiable geography/business model;
- valid business contact route;
- plausible fit with Xuzhou supply.

Top-priority opportunities must satisfy a **contactability hard gate**.

## 10. Truth discipline

For every important field use one of:

- `VERIFIED`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `UNVERIFIED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `CONTRADICTED`

Never silently upgrade `UNKNOWN` or `UNVERIFIED` to a positive conclusion.
