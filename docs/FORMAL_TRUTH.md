# Formal Truth

Last updated: 2026-09-08

This document is the project's commercial source of truth. It separates observations, hypotheses, unknowns, and decisions. No attractive narrative may override missing evidence.

## 1. Mission

Continuously detect opportunity created by social and market change, convert repeated problems into explicit demand hypotheses, identify viable capabilities/resources, and test whether the mismatch can become a real transaction.

## 2. Core operating model

```text
OBSERVE
→ DETECT
→ VALIDATE
→ ROUTE
→ TRANSACT
→ LEARN
```

The project is deliberately industry-agnostic. It may discover opportunities in consumer markets, enterprise services, labor/capability markets, local execution, software/AI, assets, domestic B2B, cross-border B2B, or other domains.

## 3. What we are NOT building

- A generic trend-report business
- A social-media sentiment toy
- A crawler whose value is merely collecting public information
- A raw lead-list resale business
- A general marketplace before transaction density exists
- A traditional intermediary that only exchanges contact details
- A system that treats complaints as proof of willingness to pay
- A system that treats LLM confidence as commercial truth

## 4. Current strategic thesis

Social development continually creates temporary imbalances between demand and capability. Commercial opportunities may appear when one or more of the following occur:

- demand grows faster than supply;
- supply exists but is difficult to discover or trust;
- buyers and sellers are fragmented;
- users rely on expensive or inefficient workarounds;
- a new technology lowers the cost of solving an old problem;
- economic pressure moves spending from premium solutions toward substitutes, reuse, automation, sharing, repair, or project-based services;
- demographic, employment, regulatory, geographic, or cultural changes create new coordination needs;
- resources or capabilities remain idle while another group experiences scarcity.

The engine's potential value is to detect these mismatches early, define the transaction correctly, route the right capability, and reduce execution friction.

## 5. Opportunity truth gates

A candidate is not considered commercially qualified until evidence exists across the following dimensions:

1. `PAIN` — a real, consequential problem exists;
2. `FREQUENCY` — it repeats or affects enough economic activity;
3. `PAYMENT` — an identifiable payer demonstrates willingness to spend money, time, headcount, procurement budget, or other material resources;
4. `SUPPLY` — a realistic capability or resource can solve it;
5. `TRANSACTIONABILITY` — scope, price, delivery, verification, and responsibility can be bounded;
6. `DEFENSIBILITY` — repeated execution can accumulate proprietary data, trust, workflow, network, or decision advantage.

`PAYMENT` and `TRANSACTIONABILITY` are hard gates before expensive build-out.

## 6. Strong demand evidence

Higher-value evidence includes observed behavior such as:

- real purchases or paid services;
- procurement/RFQ/tender activity;
- companies hiring people specifically to solve the problem;
- customers paying for imperfect substitutes;
- repeated marketplace tasks;
- customers spending significant manual time on a workaround;
- explicit budget allocation;
- repeated failed attempts to solve the problem;
- measurable switching toward substitute products or services.

Complaints, surveys, search volume, social posts, and macro indicators may generate hypotheses but are not sufficient alone.

## 7. Opportunity status vocabulary

Use one of:

- `SIGNAL` — interesting change/problem observed;
- `HYPOTHESIS` — plausible demand mechanism proposed;
- `EVIDENCED` — meaningful behavioral/payment evidence exists;
- `TRANSACTION_TEST` — small real-world test is running;
- `VALIDATED` — one or more real transactions support the thesis;
- `REPEATABLE` — repeated transactions show a recurring pattern;
- `SCALE_CANDIDATE` — density and economics justify automation/platform investment;
- `REJECTED` — evidence contradicts the thesis or economics;
- `DORMANT` — may become relevant under future conditions.

Never promote a state merely because an LLM predicts success.

## 10. Current high-priority candidate classes

These are candidates for scanning and experiments, not conclusions:

- demand migration caused by more conservative/value-conscious consumption;
- small enterprise projects that are too small for full-time hiring but valuable enough to outsource;
- China-language/internet information asymmetry for overseas clients;
- AI-enabled replacement or compression of repetitive knowledge work;
- idle inventory/equipment/assets matched to demand;
- local execution tasks where geography creates friction;
- domestic/cross-border demand-capability matching;
- new needs created by aging, employment shifts, education pressure, or household behavior.

## 11. Hard unknowns

1. Can the engine detect useful opportunities earlier or more accurately than ordinary manual research?
2. Can it distinguish loud complaints from actual payment behavior reliably?
3. Can opportunity scoring predict transaction outcomes?
4. Which initial demand category offers the shortest path to first revenue?
5. Which side should pay in each transaction structure?
6. Can capability routing reduce price, time, quality, or search friction enough to earn a margin?
7. Can repeated experiments produce proprietary learning rather than generic public knowledge?
8. When does a human-managed transaction flow justify platform automation?
9. Which categories create regulatory, licensing, labor, privacy, financial, medical, or safety constraints that should be excluded or handled only by qualified providers?

## 12. Truth discipline

For important factual assertions, use one of:

- `VERIFIED`
- `SUPPORTED`
- `UNVERIFIED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `CONTRADICTED`

Rules:

- `UNKNOWN != PASS`.
- Macro trend evidence is not automatically micro-level purchase evidence.
- Correlation is not a causal business mechanism.
- Market existence does not prove our ability to acquire customers.
- One transaction validates possibility, not repeatability.
- Code completion is never evidence of commercial success.
