```text
DISCOVERY ENGINE
Data Sources
→ Macro
→ Money Flow
→ Market / Industry
→ Actor

→ Case Mining

ORCHESTRATION ENGINE
Opportunity Evaluation
→ Hook
```

The discovery half must run **before** the system canonically selects a business opportunity.

The orchestration half determines how a validated opportunity can be captured using systems analysis, resource integration and delegated execution rather than permanent founder labor.

## 3. Data-source truth — LOCKED

Opportunity generation must not depend on ad-hoc web searching alone.

Maintain a source registry covering:
- national macro/statistical data;
- money/credit/trade/employment data;
- Jiangsu regional data;
- Xuzhou local data;
- procurement/transaction data;
- company/platform/market primary evidence;
- authorized/public behavioral and social signals.

Every recurring source must preserve:
- source identity;
- geography;
- observation period;
- publication date;
- units and denominator;
- revision state;
- access method;
- freshness;
- provenance.

Missing/stale sources reduce confidence. Missing values do not become zero.

## 4. Discovery truth — LOCKED

Do not start from the operator's skills, a favored technology or one remembered business idea.

Start from:
- GDP and sector structure;
- CPI / core CPI / service CPI;
- PPI;
- PMI / industrial production;
- household income/expenditure;
- retail and service retail;
- online consumption;
- employment/unemployment/hiring;
- fixed investment/equipment investment;
- money/credit/deposits/loans where useful;
- import/export and cross-region flows;
- demographics/aging/households;
- policy and procurement;
- technology change;
- local industrial/service-chain change;
- psychology and actual behavior.

Then zoom:

```text
China
→ Jiangsu
→ Xuzhou
→ district/county/industry/actor cluster
→ exact friction
→ exact payer
```

Macro growth is only a search direction.

## 5. Money-flow truth — LOCKED

The core question is not `which sector is large?` or `which sector grew?`.

Ask:

> **Where is money moving from, where is it moving to, who is paying, who is receiving, and what changed behavior/work does that movement create?**

Track:
- levels;
- YoY / MoM / QoQ change;
- momentum/acceleration;
- category share and share shift;
- national/provincial/local divergence;
- price vs volume;
- policy subsidy contamination;
- receiving/losing actors.

Divergence is often more useful than raw growth.

`nominal_growth != real_demand_growth`.

## 6. Psychology truth — LOCKED

Psychology changes over time and belongs inside the discovery engine.

Track aggregate signals such as:
- value-for-money;
- spending caution;
- small-trial preference;
- convenience/time value;
- trust/risk aversion;
- experience orientation;
- emotional/self-reward value;
- outcome certainty;
- health/longevity;
- repair/reuse/rental;
- social connection/belonging.

But:

`social-media salience != population share`.

Any psychology thesis should be corroborated by behavior and money-flow evidence where possible.

## 7. Case-mining truth — LOCKED

The system must continuously study successful **and failed** market cases.

Do not copy products or celebrity-founder stories.

Extract:

```text
context change
→ actor behavior change
→ friction noticed
→ opportunity insight
→ first hook
→ first payer commitment
→ resource stack
→ acceptance/economics
→ repeat loop
→ failure/bypass/moat
→ reusable mechanism
```

For every attractive success pattern seek negative controls / failed comparators.

`success story != base rate`.

## 8. Hook truth — LOCKED

The orchestrator should not enter a negotiation with only a story and a request for cooperation.

A first contact should carry a minimal, credible `Hook`:
- demand evidence;
- pre-qualified supply/capability;
- verified information;
- measurable outcome/pilot;
- distribution/channel access;
- idle resource access;
- transparent conditional economics such as deposit, milestone, success fee or revenue share.

Resource state must be explicit:

```text
OWNED
OPTIONED
DISCOVERED
HYPOTHETICAL
```

Never present a discovered/hypothetical resource as controlled.

## 9. Capital-light truth — LOCKED

The preferred first transaction minimizes irreversible capital before payer proof.

Before spending capital ask:
- can payer pre-commit?
- can a deposit fund execution?
- can provider payout occur after acceptance/milestone?
- can idle capacity be activated?
- can a partner contribute resources for revenue share?
- can staged commitments unlock the next resource?

This is legal, transparent resource leverage — not deception, fake demand or hidden liability.

## 10. Execution truth — LOCKED

The operator preferentially owns:

- opportunity evaluation;
- actor/resource mapping;
- hook design;

- capability decomposition;

- incentive design;
- route approval;
- trust/risk boundaries;

Routine acquisition, coding, research, delivery, QA, support and logistics are routable capabilities where feasible.

## 15. Current observable macro structure — RESEARCH INPUT, NOT PROJECT SELECTION

Current 2026 evidence already shows why the pipeline must compare multiple directions rather than jump to one project:
- national service retail is growing faster than goods retail;
- online consumption remains stronger than broad retail;
- some durable categories are weak while communications/certain upgraded categories are strong;
- Jiangsu equipment purchases are strong while broad investment is weak;
- public procurement exposes recurring institutional spending across many service categories;
- local and regional divergence can be material.

These facts define where to investigate. They do not select the business model by themselves.
