# Opportunity Attraction Field Principle

Status: `LOCKED / STRATEGIC DISCOVERY + ATTENTION PRINCIPLE`

## Purpose

This repository can discover formations that are logically defensible, structurally real and still commercially inert in practice. It can also over-focus on technically clean but psychologically weak routing structures because they are easy to reason about.

This principle makes **诱惑力 / ATTRACTION FIELD** a first-class strategic property.

The target is not merely a connection that *could* work. The preferred formation contains enough voluntary energy that the important actors are naturally pulled toward the exchange, and the operator is strongly motivated to own the orchestration layer without becoming the permanent manual executor.

```text
STRUCTURAL TRUTH
+
PARTICIPANT VOLUNTARY ENERGY
+
OPERATOR STRATEGIC ACTIVATION
+
LOW ENOUGH ACTIVATION FRICTION
+
DISCOVERABLE / ADDRESSABLE MOVING UNITS
+
RESOLVABLE A↔B MATCH
→ STRONG ATTRACTION FIELD
```

Attraction is the **first discovery-attention premise**: it decides which parts of broad reality deserve deep investigation before scarce research and validation effort is spent. It does **not** replace evidence, payment truth, safety, legality, transactionability or validation. Attraction decides where to look first; evidence decides what may be believed.

## 0A. Attraction-first search order

Do not begin from a catalog of needs, industries, explicit tasks or every observable
friction. Begin from **high-energy reality signals**.

Search first for combinations such as:
- actors already spending money, time, reputation or workaround effort to move;
- an underused/cheap/stranded resource that becomes disproportionately valuable in a
  different actor state;
- repeated attempts by two sides to reach one another through clumsy substitutes;
- a decision window in which budget, access, vendor choice, rights or resource
  allocation are still movable;
- a narrow missing connection whose removal changes the reachable state sharply;
- behavior where successful routing naturally makes the next routing easier;
- a bridge the operator can own as a control/routing layer rather than perform as a
  recurring job;
- A-side and B-side moving units already leaving dense public, platform, API,
  registry, transaction, community or other scalable digital traces;
- a match relation that can be resolved from accessible identity/rule evidence rather
  than recurring bespoke expert interpretation.

Canonical pre-formation beacon: `src/attraction_discovery.py`.

```text
BROAD REALITY
→ ATTRACTION SIGNAL HARVEST
→ HIGH_ATTRACTION_BEACON
→ CAUSAL / STRUCTURAL TRUTH DESCENT
→ CONNECTION PRESSURE
→ MISSING EDGE
→ VALIDATION
```

The engine must not invert this into:

```text
EVERY FRICTION
→ LONG RESEARCH
→ PRODUCT IDEA
→ ATTRACTION SCORE AT THE END
```

The second sequence wastes attention on low-energy, downstream or merely tidy
problems.

## 1. Attraction is not hype

Do not confuse attraction with:
- novelty;
- a clever story;
- theoretical TAM;
- social-media excitement;
- founder enthusiasm alone;
- a large numerical saving that no actor is actually trying to realize;
- a business that becomes interesting only after a long explanation;
- a route that works only because the founder personally persuades, searches, sells or delivers every transaction.

```text
FOUNDER EXCITEMENT != PARTICIPANT PULL
MARKET TRUTH != ATTRACTION
ATTRACTION != VALIDATION
THEORETICAL SURPLUS != VOLUNTARY ENERGY
CLEVER CONNECTION != SELF-PROPELLING CONNECTION
```

The engine must infer broadly and promote conservatively.

## 2. The three attraction vectors

### A. Actor / Resource-side attraction

Ask:

> If Actor A clearly sees the proposed state transition, why would A want to participate now rather than remain in the current state?

Strong A-side attraction may come from:
- idle or decaying value;
- recurring carrying cost;
- unused capacity;
- expiring time / rights / inventory / attention;
- blocked monetization;
- desire for access, utilization, reputation, convenience, risk reduction or liquidity;
- repeated attempts to find an outlet;
- visible willingness to accept a materially simpler route.

Evidence should come from behavior, sacrifice, search, workarounds, repeated failed attempts, accepted substitutes, current cost or other observable movement.

A resource that *could* be useful to someone else is not enough.

### B. Complementary / Outcome-side attraction

Ask:

> If Actor B sees the reachable outcome, why would B want it now rather than tolerate the current state or use an incumbent route?

Strong B-side attraction may come from:
- avoided loss;
- avoided waiting;
- avoided uncertainty;
- materially lower total cost;
- access to something currently unreachable;
- faster transition into a desired state;
- less coordination burden;
- higher reliability / trust;
- a large state-dependent value difference that is immediately legible to B.

Evidence should include existing spending, workaround behavior, repeated search, delay cost, substitution, failed attempts or other real sacrifices where possible.

A theoretically superior outcome is not enough.

### C. Operator / Rooter attraction

Ask:

> Does the operator strongly want to own and improve this bridge, or merely believe it is possible to sell?

Operator attraction is a legitimate **attention-allocation signal**, because founder attention is scarce. It is not market evidence.

Strong operator attraction usually means:
- the structural asymmetry is intellectually and economically compelling;
- the value jump created by the connection is easy to see;
- the operator can remain the routing/orchestration control plane;
- recurring manual delivery is not the main value;
- the route can produce fast learning and visible feedback;
- success creates reusable data, trust, coverage, routing power or an improved future control position;
- the work feels like discovering, structuring and controlling a value channel rather than repeatedly performing a job;
- the expected upside is meaningful relative to effort, capital and operational drag.

Weak operator attraction includes:
- “I could probably sell this” but no strong desire to pursue it;
- recurring cold outreach as the engine;
- founder-specific professional labor as the deliverable;
- long opaque projects with little compounding;
- work the operator would abandon immediately after one transaction.

Operator preference must never be used to manufacture A/B demand.

## 3. Connection attraction is an amplifier

A high-attraction connection often has this property:

> It is non-obvious before discovery, but feels obvious after it is shown.

The connection itself should create a meaningful state change, not merely introduce a professional who still has to do all of the value creation.

Positive amplifiers include:
- strong asymmetric surplus: what is low-value or costly for A becomes high-value in B's current state;
- a visible before/after contrast;
- low explanation burden;
- low behavioral-change burden;
- low permission / capital / integration friction relative to the value unlocked;
- a narrow missing edge;
- repeated actor motion already pressing against that missing edge;
- the possibility that one successful route makes subsequent routes easier.

This “why did these two sides not connect earlier?” reaction is useful, but it is not enough without participant evidence.

## 4. Attraction is weakest-link dominated

Do not average away a dead side.

```text
STRONG A + STRONG B + WEAK OPERATOR
→ real formation may exist, but it is not a current founder-priority business

STRONG A + WEAK B + STRONG OPERATOR
→ founder enthusiasm cannot create demand

WEAK A + STRONG B + STRONG OPERATOR
→ route requires supply persuasion / creation and may become founder-heavy

STRONG A + STRONG B + STRONG OPERATOR
→ eligible for high current attention, subject to all truth gates
```

Use:

```text
ATTRACTION FIELD = weakest critical attraction vector
```

as a reasoning rule, not a numeric equation.

## 4A. Discoverability / addressability is a hard attraction gate

A large value gap is not enough.

The operator must be able to **see the moving units while the decision window is
still open**.

Every high-attraction formation must separately establish:

### A-side discoverability

Can the relevant resource/supply units be repeatedly discovered through scalable
observable traces such as:
- public listings;
- platform feeds;
- searchable registries/databases;
- APIs;
- transaction/event streams;
- structured public documents;
- permissioned partner data;
- repeated self-published intent in large communities?

If each unit requires private relationships, site visits, cold calling, manual
inventory archaeology or one-off detective work, current attraction is low.

### B-side discoverability

Can the relevant demand/state-change event be detected repeatedly while the actor can
still choose a route?

If demand becomes visible only after bespoke outreach, closed-network referrals or
after the decision is already locked, current attraction is low.

### Match resolvability

Once A and B are visible, can the system determine whether they can actually connect
from accessible evidence?

Prefer:
- exact identifiers;
- standardized attributes;
- explicit compatibility rules;
- location/time/capacity fields;
- machine-readable constraints;
- reusable verification logic.

Demote when every match requires:
- bespoke engineering;
- manual expert judgment;
- physical investigation before identity is known;
- founder-specific interpretation;
- repeated human detective work.

Hard rule:

```text
VALUE GAP WITHOUT DISCOVERABILITY
= HIDDEN POTENTIAL, NOT CURRENT HIGH ATTRACTION

A VISIBLE + B VISIBLE + MATCH RESOLVABLE
= CAPTURABLE OPPORTUNITY SURFACE
```

And:

```text
SCRAPING HARDER != DISCOVERABILITY
MORE SEARCH QUERIES != ADDRESSABLE MARKET
FOUNDER HUNTING != ROUTING CONTROL
FIRST BUILD A SENSOR NETWORK != NARROW BRIDGE
```

Canonical executable dimensions:
- `a_discoverability`;
- `b_discoverability`;
- `match_resolvability`.

All three are hard-floor dimensions in `src/attraction_discovery.py`.

## 4B. Multiple strong signals use a Pareto frontier

Once several signals have already passed the hard `HIGH_ATTRACTION_BEACON` floor,
do not collapse the critical attraction dimensions into one weighted score.

Use Pareto dominance:

> Signal X dominates Signal Y only when X is no worse on every critical attraction
> dimension and strictly better on at least one.

Critical objectives are currently:
- A-side voluntary motion;
- B-side voluntary motion;
- state-dependent value jump;
- movable decision window;
- bridge compression;
- activation ease;
- self-propulsion;
- operator control;
- A-side discoverability;
- B-side discoverability;
- match resolvability.

The first deep-discovery attention set is the non-dominated frontier.

Canonical implementation:
`src/attraction_frontier.py`.

This produces **attention tiers**, not a commercial ranking or probability estimate.

```text
HARD FLOOR
→ PARETO FRONTIER
→ DEEP DISCOVERY
```

not:

```text
WEIGHTED TOTAL SCORE
→ DECLARE A WINNER
```

The reason is structural: these dimensions are not safely exchangeable. A better value
jump cannot compensate for a weaker decision window or dead participant pull.

## 5. Required Attraction Brief for retained formations

Every retained formation must state, in plain language:

```text
A-SIDE TEMPTATION:
If I am A, why do I want this now?

A-SIDE EVIDENCE:
What observed behavior proves motion rather than theoretical benefit?

B-SIDE TEMPTATION:
If I am B, why do I want this now?

B-SIDE EVIDENCE:
What observed behavior proves motion rather than theoretical benefit?

OPERATOR TEMPTATION:
Why do I actively want to own this bridge rather than merely sell a service?

OPERATOR NON-LABOR TEST:
What recurring work must not depend on me personally?

CONNECTION WOW:
What becomes surprisingly valuable only because these nodes are connected?

ACTIVATION FRICTION:
What permission, education, trust, integration, capital or behavior change is still required?

SELF-PROPULSION:
After the first successful route, what makes the next route easier or more likely?

A-SIDE DISCOVERABILITY:
Where do the relevant A units continuously become visible without bespoke hunting?

B-SIDE DISCOVERABILITY:
Where do the relevant B events/units continuously become visible while decisions are open?

MATCH RESOLVABILITY:
What accessible identifiers/rules let the system decide whether A and B can connect
without recurring expert interpretation?
```

Participant attraction must preserve truth states such as `OBSERVED / INFERRED / EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE`. Operator attraction may be recorded directly as a strategic preference, but it must remain separate from market evidence.

## 6. Anti-drift rule for white-portfolio scans

A scan must not recursively inherit the last candidate's industry, asset type or search lens merely because the prior scan ended there.

```text
BROAD REALITY > CURRENT SEARCH LENS
SEARCH LENS != ONTOLOGY
PREVIOUS WINNER / LOSER != NEXT SEARCH SPACE
INDUSTRIAL B2B != DEFAULT WORLD
TRANSFERABLE OBJECTIVE STRANDED UNIT != TOTAL COMMERCIAL WORLD MODEL
```

Fresh scans should deliberately reopen multiple kinds of reality, including when relevant:
- household / life-stage transitions;
- consumer behavior;
- SME / enterprise operations;
- city space and local infrastructure;
- cross-border movement;
- technology / AI adoption;
- relationships, trust and distribution;
- time, rights, access and capacity;
- institutional transitions;
- physical assets and industrial systems.

This is a breadth discipline, not a fixed quota. Do not fabricate weak directions merely to fill categories.

Every scan must include a short **Drift Audit**:
- Did the scan become a continuation of the previous vertical without new world evidence?
- Did one convenient search lens become the ontology?
- Did the scan over-select industrial / inventory / professional-service cases because they are easy to index?
- Did the scan auto-kill a formation merely because an incumbent exists?
- Did the scan test whether the incumbent actually resolves the value flow for the relevant population/state?
- Did attraction come from real participant behavior or from the analyst's clever story?

## 7. Existing actors do not automatically kill attraction

The existence of a platform, distributor, institution, broker, service firm or incumbent route is evidence about the current value flow. It is not an automatic rejection.

```text
EXISTING ACTOR != FLOW SOLVED
INCUMBENT PRESENCE != RESIDUAL FRICTION ABSENT
KNOWN CATEGORY != VALUE FULLY CIRCULATING
```

Ask instead:
- Which actor/state population remains poorly served?
- What repeated workaround or leakage remains?
- What part of the flow is still slow, expensive, inaccessible, low-trust or awkward?
- Does the incumbent own the real bottleneck, or only an adjacent layer?
- Is the residual edge large and recurring enough to matter?

If the current route already makes the value flow normal for the relevant population, demote it. If not, continue causal descent.

## 7A. Clean-slate epoch rule

The 2026-09-19 commercial reset clears all pre-reset formations from current strategic memory.

Historical cases may not be used as:
- parents;
- priors;
- watchlist fallbacks;
- benchmark candidates;
- search prompts;
- default examples to continue;
- negative constraints that prevent independent rediscovery.

```text
HISTORICAL CASE MEMORY != CURRENT WORLD EVIDENCE
INDEPENDENT REDISCOVERY != CASE REVIVAL
```

If current reality independently regenerates a structure similar to a deleted historical case, evaluate it from zero using current evidence and give it a new `ATTRACTION_SCAN_*` identity.

## 7B. Value-chain leverage and intervention timing — LOCKED

Attraction is not only about whether both sides would like an improved state. It also
depends on **where the bridge sits relative to value allocation and decision rights**.

Before a formation can consume scarce external validation, classify:
- intervention stage;
- decision mobility;
- economic proximity;
- consequence if the bridge is absent;
- observed/inferred participant pull.

Default demotion applies when:
- intervention occurs at closeout or after the transaction;
- budget, vendor, scope and most value allocation are already locked;
- the bridge primarily improves process quality;
- absence causes only delay, rework or minor friction.

```text
REAL FRICTION != ATTRACTIVE POSITION
MONEY ALREADY ALLOCATED != HIGH COMMERCIAL ENERGY
LATE-STAGE CONVENIENCE != STRATEGIC CONTROL POINT
```

A late-stage position survives only if it still controls payment/value release,
prevents material economic loss/risk, satisfies a hard compliance/safety boundary,
or determines whether the transaction can complete.

Canonical executable gate: `src/attraction_leverage.py`.

## 8. Current-stage priority rule

For current founder attention, prefer formations where:
- structural truth is credible;
- both participant sides show evidence-backed pull;
- the missing edge is narrow;
- participant surplus is immediately legible;
- activation friction is bounded;
- the operator has high strategic attraction;
- the operator can stay in orchestration rather than recurring execution;
- successful routing can compound into data, trust, access, coverage or lower future routing cost;
- both sides are discoverable from scalable observable traces;
- the match relation can be resolved without recurring expert search/interpretation.

A logically valid but low-attraction formation may remain in the ledger. It should not consume scarce founder attention merely because it can be defended analytically.

The shorthand is:

> **A 想放出来，B 想拿到手，根哥想搭这座桥；三边都不用被硬推，连接以后价值自己往前走。**

And the operator-specific shorthand is:

> **不是“这事我能做”，而是“这条价值流我真想控制，而且不需要我亲自扛着跑”。**
