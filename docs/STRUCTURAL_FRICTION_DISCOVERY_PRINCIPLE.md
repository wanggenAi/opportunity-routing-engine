## 1. Why this principle exists

The engine must not confuse the **first visible problem** with the deepest commercially relevant structure.

A visible cost, complaint, delay, shortage, lost order, job-search difficulty, idle capacity or workaround is often only a **surface phenomenon**. It is valuable evidence because it reveals that an actor is not reaching a desired state, but the surface description may not explain why.

The system must therefore distinguish:

```text
SURFACE PHENOMENON / SURFACE FRICTION
!=
STRUCTURAL FRICTION
!=
MISSING EDGE
!=
COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY
```

A surface friction answers:

> What is visibly going wrong, costing time/money, being delayed, avoided, improvised or left unrealized?

A structural friction answers:

> **What underlying constraint prevents this actor, given its real endowments, state, psychology, behavior and environment, from reaching the state it is actually moving toward?**

A missing edge answers a later inter-node question:

> **Once complementary nodes are identified, what prevents their value from flowing between them as a normal exchange?**

These are different layers. The repository must preserve that distinction.

## 2. Surface friction is a sensor, not the opportunity

The system may begin from a visible event such as:

- lost orders;
- inability to find suitable work;
- repeated waiting;
- overpayment;
- underused equipment;
- repeated manual coordination;
- low utilization;
- an actor repeatedly seeking help;
- a recurring workaround;
- a stated complaint.

But it must not stop at the literal wording of the event.

```text
OBSERVED COST != ROOT CAUSE
STATED NEED != LATENT OUTCOME
VISIBLE SHORTAGE != STRUCTURAL FRICTION
SURFACE FRICTION != OPPORTUNITY
```

The purpose of the visible signal is to trigger **causal descent**.

## 3. Causal descent — reconstruct the Actor's hidden state transition

For a meaningful observation, reconstruct:

```text
ACTOR
→ OBJECTIVE ENDOWMENTS
→ CURRENT STATE
→ STATE / ENVIRONMENT CHANGE
→ PERCEPTION / MOTIVE
→ OBSERVED BEHAVIOR
→ SURFACE PHENOMENON / SURFACE FRICTION
→ WHAT STATE IS THE ACTOR ACTUALLY TRYING TO REACH OR AVOID?
→ LATENT / UNFORMED OUTCOME HYPOTHESIS
→ WHAT STRUCTURAL CONDITION PREVENTS THAT TRANSITION?
→ STRUCTURAL FRICTION HYPOTHESIS
→ CROSS-EVIDENCE / ALTERNATIVE-EXPLANATION SEARCH
→ EVIDENCED STRUCTURAL FRICTION
```

The desired state must be written product-agnostically.

Do not prematurely translate:

- “cannot find a job” into “needs a job board”;
- “lost orders” into “needs an outsourcing marketplace”;
- “elderly person struggles with a phone” into “needs phone training”;
- “idle equipment” into “needs a rental platform”.

Ask what state transition is actually blocked.

## 3A. The latent outcome is itself a hypothesis

The Actor's spoken request is not automatically the Actor's governing objective.

The engine must separate:

```text
STATED REQUEST
!=
REVEALED DIRECTION OF BEHAVIOR
!=
LATENT / UNFORMED OUTCOME HYPOTHESIS
!=
EVIDENCED LATENT OUTCOME
```

A student saying "I need a part-time job" may be describing one available route, while repeated behavior may reveal a deeper desired transition such as:

> convert bounded time/capability into income, experience and useful participation without accepting the constraints of a full-time employment package.

Likewise, a manufacturer saying "we need more capacity" may be naming a surface solution rather than the desired state:

> absorb temporary order peaks with trusted elastic production while preserving quality, IP, certification, acceptance and delivery certainty without permanent fixed-capacity expansion.

These formulations are **not automatically true because they sound deeper**. They must be treated as competing outcome hypotheses and tested against observed choice, sacrifice, substitution, workaround, persistence and contradiction.

## 3B. Causal descent is recursive, not one jump

Do not perform one rhetorical "why" and call the result structural.

A causal descent may contain multiple layers:

```text
SURFACE:
cannot find suitable work
↓
PROXIMATE:
available openings do not fit time / location / schedule
↓
DEEPER:
economically useful capability is packaged mainly as full jobs/projects
↓
STRUCTURAL:
small capability units are not legible, trusted, accepted and settled cheaply
```

Or:

```text
SURFACE:
orders are lost during capacity peaks
↓
PROXIMATE:
internal production capacity is insufficient
↓
DEEPER:
outside capacity exists but switching is risky
↓
STRUCTURAL:
production capability is not callable as a bounded, trusted, acceptance-compatible elastic unit
```

The engine must preserve the chain rather than overwrite the surface event with the latest interpretation.

## 3C. Do not assume one metaphysical root cause

Real systems are often multi-causal.

The objective is **not** to discover a single philosophical "root cause". The objective is to identify the deepest **decision-useful, falsifiable causal frontier** supported by evidence.

For every serious structural-friction hypothesis, maintain at least:
- the leading explanation;
- at least one plausible competing explanation;
- evidence supporting each;
- evidence contradicting each;
- what observation would discriminate between them;
- explicit falsifiers.

```text
ONE PLAUSIBLE EXPLANATION
!=
STRUCTURAL TRUTH
```

Multiple constraints may remain simultaneously true. When removing any one of them would still leave the state transition blocked, the causal frontier is multi-causal and must be represented that way.

## 3D. Stop rule — do not descend forever

"Go deeper" can itself become storytelling. The engine therefore stops causal descent when it reaches the deepest layer that is both:

1. falsifiable from observable evidence; and
2. decision-relevant — a deeper explanation would not materially change which real-world resources, interfaces, rights, trust mechanisms or capabilities must be discovered or activated.

Valid stop reasons include:

```text
INTERVENTION_RELEVANT_BOUNDARY
NO_DEEPER_FALSIFIABLE_LAYER
EVIDENCE_LIMIT_REACHED
MULTI_CAUSAL_FRONTIER
```

`EVIDENCE_LIMIT_REACHED` does not mean PASS. It means the deeper causal truth remains unknown.

## 4. Structural friction is deeper than a named pain point

A structural friction can be latent even when the surface pain is obvious.

Illustrative structure:

```text
SURFACE:
young people cannot find suitable work

POSSIBLE LATENT OUTCOME:
convert bounded time/capability into income, experience and social usefulness
without requiring a full-time employment package

POSSIBLE STRUCTURAL FRICTION:
economically useful capability exists,
but society packages most trusted paid work as full jobs/projects;
small capability units are not legible, trusted, accepted or settled cheaply
```

Another illustrative structure:

```text
SURFACE:
a manufacturer gives up orders because capacity is insufficient

POSSIBLE LATENT OUTCOME:
obtain temporary scalable production capacity
without sacrificing IP, quality consistency, certification, acceptance or delivery certainty
and without permanent fixed-capacity expansion

POSSIBLE STRUCTURAL FRICTION:
productive capacity may exist elsewhere,
but it is not callable as a trusted, bounded, acceptance-compatible elastic capability
```

These are **hypotheses**, not facts. They become useful only when supported by behavior, objective state and corroborating evidence.

## 5. Structural-friction evidence states

Every causal claim must be labeled honestly.

### `OBSERVED`

Direct attributable fact.

Examples:
- actor lost orders;
- actor repeatedly applies for work;
- actor pays a workaround;
- capacity sits idle;
- work is routed through informal help;
- a task repeatedly fails at the same step.

### `INFERRED`

A causal or outcome hypothesis derived from observed facts.

Examples:
- the actor values flexible monetization more than a formal job title;
- the manufacturer may need trusted elastic capacity rather than simply more suppliers;
- the household may need bounded dependable assistance rather than a broad service package.

`INFERRED` must never be presented as fact.

### `EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE`

A structural-friction hypothesis supported by converging evidence such as:
- repeated behavior across actors;
- independent source classes;
- money or time sacrificed consistently with the hypothesis;
- repeated workaround/substitution patterns;
- failed attempts that expose the same underlying constraint;
- objective resource/endowment evidence;
- contradiction search that weakens major alternative explanations.

Only `EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE` may materially drive complementary-node discovery and candidate promotion.

```text
OBSERVED != INFERRED
INFERRED != EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE
EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE != COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY
```

## 5A. Psychology is one causal sensor, not a universal gate

The user's intended system can infer from psychology/behavior **or** from objective real-world structure.

Valid causal evidence may come from:
- observed behavior and revealed preference;
- money/time sacrifice;
- repeated workaround or substitution;
- state transitions;
- objective underuse/misalignment;
- institutional rules and rights;
- repeated process failure;
- technical constraints;
- spatial/temporal mismatch;
- trust/acceptance requirements;
- psychology/perception/motive where relevant.

Psychology can be highly valuable when the blocked transition depends on autonomy, fear, status, identity, convenience, trust, avoidance or aspiration.

But:

```text
NO PSYCHOLOGY SIGNAL
!=
NO STRUCTURAL FRICTION
```

Enterprise, technical and institutional structures may be evidenced without a psychology layer. If psychology is used, it must remain evidence-bound and behavior-corroborated.

## 6. Multiple valid discovery entry points

No single scan heuristic may become constitutional.

Valid discovery entry points include:

```text
BUYER COST FIRST
BEHAVIOR FIRST
STATE CHANGE FIRST
RESOURCE UNDERUSE FIRST
RESOURCE–PSYCHOLOGY DISEQUILIBRIUM FIRST
PARTIAL FLOW / WORKAROUND FIRST
CURRENT EXTERNALIZED WORKAROUND FIRST
CONTRADICTION FIRST
```

These are **sensor/search strategies**, not the definition of the business system.

In particular:

```text
BUYER COST FIRST != LATENT VALUE DOCTRINE
EXTERNALIZED WORKAROUND FIRST != LATENT VALUE DOCTRINE
```

A named buyer cost is strong evidence, but an opportunity may begin before a buyer has articulated or priced the outcome.

## 6A. Exploration may branch before proof; promotion may not

The engine must remain capable of discovering combinations nobody has named yet.

Therefore an `INFERRED` structural-friction hypothesis may be used to **explore** possible complementary world nodes, analogues and discriminating evidence.

But inferred structure cannot:
- promote a candidate;
- become a claimed fact;
- justify a broad founder outreach campaign;
- justify building the exchange mechanism;
- bypass contradiction search.

```text
INFERRED STRUCTURE
→ MAY GUIDE EXPLORATION

EVIDENCED STRUCTURE
→ MAY DRIVE PROMOTION
```

This distinction preserves generative discovery without allowing elegant stories to masquerade as reality.

## 7. Structural friction precedes complementary-node commitment

Do not search only for “who can solve the visible problem”.

First identify the deeper transition and constraint.

Then ask:

> **What resource, capability, trust relation, access right, information structure, time/space alignment, packaging rule, aggregation mechanism or other world node could remove this structural constraint?**

This preserves the repository's deeper purpose: discover value combinations that are not yet obvious market categories.

The sequence is:

```text
SURFACE SIGNAL
→ LATENT OUTCOME
→ STRUCTURAL FRICTION
→ COMPLEMENTARY WORLD-NODE SEARCH
→ CONNECTION-PRESSURE EVIDENCE
→ PARTIAL / INFORMAL / SUBSTITUTE FLOW
→ OBSERVED MISSING EDGE BETWEEN NODES
→ LATENT CONNECTION
```

Structural friction and missing edge are related but not identical:

```text
STRUCTURAL FRICTION:
why the actor cannot reach the desired state under current structures

MISSING EDGE:
why the complementary nodes cannot form a normal value flow
```

## 8. Psychology and behavior are causal evidence, not decoration

Psychology/behavior sensing must help answer:

- What changed in the actor's world?
- What does the actor now value, avoid or tolerate differently?
- What behavior reveals the desired state more reliably than stated demand?
- What contradiction exists between objective endowment and actual outcome?
- Which structural explanations best fit the repeated behavior?
- What evidence would falsify that explanation?

Do not use psychology to invent motives. Use it to generate falsifiable structural hypotheses and then corroborate them.

## 9. Human contact may validate one decisive structural unknown

Human attention remains scarce validation capital and must not become the default discovery sensor.

However:

```text
PUBLICLY PROVEN MISSING EDGE
IS NOT REQUIRED
BEFORE EVERY TARGETED HUMAN PROBE
```

When broad sensing has already established:
- a real actor/state pattern;
- a real surface phenomenon;
- a bounded structural-friction hypothesis;
- meaningful corroboration;
- one decisive unresolved causal fact;

the system may mark the hypothesis:

`DECISIVE_UNKNOWN / PROBE_ELIGIBLE`

A probe must ask about the narrow causal uncertainty, not ask the actor to brainstorm a business idea.

Examples:
- “When overflow occurs, what specifically prevents use of external capacity?”
- “When you need small paid tasks, what makes available work unusable or unacceptable?”
- “What do you do today when this situation happens, and why does that route fail?”

A probe result may falsify the hypothesis. It does not automatically promote a business.

## 10. Anti-story discipline

Deep inference increases discovery power and also increases hallucination risk.

For every structural-friction hypothesis, require:

1. explicit observed facts;
2. a product-agnostic latent outcome;
3. at least one plausible alternative explanation;
4. contradiction search;
5. evidence that would distinguish the explanations;
6. provenance for every factual claim;
7. clear `OBSERVED / INFERRED / EVIDENCED_STRUCTURE` labels.

Do not reward a hypothesis merely because it is intellectually elegant or novel.

```text
NOVEL INTERPRETATION != LATENT VALUE
CAUSAL PLAUSIBILITY != CAUSAL EVIDENCE
```

The engine should **infer broadly, descend causally, compare explanations, test counterfactually, and promote conservatively**.

## 11. Promotion discipline

A visible friction may remain:
- `OBSERVATION`;
- `SURFACE_FRICTION`;
- `STRUCTURAL_FRICTION_HYPOTHESIS`;
- `DECISIVE_UNKNOWN / PROBE_ELIGIBLE`;
- `EVIDENCED_STRUCTURAL_FRICTION`.

Only after the structural layer is sufficiently evidenced should the engine invest heavily in complementary-node and latent-connection formation.

A commercial candidate still requires the later layers:
- real complementary nodes;
- connection pressure;
- an observed or decisively testable missing edge;
- participant value;
- reachability;
- mobilization;
- payer/economic truth;
- execution/acceptance/settlement.

## 13. Governing sentence

> **不要把“看见了一个痛点”误认为“发现了机会”。表层摩擦只是传感器，Actor 自己说出的需求也只是一个解释入口。系统要从客观状态、真实行为、资源禀赋、心理动机（如适用）、替代/绕行、付出的时间和金钱继续向下做因果下降：先提出多个“Actor 真正想完成什么状态转换”的候选解释，再提出多个“什么结构阻止这个转换”的竞争性因果假设，用反证、区分性证据和可证伪条件淘汰漂亮故事，直到抵达最深的、对决策有用且仍可验证的因果前沿。这个前沿可以是多因的，也不等于后续节点之间的 Missing Edge。只有结构性摩擦被证据支持以后，才允许把它用于正式的互补节点、连接压力和潜在连接晋级；推导阶段可以大胆，事实晋级必须保守。**
