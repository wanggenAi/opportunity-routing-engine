Purpose: prevent the first external validation from degenerating into cold outreach that asks a stranger to spend scarce time educating the operator.

## 1. Public contactability is not legitimate access

A public phone number, email address, front desk or social account proves only that a communication channel exists.

```text
PUBLIC CONTACT ROUTE
!=
LEGITIMATE ACCESS
!=
COUNTERPART ATTENTION
!=
TRUST
```

Before asking a high-value actor for time, the operator must be able to answer four questions from that actor's point of view:

1. **Who are you?**
2. **Why are you relevant to this problem?**
3. **Why are you legitimately in this conversation?**
4. **What concrete value can I gain by spending time with you?**

If any answer is weak, direct outreach remains `ACCESS_BLOCKED` or `HOOK_NOT_READY`.

## 2. Table capital / ACCESS_CAPITAL

`ACCESS_CAPITAL` is the set of truthful, externally legible reasons that make it rational for another actor to admit the operator into a scarce-attention conversation.

It may include:
- relevant education or training;
- real professional delivery history;
- demonstrable technical or domain competence;
- a recognized institutional role;
- a trusted introducer;
- accepted participation in an industry / technology-transfer / park / association mechanism;
- an already committed complementary actor;
- evidence that the operator controls a useful information, demand, capability or execution path;
- prior accepted outcomes / references.

It must never be manufactured by inflated titles, borrowed logos without permission, fake partnerships or ambiguous claims.

For the current operator, a truthful initial identity may use only proofable facts such as:
- computer-engineering education;
- multi-year enterprise software / Java / database / systems-delivery experience;
- current local field research into bounded enterprise-delivery bottlenecks and resource routing.

Employer names, personal identifiers and detailed history are optional and should be exposed only when useful and consented.

## 3. Access ladder

The active route should record the strongest level actually obtained:

```text
A0 PUBLICLY REACHABLE
   public phone/email/address only

A1 LEGIBLE IDENTITY
   counterpart can understand who the operator is and why the topic is plausible

A2 RELEVANT PROOF
   education/work/sample/result makes competence legible

A3 BORROWED LEGITIMACY / WARM PATH
   trusted introduction, recognized institution, accepted role, partner, program or existing counterpart

A4 CONVERSATION PERMISSION
   target actor explicitly agrees to a bounded conversation

A5 TRANSACTION PERMISSION
   actor has authority and is willing to test a bounded economic exchange
```

`A0` alone must never be treated as `R1 PASS` for a high-trust field route.

## 4. Counterpart-specific surplus comes before the ask

Do not contact an actor merely because the engine wants information.

The contact must contain a **Hook** whose value is legible from the counterpart's side.

```text
OUR QUESTION
!=
THEIR REASON TO ANSWER
```

A valid Hook should specify:

```text
COUNTERPART CURRENT COST / RISK / DELAY / LOST REVENUE
→ WHAT WE CAN DO WITH MINIMAL INPUT
→ WHAT CONCRETE OUTPUT THEY RECEIVE
→ WHEN THEY RECEIVE IT
→ WHAT THEY RISK / DO NOT RISK
→ HOW THEY CAN REJECT IT
```

The Hook is not a PPT and not a vague promise of future cooperation.

## 5. Active enterprise Hook hypothesis — Field Run 001

Target role: project / implementation / delivery owner at a Xuzhou digital-service company.

Do **not** lead with:
- "Do you have outsourcing demand?"
- "Can I learn about your projects?"
- "I am researching your industry."
- "Can you give me five minutes?"

These primarily benefit the operator.

Preferred bounded Hook hypothesis:

> If you have one recent non-sensitive repetitive delivery task that is consuming implementation-team time, give me only a de-identified sample or task description. I will convert it into a one-page execution card: input, output, acceptance rule, privacy/permission boundary, which steps require senior judgment, which steps are rule-executable, and a go/no-go externalization verdict. You do not need to disclose customer secrets or give production access. If the result has no practical value, stop there. Only if you agree that one real task is routable do we discuss a paid pilot.

What the counterpart may gain:
- a clearer decomposition of senior-vs-routine work;
- an externalization / non-externalization decision artifact;
- explicit acceptance and failure boundaries;
- potential reduction of senior consultant time if a routable unit actually exists;
- zero obligation to proceed to a transaction.

Truth boundary:

`POSSIBLE COUNTERPART SURPLUS != PROVEN INTEREST`

The Hook itself must be tested.

## 6. Backer / introducer also needs a Hook

An institution, research institute, park, association, technology-manager office or trusted introducer is not free access infrastructure.

Before asking for an introduction, answer:

> Why should this intermediary spend reputation capital on us?

Possible value hypotheses, to be validated rather than assumed:
- de-identified evidence on recurring delivery bottlenecks among member/service firms;
- structured enterprise-need capture that improves its service or technology-transfer workflow;
- a capable external participant who can do bounded field work under clear rules;
- a real case or demand signal that fits the institution's existing mission;
- a reusable task-decomposition / acceptance method it can apply to member projects.

A request for "please introduce me to companies" without visible intermediary benefit is `HOOK_NOT_READY`.

This is not a failure of the business thesis. It is direct evidence that `AUTHORITY / ACCESS POTENTIAL` is the first observed blocker in this route.
