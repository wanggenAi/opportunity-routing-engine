## Enterprise Hook — revised

Do not begin by asking the delivery owner to teach us about internal problems.

Preferred bounded exchange hypothesis:

> If you have one recent non-sensitive repetitive delivery task that consumes implementation-team time, give me only a de-identified sample or task description. I will return a one-page execution card: input, output, acceptance rule, privacy/permission boundary, which steps require senior judgment, which steps are rule-executable, and a go/no-go externalization verdict. You do not need to disclose customer secrets or give production access. If it has no practical value, stop there. Only if you judge one real task routable do we discuss a paid pilot.

Potential enterprise benefit:
- makes senior-vs-routine work explicit;
- gives a usable externalization/no-externalization decision artifact;
- clarifies acceptance and failure boundaries;
- may reveal a way to reduce senior implementation time;
- creates no obligation to buy.

Truth boundary:

`HOOK HYPOTHESIS != COUNTERPART INTEREST`

## Fast falsification conditions

Demote the access route if:
- claimed backing does not produce any accepted role or introduction;
- an intermediary sees no benefit and will not spend reputation/time on the introduction;
- the operator identity remains too weak or irrelevant for the target conversation;
- the enterprise Hook has no visible practical value to a delivery owner.

Demote the transaction route if:
- no recurring bounded task exists;
- all meaningful work requires senior domain judgment and cannot be decomposed;
- client/upstream contracts prohibit external collaboration;
- de-sensitization is not realistically possible;
- coordination/QA cost exceeds the value of routing;
- the buyer will not make a real economic commitment after a clear accepted-output definition;
- the only available role is permanent staffing/body-shopping rather than bounded outcome routing.

A clean falsification is a successful experiment outcome.
