# Potential Flow Field Packet 002 — 2026-09-17

Status: `READY_FOR_AUTHORIZED_ACTOR-SIDE_PROBE / NO_OUTREACH_SENT`

This packet does not select a business vertical. It narrows the current P0 to accessible reality tests where a resource gradient is observable without enterprise procurement, proprietary data or upfront product building.

## Constitutional rule

A useful P0 formation must satisfy two independent questions:

1. **Does a potential gradient exist?**
   - one actor retains or gains a real resource/capability/time/trust/context surplus;
   - another actor repeatedly fails to reach an outcome that could plausibly use that resource;
   - current routes do not already close the same edge adequately.

2. **Can we touch the truth cheaply?**
   - lawful ordinary access;
   - no proprietary data required for the first fact;
   - no enterprise procurement, NDA or large capital before learning;
   - no product build before actor truth;
   - explicit kill conditions.

`POTENTIAL_GRADIENT != DEMAND`

`ACCESSIBILITY != COMMERCIAL VALUE`

`MENTORING != TRANSACTION`

`UNPAID LABOR != VALUE FORMATION`

## What the latest public evidence killed or downgraded

### Generic idle-space + host + activity

Deprioritize. Xuzhou already operates youth-night-school partner models using small shops, and communities already convert idle rooms into resident service spaces. Reopen only around a named time slot and a repeated outcome that those routes fail to serve.

### Generic student + enterprise problem matching

Deprioritize. Xuzhou OPC communities already run business matching and have produced enterprise orders for young founders. Jiangsu universities also use enterprise challenge lists and external mentors. Reopen only around a paid, objectively accepted task class below both agency and institutional-matching thresholds.

### Generic shared parking

Kill. Jiangsu is already expanding shared parking and integrating parking resources into digital public systems.

### Generic idle kitchen / community meal

Kill. Community canteens, central kitchens, public-space reuse and commercial operators already organize this exchange at scale.

# First-wave Probe A — `PF-OPC-SILVER-CHANNEL-COPILOT-007`

## Observed resource gradient

### Senior / retired side

Potentially callable resources:

- industry judgment;
- buyer-language fluency;
- credibility earned through prior roles;
- knowledge of procurement/qualification/decision pathways;
- negotiation pattern recognition;
- industry relationships, subject to lawful and ethical use;
- discretionary time after leaving full-time organizational structure.

Do **not** infer that retirement itself creates willingness to work, sell contacts, or become a consultant.

### Young OPC / AI-founder side

Current Xuzhou reporting gives direct counterparty evidence:

- technical capability can be strong;
- brand recognition can be weak;
- customer cases can be absent;
- channel resources can be limited;
- business experience can be thin;
- single-person energy constrains offline customer development.

Existing OPC communities already provide mentors, business matching, space, policy and finance support. Therefore the target cannot be generic “give young founders mentors.”

## Narrow missing-edge question

Can a senior actor contribute a **bounded commercial co-pilot role** to one exact OPC outcome, where the value is more concrete than mentoring but much lighter than employment or consulting?

Examples of bounded contributions to test, not assume:

- customer qualification before a founder spends days pursuing the wrong account;
- buyer-language translation before a first meeting;
- preparation for one industry negotiation;
- identifying the real decision chain for a named type of buyer;
- reviewing whether a proposed value proposition matches industry reality;
- a lawful warm-context introduction only where both sides explicitly consent;
- joining one customer-validation conversation as a domain credibility node.

The role must never depend on bribery, opaque influence, misrepresentation, misuse of confidential relationships or selling privileged access.

## Senior-side interview card

Capture only:

- previous domain / function;
- what knowledge or judgment remains callable today;
- whether they want any continued contribution at all;
- what they explicitly refuse to do;
- preferred commitment size: 30 min / 2 h / one meeting / one week / none;
- whether they prefer volunteer, paid, reciprocal, equity-like, or no economic relationship;
- one recent example where someone asked them for help;
- what made that help easy or annoying;
- whether current silver-talent / volunteer / expert systems already meet their desired participation mode.

Do not collect sensitive personal profiles, private relationship lists or confidential employer information.

## OPC-side interview card

Ask for the **most recent real blocked commercial outcome**, not abstract opinions:

- what customer/outcome was sought;
- where exactly the attempt stopped;
- current workaround;
- what was tried through OPC community, mentor, friend, agency or platform;
- why it did not solve the exact blockage;
- whether the blockage was trust, domain context, buyer language, qualification, negotiation, channel or something else;
- whether a senior domain node could have changed the outcome;
- what objective result would prove value;
- what commitment they would make if the role existed.

## Promotion gate

Do not pair anyone until all are true:

- `>= 3/5` OPC actors show the same bounded blockage after existing support routes;
- `>= 3/5` senior actors have callable relevant resources and prefer a bounded non-employment role;
- at least 2 plausible pair-types can define a concrete outcome;
- an ethical/legal boundary for relationship use is explicit;
- at least one side makes a real economic or reciprocal commitment.

## Kill

Kill if:

- existing mentors/community matching already solve the blockage;
- senior value is only generic advice;
- the role depends on influence-peddling or hidden access;
- seniors do not want this participation mode;
- founders will not make a real commitment;
- the founder of this project must repeatedly become the salesperson/consultant.

# First-wave Probe B — `PF-HIGHSKILL-CAREGIVER-FRACTIONAL-008`

## Observed resource gradient

The target is not “cheap mom labor.”

The target condition is:

`VERIFIABLE PROFESSIONAL CAPABILITY + FRAGMENTED RECURRING TIME`

against:

`SMALL ORGANIZATION NEEDS A BOUNDED PROFESSIONAL OUTCOME + CANNOT JUSTIFY FULL-TIME ROLE`

Public evidence supports a structural mismatch: many flexible “mom jobs” remain concentrated in production, customer service, housekeeping or other lower-value task classes, while some highly educated or experienced caregivers report difficulty finding professional-role matches. Public policy already encourages flexible professional and management roles, so the capability pool should not be assumed low-skill.

## Actor-side boundaries

Do not ask about:

- intimate family details;
- health conditions;
- relationship status;
- reasons for childbirth/care choices;
- financial desperation.

Only capture what is necessary to test work-shape fit:

- prior role / verifiable skill;
- evidence of capability;
- preferred work mode;
- stable available time windows;
- maximum synchronous requirement tolerated;
- task classes they can own to acceptance;
- minimum acceptable commercial terms;
- what current job/freelance routes fail to provide.

## Small-operator side

Capture only recent outcomes that were genuinely left undone or handled badly because a full-time hire or agency was uneconomic.

Possible categories are discovery prompts, not assumptions:

- bookkeeping preparation / reconciliation support;
- customer research;
- supplier research;
- QA/test execution;
- design/document production;
- CRM cleanup and follow-up;
- operations documentation;
- bilingual desk research;
- recruiting coordination;
- short-cycle project administration.

Exclude regulated professional work unless the actor has the required qualifications and the contract structure is lawful.

## Promotion gate

- `>= 4/8` capability-side actors show verifiable professional skills plus a recurring fragmented-time pattern;
- `>= 4/8` small operators show real repeated outcomes that do not justify a full-time hire;
- at least 2 outcome classes are asynchronous enough to survive fragmented schedules;
- objective acceptance can be written before work starts;
- pricing cannot rely on constrained workers accepting below-market compensation;
- at least one real paid pilot path exists.

## Kill

Kill if:

- all available tasks are ordinary low-value gig work already commoditized;
- tasks require continuous synchronous presence;
- coordination/rework cost consumes the surplus;
- skill verification is too expensive relative to task value;
- the model only works by underpaying caregivers;
- no payer commits.
