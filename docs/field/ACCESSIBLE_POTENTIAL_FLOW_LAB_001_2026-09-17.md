# Accessible Potential-Flow Formation Lab 001 — 2026-09-17

Status: **READY FOR LOW-PERMISSION FIELD OBSERVATION / NO OUTREACH SENT**

This packet replaces the idea that the current P0 should be one difficult enterprise/data route. The current objective is to learn how the engine discovers **accessible potential gradients** in ordinary reality and tests whether a genuinely missing exchange edge exists.

The engine is not being redefined as an elderly project, student project, community project, local-services project, or gig marketplace.

`retiree × student` is one useful laboratory because the state transitions and resource asymmetry are easy to observe. It is not the doctrine.

## 1. P0 selection principle

For the first external-truth cycles, prefer hypotheses where:

- real actors are easy to reach through ordinary lawful channels;
- the first decisive facts can be learned without proprietary systems or sensitive dossiers;
- no enterprise procurement is needed before learning whether the phenomenon exists;
- no capital-intensive build is required;
- no software must be built before the first falsification;
- the actor's state change, underused resource, behavior and workaround can be observed directly;
- the hypothesis can die after a few conversations/observations instead of months of coordination.

This does **not** mean difficult B2B/data opportunities are bad. It means they are poor first laboratories for learning whether the engine can see and organize latent value.

## 2. What “potential flow” means here

Potential flow is a field metaphor, not a new closed ontology.

Look for observable asymmetry such as:

- time surplus next to task/structure deficit;
- experience/judgment surplus next to execution/context deficit;
- AI/digital execution surplus next to real-world problem/trust deficit;
- idle space next to activity/community deficit;
- local knowledge/relationships next to packaging/distribution deficit;
- community attention/trust next to unorganized capability;
- fragmented available time next to asynchronous bounded work;
- idle local asset next to route-to-market deficit.

The engine must then ask:

`STATE CHANGE -> RESOURCE UNDERUSE -> COMPLEMENTARY DEFICIT -> EXISTING ROUTE SEARCH -> MISSING EDGE? -> CHEAP FIELD TEST`

Never promote merely because two sides look complementary.

## 3. First-wave laboratories

### A. PF-INTERGEN-JUDGMENT-EXECUTION-001

Actors:
- recent/new retirees or other experienced people leaving a full-time organizational role;
- university students / young AI-digital executors.

Potential gradient:
- experienced actor: judgment, context, relationships, credibility, time;
- young actor: AI, software, content, search, rapid execution, learning speed;
- experienced actor may lose the organization that made those resources callable;
- young actor may lack real problems, context and trust that make execution valuable.

Do **not** start with a product. Do not pitch an expert marketplace, student freelancing, digital tutoring, or consulting.

First observation:
- 5 experienced-transition actors;
- 5 students/young executors;
- interviewed separately;
- map only: state change, resources now less used, desired states, repeated workarounds, activities refused, existing routes already tried.

Promote only if a repeated cross-side outcome emerges after existing-route search.

### B. PF-OFFPEAK-SPACE-LOCAL-HOST-002

Actors:
- small shops/community spaces with recurring off-peak capacity;
- residents/creators/retired teachers/hobby experts with callable skill;
- nearby people seeking small, low-commitment activities or social connection.

Potential gradient:
- empty time-slot capacity;
- underused human skill;
- nearby low-intensity demand.

Counterevidence is strong: venue rental, community rooms, night schools and host models already exist. Therefore the generic exchange is **not** a discovery.

First observation:
- 5 reachable spaces across at least two off-peak windows;
- 5 potential hosts;
- record actual idle windows, owner objective, host skill, current alternative and coordination blocker.

The only interesting edge would be a repeated micro-outcome too small/informal for ordinary venue/class economics but still capable of producing surplus for all required actors.

### C. PF-MICROBUSINESS-REALPROBLEM-STUDENT-003

Actors:
- small local merchants/tiny organizations;
- students/young AI-capable executors.

Potential gradient:
- small actors have real but often tiny operational problems;
- students need real problems and evidence, not more simulated projects;
- AI can reduce the cost of execution.

Counterevidence is very strong: agencies, freelancers, internships, enterprise practice and OPC programs already exist.

Therefore the first question is not “can students help small business?” It is:

> Are there recurring problem classes that are below agency economic thresholds, still too real for coursework, objectively bounded, and not dependent on unpaid student labor?

First observation:
- collect 10 recent problems from reachable small actors;
- collect only outcome/current workaround/deadline/acceptance fields;
- separately verify 10 students' evidenced capability classes;
- do not match anyone until a repeated missing edge survives market-closure search.

## 4. Parallel scans, not current commitments

### PF-COMMUNITY-SKILL-ATTENTION-006

Ask whether residents have useful callable capabilities that are trapped between two unsuitable structures:
- volunteering is too open-ended/unpaid;
- normal commercial services are too formal/high-commitment.

One community/residential cluster is enough for an initial observation. No individual profiling.

### PF-FRAGMENTED-TIME-ASYNC-TASK-004

Caregivers/stay-at-home parents and others may have fragmented recurring time plus existing skills. But flexible-work programs, remote work and data-labeling markets already cover much of the generic exchange.

Only continue if a higher-value task class repeatedly fails current flexible-work channels.

### PF-RETURNER-LOCAL-ASSET-MARKET-005

Returning youth may bring external-market and digital capability while small local assets remain idle. But policy and incubators already explicitly organize this pattern.

Only inspect household/micro-community assets that are too small or irregular for formal programs and can be tested without capital-heavy activation.

## 6. Field capture card

For any accessible actor, record only:

- ACTOR_CLASS
- OBSERVED_STATE
- RECENT_STATE_CHANGE
- OBJECTIVE_RESOURCES
- RESOURCE_NOW_LESS_USED
- WHAT_CHANGED_IN_DAILY_BEHAVIOR
- WHAT_OUTCOME_THEY_NOW_VALUE_OR_AVOID
- CURRENT_WORKAROUND
- WHAT_EXISTING_ROUTE_THEY_ALREADY_USE
- WHAT_THEY_REFUSE_TO_DO
- WHAT_REQUIRES_ANOTHER ACTOR/NODE
- FIRST CHEAP FALSIFICATION

Do not begin with “what product do you want?”

Do not ask for sensitive traits that are unnecessary to the exchange hypothesis.

## 7. Potential-flow evidence before pairing

A useful early signal has four parts:

1. **observable resource asymmetry** — something materially exists and is underused;
2. **observable blocked state transition** — an actor repeatedly cannot reach a valued state using current routes;
3. **complementary node** — another reachable actor/node controls something that could relax that blocker;
4. **non-closure** — existing routes do not already solve the same state transition cheaply and acceptably.

Only then design a counterfactual exchange.
