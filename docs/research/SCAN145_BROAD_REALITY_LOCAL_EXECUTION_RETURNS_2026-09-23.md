## Initial result

### F1 — retained for research only

Current official data establish a very large replenishing cross-border goods/services field and a large pool of platform-mediated local workers. Current Upwork/Fiverr flows show real paid behavior for on-site representation, sample collection, local running and evidence capture. The hypothesized bridge is deliberately narrower than sourcing or factory inspection: a standardized bounded physical-task packet with identity, location/time proof, evidence checklist, chain-of-custody and acceptance/settlement.

This is **not** yet a commercial candidate. Upwork/Fiverr may already own enough of discovery, contracting, escrow and reputation that no distinct operator edge remains. Required next evidence: exact incumbent/control-surface, task-standardization limits, insurance/liability, chain-of-custody, city coverage and repeatable acquisition without founder-by-founder selling.

### F2 — retained for research only

Official 2026 evidence shows the overseas-warehouse footprint expanding rapidly and policy makers explicitly treating cross-border returns as a difficult, costly and slow reverse-flow problem. B-Stock proves replenishing resale/liquidation demand; ReverseLogix proves rules-based inspection/disposition orchestration already exists.

The hypothesized missing edge is a cross-border-SME rail that maps SKU/condition/value rules to local restock, relabel, repair, recommerce, liquidation, donation or consolidated return. This is **not** yet a commercial candidate. Required next evidence: exact incumbent fit for China-origin SMEs, overseas-warehouse integration/API/rights, local resale rights, disposition economics, settlement and repeatable seller acquisition.

### F3 — closed

Private-pile sharing is a real resource imbalance, but current evidence already identifies Star Charge/TELD/State Grid sharing platforms and shows that property access, liability, insurance, privacy and maintenance are the difficult control surfaces. No distinct lightweight router is evidenced.

### F4 — closed

The state actively pushes scientific-instrument sharing, while live institutional systems already support online discovery, booking, approval and charging. Instrument-specific expert operation further weakens a generic independent router.

### F5 — closed

Network freight is massively regenerative, but the 2026 regulatory model and Full Truck Alliance's current scale show that matching, contracting, transaction protection and network density are already owned at industrial scale.
