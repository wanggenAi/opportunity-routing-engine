# Scan 131 — Operational control + contracted cashflow

Date: 2026-09-23  
Primary domain: China  
Result: **0 retained / 0 commercial promotions / FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW = NOT_PROVEN**

## Why this scan exists

Scan 130 proved that transfer rails and income-right markets can exist without a source-bound small asset whose proceeds, demand and control move together. Scan 131 therefore moved outside software, content, domains and IP rights and looked for current China-native physical or operational control assets where the cashflow itself is bound to the transfer.

The admission floor was not relaxed. A formation needed a realistic small-operator entry path, transferable operating control, explicit post-transfer income rights, independently source-bound contracted receipts and low recurring human delivery.

## F1 — executed EV charging-station control transfer

A current listed-company disclosure is the strongest mechanism proof in this scan. Twenty-three Hainan charging-station assets were sold on 2026-09-17 as a complete operating asset group. The disclosed rights include equipment, land-use lease rights, co-operation agreements, sublease rights and station operating rights. Multiple automaker contracts specify fixed monthly rents or usage-linked service fees, and the acquisition agreement assigns all operating income from 2026-09-01 to the buyer.

This clears the key conceptual question: **physical operating control and contracted proceeds can transfer together in current mainland China.**

It still fails the target formation. The bundled price is RMB 10,719,284.85; the buyer had already been entrusted to operate the assets; and supporting agreements may require counterparty migration, sublease or collection-and-forwarding workarounds. That is not evidence that a fresh small operator can reproducibly acquire the same control surface.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_EXECUTED_CHARGING_STATION_CONTROL_TRANSFER_BINDS_CONTRACTED_REVENUE_AND_POST_TRANSFER_INCOME_BUT_10_7M_BUNDLED_CAPITAL_INCUMBENT_OPERATOR_AND_AGREEMENT_MIGRATION_FAIL_SMALL_OPERATOR_REPRODUCIBILITY`

## F2 — current small leased property with rent-right succession

A live Wuhu exchange listing opened on 2026-09-21. Two leased properties are offered at RMB 468,700 and RMB 522,800. If a non-tenant buyer wins, the original lease continues; the lessor identity is changed; prepaid rent attributable to the buyer is handed over; and future rent belongs to the buyer.

This is materially closer to the target entry economics than F1 because the asset is small-ticket and the payer relationship survives transfer. It nevertheless fails closed because the public listing does not disclose the rent amount, and the observed leases expire on 2027-03-21 and 2027-01-07. Without rent quantum, a source-bound yield cannot be calculated. With only a short remaining term, post-expiry cashflow would require a new tenancy rather than continuing automatically.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_CURRENT_SMALL_LEASED_PROPERTY_TRANSFER_PRESERVES_TENANT_AND_RENTS_BUT_NO_PUBLIC_RENT_QUANTUM_AND_SHORT_REMAINING_TERM_FAIL_SOURCE_BOUND_DURABLE_CASHFLOW`

## F3 — affordable charging hardware without operating control

A Henan public-resource listing offered a Pingdingshan charging-station equipment package for RMB 298,000 and allowed natural-person buyers. The apparent affordability disappears at the control boundary: the station is on leased land, the access route is leased, and the buyer must dismantle and remove the assets. No site right, charging-platform identity, customer contract or receipt stream transfers.

This is an explicit regression guard: **cheap charging hardware must never be promoted as a cheap cashflowing charging business.**

Verdict: `DEMOTED_SMALL_CHARGING_STATION_ASSET_PRICE_IS_ACCESSIBLE_BUT_REQUIRED_DISMANTLING_AND_EXCLUDED_SITE_RIGHTS_MEAN_NO_OPERATING_CONTROL_OR_CASHFLOW_TRANSFERS`

## F4 — current LED advertising operating right

A Wuhu LED billboard operating-right listing began on 2026-09-23 with a one-year floor rent of RMB 131,000 and natural-person eligibility. The installed LED asset makes scheduling technically machine-servable, but the billboard is explicitly idle. The operator must acquire advertisers, obtain/maintain advertising permissions, submit content for review, bear energy and damage costs, and reserve 10% of daily advertising time for the lessor without compensation.

This is operating access, not inherited cashflow.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_CURRENT_LED_AD_OPERATING_RIGHT_IS_SMALL_AND_MACHINE_SERVABLE_BUT_ASSET_IS_IDLE_AND_REQUIRES_NEW_ADVERTISER_SALES_COMPLIANCE_AND_MAINTENANCE_WITH_NO_EXISTING_PROCEEDS`

## Scan 132 boundary

Require the complete asset-level economic tuple before retention:

- acquisition price;
- explicit contracted receipt amount;
- contract term;
- identified counterparty;
- explicit post-transfer receipt/control right;
- low recurring human labor.

Do not lock onto property as the ontology. Search across fresh physical or contract-control assets. Do not reuse Scan 060-131 formations. Do not use regulated securities or financial products. Do not relax gates.
