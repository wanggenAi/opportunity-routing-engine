# Xuzhou Opportunity Baseline — 2026-09-08

Status: `EARLY EVIDENCE / NOT YET PRODUCT-MARKET FIT`

Purpose: establish the first local observation field for the Opportunity Routing Engine. This memo records public evidence, candidate mismatches, and what still needs direct validation.

## 2. First candidate opportunity set

These are hypotheses, not conclusions.

### XZ-001 — Managed SME Micro-Project Exchange

Mismatch:
- many SMEs have bounded tasks too small or irregular for full-time hiring;
- Xuzhou has a large student/graduate/young-skill supply pool;
- employers already pay meaningful monthly salaries for content, ecommerce, data and digital capabilities.

Potential transaction:
- enterprise submits a result-oriented task;
- operator clarifies scope and acceptance criteria;
- AI + vetted student/freelancer/specialist capability is routed to the task;
- operator performs QA and delivery control;
- enterprise pays for completed output, not labor placement.

Examples:
- 20 short videos;
- product-data cleanup;
- competitor research;
- ecommerce listing cleanup;
- PPT/design/data-processing packages;
- small automation script;
- Chinese Internet research.

Critical unknown:
Will Xuzhou SMEs pay RMB 500–5,000 for standardized external micro-projects instead of assigning the work internally or hiring full-time?

Preliminary status: `HIGH-PRIORITY INVESTIGATION`.

### XZ-002 — Youth-Shop Content / Customer-Acquisition Micro-Pack

Mismatch:
- youth shops report customer shortage and operating weakness;
- students and young creators need paid portfolio work;
- local businesses already pay full-time salaries for short-video/content operations.

Potential transaction:
- fixed deliverable pack, e.g. store photo set + 6 short videos + 12 reusable content assets + basic channel-data report;
- AI supports scripting, editing assistance, copywriting and quality checks;
- local student creator handles permitted on-site content capture where needed.

Critical unknown:
Will small shops pay a bounded project fee rather than expect revenue guarantees or free social-media help?

Preliminary status: `HIGH-PRIORITY INVESTIGATION`.

### XZ-003 — Remote SME AI / Data Micro-Automation

Mismatch:
- Xuzhou SMEs are under active digital-transformation pressure;
- AI application scenarios are being solicited;
- many tasks may be smaller than a full system implementation.

Potential transaction:
- tightly bounded remote deliverables only: Excel processing, document extraction, report generation, information classification, simple workflow automation, knowledge search, data cleanup.

Constraint:
Reject projects requiring uncontrolled on-site integration, old-system takeover, safety-critical production control, or undefined enterprise IT support.

Critical unknown:
Can a small remote package be sold directly to SMEs without becoming bespoke consulting?

Preliminary status: `INVESTIGATE`.

### XZ-004 — Skills-to-Paid-Project Conversion Layer

Mismatch:
- public and institutional programs train many people in AI, AIGC, short-video, drones, electrical work and other skills;
- training completion does not itself create a transaction or portfolio.

Potential transaction:
- route graduates into small, outcome-defined business projects;
- provide capability tests, task decomposition, QA and portfolio evidence.

Constraint:
Structure as project/service delivery, not unlicensed employment intermediation.

Critical unknown:
Who pays: the enterprise, the learner, the training organization, or a combination?

Preliminary status: `INVESTIGATE / PAYER UNCLEAR`.

### XZ-005 — Xuzhou Local Execution / Verification Desk

Mismatch:
- outside companies can research Xuzhou remotely but may need permitted local physical verification, store checks, public-information collection, sample pickup, event observation or documented field research.

Potential transaction:
- fixed evidence package with time/location/task checklist and documented output.

Critical unknown:
Frequency of paying external demand specifically for Xuzhou is not yet demonstrated.

Preliminary status: `WATCH / NEED DEMAND EVIDENCE`.

### XZ-006 — Idle Asset / Inventory Opportunity Routing

Mismatch:
- local public and private entities hold idle equipment/assets;
- buyers elsewhere may value the assets but discovery and valuation are fragmented.

Potential transaction:
- normalize asset data, find likely buyer groups, compare market references, route inquiries, assist documentation.

Critical unknown:
Can this create monetizable transactions without requiring regulated auction/brokerage activity or carrying inventory?

Preliminary status: `WATCH`.

### XZ-007 — Campus Graduation / Move-Out Liquidation Service

Mismatch:
- large annual graduating cohorts create recurring low-value idle goods;
- student buyers seek low-cost alternatives.

Potential transaction:
- aggregation, standardized listing, pickup-point coordination, resale bundles, or move-out liquidation assistance.

Critical unknown:
Margins may be too low and dominant platforms/social groups may already solve the problem adequately.

Preliminary status: `LOW-PRIORITY / MONETIZATION WEAK`.

### XZ-008 — Value-Conscious Local Service Router

Mismatch hypothesis:
- soft price environment and cautious consumption may increase demand for repair, rental, refurbishment, used alternatives and lower-cost substitutes.

Potential transaction:
- verified local service comparison / routing rather than owning inventory.

Critical unknown:
Need Xuzhou-specific transaction evidence showing actual migration toward repair/rental/used services and a payer model for the router.

Preliminary status: `WATCH / EVIDENCE GAP`.

### XZ-009 — Night-Economy Merchant Content Network

Mismatch:
- Xuzhou has large tourist and night-consumption flows;
- many small merchants need discoverability and reusable content;
- students/young creators can produce local content at low cost.

Potential transaction:
- merchant purchases standardized content assets; creators receive project fees; operator handles brief, matching, QA and delivery.

Critical unknown:
Need proof that independent merchants will repeatedly pay for content rather than rely on owner-generated content or agencies.

Preliminary status: `INVESTIGATE`.

### XZ-010 — Aging-Service Coordination Layer

Mismatch:
- home elderly-care services are fragmented across many service types and providers;
- public purchasing proves real expenditure exists.

Potential transaction concept:
- information/coordination/quality-evidence layer around licensed providers rather than providing regulated care directly.

Constraint:
Healthcare, elderly care, personal safety and consumer protection create higher liability and regulatory burden.

Critical unknown:
Whether families or institutions will pay separately for a coordination layer.

Preliminary status: `WATCH / HIGHER REGULATORY RISK`.

## 3. Current strongest wedge

The first scan suggests a potentially important local mismatch:

> **Xuzhou has a large pool of young/student capability and a large base of businesses that already spend money on fragmented digital/content/operational work, but there may be no trusted, result-oriented layer that converts small business needs into bounded projects and routes them to the right capability with QA.**

This is not yet proven.

The next evidence requirement is NOT more philosophy and NOT a platform build. It is direct payer validation.

## 4. Next field-verification questions

For 10–20 local SMEs / youth shops:
1. What small work did you need in the last 90 days but delayed, gave to an employee reluctantly, or outsourced poorly?
2. What did it cost in money or staff time?
3. Would you buy the same result as a fixed deliverable instead of hiring a person?
4. What price would make the decision easy?
5. What would make you distrust a student/freelancer provider?
6. What acceptance evidence would make the task low risk?

For 20–30 students / young skilled people:
1. What can you produce at a professional-enough level today?
2. What paid project would you accept this week?
3. What is the smallest fee worth doing?
4. What proof/portfolio can you show?
5. What tasks do you refuse or cannot safely do?
6. Would you accept standardized briefs, deadlines, QA and revision rules?

## 5. Build rule

Do not build a marketplace UI yet.

The first system should be a research + routing ledger that records:
- demand;
- payer;
- capability;
- evidence;
- price;
- delivery specification;
- outcome;
- failure reason.

Only repeated successful transactions justify platform automation.
