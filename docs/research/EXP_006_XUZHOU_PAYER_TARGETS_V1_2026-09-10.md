## Purpose

Turn EXP-006 from a generic field instruction into a prioritized set of **real Xuzhou actors and public discovery channels** that can resolve the payer question quickly.

This document does not claim any venue will pay. It only ranks targets by evidence that they:

1. already serve the relevant youth segment;
2. have used experience/activity formats;
3. have some evidence of traffic / conversion value;
4. are locally reachable;
5. can plausibly expose a venue-payer or resource-owner decision.

`PUBLIC EXISTENCE != CURRENT AVAILABILITY != PAYER PASS`.

Current storefront and decision-maker access must be rechecked on the day of field work.

## 1. Tier A — first target

### T-A1 — 空山半闲咖啡

**Role hypothesis**
- `RESOURCE_OWNER`: coffee venue / off-peak space;
- `CAPABILITY_PROVIDER`: coffee-specific capability / barista;
- `PAYER_CANDIDATE`: venue, if qualified youth traffic has measurable value;
- `BENEFICIARY`: venue + participating youth;
- `ORCHESTRATOR`: EXP-006 operator.

**Why this is the strongest first target**

Public reporting provides unusually close fit to the exact test:

- it is a youth-oriented independent coffee venue in Xuzhou's Gulou central commercial / Huanglou area;
- reporting places the store near `1818美食广场`;
- it has participated in the `青春小店 / 青年夜校` model;
- public reports explicitly mention **coffee tasting** as one of the activities hosted there;
- the owner/operator has publicly attributed roughly **30% revenue growth** to the period after youth-night-school / traffic support;
- later reporting describes the same space-reuse model as associated with about **20% higher customer unit price** and **15% higher repeat rate**;
- a 2026-07-22 Xuzhou Daily report still described the venue as a popular central-commercial-area youth destination, providing relatively recent evidence that the business remained active at that time.

**Current confidence**: `HIGH` that this is the best public-evidence venue to ask first; `UNKNOWN` on current off-peak capacity and willingness to pay an external organizer.

**Location evidence**
- Gulou / Huanglou central commercial area;
- public report: near `1818美食广场`.

Do not invent a precise storefront number. Verify current map listing before departure.

**Contact route**
1. preferred: in-person visit and ask for owner / operating manager / person who can approve event economics;
2. public map / Dianping / Xiaohongshu / Douyin listing may be used only to verify current opening and a public business contact route;
3. no private phone / private social account should be collected into this public repository.

No currently verified public phone number was found in this research pass.

**Exact first ask**

> If I bring 8–10 first-time or low-frequency young customers for a fixed 60–90 minute coffee-flavor event in one weak time window, would you approve a RMB200 guaranteed organizer contribution payable after the agreed attendance condition is met? If not, what measurable condition would make that amount rational for you?

A contingent but approved / written monetary commitment counts as Level 3 if the triggering condition is explicit. Free space or free drinks do not pass the venue-payer gate.

Before leaving:
- search current public map listing for `空山半闲咖啡`;
- confirm the storefront appears active;
- note opening hours only for navigation; do not copy private contact data into the repo.

### Step 2 — T-A1 decision-maker conversation

Goal: get one of four outcomes:

1. `VENUE_LEVEL3_PASS` — >=RMB200 approved monetary commitment, including explicit contingent guarantee;
2. `RESOURCE_ONLY` — venue/capability contribution but no money;
3. `PAYER_FAIL` — decision-maker rejects external organizer payment even under measurable conditions;
4. `REACH_FAIL` — no decision-maker route after reasonable attempt.

Do not convert `RESOURCE_ONLY` into payer PASS.

### Step 3 — two nearby merchant controls

Interview two additional independent youth-facing merchants / cafes in the same commercial cluster.

Purpose:
- determine whether T-A1 is exceptional because of public-program support;
- compare off-peak capacity and acquisition spending;
- ask the same RMB200 contingent organizer-fee question.

### Step 4 — one higher-level payer comparator

If time permits, test `云玺中心 / 青年发展型街区` or another identifiable commercial-block operator.

Purpose:
- determine whether the payer sits at store level or commercial-area / sponsor level.

### Step 5 — participants only after venue/date structure exists

Do not ask people to pay for an event with no credible venue/time.

Target first batch: 10 qualified participants.

Offer the exact same event and RMB49 refundable booking/deposit structure.

## 8. Commercial discipline discovered during target research

The public evidence around `空山半闲` creates a **selection-bias risk**.

It may be a successful case because it already received:
- official exposure;
- youth-night-school traffic;
- event programming support;
- inclusion in youth-shop promotion.

Therefore:

> One successful subsidized / officially supported venue cannot prove that an independent merchant will pay an external orchestrator.

EXP-006 must include at least two merchant controls without relying on the same public-program narrative.

This is now a required anti-bias rule for the experiment.
