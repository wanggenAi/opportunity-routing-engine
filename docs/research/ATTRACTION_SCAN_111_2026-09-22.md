## F1 — Zhejiang restaurant partnership

A 2026 Zhejiang district-court judgment reconstructs one restaurant partnership from pre-opening through shutdown. The court reviewed schedules and supporting records, confirmed RMB548,372.41 of pre-opening spending, evaluated monthly customer receipts/costs, addressed Lu's RMB5,000/month manager compensation, adjusted contaminated late-period records, confirmed RMB8,000 residual assets and found no refundable lease-deposit remainder.

Source:
- https://www.chinalawyer.co/blog/wenshu-0196c4d43563/

Important operating figures include August revenue RMB209,968.35 / profit RMB56,260.39; September revenue RMB140,697.05 with court-adjusted loss RMB132,118.43; October revenue RMB154,061.78; November RMB109,050.42; December RMB94,032.69. Because Lu simultaneously operated other restaurants, the court found possible cross-store use of purchases/labor and set October-December combined loss at RMB60,000 instead of accepting every raw line.

The judgment's RMB706,335.70 cumulative-loss number is **not** treated as clean operating P&L. It is a lifecycle settlement number incorporating pre-opening spend and residual treatment.

This is the strongest ledger closure so far, but the evidence itself kills attraction: the controlling operator's management/attention is an economic input, and the store remains a conventional labor-, procurement- and site-intensive restaurant.

**Verdict:** demoted.

This closes audit + control + transaction price better than prior scans. It still has no external operating value flow: audited/current revenue is zero.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Beijing zero-employee technology center 6% listing

A September 2026 Beijing property-exchange listing offers 6% for RMB1.4555m. The entity reports zero employees.

Source:
- https://ggzyfw.beijing.gov.cn/jyxxgqgpplxx/20260911/5707200.html

2025 audited revenue was RMB427,000 against net loss RMB5.2066m. Through June 2026, revenue was RMB63,200 and net loss RMB1.0782m. Net assets remained deeply negative.

This shows that low headcount can coexist with actual revenue, but not attractive economics. More importantly, the offer is only 6%, the 94% shareholder retains dominance and pre-emption rights, and no completed transfer is published as of 2026-09-22.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

```text
AUDITABLE LEDGER + EXIT
can close the evidence problem

but

FOUNDER-HEAVY PHYSICAL DELIVERY
or
ZERO / NEGATIVE CUSTOMER ECONOMICS
or
NONCONTROL / UNEXECUTED RIGHTS
still kills attraction
```

Scan 112 should stop spending the retrieval budget on merely finding more complete ledgers. It should keep every floor and prioritize a narrower intersection:

```text
LOW HEADCOUNT / MACHINE-LIGHT DELIVERY
+ AUDITED POSITIVE EXTERNAL CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NORMALIZED PROFIT OR CASHFLOW
+ EXECUTED CONTROLLING TRANSFER / RIGHTS EVENT
+ LOW OWNER LABOR
+ NONCOMMODITY CONTROL
```
