## Purpose

Scans 101–102 established that operator-acquirable control plus real external money is not enough: easy-to-acquire control repeatedly collapsed into local physical-asset yield or commodity digital control.

Scan 103 raises the evidence floor without turning the new property into a product category:

```text
CURRENT / RECENT CHINA PARTICIPANT-RECORDED REALITY
+ INDEPENDENT OBJECTIVE CORROBORATION
+ CURRENT EXTERNAL PAYMENT / SPEND / DIRECT MARGIN
+ OPERATOR-SPECIFIC NON-COMMODITY CONTROL
  NOT AVAILABLE TO AN EQUIVALENT COMPETENT ENTRANT
  ON MATERIALLY SIMILAR TERMS
+ FRESH-OPERATOR ACQUIRABILITY
+ EXACT INCUMBENT / RIGHTS / FOUNDER-INDEPENDENCE /
  GENERIC-AGENT / NORMALIZED-MARGIN / MACHINE-DELEGATABILITY PREFLIGHT
→ RETAIN ONLY IF THE SCARCE CONTROL CAN BECOME A FRESH,
  FOUNDER-LIGHT OPERATOR POSITION
```

## Result

| Formation | Non-commodity control + money motion | Why it still fails |
|---|---|---|
| BiliNote open source → hosted Pro | 7k+ star repository reputation, organic inbound and participant-reported RMB 24,264.30 cumulative receipts | Scarce asset is the incumbent operator's historical repository/community/inbound graph; a fresh entrant cannot acquire it on similar terms, while normalized founder-light margin remains unproven |
| Bilibili creator audience → exclusive contract | ~500k Bilibili followers, three-year exclusive agreement and current incentive/ad income | Audience, trust and contract are creator-identity-bound; recurring creative labor and platform allocation remain decisive |
| SEO-accumulated tool site → global subscriptions | Participant reports >USD 5k monthly subscriptions and server/AI cost around 20% of revenue | Exact site/ranking position is withheld and cannot be independently verified; product/SEO execution is reproducible and discovery remains Google-controlled |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — BiliNote open-source reputation to paid hosted service

A 2026-08-26 China operator retrospective reports that BiliNote had passed 7,000 GitHub stars and that accumulated open-source recognition continued to send real visits to Bilinote Pro. From 2026-05-12 through 2026-08-26 the operator reported 5,402 registered users, 602 users with at least one payment, 674 paid orders and RMB 24,264.30 cumulative receipts. Subscription revenue was reported as 89.6% of receipts, with 282 active subscriptions at the measurement date.

Participant source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1237312

The public repository independently corroborates a live MIT-licensed project with thousands of stars/forks, substantial commit history and an explicit Pro-service path.

Objective sources:
- https://github.com/JefferyHcool/BiliNote
- https://www.bilinote.net/

This is real non-commodity control for the current operator: copying the code does not copy the historical repository identity, community, stars, recommendations or organic inbound. But that same history makes the position **incumbent-owned rather than fresh-operator-acquirable**. Current alternative video-note projects and tools also show that the underlying transformation primitive is not itself scarce.

Substitute source:
- https://github.com/yyyzl/bilinote

The operator further states that maintenance, support, content, channel operation and commercialization still require continued input. Gross receipts are proven; normalized founder-light margin is not.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Creator audience to exclusive platform contract

A 2026-08-28 China creator reports roughly 300k Douyin followers, 80k Xiaohongshu followers, 70k Kuaishou followers and nearly 500k Bilibili followers after about six months of content production. The creator reports a three-year Bilibili exclusive agreement, with Bilibili advertising work contributing roughly 70% of self-media income.

Participant source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1237785

The same account reports an exclusive incentive tied to seven-day views, ordinary creation incentives around RMB 1,000–2,000, and months around RMB 10,000 when advertising orders are available. It also reports producing more than 130 topics in about six months and personally sourcing topics from current media and events.

Bilibili's official commercial materials corroborate platform-controlled creator authorization/commercial-order rails and creator advertising monetization.

Objective sources:
- https://www.bilibili.com/blackboard/activity-aJxmmsBdjy.html
- https://e.bilibili.com/observe/observe_98.html

The audience, account history, trust and exclusive contract are genuinely non-commodity. They are also identity-bound, accumulated through recurring human output and dependent on Bilibili's recommendation/commercial allocation. A generic agent may assist production but cannot inherit the creator's audience or contract.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Accumulated SEO position to global subscriptions

A 2026-08-10 China independent developer reports that a seven-month-old tool site exceeded USD 5,000 in monthly subscription revenue, with additional pay-as-you-go revenue. The same operator says a July hosting outage materially reduced Google search traffic and much of the following work went into restoring subscription traffic.

Participant sources:
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1233180
- https://origin.v2ex.com/t/1217257

Earlier reports from the same operator describe roughly 490 Stripe subscribers plus about 20 PayPal subscribers and USD 3,300 monthly subscription revenue at five months, along with automated multilingual publishing, some purchased backlinks, paid creator promotion and organic recommendations.

Google's current documentation confirms that ranking is governed by external ranking systems using many page- and site-wide signals, while link schemes and scaled content designed primarily to manipulate ranking can violate spam policies.

Objective sources:
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide
- https://developers.google.com/search/help/site-position-in-search-faq
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

The operator reports server and AI costs near 20% of revenue, which is materially stronger gross economics than the other Scan 103 samples. But the exact site is intentionally undisclosed, so the ranking asset, product surface and conversion path cannot be independently corroborated. The operator also describes the product as easy to copy, while much of the acquisition playbook is accessible to equivalent entrants. Search discovery remains externally controlled and demonstrated outage fragility.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

The first non-commodity-control pass proves an important distinction:

```text
REAL NON-COMMODITY CONTROL
+ REAL EXTERNAL MONEY
!= FRESH-OPERATOR-ACQUIRABLE EDGE
```

Historical reputation, audience, trust, contract status and ranking position can be hard to copy precisely **because an incumbent has already accumulated them**. Scarcity alone therefore does not establish a new entrant's controllable commercial wedge.

One pass is not enough to turn this observation into a new search ontology. Scan 104 must repeat the same non-commodity-control + external-money floor on fresh formation-diverse China evidence while making fresh-operator entry acquirability explicit.
