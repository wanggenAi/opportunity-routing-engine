## Why this scan changed evidence priority

After two independent broad-reality samples, Scan 034 did not add another preferred mechanism. It only raised
the evidence bar for where research time goes:

```text
DIRECT EXISTING PAYMENT
OR
REPEATED OBSERVED WORKAROUND
→ THEN APPLY THE SAME COMMERCIAL HARD FLOORS
```

The result is higher-quality pain evidence, but still zero retention.

## AI customer service → human handoff

The workaround is real. China's first national standard focused on human/AI customer-service collaboration
took effect on 2026-09-01 and explicitly targets hidden human-service entry, broken handoff and users having to
repeat their problem.

Source:
- https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pad/content/202609/02/content_30178631.html

But current Tencent Customer Service already provides the exact technical bridge:
- automatic detection of human-transfer intent;
- REST API transfer to a human;
- AI and human messages merged in one conversation;
- the human agent can see the prior AI conversation history.

Sources:
- https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/269/110624
- https://cloud.tencent.com/document/product/269/96056

Verdict: **demoted — native contact-center feature**.

## WeChat / Excel → CRM backfill

The repeated workaround is also real: current CRM vendors describe sales communication happening in WeChat,
lists maintained in Excel and follow-up later backfilled into CRM.

Sources:
- https://www.woscosmos.com/news/detail/2100876873029517313
- https://www.zoho.com.cn/crm/articles/sales-wechat0306.html
- https://qingflow.com/categories-guide/article/result/article2026_18442.html

But CRM/SCRM/low-code products already centralize, import, deduplicate and synchronize customer information.
The bridge is generic integration.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## Paid queue / reservation proxies

Direct payment is indisputable. A 2026 People's Daily investigation found restaurant queue services priced from
tens to more than 100 yuan, and paid proxy reservations for scarce public attractions.

Source:
- https://society.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0909/c428181-40795098.html

This is not a qualifying business edge. The seller does not create supply; the service captures/resells scarce
slots that venues intended to allocate fairly, and authorities/platforms actively suppress the behavior.

Verdict: **demoted — payment without legitimate new value**.

## General proxy/lifestyle services

Source:

Verdict: **demoted — recurring human delivery**.

## Main learning

Scan 034 confirms:

```text
DIRECT PAYMENT OR REPEATED WORKAROUND
IS A BETTER RESEARCH ENTRY SIGNAL

BUT

PAYMENT != FOUNDER-INDEPENDENT DELIVERY
WORKAROUND != UNOWNED CONTROL
PAID API != MOAT
```

One search-efficiency improvement is justified: future scans should kill cases earlier when the paid unit is
obviously recurring human time, physical custody/field work or capture of a platform-owned scarce resource.

That is a hard-floor prefilter, not a preferred business mechanism.
