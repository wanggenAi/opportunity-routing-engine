## Formation notes

### F1 — NYC VetConnectNYC / Combined Arms

The NYC Comptroller provides the cleanest temporal chain in this pass. DVS had already spent on prior portal delivery, allowed the Unite USA contract to expire, disabled the old portal, and fell back to manual forms. In September 2024 it contracted with Combined Arms for a replacement. The new portal launched in July 2025, eight months late. By May 2026 the City had paid $268,750 on the three-year contract.

After live use and payment, buyer-side review still documented inconsistent login, wrong or missing case routing, inaccurate case status, missing reminders and unresolved high-priority tickets. This is real same-outcome failure after money and production use.

It still fails Scan 080's required residual-layer distinction. Combined Arms is not a separately paid residual operator around another live portal; it is the replacement portal and case-coordination control surface itself. A new operator would also need governed access to veteran PII, case state and provider/referral workflows.

### F2 — Siloam Springs / BS&A utility billing

Siloam Springs approved a $318,685 BS&A software contract in June 2024. Most departments moved from Caselle to BS&A in March 2025, and utility billing began transition in October 2025.

By February-March 2026 the City documented failures severe enough for a formal material-breach and intent-to-terminate notice: meter imports failed or produced extreme billing fluctuations, auto-pay did not draft reliably, fees appeared incorrectly, tickets were closed while issues remained, and staff performed large amounts of manual correction and overtime. In July 2026 the City said it was returning utility billing to Caselle and recalculating accounts.

This is strong failure-after-live-use evidence, but it does not satisfy the exact Scan 080 money topology. The inspected public record proves an approved/live contract, not a separately executed recurring payment to a distinct non-incumbent residual provider. BS&A is the primary replacement platform/module whose own surface failed.

### F3 — DeKalb / PROSECUTORbyKarpel

DeKalb's PbK contract is useful as a negative control because the money evidence is unusually concrete: the contract includes annual subscription and companion services, runs September 2025 through December 2027, and the County reported $368,600 spent to date before approving a $32,000 data-extraction change order.

But the buyer does not describe the change order as failure. It says the extraction will support a smooth transition and let the Solicitor-General fully utilize the newly integrated system. The paid-after-go-live tail is therefore transition enablement, and both the platform and the companion work remain inside Karpel's proprietary control surface.

## Learning

Two independent passes now support the same conclusion:

```text
POST-PURCHASE FAILURE
+ REAL MONEY
!=
DISTINCT UNOWNED OPERATOR EDGE
```

The stricter procurement/audit join is also creating a discovery-source bias: it preferentially surfaces public institutional software where contracts, payments and audits are easy to join, and those records disproportionately describe already-formed vendor control surfaces.

The correct next move is therefore **not** to derive a provider-switch, multi-provider or router ontology and **not** to keep tightening the same procurement predicate. Scan 081 resets upstream to broad current reality and observed actor/state change before choosing a product category or procurement signal.
