## Question

Does the recurrent structure around low-utilization assets have enough direct real-world evidence to justify targeted commercial-structure validation?

This probe does **not** ask whether idle-asset activation is an attractive industry, whether we should enter it, or whether the operator should launch a business around it.

## Evidence dimensions

The probe evaluates five independent dimensions:

1. `STANDARDIZABILITY` — do multiple cases expose reusable process/interface/acceptance structure?
2. `COMPLEMENTARY_ACTOR_STRUCTURE` — are resource owner, operator/platform and beneficiary/counterparty roles observable?
3. `REGENERATING_EVENT_FLOW` — do vacancy, lease expiry, arrears, repeated inventory review or related lifecycle events naturally recur?
4. `REPEAT_MONETIZATION` — are there multiple independent accepted/contracted payment bases for this service/transaction class?
5. `COMPOUNDING` — does accumulated data, templates, trust or rules measurably make later cases cheaper/faster/safer/more reliable?

## Why compounding remains unknown

Digital asset platforms and recurring transaction systems show that data and workflows can accumulate. They do **not** yet prove that prior cases measurably reduce later acquisition/search/coordination/approval/QA cost or improve acceptance probability. A dashboard, database or digital platform is not compounding evidence by itself.

## Cheapest decisive local validation

If production confirms `STRUCTURE_VALIDATION_READY`, the next local probe should use 5–10 current Jiangsu/Xuzhou asset cases from multiple independent owners and preserve, for every case:

- normalized asset facts and rights/constraint profile;
- existing use/utilization state;
- candidate permissible scenarios;
- operator/user/transaction route search effort;
- approval/compliance path;
- search/coordination hours and cycle time;
- acceptance/rejection outcome;
- whether a paid mandate or accepted economic settlement exists.

Later cases must then be compared with earlier cases. `COMPOUNDING` may advance only if accumulated templates/data/rules/trust measurably improve later execution economics or reliability.

## Kill conditions

Stop or demote the structure if:

- cases are materially bespoke one-off political/administrative projects;
- independent owners will not delegate or pay for the bounded process;
- rights/approval constraints dominate execution;
- counterparties are already efficiently connected without orchestration;
- each case requires unrelated founder-led discovery/design from zero;
- accumulated knowledge does not improve later execution.
