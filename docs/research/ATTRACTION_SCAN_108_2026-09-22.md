## Purpose

Scan 107 established a hard evidence invariant: a return model is invalid if it stitches one operator's control cost to another operator's revenue, labor or cost structure.

Scan 108 repeats that floor independently on fresh formations:

```text
SAME OPERATOR
+ SAME CONTROL POSITION
+ SAME TIME WINDOW
+ EXECUTED ACQUISITION / LICENSE / CONTRACT
+ CONTROL COST
+ RECEIVED EXTERNAL CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ DIRECT OPERATING COSTS
+ OWNER LABOR OR EXPLICIT REPLACEMENT COST
+ RIGHTS CLOSURE
+ REALIZED / BOUNDED EXIT VALUE
=> NORMALIZED NET CASHFLOW
```

The pass remains formation-diverse. It does not reuse Scan 107's convenience retail, small homestay, lottery station, self-service billiards or shared-power-bank signals.

## Result

| Formation | What is actually proven | Decisive failure |
|---|---|---|
| Hangzhou mall selfie booth | Current paid order volume, per-order price and participant-reported >RMB10k monthly net after rent/equipment share | Same operator's initial control cost, full cost perimeter, owner labor, mall/brand/IP rights and exit value are not closed |
| Jiangsu 3D-print microbusiness | Same operator's equipment cost, material cost, recurring sales, some after-cost profit and outsourcing spread | Decisive control is commodity equipment; recurring design/selling labor remains; no noncommodity transferable right or bounded exit |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — Mall selfie booth: real self-service cashflow still lacks a full control packet

A May 2026 field report describes an operator running a selfie booth in a Hangzhou Xihu-district mall. On ordinary weekend days the machine reportedly handles roughly 40-50 orders at RMB39.9 per order. The operator says that after rent and equipment revenue share, the booth produces more than RMB10,000 monthly net profit.

Source:
- https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-05-04/doc-inhwtvtz0378781.shtml

That is materially stronger than a franchise ROI calculator: a current operator is tied to current paid customer behavior and a participant-reported net figure.

The packet is still incomplete. The source does not publish this operator's exact initial acquisition/franchise/license cost, mall agreement term, brand/software/IP rights, maintenance burden, consumables, taxes, owner hours or replacement labor. It also does not publish a realized resale value or a transferable point right.

The same article separately gives an approximately RMB80,000 initial cost for a named brand and payback ranges for other franchisees. Those figures are **not** imported into this operator's return model.

The report also shows that location and IP remain decisive control surfaces: mall point quality can create four-to-five-fold revenue differences, while brand/IP collaborations materially pull demand.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Jiangsu 3D-print microbusiness: tighter economics, but commodity control plus recurring human work

An April 2026 China News interview gives unusually concrete small-operator data for a Jiangsu 3D-print entrepreneur.

Source:
- https://www.chinanews.com/cj/2026/04-26/10611077.shtml

The operator reports:
- two printers costing about RMB3,500 total;
- roughly 100 rolls of material consumed at around RMB40 each;
- electricity of about RMB2.2 per day with both printers running;
- recurring Friday-Sunday market selling;
- a Spring Festival day with family generating roughly RMB1,600 combined sales;
- after materials, stall fees and labor, profit ranging from hundreds of RMB on weaker days to about RMB1,000 on better days;
- occasional outsourcing of printing at roughly RMB0.15/gram and charging customers RMB0.25/gram.

This case binds more real economics to one operator than most public small-business stories.

It still fails the control thesis. The printers and material are commodity inputs. Market attendance, product choice, selling and creative/model work remain recurring human labor. Exact owner hours and replacement cost are not bound. No exclusive transferable customer contract, location right, design-right packet or realized exit value is published.

The same report explicitly notes that AI modeling lowers barriers and increases product homogeneity. That further weakens generic equipment/design scarcity.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Rail smart-locker concession: rights can close before economics do

A July 2026 public concession-result aggregation reports that Dongguan Rail Transit Line 1 smart-locker points were awarded for two years to Fengchao at RMB17,200 per month, above the RMB14,200 monthly floor.

Source:
- https://www.qianlima.com/gjxx/221722_5_4

Current Beijing municipal station guidance separately confirms that travelers actively pay for smart luggage lockers at station sites, including Fengchao and Xiaotie equipment.

Official current-use source:
- https://zdzqgw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk/zqyw/202607/t20260731_4802985.html

This is a useful opposite failure from F1. The point right and control cost are much clearer, but same-position economics are not. The Dongguan result does not disclose customer receipts, occupancy, hardware depreciation, network/payment costs, field maintenance, tax, labor or cash distribution for those awarded points.

The Beijing end-user prices are **not** imported as Dongguan revenue. They prove only that the category has current paid customer use.

The awarded operator is also an established national network operator, not the fresh individual/small-operator entry required by Scan 108. The two-year point right is term-bounded and no transferable residual/exit value is evidenced.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

Scan 108 confirms the Scan 107 boundary from a different set of formations:

```text
MACHINE-LIGHT
+ REAL CUSTOMER PAYMENT
+ REPORTED "NET PROFIT"
!= NORMALIZED CONTROL CASHFLOW

SMALL-OPERATOR ECONOMICS
+ COMMODITY EQUIPMENT
+ RECURRING OWNER SELLING
!= FOUNDER-LIGHT NONCOMMODITY CONTROL

EXECUTED POINT RIGHT
+ CATEGORY CUSTOMER PRICING ELSEWHERE
!= SAME-POSITION P&L
```

Two independent passes now show a retrieval problem more than an ontology problem: public sources often close either the **rights/control** side or the **small-operator cashflow** side, but not both under one identity and time window.

The next scan should therefore keep the commercial floor unchanged while changing evidence retrieval priority toward direct operator records or cross-corroborated identity packets that bind control cost, receipts, full costs, labor, rights and exit value. That is an evidence-method change, not a new vertical or product thesis.
