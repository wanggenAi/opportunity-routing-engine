## F1 — Mainland cloud migration and ICP access filing

A January 2026 Chinese participant described a project that moved an already-filed domain onto ByteDance/Volcengine infrastructure. The team initially treated the failure as nginx/port configuration, but Volcengine later displayed that the domain had not completed the required provider-side filing/access relationship.

Participant source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1189197

Current Volcengine documentation states that when a website/App already has an ICP filing through another provider but moves to a Volcengine mainland server, the site must complete access filing with Volcengine; otherwise it can be treated as unfiled on that platform and cannot conduct the hosted business there.

Objective sources:
- https://www.volcengine.com/docs/6428/68740?lang=zh
- https://www.volcengine.com/docs/7048/1518355?lang=zh
- https://help.aliyun.com/zh/icp-filing/basic-icp-service/support/access-for-the-record-the-faq

This is a real operator control surface because code changes cannot mint the provider/regulatory access state. It still fails white-space ownership: the actual access provider and regulatory process own the right, and Volcengine itself already sells filing-assistance services.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Apple Developer organization identity and distribution right

A June 2026 Chinese participant documented repeated organization-enrollment failures across account region, enrollment channel, payment, work-domain email and account blocking. After multiple support contacts and escalation, the participant completed the process through another Apple account by submitting the responsible person's ID, employment proof, business license and D-U-N-S information.

Participant source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1218149

Apple's current enrollment rules require organization legal-entity status, a D-U-N-S number, legal binding authority, a work email tied to the organization's domain and a functional public website. Current identity-verification rules also state that mainland-China Account Holders require identity verification and that organizations may be asked for additional/notarized business documents.

Objective sources:
- https://developer.apple.com/cn/help/account/membership/program-enrollment
- https://developer.apple.com/cn/help/account/membership/identity-verification

A preparation/status service could reduce mistakes, but the decisive control is Apple/D&B identity and membership authority. An independent operator cannot grant seller/distribution status.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Cross-carrier consumer parcel pickup data aggregation

A June 2026 Chinese participant reported that Taobao showed a parcel delivered into a Fengchao locker but did not expose the pickup code unless the user authorized Fengchao. The participant refused and recovered the code only after courier customer service resent it by SMS.

Participant source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1220356

A separate November 2025 Chinese developer attempted to reason toward a single app that could aggregate all parcels after phone-number authorization because YTO parcels had shifted from Cainiao to a Pinduoduo pickup-station ecosystem. The available API path did not provide that universal consumer graph.

Additional participant source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1170438

Kuaidi100's current documentation confirms that phone-number parcel lookup is not simply an open public lookup primitive. Its cooperation agreement describes logistics service providers supplying phone-query interfaces to Kuaidi100, and its carrier onboarding materials reference a signed phone-query data-use authorization.

Objective sources:
- https://api.kuaidi100.com/document/6050507ca62a19500e198796.html
- https://api.kuaidi100.com/document/search/index.html?wd=100
- https://www.kuaidi100.com/service/quickQueryIntroduction.shtml

This is the strongest Scan 100 data-control example because an authorized cross-carrier data graph can outperform a generic agent. It still fails retention today: a new small operator has no proven path to enough carrier/station data agreements, existing aggregators already hold partner relationships, and the participant evidence does not bind the convenience outcome to a recurring external payer.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Cross-pass learning

Scans 099 and 100 now provide two independent operator-control-advantage passes.

The repeated failure mode is:

```text
NON-GENERIC CONTROL EXISTS
≠
INDEPENDENT OPERATOR CAN ACQUIRE / OWN IT
```

The strongest control surfaces repeatedly cluster around regulated principals, native platforms, identity authorities, access providers or bilateral sensitive-data gatekeepers. That makes control existence useful for excluding generic-agent substitutes, but insufficient for finding white space.

The next evidence priority should therefore change without inventing a product category:

```text
OPERATOR-ACQUIRABLE CONTROL
+ EXTERNAL ECONOMIC MOTION
```

Scan 101 should only spend deep research on a control asset/right/relationship that an independent small operator can realistically obtain through standardized legal channels, while also binding the unresolved outcome to current external economic motion.
