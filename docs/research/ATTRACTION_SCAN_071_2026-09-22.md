## Boundary

Scan 071 deliberately breaks the provider-abstraction mechanism chain that accumulated across Scans 065–070.

The pass samples unrelated current actor/state transitions and requires downstream evidence that somebody is already allocating external budget to a bounded outcome. Job, contract and procurement pages are used only as **budget observations after the formation is framed**; they are not treated as the opportunity ontology.


## Result

Six formation-diverse current budget signals were examined. All six prove real external spending or long-running procurement intent. None survives the commercial hard floors.

The repeated pattern is not “buyers will not pay.” They do pay. The failure is that the repeatable execution layer is already productized, while the residual spend buys setup, exception ownership, local coordination, regulatory interpretation, accountability or adoption.

## Formations

### F1 — Industrial utility bill / energy-cost audit

A current buyer offers **£2,500 per completed client audit**, guarantees at least **£2,500 per month**, and expects multiple client audits. This is unusually strong direct budget evidence.

EnergyCAP already performs configurable bill audits automatically at import, flags cost/usage anomalies and errors, assigns review actions and tracks recovery. The remaining paid scope includes tariff structure, procurement opportunity, engineering interpretation and executive-grade recommendations.

**Closure:** real money, but the machine-repeatable detection layer is mature and the residual is recurring domain expertise.

### F2 — Commercial BESS permit expediting

A current BESS developer is hiring for **30+ hours/week, 6+ months, $15–$35/hour**, covering permit preparation/submission, municipal portals, AHJ/utility/fire-department follow-up, fee payment, code research and rejected comments.

PermitFlow now exposes AI agents across intake, requirements research, submission, coordination and issuance for thousands of AHJs.

**Closure:** the standard lifecycle is already an exact control surface; the remaining paid work is local exception and stakeholder coordination.

### F3 — Medical credentialing and payer enrollment

A current RCM buyer is adding another full-time ongoing credentialing specialist for CAQH, commercial/government payer enrollment, follow-up, deficiencies, rosters and EFT/ERA.

Medallion currently automates provider credentialing/enrollment operations, including payer follow-ups across portals, email, SMS, fax and phone with workflow visibility.

**Closure:** the replenishing provider-to-billing-ready state transition is real, but the repeatable control loop is mature and the residual is payer-specific exception/authorization work.

### F4 — Amazon EU product / EPR compliance operations

A current seller is hiring a long-term specialist at **$20–$50/hour for 6+ months** across EPR/PPWR, WEEE, batteries, GPSR, CE, REACH/RoHS, labeling and Amazon Account Health. The seller already pays a separate VAT provider.

Amazon itself now provides EPR policy rails, Pay-on-Behalf coverage in supported markets, GPSR guidance and compliance tooling, while the uncovered remainder requires product-specific regulatory interpretation and third-party coordination.

**Closure:** strong recurring spend, but the purchased outcome is accountable regulatory ownership rather than a clean founder-independent execution unit.

### F5 — Procurement function setup / vendor onboarding recovery

A nearly 3,000-unit multifamily operator offers **$4,500 for a three-month procurement setup** with potential full-time conversion. The buyer explicitly describes scattered vendor accounts, invoices and balances and wants systems, relationships and workflows rebuilt.

Zip and Coupa already supply structured procurement intake/orchestration and supplier onboarding. What remains in this engagement is historical cleanup, vendor calling, relationship formation, policy and change management.

**Closure:** current budget passes, but the residual is bespoke organizational recovery rather than a cross-customer execution asset.

### F6 — Shopify multi-state sales-tax filing

A current merchant already using Shopify Tax seeks **ongoing 6+ month filing** across eight U.S. states on a fixed price per return, with more states expected.

Shopify Tax now supports automated filing for all U.S. sales-tax states and publishes a per-return price: **$75** on Basic/Grow/Advanced and **$50** on Plus.

**Closure:** this is strong post-native external spend, but the exact standard filing unit is now sold natively. The remaining cases are eligibility, registration, unresolved errors, prior/amended returns or tax advice.

## Cross-case finding

```text
DIRECT CURRENT BUYER BUDGET
!=
UNOWNED FOUNDER-INDEPENDENT VALUE LAYER
```

Current buyers keep paying humans even after exact software appears because software does not eliminate accountability, rollout, exceptions, local authority coordination, regulated judgment or messy historical state.

That is useful evidence, but it should not be misread as software or orchestration white space.
