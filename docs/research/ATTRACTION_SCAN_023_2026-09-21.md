## 1. Robot event rental meta-router

### Attraction is real

Robot rental has strong current motion. Economic Observer reported:
- mainstream humanoid daily rental prices fell sharply over a year;
- more than 1,500 robot-rental companies were newly registered in 2025;
- Qingtianzu's 2026 Spring Festival orders exceeded 5,000;
- JD's robot-rental order volume rose sharply during the holiday.

Source:
- https://m.eeo.com.cn/2026/0313/810518.shtml

### Why the bridge is not ours

Qingtianzu already connects multiple robot brands/suppliers to customers and uses inventory, distance and
provider fulfillment signals in matching. JD and other rental channels also participate.

More importantly, the delivered product is often not simply "ship a robot":
transport, setup, debugging, engineer attendance and on-site interaction remain common parts of fulfillment.

So the attractive high-level shape already collapses into one of two bad positions:

```text
META ROUTER
→ DUPLICATES EXISTING RENTAL MARKETPLACE MATCHING

OWN THE ORDER
→ INHERITS LOCAL ENGINEER / TRANSPORT / OPERATION DELIVERY
```

Verdict: **DEMOTED_INCUMBENT_MATCHING_AND_HUMAN_DELIVERY**.

## 2. Inbound visitor local-life orchestrator

The actor pull is obvious: an overseas visitor landing in China has a short decision window and immediate
needs across payment, transportation, tickets, hotels, local commerce, tax refund and language.

But the broad integration opportunity is already being internalized.

Beijing's official `GO BEIJING` launched in April 2026 inside Alipay International with 39 services covering
food, lodging, transport, sightseeing, shopping, entertainment and public services. It includes ride-hailing,
ticket purchasing, hotel booking, departure tax refund and multilingual guidance.

Sources:
- https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/sy/jrts/202604/t20260429_4621482.html
- https://www.beijing.gov.cn/ywdt/gzdt/202604/t20260429_4621961.html

A third-party "all-in-one China travel orchestrator" would therefore sit above rails whose owners are already
aggregating the same context.

Verdict: **DEMOTED_CONTROL_SURFACE_INTERNALIZED**.

## 3. AI-glasses cross-brand try-before-buy network

The category has strong voluntary energy. The Ministry of Commerce reports strong smart-glasses sales growth,
and physical trial has unusually high value because comfort, display, optics, prescription compatibility and
interaction are difficult to infer from a product page.

But the trial layer is already becoming channel infrastructure.

- XREAL publishes a nationwide set of physical try-on locations:
  https://www.xreal.com/cn/stores
- Doctor Glasses opened a multi-brand smart-glasses concept store combining experience, purchase and optical
  fitting:
  https://www.doctorglasses.com.cn/sys-nd/191.html

This is not evidence that every city is perfectly served. It is evidence that the natural owners of the
customer relationship — brands and optical retailers — are actively internalizing the experience gap.

Verdict: **DEMOTED_VENDOR_AND_RETAIL_CHANNEL_INTERNALIZATION**.

## 4. Event-day microservice capacity router

Events create real temporary state changes.

Beijing reported 38 large Bird's Nest concerts in H1 2026 attracting nearly 1.9 million attendees, while
concert-driven visitors extended spending into restaurants, hotels, shopping, attractions and local travel.

Source:
- https://www.beijing.gov.cn/gongkai/shuju/sjjd/202607/t20260725_4786640.html

A possible formation is to route short-lived demand for makeup, styling, props, luggage storage, photography,
transport and other event-day services into spare local capacity.

The problem is not lack of demand. It is control and delivery.

Ticket-root programs already turn the event identity into merchant discovery and benefits. Jing'an GO, for
example, allows recent ticket holders to unlock offers from more than 100 merchants and venues.

Source:
- https://www.jingan.gov.cn/rmtzx/003001/20260313/27247551-664b-4f66-ad06-947fb60d9c89.html

The remaining microservices are highly local and mostly human-delivered. Owning the route would require
continuous merchant acquisition, service vetting, availability synchronization and quality operations.

Verdict: **DEMOTED_DISCOVERY_RAIL_CROWDED_AND_HUMAN_DELIVERY**.

## 5. Home-care trust and capacity router

The demand is direct and urgent. Beijing's September 2026 home-care/domestic-service plan explicitly names
elder care and maternal care as pressing demand and seeks better supply-demand matching.

It also shows why a generic trust/matching entrant is weak:
- public/community digital channels are being used for matching;
- the national domestic-service credit platform is being promoted for unified verification;
- enterprises are expected to perform ongoing worker and service management;
- the delivered value is still recurring human care.

Source:
- https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/202609/t20260905_4851778.html

Verdict: **DEMOTED_RECURRING_HUMAN_DELIVERY_AND_PUBLIC_TRUST_RAIL**.

## The important Scan 023 lesson

This scan separates two questions that were previously too easy to blend:

```text
IS THE FLOW HOT?
!=
IS THERE AN UNOWNED CONTROL EDGE?
```

Robot rental, inbound visitor services, event commerce, smart glasses and home care all contain real
participant pull. That is better raw material than a merely elegant policy workflow.

But high energy often attracts platform owners quickly. The obvious "aggregate the whole market" layer is
therefore frequently the **first** layer to disappear.

The next search should become narrower, not colder:

```text
HIGH-ENERGY EXISTING FLOW
→ OBSERVE WHERE ACTORS STILL LEAK TIME / MONEY / CERTAINTY
→ FIND ONE NARROW MISSING EDGE
→ REQUIRE MACHINE-RESOLVABLE STATE
→ CALL EXISTING EXECUTION RAILS
→ DO NOT REBUILD THE WHOLE MARKETPLACE
```
