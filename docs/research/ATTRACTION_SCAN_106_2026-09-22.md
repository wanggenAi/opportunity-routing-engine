## F1 — Sodium-ion storage technology licensed to Hunan Qibiao New Energy

Hunan provincial government and science-and-technology reporting document that Central South University professor Guo Xueyi's team licensed sodium-ion battery and storage-system technology to Hunan Qibiao New Energy under a “use first, pay later” arrangement.

Official sources:
- https://www.hunan.gov.cn/topic/qdgj/kcfn/gzjxs/szx/202507/t20250730_33753163.html
- https://kjt.hunan.gov.cn/kjt/xxgk/gzdt/szdt/xt/202508/t20250821_33779417.html

The company began production in April 2025 and reported more than RMB100 million of orders within roughly four months. That is real external market motion after an executed license.

It still fails closed. The evidence reports orders rather than received customer cash or audited revenue. The commercial value depends on battery manufacturing, quality, supply chain, application engineering and customer delivery. The parties also planned a joint sodium-ion laboratory, so continuing technical collaboration remains part of the operating system. License economics, unit margin, working capital, warranty burden and owner labor are not public.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Qianshu Dragon Kiln exclusive IP authorization

Yixing government reporting states that Dingshu town government authorized Jiangsu Zhuoyi Culture in 2023 as the exclusive IP operator for Qianshu Dragon Kiln. The same official report states that the operator used its e-commerce platform to sell kiln products and achieved annual sales above RMB50 million in 2024.

Official sources:
- https://www.yixing.gov.cn/doc/2025/03/03/1300804.shtml
- https://www.yixing.gov.cn/doc/2025/10/31/1362942.shtml

This is a strong rights-to-revenue chain: exclusive authorization is explicit, product identity is protected with official certificates and unique digital codes, and post-authorization sales are material.

It is not a clean fresh-small-operator formation. The licensee already operated a substantial digital/supply ecosystem around creators, brands and merchants. Value delivery still requires physical ceramic production, creator/supplier coordination, merchandising, fulfillment, e-commerce/live-commerce operations and brand enforcement. Gross sales do not reveal license cost or normalized operator profit.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Distributed-PV project-company control transfer

Jinko Power's audited 2025 annual report documents a completed transaction transferring 100% of the holding company for four industrial/commercial distributed-PV project companies together with station equipment, at a total price of about RMB286.5 million. By year-end, the equity transfer had completed registration changes and substantially all transaction consideration had been received.

Current operating-rule corroboration:
- https://www.nea.gov.cn/20250730/30f3a8b4af5f4e9d98bb8754ededc701/c.html

The National Energy Administration confirms that a distributed-PV investor may appoint a professional third party as the O&M responsible entity after commissioning. This proves that ownership and recurring maintenance execution can be separated.

The formation still fails the project boundary. The transaction size is far outside a fresh small operator. Grid connection, settlement, financing, safety, curtailment and regulation remain decisive. The reviewed report does not isolate actual post-closing electricity receipts or normalized net return for these exact four transferred projects, so buyer-side continuity must remain unknown-to-partial rather than inferred.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

Across Scans 105–106:

```text
TRANSFERABLE + POST-TRANSFER ECONOMIC MOTION
!= SMALL-OPERATOR EDGE

EXCLUSIVE RIGHT + LARGE SALES
!= NORMALIZED FOUNDER-LIGHT CASHFLOW

DELEGABLE OPERATIONS
!= LOW-CAPITAL CONTROL POSITION
```

Two independent executed-transfer passes are now enough to stop testing transferability as the main unknown. The next evidence priority should be narrower and harder:

```text
ACTUAL SMALL-OPERATOR-SIZED CONTROL COST
+ RECEIVED POST-TRANSFER CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ DIRECT COSTS
+ OWNER LABOR
+ RIGHTS CLOSURE
=> NORMALIZED NET CASHFLOW ESTIMATE
```

That is an evidence floor, not a new product ontology.
