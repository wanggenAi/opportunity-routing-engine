## Admission rule

Scan 135 rejected inseparable bundles, pooled rents and attachment-only economic tuples before deep diligence. A formation had to show, on a current China asset:

- one asset or an economically separable entry price;
- exact asset-attributable rent/receipts;
- future post-transfer buyer receipts;
- automatic lease/contract continuity;
- at least 24 months of future contracted coverage;
- gross receipt-to-entry economics not obviously disqualifying;
- low recurring human delivery.

A research retention is **not** a commercial promotion and grants no authority to bid, pay a deposit or buy an asset.
