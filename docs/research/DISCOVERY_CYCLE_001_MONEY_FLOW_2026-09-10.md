# Discovery Cycle 001 — Money Flow Snapshot

Date: 2026-09-10
Geography: China → Jiangsu → Xuzhou
Status: `EVIDENCE SNAPSHOT / NOT BUSINESS PROOF`

This is the first clean-slate money-flow snapshot after the discovery-stack rebuild. It deliberately separates macro direction from exact commercial capture.

## 1. National regime

### 1.1 Households are not simply `stopping consumption`

2026 Jan-Jul data show a structural split:
- total retail sales of consumer goods grew only about 1.2%;
- goods retail grew about 1.1%;
- service retail grew about 5.0%;
- online retail of goods + services grew about 4.8%;
- tourism consulting/rental and culture/sports/leisure services grew at double-digit rates in H1 data;
- rural retail growth exceeded urban growth.

Interpretation:

> discretionary money is selective and price-sensitive, but service/experience/convenience demand is materially stronger than broad goods demand.

Sources:
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260817_1965056.html
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260817_1965052.html

### 1.2 Durable / asset-heavy consumption is weaker than selected services

Jan-Jul retail structure includes:
- convenience stores and supermarkets positive;
- department stores / specialty formats weaker;
- communications equipment strong;
- appliances, furniture, automobiles and building materials weak in broad reported growth.

This is consistent with `value-for-money + selective upgrading`, not indiscriminate upgrading.

Source:
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260817_1965052.html

### 1.3 Household income is still growing, but spending growth is slower

2026 H1:
- per-capita disposable income: RMB 22,981, +5.2% nominal / +4.2% real;
- median disposable income: RMB 19,036, +4.7%;
- per-capita consumption expenditure: RMB 14,836, +3.7% nominal / +2.7% real;
- rural consumption expenditure grew faster than urban;
- education/culture/entertainment, household goods/services and other goods/services grew faster than housing/healthcare categories.

Interpretation:

> households still have incremental income, but marginal spending is being allocated selectively rather than broadly.

Source:
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202607/t20260717_1964185.html

### 1.4 Enterprises show a strong `equipment / information` divergence

2026 Jan-Jul fixed-asset investment is weak overall, while selected categories remain strong:
- total fixed-asset investment down materially;
- real-estate development investment down sharply;
- equipment/tool purchases positive;
- information transmission / information-service investment strongly positive.

Interpretation:

> enterprise money is not broadly expanding; it is selectively moving toward equipment renewal, digital/information capacity and productivity infrastructure.

Sources:
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260817_1965056.html
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260817_1965053.html

### 1.5 Small manufacturers remain under pressure

August manufacturing PMI:
- overall: 49.8;
- large firms: 50.6;
- medium: 49.4;
- small: 47.9;
- new orders: 50.6.

Interpretation:

> improving order signals coexist with weak small-firm operating conditions. Opportunities that reduce fixed cost, downtime, staffing burden or transaction friction deserve attention.

Source:
- https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202608/t20260831_1965287.html

### 1.6 Cross-border money is expanding

2026 Jan-Jul goods trade was reported at about RMB 30.13 trillion, +17.3%; ASEAN trade also grew strongly; machinery/electronics exports substantially outgrew labor-intensive exports.

Interpretation:

> recurring post-sale, documentation, logistics, spare-parts, local support and cross-border operating tasks should be watched, especially around manufacturing clusters.

Sources:
- https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202608/content_7044926.htm
- http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0811/c1004-40540155.html

## 2. Jiangsu divergence

### 2.1 Production services outperform broad retail

2026 H1 Jiangsu:
- GDP +5.2%;
- leasing/business services roughly +14%;
- information/software services roughly +10.9%;
- production-service revenue +8.9%;
- broad retail only around +0.9%;
- rural retail materially faster than urban.

Interpretation:

> Jiangsu's stronger flows are not broad consumer goods. Production services, information/software, business services and selected rural consumption are stronger channels.

Sources:
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/22/art_34151_11806875.html
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/21/art_34151_11825271.html

### 2.2 Equipment renewal is a strong relative flow

Jiangsu fixed investment is weak overall, while equipment/tool purchases and manufacturing equipment purchases remain double-digit positive.

Interpretation:

> installed equipment creates a downstream service surface: installation, maintenance, inspection, uptime, repair, spare parts, resale/remanufacturing and operator support.

Source:
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/21/art_34151_11825271.html

### 2.3 Policy support is contaminating some consumer growth

Telecom/smart-device categories show extreme growth around subsidy programs. Treat these as `POLICY_EFFECT_PRESENT`; do not infer durable private demand from nominal category growth alone.

Source:
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/21/art_34151_11825271.html

## 3. Xuzhou — local money-flow map

### 3.1 Xuzhou consumer spending is stronger than the provincial average

2026 H1 Xuzhou:
- GDP +5.2%;
- tertiary industry +6.3%, faster than secondary industry;
- disposable income +5.4%;
- retail +4.4%;
- online retail of unit goods +13%;
- information/software/IT services +15.2%;
- leasing/business services +15.1%.

Interpretation:

> Xuzhou is not simply a low-demand city. Local services, online consumption and selected modern services are expanding materially faster than broad Jiangsu retail.

Source:
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/31/art_33718_11812411.html

### 3.2 Tourism is a major external-money inflow

2026 H1 monitored Xuzhou tourism-related venues:
- 36.2981 million visits, +12.59%;
- tourism consumption RMB 34.331 billion, +12.12%;
- both growth rates were reported as #1 in Jiangsu.

During the 2026 Dragon Boat period, outside-city visitors accounted for roughly 71% of visitors in telecom monitoring.

Interpretation:

> Xuzhou receives substantial money from actors who do not live in the city. The relevant opportunities are not only attractions; they include converting transient traffic into local consumption, time-saving, logistics, experience and merchant revenue.

Sources:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202608/t20260803_8586728.shtml
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202606/t20260622_8579293.shtml

### 3.3 Existing events can move merchant revenue — but intervention is material

Xuzhou `ticket-stub economy`, sports events and city promotion show that externally aggregated traffic can be converted into local merchant spend. These are useful mechanism cases, but public promotion, coupons and major events contaminate independent-payer inference.

Source:
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202603/30/content_47867.html

### 3.4 Housing has moved from pure new-build growth toward stock-market operation

2026 H1 Xuzhou urban new + second-hand home transactions totalled 23,679 units, about 22% higher than 2025 H2. The city also published its first main-urban rental reference based on actual registered transactions.

Interpretation:

> stock housing creates recurring downstream flows around repair, cleaning, renovation, rental turnover, asset management and move-in/move-out service — even when new-construction economics are weak.

Sources:
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pc/con/202607/22/content_53101.html
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202607/30/content_53509.html

### 3.5 Property companies are actively seeking non-property-fee revenue and service value

Local reporting in August 2026 shows Xuzhou property firms extending into community convenience, idle-space activation and resident services. Xuzhou has:
- 2,237 residential communities;
- 690+ property-service enterprises;
- roughly 73,000 property-sector workers.

Some local operators explicitly discuss rental management, home services and other value-added services as ways to improve economics and resident satisfaction.

Interpretation:

> property can function as a dense trusted demand-entry layer, not merely as a service provider.

Sources:
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202608/10/content_53962.html
- https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_34024461
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/dscb/pad/con/202607/16/content_52808.html

### 3.6 Consumer trade-in is a large flow, but policy dependent

By 2026-05-25, Xuzhou reported RMB 460 million in trade-in subsidies driving about RMB 4.8 billion of market consumption.

Interpretation:

> recycling, old-product removal, installation, delivery and after-sales work are real transaction surfaces, but the incremental demand is heavily policy-supported. Do not use it as the sole regenerative-demand thesis.

Source:
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202606/t20260602_8576126.shtml

### 3.7 Engineering machinery is shifting from product to lifecycle service

Xuzhou explicitly treats engineering-machinery aftermarket, remanufacturing, second-hand equipment and O&M as a second growth curve. The modern-service industrial park is planned around:
- global spare-parts dispatch;
- remanufacturing;
- second-hand machinery trading;
- digital O&M dispatch;
- bonded repair and related services.

2025 engineering-machinery cluster output reportedly grew 19%; more than 100 enterprises exceed RMB 100 million revenue.

Interpretation:

> installed equipment and global customers create potentially recurring high-value service demand, but professional trust, liability and industry access are higher barriers than community services.

Sources:
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/21/art_33718_11808010.html
- https://jsnews.jschina.com.cn/xz/a/202604/t20260421_s69e717c2e4b09a6c3e098353.shtml
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202602/26/content_46394.html

### 3.8 County/rural logistics infrastructure is becoming denser

Xuzhou has built county/town/village shared distribution infrastructure and is investing further in cold-chain and agricultural logistics. This creates capacity-routing and utilization questions, but logistics margin is likely thin and geography raises coordination cost.

Current project evidence includes Xuzhou/Yunlong cold-chain infrastructure and county fruit cold-storage investment.

Sources:
- https://ggzy.zwb.xz.gov.cn/jyxx/003001/003001001/20260616/fb734474-a2bb-4b60-8d28-c0dee55dc56d.html
- https://ggzy.zwb.xz.gov.cn/jyxx/003009/003009001/20260401/b387285a-420e-499f-9feb-82312030d9ba.html

### 3.9 Public procurement is a daily observable institutional money stream

Xuzhou public-resource procurement publishes a high-volume continuous feed across operations, maintenance, data collection, events, housing, inspection, public services and facilities.

Interpretation:

> the feed is valuable as a demand sensor. Directly becoming a government contractor is not necessarily an early opportunity because credentials, procurement procedure, payment cycles and subcontracting limits apply.

Source:
- https://ggzy.zwb.xz.gov.cn/

## 5. What this snapshot does NOT prove

It does not prove:
- that any exact payer will buy from this engine;
- that a growing category has unresolved friction;
- that policy-driven growth persists without subsidy;
- that high market size implies easy acquisition;
- that Xuzhou residents match national social-media samples;
- that a high-value industrial service is accessible to a new orchestrator.

These claims must be resolved by psychology/behavior corroboration, case mining, opportunity scoring and exact transaction tests.
