# Xuzhou Actor-First Opportunity Scan V1 — 2026-09-08

Status: `EVIDENCE-BACKED / PRELIMINARY / NOT PROOF`

This scan deliberately starts from **people and groups**, not companies. It compares individual, household, group, merchant, enterprise and institution demand under the same evidence discipline.

## 1. Structural baseline

### Youth / student density
- Xuzhou had 12 ordinary higher-education institutions in 2025, with about 256,500 students and 83,400 graduates.
- Xuzhou is explicitly building a youth-development city and has launched the 2026 Xuzhou Youth Card covering job search, housing, youth night school, marriage/social events, public childcare and other youth services.

### Youth capability / skill-upgrading signal
- Xuzhou's 2026 Skills Night School launched courses including AI, internet marketing, electrical work, elder care, maternal/infant care, auto repair and UAV-related skills.
- The first cohort drew 170+ learners and subsequent course supply expanded substantially.

### Consumption / youth-experience signal
- Xuzhou is expanding youth economy, night economy, pet economy, emotional-value consumption, sports-event consumption and experiential retail.
- One Xuzhou youth-night-school + small-store operator publicly reported roughly 30% revenue growth in two months; 12 youth-night-school partner locations had covered about 600 participant visits.

### Pet signal
- In Quanshan District, registered dog/cat count exceeded 180,000 and pet diagnosis institutions grew from 11 in 2020 to 67 in 2025.
- Jiangsu's 2026 resident consumption survey reports pet spending is concentrated among younger/middle-aged consumers and 30.8% of pet owners planned to increase spending.
- Paid pet-sitting / home-feeding behavior is observable; public market examples show per-visit pricing and national holiday demand spikes.

### Family / child-care signal
- Xuzhou repeatedly describes holiday child supervision as a real problem for dual-income and new-employment households.
- Public-interest holiday care has expanded, which proves the pain but also creates a strong free substitute.

### Household-service signal
- Jiangsu 2026 resident survey: 22.7% had used household services; service quality and price were the top concerns. Daily cleaning and deep cleaning were the most common use cases, and nearly 90% of preferred hourly price ranges were below RMB 80/hour.

### Youth social / relationship signal
- Xuzhou has repeatedly run youth friendship / marriage / interest-based social events in 2026.
- A 2026-08 Xuzhou event gathered 160 single young professionals; other 2026 events used scripts, music, art or camping as low-pressure social mechanisms.
- The Xuzhou Youth Card now explicitly includes marriage/social services, indicating the city recognizes the need as persistent.

### Tourism / personal experience signal
- In 2026 H1, monitored Xuzhou tourism destinations received 36.2981 million visits, +12.59% YoY, and tourism consumption reached RMB 34.331 billion, +12.12%.
- Young visitors are a material growth segment; Hanfu / photography / immersive experiences have explicit paid offerings. Xuzhou historic areas report Hanfu + styling packages commonly around RMB 60–150, and travel platforms list Xuzhou paid travel-photography products.

## 2. Actor-first candidate hypotheses

### XZA-001 — Student / young capability → bounded paid projects
**Need actor / beneficiary:** students, graduates, young workers who have skill/time but weak access to real paid projects.

**Payer:** varies by transaction: individual, merchant, SME or institution.

**Capability provider:** student / young worker / operator / AI composite.

**Observed friction:** training and skill-building are active, but training is not the same as converting capability into paid work and proof-of-delivery.

**Transaction hypothesis:** convert a capability into a bounded deliverable with requirement definition, QA and acceptance; sell the result rather than employment placement.

**Key risk:** payer is too heterogeneous; if it becomes employment placement or labor dispatch, compliance changes.

**Status:** `HYPOTHESIS / EVIDENCED SUPPLY / PAYER VARIES`.

---

### XZA-002 — Trusted neighborhood pet-care micro-network
**Need actor / beneficiary / payer:** pet owner.

**Capability provider:** vetted nearby pet sitter / student / flexible worker / pet-service operator.

**Observed friction:** owners already pay for boarding and home feeding. Core unresolved issues are trust, stranger access to the home, reliability, evidence of visit, pet safety and liability.

**Transaction hypothesis:** standardized home-feeding / dog-walking package with identity verification, service checklist, timestamped evidence, pet profile, emergency rules and transaction protection.

**Why this is interesting:** individual payer is clear, paid behavior exists, service is local, supply can be flexible, and the first transaction can be tested rapidly.

**Key risk:** home access and animal-safety responsibility.

**Status:** `B: INVESTIGATE / HIGH PRIORITY PERSONAL-PAYER CANDIDATE`.

---

### XZA-003 — Youth interest-based micro-social experiences
**Need actor / beneficiary:** single / socially narrow / newly arrived young adults, not necessarily people seeking formal matchmaking.

**Payer:** participant, venue, sponsor, or hybrid.

**Capability provider / resource owner:** organizer + shop / cafe / studio / sports venue / instructor.

**Observed friction:** repeated Xuzhou public events explicitly target narrow social circles and relationship/socialization difficulty. Interest, music, scripts, camping and art are repeatedly used to reduce awkwardness.

**Transaction hypothesis:** small recurring interest-based groups with a host, defined participant profile, venue and activity — social connection is an outcome of doing something together, not a traditional matchmaking pitch.

**Key unknown:** Xuzhou self-pay willingness because many current events are publicly subsidized or free.

**Status:** `B: INVESTIGATE`.

---

### XZA-004 — Trusted home micro-services / household task routing
**Need actor / payer:** household / individual consumer.

**Capability provider:** cleaner / organizer / local service worker / vetted flexible worker.

**Observed friction:** people already buy household services, but price and quality are major concerns.

**Transaction hypothesis:** narrower, highly standardized small household outputs rather than a generic home-services marketplace.

**Key risk:** mature incumbents, trust, quality variance and low ticket acquisition economics.

**Status:** `B/C BORDER`.

---

### XZA-005 — Second-hand digital-device inspection / transaction assurance
**Need actor / payer:** value-conscious second-hand buyer.

**Capability provider:** technician / standardized inspection workflow / existing authorized inspection services.

**Observed friction:** second-hand devices offer large savings but buyers fear repaired, modified, locked or misrepresented devices. Consumers already pay for third-party inspection products on large platforms.

**Transaction hypothesis:** local pre-purchase / in-person inspection or buyer-side transaction assurance.

**Key risk:** strong platform incumbents (`验货宝`, professional resale platforms), technical liability and narrow margins.

**Status:** `C: WATCH`.

---

### XZA-006 — Graduation / move-out liquidation and reuse
**Need actor:** graduating students / movers.

**Beneficiary / payer:** seller may accept a fee or reduced recovery price; buyer pays for goods.

**Capability provider:** campus operators / storage / resale / logistics capability.

**Observed friction:** Xuzhou campuses hold graduation flea markets and reuse events; graduates need to dispose of dorm goods quickly.

**Transaction hypothesis:** pickup + classify + bundle + resale / donation + settlement.

**Key risk:** seasonal, low-margin, strong C2C substitutes and logistics intensity.

**Status:** `C: WATCH / SEASONAL`.

---

### XZA-007 — Value-for-money local experience router
**Need actor / payer:** conservative/value-conscious consumers who still want leisure and emotional reward.

**Capability provider / resource owner:** restaurants, cafes, classes, studios, attractions, idle-time merchants.

**Observed friction:** consumers want high perceived value and low commitment; Xuzhou is actively expanding night economy, trial/experience formats and youth consumption.

**Transaction hypothesis:** verified low-cost local experiences, ideally using off-peak merchant capacity rather than another coupon directory.

**Key risk:** Dianping/Meituan/Xiaohongshu/coupon channels are strong incumbents; simple aggregation is not enough.

**Status:** `C: WATCH`.

---

### XZA-008 — Adult-child sponsored elder digital / life assistance
**Need actor / beneficiary:** elderly person.

**Payer:** adult children, institution or government depending on service.

**Capability provider:** trained helper / existing elder-service institution / qualified specialist where needed.

**Observed friction:** Xuzhou public procurement already pays for in-home elder services and the city is expanding 'home-nearby' elder care.

**Transaction hypothesis:** non-medical digital/life coordination only, with family visibility and strict boundaries.

**Key risk:** trust, vulnerable-person safety, overlap with regulated or professional care, and strong public-service coverage.

**Status:** `C: WATCH / G3 CONDITIONAL`.

---

### XZA-009 — Holiday child-care / activity coordination
**Need actor / beneficiary:** child and dual-income / new-employment household.

**Payer:** parent, institution, sponsor.

**Observed friction:** Xuzhou repeatedly identifies holiday supervision as an urgent family pain.

**Transaction hypothesis:** structured activity / supervision coordination.

**Key risk:** child safety, licensing/qualification, high liability, and extensive free public childcare substitutes.

**Status:** `D: DO NOT ENTER DIRECTLY NOW`.

---

### XZA-010 — Single / renting young-adult local micro-help
**Need actor / payer:** young renter / single resident.

**Capability provider:** nearby worker / student / service provider.

**Possible tasks:** pickup, simple assembly, item handoff, queueing, photo verification, non-regulated errands.

**Observed friction:** plausible but direct Xuzhou payment evidence remains weak in this scan.

**Key risk:** Meituan/errand platforms, household-service platforms and C2C channels already solve many variants.

**Status:** `SIGNAL / LOW CONFIDENCE`.

---

### XZA-011 — Youth interest-skill micro-classes / experience exchange
**Need actor / payer:** young person seeking low-cost novelty, skill and social connection.

**Capability provider / resource owner:** instructor, student, artisan, small shop, cafe, studio.

**Observed friction:** Xuzhou's night-school model has very strong participation and small-store traffic effects.

**Transaction hypothesis:** paid small-group interest experiences that combine learning + social + venue consumption.

**Key risk:** public/free night school is a substitute; must prove willingness to pay for better timing, quality, continuity or niche topics.

**Status:** `B/C BORDER`.

---

### XZA-012 — Visitor micro-experience / photography capability routing
**Need actor / payer:** incoming visitor, couple, family, young tourist.

**Capability provider:** local photographer / Hanfu shop / makeup / local experience provider.

**Observed friction:** Xuzhou tourism is growing rapidly and paid Hanfu, styling, photography and deep-experience products are already sold.

**Transaction hypothesis:** route tourists to verified small experience packages; potentially combine photography + outfit + route + local story.

**Key risk:** mature OTA / local-store supply, acquisition cost, seasonality and limited orchestration margin.

**Status:** `C: WATCH`.

## 3. Important negative findings

1. **Pain is not enough.** Childcare and elder care are real needs, but direct entry carries trust/safety/regulatory burdens.
2. **Existing payment is not enough.** Home services, second-hand and local-deal discovery have strong payment behavior but are already heavily platformized.
3. **Public programs are both evidence and competition.** Youth social, night school and childcare programs prove demand, but free/subsidized supply can reduce direct consumer willingness to pay.
4. **The most interesting personal markets are trust markets.** Pet care, household help, second-hand inspection and elder assistance all show that the transaction friction is often not discovery but trust/verification/accountability.
5. **The orchestrator needs to create more than contact.** Identity checks, task definition, checklists, evidence, QA, dispute rules, matching and outcome history are potential value layers.
