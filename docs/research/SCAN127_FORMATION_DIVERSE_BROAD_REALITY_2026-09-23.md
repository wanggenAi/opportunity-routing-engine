## Fresh evidence field

### F1 — National registered data property -> multi-bank financing

On 2026-09-11 the National Data Property Registration Service System entered trial operation. It creates a new nationally interoperable registration/result-query layer. Shanghai reported 15 of the first 56 national certificates, and separate Shanghai data-product-IP practice reported 1,592 certificates by end-July plus 24 enterprises obtaining RMB547m in pledge financing.

This is a real and unusually fresh state change. It initially looks like a strong routing surface:

```text
PUBLIC REGISTERED DATA RIGHT
-> FINANCING NEED
-> BANK PRODUCT / CREDIT
```

But the action/control layer fails. Shanghai's official financing workflow still involves one-to-one enterprise-bank discussion, bank review of operating condition, data-IP compliance and market value, comprehensive credit approval, then bilateral contracting. SPDB and the data exchanges already package registration, valuation/ecosystem and customized financing services. No standardized multi-bank machine-callable underwriting/offer rail or external operator-owned realized financing outcome graph is evidenced.

**Verdict:** demote.

### F2 — Electronic transferable document -> collateral finance

The electronic-document regulation became effective on 2026-09-01 and expressly recognizes systems offering issuance, storage, amendment, conversion, transfer, pledge and circulation functions.

The value jump is real: Chongqing's digital NCD mechanism had released more than RMB22m of financing since May, and the August direct connection to the unified movable-property financing registry lets financial institutions complete NCD pledge registration/cancellation/verification inside the same infrastructure. Bank of China completed a live eNCD pledge-finance transaction.

The problem is the opposite of missing infrastructure: the incumbent public system already closes the loop.

```text
DOCUMENT CONTROL
-> PLEDGE REGISTRATION
-> BANK VERIFICATION
-> FINANCING
= ALREADY IN ONE CONTROL SURFACE
```

**Verdict:** demote.

### F3 — Green-certificate buyer/supply routing

This field has strong voluntary motion and native settlement. The national system reports 1.970bn certificates issued and 481m traded in 2026 through July; July alone had 54.68m trades. Official rules already support recognized nationwide transaction platforms, listing/bilateral/auction mechanisms, synchronized transaction records and public price statistics/indexing.

That is strong market evidence but weak new-operator evidence. The official system/exchanges own certificate identity, price discovery, transaction and settlement. A general Agent can already use public price/year/source information without needing a distinct external router.

**Verdict:** demote.

### F4 — Product carbon-footprint certification routing

The pilot is becoming standardized: a general implementation rule, 17 product-specific rules and an authorized certification-institution set now exist.

But the non-generic value is precisely regulated professional judgment and certification trust. Current supervision removed pilot qualification from institutions that failed capability review. An external operator that does not control certification authority collapses to lead generation, document preparation or consulting; the difficult part remains recurring expert work.

**Verdict:** demote.
