## 2. Macro layer — China / Jiangsu / Xuzhou

### China

Current national signals are mixed rather than uniformly strong:

- 2026 Jan-Jul total retail sales of consumer goods grew only 1.2% YoY;
- service retail grew 5.0%, clearly faster than goods retail;
- online goods + service retail grew 4.8%;
- fixed-asset investment fell 6.7%, manufacturing investment fell 1.7%, while high-tech investment still grew;
- August CPI rose 0.8% YoY and core CPI 1.0%.

Interpretation: broad discretionary goods demand is not a safe default growth thesis. Service, digital, information and high-value problem-solving activity are relatively stronger directions.

### Jiangsu

The province shows a similar split:

- Jan-Jul retail sales grew 1.1%;
- equipment-purchase investment grew 15.8%;
- industrial production remained relatively strong, with high-tech and digital-product manufacturing growing faster;
- production services and business services grew faster than broad retail;
- household income continued to grow, while property investment remained weak.

Interpretation: capital is still moving into equipment, digital production and production services even while broad consumption and property are much softer.

### Xuzhou

Xuzhou is stronger than the national/provincial retail averages and is structurally mixed:

- H1 GDP: RMB 4846.9bn, +5.2%;
- services: +6.3%, faster than secondary industry (+3.9%);
- industrial value added: +6.1%;
- retail sales: +4.4%, among the stronger Jiangsu readings;
- information/software/IT services value added: +15.2%;
- leasing/business services: +15.1%;
- online retail by monitored units: +13.0%;
- fixed investment remained weak overall;
- engineering machinery remains the city's No.1 strategic industry and is moving toward intelligent, service-oriented and international lifecycle models.

This suggests that Xuzhou's strongest current value fields are not simply `sell more products`. They cluster around **production services, digital capability, lifecycle services, service consumption and the conversion of existing assets/capabilities into higher-utilization states**.

## 3. Psychology layer

National 2026 survey/price evidence points to a cautious but not inactive consumer:

- savings preference remains high;
- consumers increasingly optimize for actual effect, quality/price ratio and safety;
- weakly necessary, status-heavy or premium-for-premium's-sake consumption is under pressure;
- consumers still pay for experiences, health, convenience, learning and services when value is concrete;
- AI is increasingly influencing search, comparison, choice and post-purchase behavior.

The practical psychology is better summarized as:

```text
NOT: do not spend
BUT: prove value before I spend
```

For firms, an analogous decision logic is likely relevant:

```text
NOT: digitalize because technology is fashionable
BUT: show measurable output, subsidy/acceptance fit, reduced failure cost, faster delivery or reusable capability
```

This is a hypothesis until directly observed in Xuzhou firms/service providers.

## 4. Behavior layer

Observed local behavior changes now include:

1. **manufacturing digitalization moving from policy to implementation/acceptance**;
2. **engineering machinery shifting toward full-lifecycle services, digital O&M, remanufacturing and international service networks**;
3. **service and experience consumption rising faster than goods in important local scenes**;
4. **tourism and event-driven consumption pulling outside-city demand into Xuzhou**;
5. **online retail and digital decision journeys continuing to grow**;
6. **aging creating persistent local service demand, but public/community supply remains central and payer structure must be handled carefully**.

## 5. Industry / value-field scan

### Field A — Manufacturing digital continuity and exception routing

Observed change:
Xuzhou's SME digital-transformation pilot targets 650 firms by 2027, with 20+ service providers, 120+ small/fast/light/precise solutions, AI deployment, assessment, audit, acceptance and funding workflows.

Latent-value hypothesis:

```text
fragmented SME systems/data/processes
+ service-provider bursty implementation/acceptance workload
+ underused local/remote technical capability
→ bounded technical CapabilityUnits
→ accepted fixes / integration / operation / evidence outputs
```

Potential units:
- ERP/MES/WMS/API integration repair;
- database cleanup/migration/reconciliation;
- deployment/monitoring/backup/recovery;
- data extraction and acceptance-evidence automation;
- post-go-live exception triage;
- small AI/data preparation tasks with measurable acceptance.

Critical unknowns:
- who pays;
- whether service providers have real overflow they can route;
- whether enterprise contracts forbid external execution;
- whether underused technical capacity can be optioned;
- whether task frequency is enough to form a queue.

Preliminary attractiveness: **HIGH / validate first**.

### Field B — Engineering-machinery lifecycle digital service orchestration

Observed change:
Xuzhou is explicitly moving the engineering-machinery cluster from equipment production toward full-lifecycle services including global spare-parts dispatch, remanufacturing, used-equipment circulation and digital O&M.

Latent-value hypothesis:

```text
large installed equipment base
+ fragmented service/data/parts/knowledge workflows
+ local engineering + software + language + operations capability
→ routable lifecycle micro-capabilities
```

Possible units:
- parts/service data cleanup and mapping;
- service record digitization;
- remote technical coordination;
- quotation/document/translation support;
- dealer/service-network information flows;
- digital O&M exception handling;
- supplier/service-provider coordination.

Critical unknowns:
exact recurring friction, payer, access permission, data confidentiality, route economics.

Preliminary attractiveness: **HIGH / structurally strategic but needs direct actor evidence**.

### Field C — AI problem-translation and small measurable automation for SMEs

Observed change:
AI adoption is explicitly promoted in the local manufacturing pilot and Jiangsu industrial policy, while information/software services are growing quickly.

Latent-value hypothesis:
The scarce capability may not be the AI model itself, but converting vague operational friction into a measurable, data-ready, acceptance-defined task.

```text
workflow friction
+ enterprise process/data context
+ AI/software capability
→ bounded AI/automation task
→ measurable output
```

This should reject cases without usable data, permission, measurable benefit or a real payer.

Preliminary attractiveness: **MEDIUM-HIGH / likely component of Field A rather than standalone business at first**.

### Field D — Experience-consumption coordination and merchant utilization

Observed change:
H1 Xuzhou tourism consumption reached RMB 34.331bn (+12.12%); monitored tourist arrivals rose 12.59%. Retail grew 4.4%, restaurant growth was strong, outside-city consumers are material, and event/night-economy scenes are generating cross-category spending.

Latent-value hypothesis:

```text
event/tourist demand flow
+ merchants/venues/experiences with uneven utilization
+ fragmented discovery/booking/coordination
→ itinerary / event / group / merchant routing
```

This is real macro/behavior evidence, but the operator's technical/system capability advantage is weaker here unless the opportunity requires strong orchestration/data/automation rather than manual merchant BD.

Preliminary attractiveness: **MEDIUM / market strong, operator fit weaker**.

### Field E — Silver-life service coordination

Observed change:
Xuzhou has more than 2.29m residents aged 60+, and is expanding community/at-home eldercare, meal, care and medical coordination infrastructure.

Latent-value hypothesis:

```text
large recurring household service need
+ fragmented community/service resources
+ scheduling/trust/payment/quality friction
→ service orchestration
```

However, vulnerable-person safeguards, trust, public-service boundaries and payer complexity are high. This is not an automatic business opportunity.

Preliminary attractiveness: **MEDIUM / large persistent need, high trust/regulatory burden, lower immediate fit**.

### Field F — Local SME digital-commerce capability activation

Observed change:
Xuzhou online retail by monitored units grew 13.0%, while consumers increasingly use AI/search/comparison and demand concrete value.

Latent-value hypothesis:
Many local merchants/manufacturers may have product, inventory, domain knowledge or manufacturing capability but weak digital packaging, data operations, cross-platform operations or content conversion.

The danger is collapsing into low-margin agency work or founder-led sales. Only retain this field if tasks can be decomposed, delegated and tied to measurable outcomes.

Preliminary attractiveness: **MEDIUM / evidence needed before promotion**.

## 7. Operator role hypothesis

The operator should not default to `coder`, `salesperson`, `implementation engineer` or `merchant BD`.

The strongest role hypothesis is:

```text
observe hidden structure
→ define exact problem/outcome
→ decompose into CapabilityUnits
→ discover/option resources
→ design acceptance + trust + economics
→ delegate acquisition/delivery
→ learn from outcomes
```

Technical background is valuable because it improves decomposition, validation and QA of digital/industrial tasks. It should not force the operator to personally perform all tasks.

## 8. First decisive validations

### Validation A1 — service-provider overflow reality

Interview/observe a bounded sample of Xuzhou manufacturing digital-transformation service providers.

Do not ask: `Do you need outsourcing?`

Ask for recent real cases:
- which small tasks delayed acceptance or go-live;
- which tasks consumed senior staff but were too small to staff permanently;
- whether local onsite presence was required;
- whether external bounded execution is contractually allowed;
- what exact output/acceptance proof would make delegation safe;
- whether they have paid third parties for such tasks in the last 6-12 months.

Pass condition: repeated recent paid/accepted overflow tasks + explicit delegation boundary.

### Validation A2 — enterprise post-go-live friction

Observe pilot SMEs after implementation/acceptance.

Look for recurring manual reconciliation, broken interfaces, data quality, deployment/ops, reporting/acceptance, backup/recovery, training/ownership gaps.

Pass condition: repeated costly workaround + identifiable payer + task can be bounded and accepted.

### Validation B1 — lifecycle-service friction

Interview engineering-machinery chain actors around aftermarket/service/digital O&M.

Pass condition: repeated lifecycle micro-task with measurable economic cost, accessible data/process boundary, identified payer and routable capability.
