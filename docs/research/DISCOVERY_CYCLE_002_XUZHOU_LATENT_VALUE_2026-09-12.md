# Discovery Cycle 002 — Xuzhou Latent-Value Scan

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: `RESET / SEARCH ZONES, NOT BUSINESS CANDIDATES`
Constitutional parent: `docs/LATENT_VALUE_DOCTRINE.md`

## 1. Why this cycle was reset

The first version correctly preserved the macro → psychology → behavior direction, but then moved too quickly from visible industry activity into already-recognized problems such as digital implementation, AI automation and outsourced technical execution.

That is useful market observation, but it risks confusing **visible demand** with **latent-value discovery**.

Cycle 002 is therefore reset around this question:

> **What value already exists in Xuzhou but is not yet recognized, packaged, priced, callable, combined or operating as a market object?**

## 2. Locked discovery sequence

```text
MACRO REALITY
→ MONEY FLOW
→ CONSUMER / OWNER / OPERATOR PSYCHOLOGY
→ OBSERVED BEHAVIOR CHANGE
→ INDUSTRY STRUCTURE
→ ACTOR STATE / ENDOWMENT / CONSTRAINT
→ HIDDEN OR UNDERRECOGNIZED VALUE
→ COMPLEMENTARY ACTOR
→ TRANSFORMATION MECHANISM
→ WHY EXCHANGE DOES NOT ALREADY HAPPEN
→ EVIDENCE
→ BOUNDED VALIDATION
```

A procurement notice, explicit outsourcing request, job post, product listing or software requirement is a sensor only.

## 3. Macro / money-flow observations retained

The earlier Cycle 002 macro scan remains directionally useful:
- broad goods consumption is weaker than selected service/digital activity;
- Jiangsu equipment/digital/production-service activity is relatively stronger than broad property/consumption;
- Xuzhou shows meaningful industrial, software/information-service, business-service, online-retail and tourism/service activity;
- engineering machinery remains a major local industrial structure;
- enterprise and consumer decision logic increasingly emphasizes measurable value, certainty and cost-effectiveness.

These observations identify **where actor states may be changing**. They do not identify the business by themselves.

## 4. Psychology / behavior observations retained

Current search-relevant behavior hypotheses include:
- firms prefer measurable outcomes over fashionable technology narratives;
- permanent headcount may be avoided while episodic capability needs remain;
- consumers remain selective but still pay for clear experience/convenience/value;
- installed industrial systems create accumulated data, workarounds and tacit operating knowledge;
- service/visitor demand and merchant utilization may be uneven across time and channel;
- aging and workforce transitions may strand experience, time and trust resources.

These remain hypotheses until Xuzhou-specific evidence is observed.

## 5. Industry layer is now a search map, not a ranking

The engine may search inside:
- manufacturing and engineering machinery;
- production services;
- software/information services;
- local logistics/service networks;
- tourism/experience consumption;
- merchants/venues;
- community/aging-related service structures;
- SME supply chains.

No industry receives priority merely because it has a large market, public policy support, an explicit payer or strong operator skill fit.

## 6. Hidden-value search zones

### HV-01 — Person-bound industrial knowledge

Search for knowledge that organizations/people currently treat as personal experience rather than an asset:
- fault diagnosis;
- commissioning/debugging;
- repair sequencing;
- quality judgment;
- substitution/vendor knowledge;
- exception handling;
- field-service heuristics.

Required evidence:
- observable dependence on particular people;
- measurable cost when that person is unavailable;
- knowledge is repeatable enough to extract/verify;
- rights/confidentiality allow use.

### HV-02 — Dormant operational data

Search for logs, repair history, exception history, quality records, service records or customer records that are collected but do not change decisions.

Required evidence:
- data already exists;
- repeated decision/error/cost exists;
- transformed output could reduce that cost;
- permission and data quality are sufficient.

### HV-03 — Fragmented demand as an asset

Search for many actors making the same small purchase/service request under poor economics because each is too small individually.

Required evidence:
- repeated comparable demand fragments;
- aggregation changes supplier attention, price, response time or minimum order economics;
- commitments can be credibly aggregated.

### HV-04 — Underused local capacity

Search for actual idle/low-utilization:
- equipment;
- workshops;
- technicians;
- vehicles/return routes;
- venues;
- service windows;
- channels/access.

Required evidence:
- measured/observed underuse, not mere existence;
- conditional availability;
- complementary scarcity elsewhere.

### HV-05 — Trust / relationship / installed access

Search for actors with trusted access to a network that is used only for a narrow purpose but may lawfully reduce another transaction's search/trust cost.

Required evidence:
- permission;
- no confidentiality/conflict violation;
- measurable trust/access benefit;
- value persists beyond a one-off introduction.

### HV-06 — Uneven service / merchant / venue utilization

Search for valuable but empty time/space/service capacity alongside visitor/local demand concentrated elsewhere.

Required evidence:
- real utilization gap;
- real complementary demand flow;
- packaging/routing can create incremental utilization;
- outcome attribution is possible.

## 7. What is no longer a core candidate by itself

The following are now explicitly classified as downstream `EXPLICIT_DEMAND_EXECUTION` unless a deeper hidden-value structure is discovered:
- digital service-provider overflow;
- published software/RFQ needs;
- explicit AI automation requests;
- generic outsourced coding;
- generic merchant lead generation;
- known buyer-to-known-provider matching.

They may be tested for cash flow, but cannot define the core discovery engine.

## 11. Current conclusion

There is no canonical business winner.

The correct question for the next phase is not:

> `What service can the operator sell in Xuzhou?`

It is:

> **What real value in Xuzhou is currently invisible or trapped; who owns it without fully realizing it; which other Actor becomes better off if it is transformed; and what structure makes that exchange possible?**
