## Search boundary

Scan 050 deliberately stopped using one-time build contracts as the primary signal.

```text
RECURRING PAID OUTCOME / REPEATED PAID WORKAROUND
→ POST-EXECUTION RESULT IS MEASURABLE
→ CUSTOMER CAN AUTHORIZE ACTION
→ EXACT INCUMBENT PREFLIGHT
→ INDEPENDENT OPERATOR CONTROL
→ REPEATABLE ECONOMICS
```

That produces commercially stronger evidence than Scan 049. It still does not automatically create white space.

## Examined recurring outcome flows

### Email deliverability / primary-inbox maintenance

A current ongoing job asks one operator to keep 36 mailboxes landing in Primary, monitor every live campaign and proactively replace unhealthy inboxes. The acceptance metric is unusually clear: inbox placement.

This still closes because the control loop is already a category. InboxAlly and Warmy expose placement tests, reputation/DNS health, mailbox monitoring, warm-up and repair functions. The unresolved cases depend on Gmail/Outlook filtering behavior, reputation dynamics and specialist diagnosis rather than a deterministic operator-owned action rail.

### Amazon PPC profitability

A current seller explicitly hires for lower ACoS/TACoS and profitable scaling on an ongoing basis.

The outcome is measurable and repeated, but Perpetua and Scale Insights already automate bids, keyword harvesting, negative keywords, budgets, placement and target-ACoS logic. Amazon controls the auction and conversion surface. The remaining human value is strategy and catalog-specific judgment.

### Technical SEO maintenance

Current buyers pay monthly for audits, fixes, crawl/index monitoring and visibility improvement.

Semrush already runs scheduled recurring audits, prioritizes technical issues, monitors search/AI crawler access and tracks resolution. The final ranking/traffic outcome is not controlled by the operator and meaningful gains still require expert architecture/content/off-page decisions.

### CRM data quality

A current long-term role pays someone to own clean, accurate, deduplicated CRM records across repeated imports and client workflows.

This is one of the cleaner acceptance surfaces in the scan. It nevertheless closes because Insycle already provides scheduled and workflow-triggered deduplication, standardization, relationship repair, import controls, health assessment and audit trails across HubSpot/Salesforce.

### Multichannel ecommerce stockout / exception control

A current ecommerce operations role has explicit recurring KPIs: no preventable stockouts on hero SKUs, low order-defect rate, fast support resolution and daily anomaly detection across Shopify, Amazon and 3PLs.

Inventory and order control are already strongly productized by Shopify and Cin7. The residue is a heterogeneous human bundle: supplier chasing, support, feed exceptions, marketplace health and ad-hoc projects.

### Performance-based qualified lead generation

A current buyer offers $100+ per accepted qualified lead and requires a real business with an active buying need. This is stronger evidence than paying for a list.

It still fails the project floor. Clay and similar outbound stacks already automate sourcing, research, scoring and personalization, while the actual result depends on repeated prospecting, outreach, sender/domain reputation, message strategy and sales judgment. Performance-based pricing proves outcome value; it does not remove recurring acquisition work.

## Result

```text
RECURRING PAYMENT = YES
MEASURABLE OUTCOME = YES
CUSTOMER-AUTHORIZED ACTION = OFTEN YES
INDEPENDENT DURABLE CONTROL = NO
NEW COMMERCIAL FORMATIONS = 0
```

The useful advance is that the search signal is now economically stronger. The remaining false positives cluster around two failure modes:

1. the exact recurring control loop is already a mature product; or
2. the result is materially controlled by an external ranking/filtering/auction platform or recurring expert/human execution.

## Scan 051 reset

Do not return to one-time build budgets.

Search for recurring paid **outcome units** where:
- acceptance can be verified mechanically or with a tight binary rubric;
- execution can be decomposed and replaced without the founder;
- the result is not primarily controlled by Google/Gmail/Amazon/another platform algorithm;
- repeatable unit economics are visible;
- the operator has a real action right or durable execution position;
- exact incumbents are checked before causal descent;
- Scan 050 verticals do not seed the search.

This moves closer to a self-propelling value flow without lowering the commercial truth gates.
