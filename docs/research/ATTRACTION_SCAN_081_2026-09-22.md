## F1 — Korean exporter carbon-data request cascade

The signal starts from behavior, not a product category. KCCI and KICOX surveyed 300 exporters in 2026. Almost every respondent reported current or expected buyer demand for carbon-emissions data, and nine in ten are pushing the requirement to their own suppliers.

The operational seam is concrete: fragmented internal systems, repeated spreadsheet/manual re-entry and customer-specific templates. Stated three-year spending expectations are also substantial, although they are not executed external payments to a new operator.

The incumbent preflight closes the formation. KICOX has already launched an MRV platform for measurement, reporting and verification. Glassdome advertises direct MES/ERP integration, PCF/CBAM reporting and Catena-X-certified exchange, and current Korean automotive projects demonstrate multi-tier PCF exchange. The remaining difficult work includes source measurement, calculation boundaries, third-party verification, supplier consent and integration—important work, but not yet a distinct founder-independent operator asset.

## F2 — France mandatory B2B e-invoicing

France's 1 September 2026 change is a clean actor-state transition: every business must be able to receive electronic invoices, while large and mid-sized businesses must also issue them and transmit e-reporting data. Government guidance says more than 10 million economic actors are affected and explicitly acknowledges launch-period difficulty.

This still fails as commercial white space because the execution rail is legally assigned. Businesses must use an accredited platform, directly or through a compatible solution, and the European Commission country sheet reports nearly 100 accredited private platforms. The state change creates demand, but it does not leave invoice validation/routing/reporting control unowned.

## F3 — Enterprise AI-agent identity sprawl

This is the strongest non-regulatory operational formation in the reset. CSA's 2026 survey reports unknown agents in 82% of organizations, agent-related incidents in 65%, and formal decommissioning in only 21%. Okta separately reports that fewer than half of surveyed CISOs can confidently say where agents are, what they can access or which actions they are allowed to take; shared credentials and ad-hoc control remain common.

The task shape is attractive—discovery, identity, policy evaluation, revocation and audit are primarily machine-executable, and a generic agent cannot safely be its own root of trust. But the privileged control plane is already becoming a core IAM/security product surface. Microsoft Entra Agent ID is generally available with purpose-built identities, authorization, lifecycle governance, risk controls and audit, including support for agents built outside Microsoft's own agent stack. Specialist identity/security vendors are also directly productizing the same problem.

## Exclusions

- Agentic-commerce merchant discoverability and third-party shopping-agent blocking: excluded before formation because Scan 035 already demoted GEO/brand visibility and repository rules explicitly prevent MCP/spec/discoverability terms from seeding another ontology.
- Generic spreadsheet-heavy inventory, finance and professional-services operations: excluded because spreadsheet use by itself is not white space and these categories overlap prior workaround passes.
- Generic EU AI Act inventory/labeling: excluded because inventory, labeling and policy work collapse to current GRC/provider-native surfaces plus legal interpretation rather than a distinct machine-owned value flow.

## Learning

The source reset worked in the narrow sense: it restored formation diversity and surfaced live behavior changes without using a procurement category as the starting ontology.

It did **not** produce a commercial promotion. The recurring pattern is now different from Scan 080:

```text
CURRENT ACTOR / STATE CHANGE
+ OBSERVED WORKAROUND OR FAILURE
!=
UNOWNED CONTROL SURFACE
```

One broad-reset pass is not enough to infer a new mechanism or payer signal. Scan 082 will repeat the same upstream method on an independent formation-diverse sample before any new boundary is derived.
