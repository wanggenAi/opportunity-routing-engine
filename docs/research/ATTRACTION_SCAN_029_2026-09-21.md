## Search rule

Scan 029 moved one layer earlier than visible marketplaces.

It searched for real exchange already happening through:
- WeChat groups;
- community organizers;
- park/industry alliances;
- shared spreadsheets/forms;
- manual direct-contact bulletins.

The test was not "is the group messy?" It was:

```text
REAL EXCHANGE EXISTS
+ HUMAN BRIDGE IS REPEATED
+ NO DOMINANT CONTROL SURFACE
→ CAN THE BRIDGE BECOME NON-HUMAN AND REUSABLE
WITHOUT REBUILDING A MARKETPLACE?
```

The answer across the tested set was **no retained formation**.

## 1. Shared-employee groups — real exchange, wrong execution shape

A Ningbo industrial-park union created a "shared employee group" where companies post temporary labor demand
and workers/other enterprises provide available labor. The reported group covered 62 companies and 376 workers
and had already produced hundreds of worker-sharing matches.

Source:
- http://acftu.people.com.cn/n1/2025/0218/c67502-40420868.html

This proves the pre-platform exchange is real.

But the bridge is not just message matching. It includes:
- worker availability;
- job/skill fit;
- training;
- attendance;
- labor rights/compliance;
- wage settlement;
- fulfillment reliability.

By 2026, Tencent Maps had already launched a merchant temporary-hiring flow with nearby worker matching,
attendance and wage settlement.

Source:
- https://cloud.tencent.com/developer/article/2673948

Verdict: **demoted — recurring human supply/compliance + platformization**.

## 2. Paid "dazi" groups — money is real, software is not scarce

2026 reporting shows self-organized "dazi" groups charging roughly 9.9–39.9 yuan for entry. Organizers also
monetize merchant referrals. Most activity payments are AA.

Source:
- https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-07-24/doc-iniivpqu9319601.shtml

This is genuine exchange and genuine small-scale monetization.

But the obvious software bridge is already commodity:
- signup;
- payment;
- reminders;
- AA calculation;
- attendance;
- group/event management.

Examples:
- https://baominggongju.com/
- https://www.bmguanjia.com/scene/community
- https://cuqu.net/

The scarce layer is the organizer's traffic, moderation, group culture, safety and offline activity operations.

Verdict: **demoted — mature tools + recurring community operations**.

## 3. Surplus meals in WeChat groups — exact platform already exists

In 2026, a Zhejiang community and local restaurant began selling compliant surplus meals at low prices to
delivery riders and other workers. A WeChat group publishes daily menu and pickup timing.

Source:
- https://zj.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0823/c186327-41675318.html

The flow is attractive: merchant reduces waste; worker gets cheaper dinner.

But the exact formation is already platformized. "惜食魔法袋" connects merchants with users for same-day
surplus food and reports coverage across 300+ cities and tens of thousands of merchants/partners.

Sources:
- https://magicbag.xishi.life/
- https://magicbag.xishi.life/business

Food safety, perishability and pickup also remain local physical constraints.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## 4. Logistics-park shared capacity — valuable, but physical and locally governed

In 2026, Changsha Yuhua logistics companies formed a voluntary alliance to share capacity and resources,
including transport and warehousing; the arrangement reportedly helped activate substantial warehouse space.

Source:
- https://hn.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0709/c356886-41633857.html

This is a high-value self-organized exchange, but the bridge includes:
- real warehouse availability;
- vehicles;
- local contracts;
- timing;
- physical dispatch;
- enterprise relationships.

The alliance/park itself becomes the trust and coordination node. A thin independent layer would either become
lead generation or inherit physical/local operations.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## 5. Industrial-park supply/demand bulletins — structured, but only lead generation

Shanghai Qingpu Industrial Park opened an official upstream/downstream cooperation bulletin in 2026. Companies
submit purchase/sales requirements including specifications, quantities, delivery timing, budget and direct
contacts through a form, after which companies connect directly.

Source:
- https://industry.shqp.gov.cn/industry/yqdt/20260403/1368115.html

This is almost the ideal input for automatic text matching. But that is exactly the trap.

The park already provides free publication and discoverability. The automatcher would not own:
- acceptance;
- supplier qualification;
- contracting;
- transaction;
- fulfillment;
- outcome data.

Verdict: **demoted — public bulletin + generic B2B lead matching**.

## Main learning

Scan 029 validates the "look at groups first" method but rejects a simplistic conclusion:

```text
WECHAT GROUP
!=
SOFTWARE OPPORTUNITY
```

Groups often appear because the missing layer is:
- trust;
- new supply;
- local logistics;
- regulation/compliance;
- human moderation;
- settlement.

Those are platform operations, not thin founder-independent software.

## Scan 030 boundary

The next scan removes the two-sided-market problem entirely.

Search one-actor workflows where:
- the actor already owns the required input data;
- the actor already has the right to execute the final action;
- no new supplier/customer marketplace must be created;
- no recurring external expert is needed;
- but the workflow still relies on chat, Excel, copy/paste, screenshots or manual reconciliation across systems.

```text
ONE OWNER
+ INPUT RIGHTS ALREADY OWNED
+ ACTION RIGHTS ALREADY OWNED
+ REPEATED MANUAL CROSS-SYSTEM BRIDGE
→ TEST WHETHER THE BRIDGE IS A REAL NON-GENERIC CONTROL EDGE
```

Do not assume the answer is integration software. Generic automation and existing ERP/vertical SaaS remain hard
falsifiers.
