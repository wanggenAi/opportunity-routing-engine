# ATTRACTION_SCAN_117 — Intrinsically Low Current Labor / Clean Post-Control Ledger

Date: 2026-09-23  
Status: COMPLETE — zero retained formations / zero commercial promotions

## Why this scan exists

Scan 116 closed the standalone-ledger visibility gap but showed that positive OCF and low assets are not enough when current expert labor and project delivery remain core. Scan 117 therefore moves the retrieval priority to **intrinsically low current FTE** while preserving completed-control, clean post-control economics, positive explicit OCF and fresh-operator reproducibility as hard boundaries.

```text
COMPLETED FRESH-OPERATOR-REPRODUCIBLE CONTROL
+ CLEAN POST-CONTROL SAME-ENTITY PERIOD
+ POSITIVE EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ EXPLICIT POSITIVE OCF
+ CURRENT FTE INTRINSICALLY LOW
+ NO PROJECT / FIELD / MANUFACTURING / AFTER-SALES DELIVERY ENGINE
+ BOUNDED CONTROL + OPERATING + WORKING CAPITAL
+ NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE VERIFIED FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

## F1 — Tianwu / now Xunzhi Technology (832418)

This is the closest formation in Scan 117.

- The 4,999,600-share / 99.9920% transaction was priced at RMB999,920.
- Share-transfer registration completed on 2025-11-26, changing the first-largest shareholder, controlling shareholder and actual controller.
- 2026 H1 is therefore a clean post-control period.
- Current staff: **11**.
- Current total assets: about **RMB6.8205m**.
- 2026 H1 revenue: **RMB5.362277m**.
- 2026 H1 net profit: about **RMB0.4217m**.
- Explicit operating cashflow per share: **negative RMB0.0029**.
- Current business is light-asset software around recycling vehicles/cabinets and a community recycling mini-program, but disclosed revenue mechanisms still include custom development, annual software fees and maintenance.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

This is stronger than Scan 116's low-asset case because completed near-total control, a clean post-control period, positive revenue/profit and only 11 current employees coexist. It still fails the required positive-OCF gate. Positive accounting profit is not promoted over negative operating cashflow. The project/customization and maintenance components also mean the recurring machine-only delivery floor is not yet closed.

## F2 — Henglilai (839773)

- Shanghai Keni Steel Pipe agreed to acquire 6,112,225 shares / 55.54% for **RMB1.405812m**.
- The seller delegated the corresponding voting rights immediately, but the underlying share delivery remains pending release/transfer; the 2026 H1 shareholder table still shows the seller at 55.54%.
- Current staff: **4** — two management and two administrative.
- Current assets: about **RMB0.6597m**.
- 2026 H1 revenue: **zero**.
- 2026 H1 net loss: **RMB33,901.20**.
- 2026 H1 OCF: **positive only RMB93.88**.
- The acquisition plan explicitly contemplates injecting related steel-pipe business, production equipment, personnel, suppliers and customers and financing initial production.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

Four employees and a small headline control price describe a transitional shell, not a proven low-labor cashflow engine. The equity transfer is not complete, current external revenue is zero, and the planned operating future requires physical manufacturing resources and people. Current low FTE cannot be projected through a pending business injection.

## F3 — Yangzhu 5 (400177)

- On 2026-05-19, 56,755,678 shares / 20.9994% of voting rights were irrevocably delegated to Pan Donghan for one year, making him controlling shareholder and actual controller.
- This is a real governance change but not a fresh independent purchase of the decisive ownership position.
- 2026 H1 current staff: **142**, up from 118.
- 2026-06-30 assets: **RMB322.4472m**.
- H1 revenue: **RMB55.4790m**.
- H1 OCF: **negative RMB18.1895m**; net profit is negative.
- The principal operating subsidiary is physical bamboo-plastic materials production and the report cites added production-equipment investment.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

The H1 period also straddles the May controller change, so it is not relabeled as clean post-control continuity. Independently, 142 FTE, physical production, large assets and negative OCF directly fail the Scan 117 low-labor machine-control boundary. A time-limited voting delegation is not treated as a reproducible fresh-operator ownership purchase.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The scan narrows the missing evidence again:

1. **Low current FTE is now directly observable in a completed-control target.** Tianwu reaches 11 employees after a near-total completed transfer.
2. **The next hard accounting separator is positive post-control OCF in the same low-FTE entity.** Tianwu has positive revenue and profit but negative OCF.
3. **Low FTE must describe the durable operating model, not a dormant transition.** Henglilai has four employees but zero revenue and an explicit future manufacturing/personnel injection.
4. **Governance control is not enough.** Temporary voting-right delegation is not the same as a reproducible fresh-operator ownership position.
5. **Recurring non-project revenue now matters.** A low-FTE target that still earns through customization/maintenance has not yet proven the founder-light machine rail.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 118

Prioritize fresh formations where all of the following already coexist:

- completed control before the reporting period;
- clean same-entity post-control positive revenue, positive net profit and explicit positive OCF;
- current FTE intrinsically low;
- existing revenue is repeatable and not primarily project customization, field work, manufacturing, after-sales or a pending business injection;
- the repeatable flow may be license, royalty, subscription, automated transaction or another evidenced non-project mechanism, without choosing a product vertical in advance;
- fresh-operator control is reproducible and consideration/operating/working capital are bounded;
- the decisive control is noncommodity and machine-operable.
