## Purpose

Consume the verified Scan 121 Jev continuation and test the stricter retrieval rule that was earned by Scans 120-121: a current packet is not a primary formation unless the same entity and current period directly show intrinsically low FTE, positive external revenue, positive net profit and unambiguous positive consolidated operating cashflow together.

## Result

Two fresh packets passed all four current-economic admission legs. Neither survives control and delivery deepening.

Current 2026 H1 evidence binds 17 FTE, RMB48,034,052.28 revenue, RMB1,706,441.35 net profit and RMB20,230,573.56 consolidated OCF to the same entity and period. This is a legitimate primary formation under the stricter gate.

It still closes. Yao Xuegen and spouse Yu Meidi own 100%; Yao is chairman/general manager; current reporting says controllers did not change. The business wins orders through public tendering, buys steel plate, flanges, coatings and accessories after sales contracts, manufactures against orders and delivers physical wind-tower equipment in batches.

Verdict: **DEMOTED**. Positive current economics do not prove a fresh-operator-reproducible control position or founder-light machine delivery.

### ATTRACTION_SCAN_122-F2 — Kangmeifeng / Guangdong Comifo CNC Equipment

Current 2026 H1 evidence binds 15 FTE, RMB8,719,805.71 revenue, RMB1,423,975.92 attributable net profit and RMB1,587,115.80 consolidated OCF to the same entity and period. Net margin is about 16.33%.

It still closes. Wang Fenquan and Zhang Guifang are spouses, directly control about 88.89%, current reporting says actual control did not change, and historical disclosure traces the same couple's control to the 2006 company formation. Verdict: **DEMOTED**. Strong current economics do not make longstanding founder-family control reproducible by a fresh operator, and customer-specific equipment production is not machine-only delivery.
