## Strongest structural exemplar: CPG deduction recovery

A current engineering role describes a live production system serving paying CPG customers. The loop is almost exactly the project's desired physics:

```text
DEDUCTION EVENT
→ PORTAL / EDI / EMAIL INGESTION
→ PO / BOL / POD / PROMOTION EVIDENCE
→ GROUNDED VALID / INVALID DECISION
→ GOVERNED DISPUTE SUBMISSION
→ PARTNER RESPONSE
→ ERP RECONCILIATION
→ MONEY RECOVERED
```

This is excellent evidence that the target architecture can create real value.

It is not a new opportunity. Current deduction/AR products already automate short-pay detection, evidence assembly, disputes and recovery across wholesale/CPG accounts.

The correct learning is **shape proof, not opportunity retention**.

## Other closures

### Ecommerce chargebacks

Merchants still pay human specialists for recurring Shopify/Stripe/PayPal dispute handling, and productized AI automation is sold as a service.

The edge is already compressed from both sides:
- Shopify itself auto-populates available evidence and adds AI-generated evidence insights for some disputes;
- specialist automation products/services already connect dispute events to order/shipping evidence and submission.

Final adjudication remains with card networks/banks.

### Subcontractor COI expiry and renewal

The trigger is ideal: certificate expiry is deterministic, recurring and operationally important. Businesses still pay people to chase documents.

But the exact category is already dense. Current COI software collects documents, sends reminders, checks compliance and tracks expiration.

### Freight invoice / accessorial audit

Invoices, rate agreements, fuel tables, BOL/POD and dock timestamps form a strong deterministic evidence graph. Current paid operations work includes freight-invoice audits and accessorial error follow-up.

Again, the exact product category already exists: freight-audit systems compare charges to contracts and shipment records and can create evidence-backed disputes.

### Construction lien-waiver payment gate

A current $6,500 specialist engagement proves meaningful payer demand around lien-waiver sequencing and portal submission.

But 2026 products now watch accounting payments, draft the correct waiver, collect e-signatures, track status and even gate payment. This is no longer an unowned workflow.

### MDF / co-op reimbursement claims

The money pool is real and time-bounded. Partners submit proof of performance against allocated funds and lose unused entitlements after claim deadlines.

The control surface is mature: partner-relationship and rebate platforms already support allocation, fund requests, claims, evidence, approvals, settlement and audit trail.

## Scan 049 reset

Do not lower the floors.

The next scan should move earlier in time rather than deeper into old operational categories:

- start from paid workflows **created or materially changed since 2026-07-01**;
- require direct payer or repeated-workaround evidence;
- perform exact full-loop product saturation preflight immediately;
- exclude software/protocol migrations and regulatory-expert work already shown to collapse into services;
- do not inherit deductions, chargebacks, COI, freight audit, lien waivers or MDF as vertical priors.

The purpose is to reach a flow before a mature control surface forms, while still demanding real money motion.
