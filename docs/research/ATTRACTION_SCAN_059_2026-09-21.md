# Attraction Scan 059 — 2026-09-21

## Boundary

Scan 059 is the second broad, formation-diverse pass after Scan 058. It excludes Scan 058 verticals and requires stronger recurring-spend or contract-to-hire evidence while keeping digital/delegatable delivery, exact-incumbent preflight, founder independence, operator control and fail-closed promotion.

## Result

**Commercial promotions: 0. Retained research formations: 0. FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW: NOT_PROVEN.**

Six unrelated recurring paid outcomes were tested:

1. **QuickBooks recurring month-end close** — 6+ months, contract-to-hire. QuickBooks AI now absorbs reconciliation and issue-finding; unusual transactions, journal entries and accountable close judgment remain human.
2. **High-volume recruiting coordination** — $8–$15/hour, 6+ months, contract-to-hire. Greenhouse now automates sourcing/screening/scheduling and offers Voice AI first interviews; selection and persuasion remain human.
3. **Medical billing and claims follow-up** — $15–$25/hour, 6+ months. Waystar automates claim status, denial/appeal workflows, payment posting and reconciliation; regulated exceptions and payer/patient communication remain human.
4. **Affiliate/creator recruitment** — long-term base + commission, contract-to-hire. impact.com already owns discovery, AI recommendations, outreach workflows, contracting, tracking and payout infrastructure; relationships remain core.
5. **Warm-lead appointment setting** — paid per qualified appointment and signup. HighLevel Voice AI now supports outbound calling, qualification and direct calendar booking; consent, persuasion and sales judgment remain.
6. **Multi-brand Klaviyo email ownership** — $25–$40/hour, 6+ months, contract-to-hire. Klaviyo Composer drafts campaigns, flows and segments; brand strategy, creative judgment, deliverability/compliance and approval remain human.

## Structural finding

```text
STRONG RECURRING HUMAN SPEND
!=
DISTINCT REPEATABLE OPERATOR LAYER
```

The second broad sample strengthens Scan 058 without creating a new mechanism thesis. Buyers continue to hire humans after the repetitive execution surface is already automated. Native AI/SaaS compresses routine work and concentrates residual spend in judgment, relationships, consent, compliance and accountable ownership.

Contract-to-hire and performance pay prove that money moves, but they do not prove an independent operator can own the value flow. After two broad role-based samples, the next empirical improvement should be a change in **evidence source**, not another tighter mechanism ontology.
