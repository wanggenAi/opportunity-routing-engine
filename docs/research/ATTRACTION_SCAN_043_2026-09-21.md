## Search rule

Scan 043 started from direct payer or repeated workaround evidence, not from a product theory. It then required machine-executable digital delivery, an unowned compounding operator asset, no mature control surface and no recurring expert delivery.

## Results

Six paid workflows were tested:

- ecommerce product-feed/disapproval management;
- EDI trading-partner onboarding and mapping;
- construction takeoff and estimating;
- security questionnaire/RFP response;
- short-form video editing retainers;
- Amazon seller account appeal/reinstatement.

Every one has real willingness to pay. None survives the asset/control test.

Feed management, EDI, estimating and questionnaire automation already have exact mature products or networks that own the relevant rule graph, partner map, cost catalog or approved-answer library. Short-form editing remains recurring creative delivery while its mechanical layer is commoditized by AI. Amazon appeals have strong willingness to pay, but specialist and attorney-led firms already occupy the workflow and difficult cases remain case-specific.

## Main learning

```text
DIRECT PAYER
+
REPEATED WORKAROUND
+
AUTOMATABLE TASK
!=
UNOWNED COMPOUNDING BUSINESS
```

The next scan will require evidence that **doing the work itself** creates or strengthens a reusable operator-owned asset — outcome data, network relationship, verified capability, reputation or legally reusable rights — rather than merely assuming an asset could exist.
