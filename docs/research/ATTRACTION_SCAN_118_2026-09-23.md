## Why this scan exists

Scan 117 proved that a completed low-cost control transfer, a clean post-control period, positive revenue/profit and only 11 current employees can coexist, but Tianwu still failed explicit positive operating cashflow and non-project delivery. Scan 118 therefore keeps low current FTE and positive OCF as independent hard gates and adds an existing repeatable non-project revenue requirement.

## F1 — Leyou (873412): low FTE and bounded completed control, but current post-control ledger is unclosed

- Shi Guangchao acquired 3,375,000 shares / 67.50% at RMB0.74 per share for **RMB2.4975m**.
- Registration completed on **2025-12-29**; with the prior 2.50% position, the controller holds 70%.
- Current company profile reports **10 employees**.
- The business is software development/service, including electric-power software, telecom software outsourcing and project implementation.
- 2025 annual revenue, profit and OCF were positive, but the transfer occurred only on December 29.
- No 2026 H1 or equivalent current post-control standalone ledger was retrieved in the Scan 118 evidence packet.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

This is the strongest low-labor completed-control shape in the scan, but the exact current economic packet is missing. The engine does not relabel pre-control positive OCF as post-control continuity. Project implementation, outsourcing and contract-performance costs also remain explicit, so low FTE alone does not establish a repeatable machine-only revenue rail.

## F2 — Haitu Technology (874022): current positive accounting can coexist with fatal labor/capital burden

- Shanxi Keda Automation acquired 25,500,001 shares / **51%** for **RMB209.1m**.
- Share-transfer registration completed on **2025-12-30** and the buyer remains the 51% holder at 2026-06-30.
- The buyer's H1 combination note uses **2026-02-01** as the accounting purchase date; legal transfer timing and accounting treatment are therefore kept separate.
- 2026 H1 target revenue: **RMB73.1325m**.
- 2026 H1 target net profit: **RMB16.4594m**.
- A current finance-change table reports OCF **positive RMB3.8282m**, while a separate public per-share field surfaced inconsistently; the OCF packet is therefore not treated as unambiguous.
- Current company-profile employees: **113**.
- Target assets: about **RMB242.4149m**.
- Current delivery combines intelligent audio/video software, embedded modules, industrial cameras, custom design/manufacturing and technical service.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

Even if the positive absolute OCF table is accepted, the formation independently fails the small-operator and founder-light floors: 113 employees, RMB209.1m control consideration, a RMB242.4m asset base and custom hardware/software service delivery. The OCF-source disagreement and February accounting purchase-date treatment are preserved as additional fail-closed evidence-quality issues rather than averaged away.

## F3 — Taizhong Xiangming (871757): same-target H1 financial closure, but negative OCF and non-reproducible industrial control

- Taiyuan Heavy Industry acquired **67%** for **RMB299.515795m**.
- Registration completed on **2025-12-31**.
- This was a related-group restructuring: seller Taizhong Group controls the buyer and the ultimate controller remained Shanxi SASAC.
- The buyer's 2026 H1 important-subsidiary table reports target revenue **RMB258.4783m**, net profit **RMB6.9628m**, and explicit OCF **negative RMB43.7289m**.
- Target assets are about **RMB938.6597m**.
- Latest disclosed/current-profile employee count is **323**.
- The company manufactures, installs and services bulk-material conveying equipment and performs engineering/on-site technical work.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

This packet closes current same-entity revenue/profit/OCF cleanly enough to falsify the formation directly. Positive accounting profit does not override deeply negative OCF, hundreds of employees, high capital and physical project delivery. The same-group transaction also does not prove a control surface reproducible by an unrelated fresh operator.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The useful closure is now narrower:

1. **Low FTE + completed bounded control is real**, but Leyou shows that old positive OCF cannot substitute for a current post-control ledger.
2. **Current positive revenue/profit and even a reported positive OCF can be real**, but Haitu shows that labor, acquisition capital and custom hardware/service can still dominate.
3. **Source reconciliation is now a hard evidence property.** Conflicting OCF fields or legal-transfer/accounting-purchase-date differences remain explicit until reconciled.
4. **Related-group restructuring is not fresh-operator reproducibility.**
5. The next retrieval pass should refuse to deepen a case unless a current post-control report and intrinsically low current/recent FTE are available early.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 119

Prioritize fresh formations only when the following can be established before deep research:

- completed fresh-operator-reproducible control before the reporting period;
- a current post-control report is available;
- current or latest directly disclosed FTE is intrinsically low;
- same-entity positive revenue and positive net profit;
- **unambiguous positive OCF**, reconciled across primary/equivalent disclosures;
- existing standardized repeatable non-project revenue;
- no custom implementation, outsourcing, field work, manufacturing, after-sales or pending business injection as the recurring delivery engine;
- bounded control consideration, operating assets and working capital;
- noncommodity machine-operable control.

Do not infer a product or vertical from the zero-retention result.
