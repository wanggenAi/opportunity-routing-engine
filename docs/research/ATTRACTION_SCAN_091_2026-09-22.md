# Attraction Scan 091 — paid residual failure plus second economic cost

Date: 2026-09-22  
Epoch: `ATTRACTION_FIELD_V1`  
Status: complete; zero retention; zero commercial promotion.

## Why this scan raises the evidence floor

Scans 089 and 090 established that users really do pay for cross-component coordination. They also established that payment by itself is weak white-space evidence because mature products already capture many coordination layers.

Scan 091 therefore asks a stronger question:

> After a user has already paid a coordinator, does the same outcome still fail often enough that the user bears a second real economic cost to finish it?

The floor is intentionally strict:

- at least two unaffiliated paying participant accounts in the same formation;
- recurring, high-value incompleteness after the first paid coordinator;
- an **executed** second cost: another paid service/provider or repeated explicitly quantified manual work;
- future announced fees, generic category pricing and analyst inference do not count as payment;
- exact incumbent, native absorption, rights, founder-independence, normalized economics, machine-delegatability and generic-agent substitution are preflighted before promotion.

## Result

The signal is materially stronger than “coordination payment exists,” but it is still not sufficient.

```text
PAID COORDINATOR
+ RESIDUAL FAILURE
+ SECOND REAL COST
=
STRONGER ECONOMIC PAIN

BUT

STRONGER ECONOMIC PAIN
!=
UNOWNED CONTROL EDGE
```

One formation cleanly proves replicated double cost, one has real residual economics but fails the strict two-user evidence-binding standard, and one fails because the second cost is still future rather than executed. None survives the control-surface preflight.

## F1 — paid ecommerce ledger + settlement reconciliation connector

Multi-channel ecommerce accounting produces a clear residual after the paid ledger.

A recent operator explicitly reports using QuickBooks Online plus A2X at **$110/month** to import Etsy, Amazon and Shopify sales. Current 2026 QBO discussions still report payout mapping, manual review and reconciliation friction. A July 2026 seller reports currently using Link My Books with Xero, and Link My Books publishes active 2026 subscription pricing.

This is real economic evidence, but Scan 091 does **not** promote the replication check to a clean pass. Public evidence does not bind two unaffiliated current participant accounts to both the paid ledger and executed second-layer payment at equal evidentiary quality.

The larger commercial problem is even clearer:

- A2X and Link My Books already productize the settlement-translation layer;
- Webgility and other connectors occupy the same category;
- Intuit rebuilt its ecommerce connectors in 2026 with native payout reconciliation, retry logic and unified transaction review.

Verdict: **demoted**. The residual is real, but the exact mature connector layer is already owned and native ledger absorption is increasing.

## F2 — paid cold-email orchestration + deliverability / verification stack

This is the cleanest Scan 091 double-cost observation.

A 2026 outbound operator reports:
- Smartlead: about **$189/month** for multi-campaign sending and reply management;
- GlockApps: about **$90/month** for weekly inbox-placement tests because provider dashboards can disagree with actual placement.

An unaffiliated participant reports a similar Smartlead stack but adds NeverBounce at about **$50/month** after poor address accuracy drove bounce rates to roughly 8%; the second layer reduced that to under 2%.

So the new floor passes:

```text
PAID SENDER
→ RESIDUAL DELIVERY / DATA FAILURE
→ SECOND RECURRING PAID TOOL
```

Yet the formation still closes.

Smartlead now includes SmartDelivery for inbox-placement testing, delivery optimization, bounce protection and related diagnostics. That is direct native absorption of a major part of the observed second layer. At the same time, Gmail and other mailbox providers retain final filtering and reputation authority; Google explicitly states that it does not guarantee messages sent through third-party providers will pass Gmail spam filters.

Verdict: **demoted**. Double cost is proven, but the repair layer is being absorbed into the paid coordinator while decisive inbox authority remains external.

## F3 — paid Home Assistant Cloud + upcoming SmartThings API toll

This case is useful because it tests truth discipline.

Current 2026 participants explicitly pay Home Assistant Cloud / Nabu Casa for remote access and voice-assistant coordination. Samsung announced a **$4.99/month** personal SmartThings API plan, and current Home Assistant users are discussing migration because the Home Assistant ↔ SmartThings path depends on the API.

However, Samsung's official notice says:
- free API access remains through Q3 2026;
- the new paid plan is targeted for **October 2026**.

As of this scan date, **2026-09-22**, that second fee has not yet become an executed user payment.

```text
ANNOUNCED FUTURE FEE
!=
EXECUTED SECOND COST
```

Users can also bypass many SmartThings dependencies by moving Zigbee/Z-Wave devices to direct Home Assistant radios or using Matter paths. Samsung remains the rights owner for cloud/API access to Samsung-linked devices.

Verdict: **demoted**. The second-cost gate fails on timing, and the residual is a platform-rights toll rather than an unowned orchestration layer.

## First-pass learning

Scan 091 establishes three useful distinctions:

1. A second real cost after coordinator payment is stronger evidence than coordinator payment alone.
2. A mature complement can already own that second-cost layer, so double spend is not equivalent to white space.
3. Future fees and category-level subscription evidence cannot be promoted into executed participant payment.

One pass is not enough to derive another mechanism or payer rule. The next scan should therefore repeat the **same** residual-second-cost floor on fresh, formation-diverse markets.

This avoids cycling back into the pre-reset Scan 060–090 discovery signals and avoids deriving a product shape from one surviving pattern.
