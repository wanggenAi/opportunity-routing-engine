# ATTRACTION_SCAN_115 — Post-control OCF + labor + total-capital binding pass

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Primary research domain:** CHINA  
**Commercial promotions:** 0  
**Retained research formations:** 0  
**FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW:** NOT_PROVEN

## Purpose

Scan 115 tightens the remaining boundary after Scan 114 proved that completed control plus positive post-purchase revenue, net profit and explicit operating cashflow can coexist.

The pass requires the same acquired target to bind:

```text
COMPLETED CONTROL
+ POSITIVE POST-CONTROL EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE POST-CONTROL NET PROFIT
+ EXPLICIT POSITIVE TARGET OPERATING CASHFLOW
+ CURRENT LOW FTE OR LOW REPLACEMENT-SERVICE COST
+ BOUNDED TOTAL USABLE CAPITAL
+ LOW RECURRING EXPERT / FIELD / MANUFACTURING / AFTER-SALES DELIVERY
+ NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

Historical headcount is not upgraded to current fact. Parent-company operating cashflow is not attributed to a target unless the target-level table says so. Equity consideration is not treated as total usable capital.

## F1 — Zhuotai: a modest equity price does not mean a low-capital, low-labor operator

Lets Holdings' 2026 half-year report records a 100% acquisition of Chongqing Gaoxin Zhuotai Building Engineering Quality Testing Co., Ltd. on 2026-03-31.

Source:
- https://money.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12522230&stockid=002398

The acquisition table reports:
- acquisition cost: RMB7.0637 million;
- purchase-date-to-period-end revenue: RMB4.4814 million;
- net profit: RMB0.6734 million;
- cash flow: RMB2.1581 million.

That last field is generic **cash flow**, not explicitly operating cashflow.

A Chongqing High-tech Zone environmental-impact filing for the same operating entity gives the physical/labor packet:

Source:
- https://gxq.cq.gov.cn/hbslgg/202406/W020240621652520331403.pdf

It records:
- project investment RMB15 million;
- 3,679.89 m² laboratory;
- about 600 testing instruments;
- 140 testing items / 927 parameters;
- about 90,000 test groups per year;
- labor quota 66 people;
- on-site testing for exterior insulation, foundations, main structures, indoor environment, municipal roads/bridges and steel structures.

The EIA predates the 2026 transfer and therefore its 66-person number is **not** relabeled as current transfer-date FTE. It is still direct evidence that the underlying operating design is not intrinsically one-person/machine-only.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Hangzhou Nano IC: explicit positive OCF exists, but bounded capital and current low labor do not

GreatStar's 2025 annual report records control transfer of Hangzhou Nano IC Technology Co., Ltd. on 2025-07-14.

Sources:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225224529.PDF
- https://nanosic.com/about.html

The acquisition accounting reports:
- accounting acquisition cost: RMB138.3447 million;
- purchase-date-to-year-end revenue: RMB73.7647 million;
- net profit: RMB4.6009 million;
- **operating-activity net cash inflow: RMB7.3001 million**;
- investment cashflow: negative RMB2.7962 million;
- financing cashflow: negative RMB0.1556 million.

The OCF evidence is exact and explicitly separated from investment and financing cashflow.

The target is a specialized HMI/audio integrated-circuit and solution provider with chip products, algorithms, wireless protocols, middleware, applications and cloud-side capabilities. Specialized IP survives generic-agent substitutability, but the transaction is not bounded small-operator capital and the current acquisition packet does not disclose current target FTE or replacement engineering cost.

A 2016 public-transfer statement historically reported 44 employees, 25 in R&D. That number is used only to establish that expert labor has long been embodied in the operating organization; it is not treated as current headcount.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Gadlee: target-level reporting prevents parent OCF misattribution

Nanhua Instruments completed control of Guangdong Gadlee Green Cleaning Technology Co., Ltd. in 2025 and consolidated it from 2025-11-01.

Sources:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225166996.PDF
- https://vip.stock.finance.sina.com.cn/corp/view/vCB_AllBulletinDetail.php?id=12572038&stockid=300417

The 2025 acquisition accounting reports:
- cash consideration: RMB76.3832 million;
- prior stake fair value: RMB29.0250 million;
- total combination cost: RMB105.4082 million.

The 2026 half-year **target-level important subsidiary table**, not the parent's consolidated cashflow statement, reports:
- revenue: RMB52.9509 million;
- net profit: RMB1.2366 million;
- **operating cashflow: negative RMB4.1577 million**.

This is exactly the kind of target-level ledger visibility Scan 115 needs. Positive revenue and accounting profit do not survive the OCF floor.

The current parent report also describes Gadlee as manufacturing/selling/leasing commercial cleaning equipment and providing warehousing, distribution, training, maintenance and repair. It describes a professional after-sales team and service points in more than 30 domestic cities. Pre-control NEEQ reports showed 172 employees at 2023 year-end and 181 at 2024 half-year; these are historical context only, not current 2026 FTE.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Excluded observations

The wider screen also found:
- small automation/control acquisitions completed at the reporting-period end, with no usable post-control economic window;
- small technology targets with positive cashflow but negative post-purchase profit;
- mining-equipment and physical-manufacturing targets with positive OCF but clearly heavy production/capital delivery;
- securities/regulated-financial control transfers with positive OCF but dominant license/regulatory-capital requirements.

These fail earlier hard floors or duplicate already-closed capital-intensive formations.

## Learning

```text
POSITIVE TARGET OCF
!= LOW-CAPITAL CONTROL

LOW EQUITY CONSIDERATION
!= LOW TOTAL USABLE CAPITAL

HISTORICAL FTE
!= CURRENT FTE

PARENT OCF
!= TARGET OCF

TARGET-LEVEL SUBSIDIARY / STANDALONE REPORTING
> ACQUISITION-TABLE-ONLY RETRIEVAL
WHEN THE MISSING VARIABLES ARE OCF + LABOR + CAPITAL
```

The main retrieval lesson is structural rather than vertical-specific: acquisition tables are good at proving control and some purchase-date economics, but often omit current FTE and replacement-service cost. Acquired targets that remain standalone reporters, or targets that receive important-subsidiary financial disclosure, are a higher-value route for binding post-control OCF, labor and total capital to the same entity.

Scan 116 should therefore start from **ledger visibility**, not from a product category.
