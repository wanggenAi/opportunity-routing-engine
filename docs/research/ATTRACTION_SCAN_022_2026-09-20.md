## Retained research beacon

### ATTRACTION_SCAN_022-F1 — BATTERY_EXCISE_TAX_EVIDENCE_RECONCILER
Chinese: **电池消费税抵扣与减免证据对账器**

This is not a tax-advice chatbot and not a declaration agency.

The hypothesized machine role is narrower:

1. import eligible battery purchase invoices, processing tax documents and customs tax-payment documents;
2. import production issue/consumption records from ERP/Excel;
3. reconcile tax-paid inputs to the quantity actually consumed in continued production of taxable battery products;
4. generate the statutory battery tax-credit ledger and highlight unmatched or unsupported entries;
5. track exemption evidence: qualified test report + covered product list + accounting/invoice/SKU consistency;
6. produce accountant-reviewable declaration inputs and an evidence pack;
7. fail closed on ambiguous product classification, tax interpretation or missing source documents.

### Why it survives research attention

China's adjusted battery consumption-tax policy took effect on 2026-09-01. Relevant batteries are taxed at
2% now and 4% from 2027-09-01. Specified sodium-ion, solid-state, fuel-cell and certain photovoltaic battery
categories receive temporary exemptions, subject to the stated conditions.

Official policy:
- https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5251171/content.html

The tax administration rule requires a `电池税款抵扣台账`. Credits depend on the tax-paid input document and
the quantity actually consumed in continued production of taxable battery products. Exemption evidence also
requires a qualified test report and a product list whose names/specifications/models align with accounting
and sales-invoice records.

Official administration rule and explanation:
- https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5251620/content.html
- https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100015/c5251623/content.html

This is materially different from Scan 021-F1. The core work is not merely:

`private documents + public rule → drafted document`.

It is recurring multi-source reconciliation:

```text
TAX-PAID SOURCE DOCUMENTS
× PRODUCTION CONSUMPTION
× PRODUCT IDENTITY
× TEST/EVIDENCE STATUS
→ ELIGIBLE CREDIT / EXEMPTION EVIDENCE
→ STATUTORY LEDGER + EXCEPTION QUEUE
```

The official examples explicitly calculate credit from actual production-use ratios. That creates a direct
cash-linked operational state.

Public company responses also show the policy already affects pricing and internal management. EVE Energy
said it would share the new tax burden through the supply chain and improve internal management precision;
market reporting says some domestic battery pricing was adjusted to add the 2% tax cost.

Market signals:
- https://stock.stockstar.com/RB2026072100031257.shtml
- https://www.ithome.com/0/988/030.htm

These signals prove the tax matters. They **do not** prove standalone software willingness to pay.

## Why F1 is not a commercial candidate

Decisive unknowns remain:

- whether battery manufacturers/assemblers will pay separately rather than use accountants, Excel or ERP;
- whether Kingdee/Yonyou/SAP/local tax software already has or will rapidly add the same workflow;
- whether real invoice/production data can be imported self-serve without recurring implementation;
- whether the workflow remains deterministic enough to avoid recurring tax-expert judgment;
- whether a distinct operator asset compounds beyond public rules plus customer-owned records;
- whether the high-intent buyer pool is discoverable cheaply;
- whether liability and product-tax classification can remain safely outside the product.

Search did not establish a dedicated current product whose public primary workflow is exactly the statutory
2026 battery-tax credit/exemption reconciliation. **Search-result absence is not proof of absence.**

The operator-asset hypothesis is only a hypothesis: reusable adapters, versioned reconciliation tests and a
failure taxonomy. If generic agents or incumbent ERP products can reproduce the output after ordinary data
export, F1 must be demoted.

## Other mechanisms tested

### Generic AI-agent security testing — real money, crowded control surface

Current policy explicitly calls for agent testing, compliance self-checks, distribution-platform governance
and third-party evaluation. Government and telecom procurement also proves material budgets. But generic
testing is already being occupied by professional laboratories, cloud-security products, research platforms
and certification frameworks.

Examples:
- https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-05/08/c_1779979789523320.htm
- https://www.ccgp.gov.cn/cggg/zygg/gkzb/202604/t20260417_26423670.htm
- https://www.skdlabs.com/2026/news_0826/788.html

Verdict: **high demand, low generic white space**.

### CBAM evidence/verification workbench — real urgency, current incumbents

The EU verification phase creates a real evidence-chain requirement for Chinese factories, but specialized
Chinese platforms already sell CBAM monitoring, supplier collaboration, verification preparation and
reporting.

Examples:
- https://chinawto.mofcom.gov.cn/article/jsbl/zszc/202608/20260803641119.shtml
- https://www.carbonbridge.com.cn/pricing
- https://www.1mi1.cn/price/

Verdict: **demoted for current incumbent crowding**.

### PPE selection/compliance — mandatory, but generic layer

Forty PPE mandatory national standards took effect on 2026-09-01 and apply across production, sales and
employer selection/use. The obvious software layer is still mostly public-rule mapping plus product catalogs,
while testing/certification remains specialist work.

Source:
- https://www.samr.gov.cn/xw/sj/art/2026/art_4794bbfb9ccd455fadf67fcc870ea012.html

Verdict: **demoted**.

### Battery lifecycle traceability — mandatory, but control plane internalized

The lifecycle traceability requirement is real, but the national traceability platform itself owns the
reporting/control surface. A generic connector risks becoming ordinary integration work.

Verdict: **demoted**.

### Industrial robot safety automation — demand exists, expert-heavy

Mandatory robot safety standards are being revised, but the current ecosystem already centers specialist
institutes, testing/certification bodies and integrators.

Sources:
- https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=446A14CE183E4C78E06397BE0A0A8101
- https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=4469F04ADC9543C5E06397BE0A0A25C8

Verdict: **demoted**.
