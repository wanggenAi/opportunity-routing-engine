3. **B2B product-catalog web enrichment** — current buyers still pay for large-scale SKU/MPN/image/spec research, but Akeneo's 2026 PIM now performs live-web attribute research with citations and human review; commodity human pricing is also weak.
4. **Rent Manager AI lease abstraction/write-back** — a current $2,000 phased integration request looked unusually close to a reusable product, but Rent Manager officially lists Bryckel for the exact AI lease-abstraction/document-automation integration.
5. **Export documentation and trade compliance** — long-running paid specialist demand exists, but standard document creation is already automated by platforms such as IncoDocs; the residual is corridor/jurisdiction-specific trade expertise.
6. **SOC 2 readiness/evidence/audit support** — current $25–$75/hour demand exists, while Vanta/Drata automate evidence and continuous monitoring; the remaining work is architecture-specific expert judgment and independent audit/attestation.

This broad reset produced a cleaner split than the previous trigger/action scans:

- Some buyers still hire humans even though an exact SaaS or official integration exists.

- Custom implementation demand can coexist with an exact product because the buyer wants a particular stack, ownership model, workflow or bespoke integration.

The Rent Manager case is especially useful: a fresh, technically specific paid request still failed the exact-incumbent preflight because an official integration already offers AI lease abstraction.
