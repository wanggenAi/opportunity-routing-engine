## Search rule

Scan 044 required direct payer/repeated-workaround evidence plus a much stronger property: performing the work must itself create or strengthen a reusable operator-owned asset.

## Results

Six asset-producing workflows were tested:

- email deliverability seed networks;
- AI voice-agent call outcomes;
- cross-merchant fraud networks;
- intelligent document processing feedback loops;
- real-device/browser testing clouds;
- LLM observability/evaluation traces.

The strongest theoretical pattern is real: transaction or execution feedback can create a durable advantage. But the ownership boundary is decisive.

Cross-merchant fraud is the cleanest example of a true reusable network effect — and mature fraud platforms already own it at scale. Email deliverability's decisive reputation mostly accrues to the customer sender while established vendors own the seed network. Voice calls, document corrections, application test results and LLM traces are primarily customer-specific or confidential, so they do not automatically become a legally reusable cross-customer operator asset.
