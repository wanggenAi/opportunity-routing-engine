## Wave 1 design

Cold outreach should not begin with the full eight-question legal/technical checklist.
The first message asks only the three questions that can kill or preserve the
formation.

### Q1 — multi-rail comparison

With each rail separately authorized, may the partner display/compare current estimates
from multiple licensed recycling partners and recommend a route?

### Q2 — anonymous outcome retention + future use

After fulfillment, may the partner retain minimized non-identifying records such as:
- device/model/config bucket;
- non-identifying condition features;
- initial estimate;
- post-inspection quote;
- accept/reject;
- final payout;
- elapsed time / terminal state;

and use those records to measure channel performance and improve future routing?

### Q3 — contractual restrictions / disclosure path

Does the partner agreement restrict:
- competitor comparison;
- channel ranking;
- benchmarking;
- anonymous aggregate statistics;
- derived routing models?

If these terms are only available after NDA, enterprise qualification, or formal
onboarding, what is the required path to obtain the agreement or written confirmation?
