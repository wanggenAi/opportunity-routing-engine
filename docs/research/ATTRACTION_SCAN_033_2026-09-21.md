## 1. National data-property registration

The national data-property registration service system entered trial operation on 2026-09-11. First-day
applications covered software/IT, transport and warehousing, scientific services, health/social work and other
sectors.

Source:
- https://www.nda.gov.cn/sjj/swdt/xwfb/0911/20260911171614017938821_pc.html

The tempting edge is enterprise evidence preparation: convert internal data provenance, contracts, metadata
and data-governance records into registration evidence.

But the authorized registration institutions already own much of this layer.

Beijing's registration practice uses structured fields and intelligent prompts plus application guidance and
professional review teams for compliance, security and subject qualification.

Sources:
- https://www.nda.gov.cn/sjj/zwgk/zjjd/0914/20260914194221845015741_pc.html
- https://www.nda.gov.cn/sjj/zwgk/zjjd/0404/20260404140256480839323_mobile.html

Shenzhen explicitly describes a registration → circulation → assetization full-chain service.

Source:
- https://gzw.sz.gov.cn/zwgk/qt/pcgz/content/post_12989587.html

Verdict: **demoted — institution-owned workflow + professional review**.

## 2. Enterprise drone training / inspection support

Current enterprise demand is directly paid, not hypothetical.

A September 2026 Southern Power Grid tender seeks 2026–2027 drone training and support covering:
- certification;
- power-inspection training;
- training support;
- site/accommodation support.

Source:
- https://www.bidding.csg.cn/zbgg/1200443273.jhtml

Other current power-sector tenders bundle drone nests/robots with installation, route planning, modeling,
maintenance, personnel, platform development and insurance.

Source:
- https://www.bidding.csg.cn/zbgg/1200422681.jhtml

This proves payer pull, but also proves the delivered unit is a heavy bundled field service.

Verdict: **demoted — real spend, wrong founder-independent execution shape**.

## 3. Smart-home lifecycle service

China's September 2026 smart-home consumption action plan promotes community service stations that integrate
maintenance, storage, recycling/replacement and full-home smart experiences.

Source:
- https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/gnmygl/art/2026/art_10bdf8f3a8a14d0cb567fe7ec4013e0f.html

"试、装、换、修" full-lifecycle capability.

Source:
- https://tech.cnr.cn/techgd/20260916/t20260916_527815668.shtml

Verdict: **demoted — retail-channel internalization + field installation/repair**.

## 4. Concert luggage storage

Cross-city concert travel creates a direct paid microservice: luggage storage near venues.

A 2026 guide describes storage as a mainstream concert-support need already served by specialist storage apps,
smart lockers and logistics firms including SF-related services.

Source:
- https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1786784549129899.html

The demand is strong, but the asset is physical custody and local supply density.

Verdict: **demoted — current incumbents + physical custody**.

## 5. Night-tour / immersive experience demand

Chengdu night-tour product booking heat increased sharply in summer 2026; Sichuan night cultural-tourism spend
accounts for a large share of tourism consumption.

Source:
- https://sc.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0904/c345167-41686431.html

But OTAs, local-commerce platforms and venues already own ticket inventory, booking and settlement. The
experience itself is content/venue operations.

Verdict: **demoted**.
