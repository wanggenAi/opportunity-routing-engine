## Purpose

Scan 112 keeps Scan 111's normalized-cashflow and control-right floors intact and raises only the evidence priority: look for low-headcount or machine-light operating entities with audited positive external economics, then independently test whether a controlling equity/asset/license/contract transfer has actually executed.

The transaction market is not the discovery ontology. Current operating/corporate evidence establishes the formation first; public exchange disclosures are used to validate control rights, transfer conditions and execution status.

```text
LOW HEADCOUNT / MACHINE-LIGHT ENTITY
+ SAME ENTITY / CONTROL POSITION / TIME WINDOW
+ AUDITED POSITIVE REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NORMALIZED PROFIT / CASHFLOW
+ LOW OWNER LABOR OR REPLACEMENT COST
+ COMPLETED CONTROLLING TRANSFER
+ RIGHTS CONTINUITY
+ REALIZED / BOUNDED EXIT VALUE
+ NONCOMMODITY CONTROL
+ FOUNDER-LIGHT MACHINE DELIVERY
=> POSSIBLE VERIFIED CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

## F1 — Nandian (Chengmai): positive zero-employee cashflow is not an executed machine-control transfer

A current listed-company disclosure identifies Nandian (Chengmai) New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. as a wholly owned photovoltaic operating company. It reports 2025 audited revenue of RMB14.2077 million, net profit of RMB4.6791 million and operating cashflow of RMB5.0469 million.

Sources:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-06-06/1225355371.PDF
- https://ggzyfw.beijing.gov.cn/jyxxgqgpplxx/20260826/5686088.html

The current public-exchange packet reports zero employees and a 100% listing price of RMB82.1275 million. It also discloses a bank-loan balance of about RMB65.6523 million and states that the buyer must first repay or replace the financing on lender-accepted terms before the equity transfer and actual-controller change can proceed.

This is materially stronger than a low-headcount story: audited revenue, profit, cashflow and a formal full-control path bind to one entity. It still does not pass. The current packet is a listing, not a completed transfer, so there is no post-transfer cashflow period. Zero employees also does not close outsourced O&M, parent support, grid coordination or replacement operating cost. The asset's economics are materially tied to physical solar infrastructure, subsidy collection assumptions and financing.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Qingdao Zhongtian: outsourced operation lowers company headcount but does not create machine delivery

Qingdao local reporting states that Zhongtian's hotel property is leased as a whole to a professional hotel-management team for daily operation. It reports 2025 revenue of RMB2.8272 million and net profit of RMB1.4513 million. The first 2026 RMB100 million full-equity listing expired without a buyer.

Sources:
- https://finance.qingdaonews.com/content/2026-05/29/content_23737044.htm
- https://finance.qingdaonews.com/wap/2026-08/17/content_23751488.htm
- https://ggzyfw.beijing.gov.cn/jyxxgqgpplxx/20260903/5697695.html

The current September exchange packet relists 100% at RMB83.7811 million, reports one employee, and reports 2026-H1 revenue RMB1.991 million and net profit RMB1.1762 million.

This is founder-light at the corporate wrapper level, but not machine-native. A professional human team performs the actual hospitality operation, the public packet does not expose revenue composition or replacement operating cost, and the scarce asset is prime urban real estate. The prior failed round plus current relisting also means no executed control change is proven.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Yongtai Guozhen: strong concession continuity still does not close executed transfer or machine operation

A current operator procurement notice confirms that Yongtai County Guozhen Water Treatment Co., Ltd. runs a 10,000-ton/day wastewater-treatment plant and externally procures a year of monitoring/testing service.

Sources:
- https://new.ebidding.cecep.cn/cgxx/013002/013002001/20260815/eb6cc682-501b-442d-9656-c64eee052017.html
- https://ggzyfw.beijing.gov.cn/jyxxgqgpplxx/20260921/5722516.html

The current 100% equity packet reports six employees, 2025 audited revenue RMB3.2024 million and net profit RMB57,600, plus 2026 through August revenue RMB2.132 million and net profit RMB285,200. It also binds the company's principal asset to the Yongtai East New City wastewater concession through October 2043 and states that the housing authority has consented to the contemplated 100% equity transfer. If the transfer completes, the company remains the concession-performance entity.

Rights continuity is therefore unusually strong. But the transfer is still only listed, no post-transfer period exists, and the buyer must accept performance guarantees plus about RMB17.3791 million of related-party debt. Wastewater treatment remains physical regulated service delivery with staff, plant operations, maintenance, monitoring and compliance.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

```text
LOW HEADCOUNT
+ AUDITED POSITIVE REVENUE / PROFIT
+ 100% TRANSFER PATH
!= EXECUTED CONTROL CHANGE

ZERO / ONE EMPLOYEE
!= MACHINE DELIVERY

CAPITAL ASSET OR CONCESSION YIELD
!= REUSABLE MACHINE ROUTING CONTROL
```

The stronger retrieval boundary is now an **actually completed controlling transfer plus independent post-transfer positive cashflow continuity**. The next pass must also separate machine-operated control economics from returns that are primarily land, plant, grid, concession or outsourced-human asset yield.

This is an evidence-priority change, not a new vertical or product thesis.
