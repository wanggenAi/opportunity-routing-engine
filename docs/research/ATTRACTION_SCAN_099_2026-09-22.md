## Why the boundary changed

Scans 097 and 098 established that participant-owned credentials and digitally executable actions make delivery easy but usually make exclusivity weak: the same rights are available to generic agents, open-source tools and workflow platforms.

Scan 099 therefore changes only the **evidence priority**, not the product ontology:

```text
CURRENT CHINA PARTICIPANT-RECORDED FRICTION
+ INDEPENDENT OBJECTIVE CORROBORATION
+ EVIDENCE OF A REUSABLE OPERATOR CONTROL ADVANTAGE
  (control surface / execution right / data advantage / settlement rail / routing asset)
+ ADVANTAGE MUST MATTER BEYOND PARTICIPANT CREDENTIALS AND GENERIC AUTOMATION
→ EXACT INCUMBENT / UPSTREAM AUTHORITY / FOUNDER-INDEPENDENCE / MARGIN PREFLIGHT
```

## Result

| Formation | Non-generic control advantage | Why it still fails |
|---|---|---|
| Independent-software global collection | Merchant-of-Record settlement, tax and chargeback rail | Mature MoR/payment providers already own the rail; new entry requires regulated financial/compliance rights |
| Agent credential/programmatic-access governance | Secret-holding broker, app identity, scoped/short-lived execution | GitHub native controls plus OOMOL/OpenConnector-style brokers already occupy the reusable control surface |
| Team foreign-AI official access | Upstream service eligibility, contracted workspace and billing/admin rights | Eligibility is controlled by the upstream vendor; native Business/Enterprise controls already own authorized team administration |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — Global software collection and Merchant of Record

A February 2026 Chinese independent developer did more than ask what payment provider to use. The participant compared multiple providers, tried a domestic cross-border route, implemented PayPal checkout/webhooks and then integrated Creem subscriptions while continuing to investigate compliant payout.

Source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1193329

This is a stronger control test than another generic automation. The scarce object is not checkout code; it is the ability to accept global payments, settle funds, absorb chargeback operations and assume tax obligations. A Merchant of Record can genuinely change the feasible outcome.

But that exact operator-control rail is already mature. Paddle explicitly acts as Merchant of Record and handles tax calculation, collection and remittance across supported markets:

- https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales/

The participant is already evaluating several providers. A new generic intermediary without its own regulated acquiring/settlement/tax rights would simply sit on top of those incumbents.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Agent third-party credential and programmatic-access governance

A July 2026 Chinese developer documented repeated GitHub compromise and later Gemini API-key suspension. The participant linked likely risk to broad, long-lived PAT/SSH credentials used around an AI agent, then reduced privileges, removed unnecessary keys, moved toward GitHub Apps and short-lived installation tokens and added monitoring.

Source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1225440

A secret-holding execution broker is a real operator control advantage: a safe design can let an agent invoke allowed actions without ever receiving raw provider credentials.

However current native and third-party control surfaces already exist:

- GitHub organization PAT restriction, lifetime, approval and revocation controls:
  - https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-programmatic-access-to-your-organization/setting-a-personal-access-token-policy-for-your-organization
  - https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-programmatic-access-to-your-organization/reviewing-and-revoking-personal-access-tokens-in-your-organization
- OOMOL connection/action/member access control:
  - https://oomol.com/zh-cn/docs/access-control/
- OpenConnector credential runtime:
  - https://github.com/oomol-lab/open-connector/blob/main/docs/credentials.md

The operator boundary is meaningful, but it is not unowned.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Stable official foreign-AI access for a China team

A May 2026 Chinese participant reported roughly USD 200/month of approved project budget and explicitly prioritized stable, safe, official access over discount arbitrage. The proposed workaround combined overseas identity/payment/network components and internal redistribution.

Source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1210117

The decisive right is upstream service eligibility. A third-party relay, card, proxy or account-sharing layer does not manufacture an official service right.

OpenAI's current supported-country documentation warns that accessing or offering access from outside listed supported regions may result in account blocking or suspension, and mainland China is not on the current supported list:

- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663

Where service eligibility exists, native managed workspaces already own billing, seats and identity/admin controls:

- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792536-chatgpt-team-billing
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10011769

The observed willingness to pay is real, but the control advantage belongs to the upstream vendor or a formally authorized contractual channel.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

Scan 099 validates the usefulness of the new filter: all three formations expose control surfaces that are materially more defensible than browser automation.

It also exposes the next trap:

```text
NON-GENERIC CONTROL ADVANTAGE
≠
UNOWNED CONTROL ADVANTAGE
```

Financial settlement/tax liability, credential brokerage and service eligibility are strong because they depend on rights or trust boundaries generic agents cannot mint. Yet that is also why mature regulated, native or upstream providers often already own them.

One pass is not enough to derive a new mechanism. Scan 100 should repeat the same operator-control-advantage evidence priority on a fresh, formation-diverse China sample.
