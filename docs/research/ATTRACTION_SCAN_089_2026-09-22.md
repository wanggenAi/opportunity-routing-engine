## Search boundary

Scan 089 raises the discovery floor from “participants manually stitch multiple components” to **actual payment for the cross-component coordination function itself**.

A formation only enters this pass when:
- at least two unaffiliated participant accounts show actual payment to a distinct non-incumbent software/service for coordination, aggregation, search or sync;
- the payment is not merely for an underlying component;
- two or more existing components/control surfaces remain involved in completing the broader outcome;
- no single current control surface owns the entire end-to-end outcome;
- decisive completion does not depend on an incumbent privileged manual exception;
- incumbent/control, rights, founder-independence, normalized economics, machine-delegatability and generic-agent substitution are preflighted before any promotion.

No mechanism is inherited. Job/gig/RFQ/procurement feeds remain excluded as discovery ontology.

## Result

All three formations clear the new **paid coordination evidence floor**. None survives exact incumbent/control-surface preflight.

```text

!=
UNOWNED COMMERCIAL OPERATOR EDGE
```

The paid signal is materially stronger than manual assembly, but this first pass shows it can simply identify a mature paid aggregation/search/sync category whose authoritative action or data rights remain elsewhere.

## F1 — paid award-travel multi-program search -> airline booking

Current participants explicitly pay for Seats.aero Pro to search and monitor award availability across programs. A June 2026 participant says they pay for Pro and gives two concrete successful redemptions discovered through it; a separate January 2026 participant says they pay for Pro for Alaska/AA award options and alerts and reports one otherwise-missed deal.

The coordination payment is real. The end-to-end outcome is still split:
1. Seats.aero searches/alerts across programs.
2. The traveler confirms availability.
3. Points are transferred when needed.
4. The airline or loyalty program issues the ticket.

Closure:
- Seats.aero is already the exact paid coordination incumbent at $9.99/month or $99.99/year.
- Its commercial API is also an owned/licensed surface rather than free white space.
- Ticket issuance, loyalty balances and authoritative inventory remain airline/program controlled.
- A new generic agent can reason over results but does not inherit search-data licenses or ticketing rights.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## F2 — paid multi-institution financial aggregation -> bank action

Current Monarch users explicitly pay for aggregation. In September 2026, participants with 20+ accounts describe the approximately $100/year subscription as worthwhile specifically because it fetches and centralizes bank, card and investment data; a separate July 2026 long-time user says they are currently paying while evaluating synchronization quality.

The broader outcome is still split:
1. Monarch aggregates balances/transactions and supports planning.
2. Plaid, Finicity, MX and institution relationships provide data connectivity.
3. The user executes authoritative transfers, payments or trades in bank/broker surfaces.

Closure:
- Monarch Core is already a mature paid aggregation product at $99.99/year with 13,000+ supported institutions.
- Connectivity is an established provider/institution rights layer, not a generic-agent entitlement.
- Authoritative money movement remains institution controlled.
- The residual is reliability/coverage, not an unowned commercial coordination function.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## F3 — paid workout-data sync across fitness platforms

Current participants explicitly pay for HealthFit or RunGap to bridge workouts between otherwise incomplete platform integrations. An August 2026 Strava user says they had to pay for HealthFit solely to move third-party workouts from Apple Health to Strava. A March 2026 RunGap participant reports successfully consolidating Apple Health and Wahoo data into Garmin and describes the paid/subscribed path required for complete resynchronization.

The flow remains multi-surface:
1. a device or source app records the workout;
2. Apple Health or another source holds data;
3. HealthFit/RunGap maps and exports it;
4. Garmin, Strava or another destination remains a separate system of record.

Closure:
- RunGap is already an exact maintained sync incumbent supporting about 50 fitness services; sharing/export requires its paid Swag Bag.
- HealthFit is an established paid alternative for the same bridge.
- 2026 RunGap releases repeatedly repair Garmin/Strava integrations, proving the residual dependency is upstream API/compatibility control.
- A new agent does not inherit HealthKit permissions, platform APIs or credential rights.

Verdict: **demoted**.

## Learning

Scan 089 changes the evidence picture in one important way: willingness to pay for coordination is no longer hypothetical. It is directly observed in three unrelated current markets.

What it does **not** prove is that the paid coordination position is unowned.

This pass therefore does not introduce a new mechanism requirement. Scan 090 repeats the same paid-coordination floor on a fresh, formation-diverse set. That prevents one successful evidence pass from prematurely hard-coding a new theory.
