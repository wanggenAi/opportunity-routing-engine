## Purpose

Scan 114 tightens the cash-generation boundary introduced by Scan 113.

The key accounting correction is:

```text
PURCHASE-ACCOUNTING "CASH FLOW"
!=
OPERATING CASH FLOW
UNLESS THE SOURCE EXPLICITLY SAYS SO
```

The pass therefore requires a completed control transfer and attempts to bind the same target to:

```text
POST-TRANSFER EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POST-TRANSFER NET PROFIT
+ EXPLICIT POST-TRANSFER OPERATING CASH FLOW
+ LOW FTE / REPLACEMENT SERVICE COST
+ BOUNDED TOTAL USABLE CAPITAL
+ NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

Equity consideration alone is also insufficient. Working capital, receivables/payables, operating organization and replacement-service burden must be included before calling entry "bounded."

## F1 — Trillion Max: a tiny equity price can hide a large operating-capital structure

Keda Manufacturing's 2026 half-year report states that Trillion Max Limited was acquired for 100% control on 2026-04-01 and that the purchase date was determined by actual transfer of control.

Source:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-25/1225498065.PDF

The purchase-accounting table reports:
- acquisition cost: RMB0.2114 million;
- purchase-date-to-period-end revenue: RMB37.9226 million;
- net profit: RMB8.0812 million;
- cash flow: RMB6.1568 million.

The same report identifies Trillion Max as a Hong Kong import-export trading company.

The nominal acquisition cost looks unusually small, but the purchase-date balance sheet is not small: approximately RMB13.48 million cash, RMB54.74 million receivables and RMB83.13 million payables are disclosed for the target. The control price therefore cannot be used as a proxy for the total operating capital required to reproduce the business.

More importantly, the acquisition table labels the last metric only as **cash flow**. It does not say **operating cash flow**. Scan 114 therefore leaves the OCF requirement unresolved rather than upgrading the number by inference.

There is also no target-level FTE/replacement-labor packet, and the evidence describes an operating import-export company rather than a distinct noncommodity machine-callable control surface.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Zhongteng Microgrid: explicit positive OCF exists, but so does explicit human EPC delivery

Shanghai Holystar obtained control of Zhongteng Microgrid (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd. on 2026-03-10 and held 60.0053% after the transaction.

Sources:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-29/1225524861.PDF
- https://dataclouds.cninfo.com.cn/shgonggao/hsomarket/2026/20260127/46f0535127734048bde8b9630c66f31f.PDF

The target's purchase-date-to-period-end important-subsidiary table reports:
- revenue: RMB218.1016 million;
- net profit: RMB8.9512 million;
- **operating cash flow: RMB9.2551 million**.

That table explicitly states that the financial data cover the period from purchase date to report-period end.

This is stronger than the acquisition table's separate RMB49.7518 million generic "cash flow" number. The two figures are different, which is direct evidence that a generic acquisition-table cashflow field must not be silently relabeled as operating cashflow.

The economics still fail the commercial hard floors. The acquired 50.9323% stake was priced at RMB341.2463 million, the target's negotiated total equity value was RMB670 million, and acquisition accounting reports RMB402.0354 million combination cost after including prior ownership and contingent consideration.

The operating model is also explicitly human/project intensive. The half-year report describes microgrid EPC turnkey work including site survey, scheme design, equipment procurement, civil works, installation, commissioning and O&M, executed by project teams. This is a real specialized engineering company with positive OCF, not a founder-light machine-operated control asset.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Suzhou Lifu Hui: tiny profitable control does not solve the labor problem

Huali Family's 2026 half-year report records 100% control of Suzhou Lifu Hui Property Management Co., Ltd. on 2026-05-31.

Source:
- https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-29/1225526199.PDF

The purchase-accounting table reports:
- purchase-date-to-period-end revenue: RMB67,127.64;
- net profit: RMB44,800.61;
- cash flow: RMB45,596.37.

The subsidiary register identifies the business as **property management**.

This is useful because it removes one objection seen in prior scans: control-accounting amounts are tiny. But the result still does not survive. The table again says only "cash flow," not operating cashflow; the acquisition accounting is step/mixed, so a single headline price does not cleanly represent replicable total entry cost; and the operating category itself remains recurring site/service work with no target FTE or replacement-service-cost packet.

Small size therefore does not equal machine delegatability.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

```text
GENERIC CASHFLOW
!= OPERATING CASHFLOW

LOW EQUITY PURCHASE PRICE
!= LOW TOTAL USABLE CAPITAL

POSITIVE POST-TRANSFER OCF
!= FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL
WHEN EPC / ENGINEERING / FIELD DELIVERY REMAINS CORE

TINY PROFITABLE SERVICE ENTITY
!= MACHINE-DELEGATABLE NONCOMMODITY ASSET
```

Scan 114 closes one previously open question: a current-China completed-control target can have positive post-purchase revenue, positive net profit and **explicit positive operating cashflow** in the same purchase-date-to-period-end packet. Zhongteng Microgrid proves that.

The remaining decisive boundary is narrower: bind that same level of explicit OCF evidence to **explicit low FTE or replacement-service cost and bounded total usable capital including working capital**, while preserving noncommodity machine-operable control.

This is an evidence-priority change, not a product or vertical thesis.
