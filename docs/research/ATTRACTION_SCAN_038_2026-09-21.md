## Search rule

Scan 038 raised the bar from "residual manual work" to an actual structural control/data/action-right gap.

That was still not enough.

```text
STRUCTURAL GAP
+
NO OPERATOR-ACCESSIBLE RIGHT / DATA POSITION
=
NOT A DURABLE BUSINESS EDGE
```

A formation could survive only if the structural gap remained after incumbent preflight and an independent operator could actually obtain or compound the rights/data needed to execute the missing action.

## Results

Six unrelated structural gaps were examined.

### Agentic commerce authorization interoperability

AP2 explicitly splits responsibility among shopping agents, credential providers, merchants, payment processors and trusted surfaces. UCP, ACP, Stripe Agentic Commerce Suite and Mastercard Agent Connect/Agent Pay are already standardizing and bundling the same handoff.

Verdict: **demoted — structural gap is real, but merchant/payment authorization rights remain with control planes that are absorbing interoperability.**

### Privacy erasure propagation

Deletion must propagate across many downstream systems. MineOS already orchestrates automated connectors and tracks manual-only systems. What remains is mostly endpoint/API permission coverage.

Verdict: **demoted — endpoint rights are not an independent transferable operator asset.**

### Supplier bank-change trust verification

The data required to trust a bank-change request spans supplier identity, banking ownership, ERP records and payment context. Trustmi and PaymentWorks already occupy this edge with verification networks, continuous revalidation and risk-transfer positions.

Verdict: **demoted — the defensible asset is the incumbent trust network itself.**

### Demurrage and detention disputes

Carrier invoices, free-time terms, terminal events and container-return evidence must be reconciled across systems. AuditDray directly sells automated line-level audit and dispute generation.

Verdict: **demoted — exact cross-system dispute loop is already productized.**

### Cross-border returns duty/VAT recovery

Import declarations, export/return events, logistics and customs filings are structurally fragmented. Redo, Zollback, Valorra and related products already bundle return orchestration, duty recovery and licensed/customs filing.

Verdict: **demoted — the necessary regulated/logistics execution rights are already bundled into specialist control planes.**

### Dealer → OEM warranty reimbursement

Repair data originates in dealer DMS systems while claim adjudication and reimbursement rights sit with OEM systems. ServiceNow MCO now exposes a dealer portal plus STAR-compliant DMS/OEM claim and status integration.

Verdict: **demoted — exact bridge exists and OEM adjudication/payment rights remain with the OEM.**

## Main learning

```text
STRUCTURAL GAP
→ identify the missing right/data/action
→ ask who can actually hold it
→ ask whether an independent operator can access and compound it
→ reject if it remains captive to platform/network/regulator/endpoint owner
```

Scan 039 should therefore look for the rarer combination:

**structural gap + independent operator-accessible rights/data position + no incumbent control plane already bundling that edge.**
