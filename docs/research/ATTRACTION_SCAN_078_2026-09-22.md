# ATTRACTION_SCAN_078 — Executed non-incumbent residual-payment test

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Commercial promotions:** 0  
**Retained research formations:** 0  
**FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW:** NOT_PROVEN

## Boundary

Scan 078 raises Scan 077 from “the buyer still pays something after go-live” to:

```text
LIVE REPLACEMENT / PLATFORM
+ BUYER-AUTHORED SAME OUTCOME STILL UNRESOLVED
+ EXECUTED RECURRING PAYMENT
+ PAID PROVIDER / ASSET IS DISTINCT FROM THE PRIMARY INCUMBENT
```

Budget intent, solicitations, internal labor and ordinary AMS/OEM/partner support, integrators, consultants, BPO, authority-only, expert-only and physical lifecycle services do not satisfy the floor. No router, multi-provider, abstraction or other mechanism shape is inherited.

## Formation notes

### F1 — University Health Epic + Abridge

University Health's 2023 budget says Epic went live July 11, 2020 and that system/workflow optimization continued, including clinical-documentation improvements. The hospital district then approved Abridge Ambient Clinical Intelligence for $1,223,100 in April 2025 and an $882,700 amendment in April 2026.

This satisfies real repeat non-incumbent money motion. It does **not** prove an unowned residual layer: the public buyer record does not attribute the purchase to Epic failure, Abridge is an established Epic-integrated ambient-AI product, and Epic itself is expanding AI charting.

### F2 — Army IPPS-A + Whatfix

Army buyer updates show the system live at scale while training, CRM cases, job aids and workflow simplification continued. The official IPPS-A industry deck records a May 2024 Digital Adoption Platform award to Whatfix. That is materially stronger than budget intent, but the inspected buyer artifact does not disclose amount or recurring term, and the paid layer is the named mature DAP category itself.

### F3 — Durham County ServiceNow + Moveworks

Durham County documented employees waiting hours or days for support answers and authorized a three-year $271,578.95 Moveworks purchase to automate HR/IT/Finance/Facilities support and free the service desk. This is the strongest Scan 078 executed-payment chain.

It still closes. Moveworks is already the exact mature enterprise-AI front door for the residual. More decisively, ServiceNow completed its acquisition of Moveworks in December 2025. A distinct residual payment can be real and still be absorbed into the incumbent control surface.

### F4 — Ivy Tech Salesforce + WalkMe

Ivy Tech's own DAP account says the Health Sciences Salesforce application went live in March 2021 and generated 3,153 tickets in its first year. WalkMe content launched the following March and reduced comparable tickets by 53%. Ivy Tech's trustees approved a three-year enterprise WalkMe extension for $777,615.79 in June 2022.

This is a strong causal and recurring-payment proof. It does not establish white space because the residual is exactly the mature Digital Adoption Platform category WalkMe sells and controls.

### F5 — Riverside County PeopleSoft + UiPath

Riverside County approved an annual $436,235.06 UiPath licenses/services agreement in 2024 and continues large PeopleSoft HCM/Financial Systems licensing/support. This is useful as a negative control: two distinct software payments around the same enterprise environment do not prove they buy the same outcome. The buyer record inspected does not causally bind UiPath to a specific unresolved post-PeopleSoft result.

## Drift Audit

The scan did not inherit Scan 077's government-ERP failure examples as candidate priors. However, public board and procurement records make software complement awards unusually indexable, so the strongest payment evidence clustered around ambient AI, digital-adoption platforms, conversational AI and RPA.

That cluster is treated as a **finding about incumbent/complement capture**, not as a new ontology. Scan 079 therefore does not require a router, multiple suppliers, an abstraction layer or any particular product shape.

## Learning

```text
LIVE PLATFORM
+ SAME OUTCOME RESIDUAL
+ REAL NON-INCUMBENT RECURRING MONEY
!=
UNOWNED OPERATOR WHITE SPACE
```

The strongest new boundary is causal, not architectural:

```text
BUY THE DISTINCT RESIDUAL LAYER
→ DOES THE BUYER STILL DOCUMENT THE SAME OUTCOME AS UNRESOLVED?
```

If the paid complement resolves the residual, there is no reason to invent another operator layer. If the same buyer-authored outcome remains unresolved after the non-incumbent residual purchase, that is stronger evidence of a remaining control gap.

## Next

ATTRACTION_SCAN_079 starts from broad current reality and requires buyer-authored evidence that the same outcome remains unresolved **after** an executed recurring non-incumbent residual layer has already been purchased. It remains formation-diverse and mechanism-neutral.

Parallel validation remains ATTRACTION_SCAN_015-F1; no response is still neither a pass nor a denial.
