## Reviewed semantic alignment rule

Source-native concepts are immutable. For example:

```text
IDLE_SPACE_SCENARIO_REPURPOSING
IDLE_HOUSING_OPERATION_RIGHTS_TO_TALENT_RENTAL_OPERATION
IDLE_HOTEL_SPACE_TO_MULTI_USE_OPERATION
FORMER_KINDERGARTEN_TO_YOUTH_MIXED_COMMERCIAL_SPACE

IDLE_STATE_OWNED_HOUSING_TO_TALENT_APARTMENTS
DIVERSE_IDLE_ASSET_FUNCTIONAL_RESHAPING_AND_SCENE_REDESIGN
```

are not silently rewritten into one canonical label.

A reviewed alignment may propose:

```text
candidate concept: IDLE_ASSET_SCENARIO_REPURPOSING
```

only with:
- an explicit definition;
- a boundary;
- counterexamples;
- exact supporting claim refs;
- an alignment rationale;
- preserved source-native concepts and epistemic status.

The existing emergent-taxonomy diversity gate then decides only whether the candidate is ready for **promotion review**.

```text
PROMOTION_REVIEW_READY != TAXONOMY_PROMOTED
TAXONOMY_REVIEW != OBSERVED_PATTERN
TAXONOMY_REVIEW != BUSINESS_OPPORTUNITY
```

## Structure A — idle asset → different operating scenario/capability

Independent 2026 observations include:
- Xuzhou Metro: idle station/space activation through one-station-one-strategy operating scenarios;
- Jiangyin: idle housing operating rights converted into talent/rental housing and broader marketized asset activation;
- Nanjing hotel: idle meeting and floor space converted into training, exams, shared office, rental and differentiated dining uses;
- Nanjing former kindergarten: signed conversion into youth/social/cultural/commercial mixed use;
- Changzhou Tianning: degraded/underused edge space converted into parking, charging and convenience-service functions;
- Nanjing Gulou: idle state-owned housing converted into 100 talent/youth apartments;
- Huai'an Guolian: idle rooftops, kindergarten and land converted into PV, rehabilitation/care and solar-storage-charging functions.

These observations deliberately span different industries and end uses. The proposed commonality is the **transformation mechanism**, not a market category.

The reviewed alignment is expected to satisfy the current emergent-taxonomy review gate because it has multiple observations, sources, actors and periods. Most supporting cases remain `REPORTED` official/government reports rather than direct first-party actor telemetry, which is preserved in the assessment.

Even if review-ready, this establishes only that the concept may deserve a stable ontology node. It does not prove:
- the operator can access/control any asset;
- a private payer exists;
- the same transformation is commercially replicable;
- an orchestration margin exists;
- a Demand Pump exists;
- a regenerative business loop exists.

## Structure B — AI service → human escalation / synchronization friction

Run 001 contained consumer-complaint evidence about blocked transfer to human service. Run 002 adds the national standard and SAMR interpretation, which explicitly identify broken service switching, poor data synchronization, arbitrary task allocation and inconsistent service quality as current industry problems.

However, the candidate `AI_HUMAN_ESCALATION_FRICTION` remains intentionally weaker:
- too few distinct reviewed observations;
- insufficient identified affected Actor diversity;
- current support is primarily reported industry/consumer evidence rather than direct enterprise workflow telemetry.

Therefore its correct state is `CANDIDATE`, not `PROMOTION_REVIEW_READY` and not an opportunity.

## Policy/regime context is separate from actor recurrence

Jiangsu's urban-renewal policy explicitly supports converting low-efficiency idle public/SOE property and land into student housing, eldercare, childcare, community service, cultural/tourism consumption, community commerce and digital-creative uses.

This policy is useful context for why the asset-repurposing mechanism may be increasing, but it is not counted as an executed `BEHAVIOR` observation in the candidate alignment. Policy intent cannot manufacture actor recurrence.

Likewise, the existence of AI customer-service standards proves governance/standardization change. It does not by itself prove a user-facing friction event.
