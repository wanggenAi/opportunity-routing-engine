## Boundary

Scan 077 raises the Scan 076 bar from “recurring support exists after go-live” to a buyer-authored causal chain:

```text
REPLACEMENT / NEW PLATFORM IS LIVE
→ THE SAME TARGET OUTCOME IS STILL UNRESOLVED
→ THE BUYER ACTUALLY PAYS A SEPARATE RECURRING RESIDUAL CONTRACT BECAUSE OF THAT FAILURE
```

Routine AMS, OEM/partner support, maintenance, monitoring, enhancement, training, transition, implementation, authority-only, expert-only and physical-only lifecycle services do not qualify. No mechanism shape is inherited.

## Result

Six formation-diverse buyer-side post-live failures were tested. None survives the full commercial floor.

| Formation | Post-live unresolved outcome | Residual money motion | Closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baltimore County Workday + PRiSM | Workday broke the already-fragile PRiSM data path; monthly MBE compliance fell back to Excel/email/manual entry and remained incomplete | PRiSM vendor payments continued after Workday go-live | Strongest causal chain, but the paid residual is the exact incumbent MBE-compliance platform and the County’s own remedy is fix the integration or replace the software |
| NYC FDNY FIRES | Replacement system did not deliver required inspection/enforcement automation; Outlook, Excel, paper and manual processes continued | No distinct recurring paid residual contract proven | Fails the payment gate; unfinished functionality remains inside FIRES/Accela/GCOM and public-authority workflows |
| BLM AFMSS2 | Fully deployed modernization reduced productivity and could not become the authoritative system of record; paper fallback persisted | Lost productivity and internal paper work, not a separate recurring external contract | Strong failure signal but no paid residual asset |
| VA new EHR | Live at five sites with extensive workarounds, configuration backlog and unresolved usability/workflow issues | Continuing modernization/configuration inside the EHR control surface | Fails independent residual-provider and high-stakes rights gates |
| NDIA PACE | Replacement delivery completed but payment-validation controls remained rudimentary and highly manual | Internal/manual control work; no separate recurring paid residual proven | Fails payment, authority and sensitive-data rights gates |
| St. Johns County Workday HR | Workday is live while recruiting/equity/employee-relations work remains manual; department seeks additional tools/integrations | New-tech budget request, not executed recurring payment | Useful demand signal, but budget intent is not external value flow |

## Strongest evidence

Baltimore County is materially stronger than Scan 076’s generic AMS examples. The County OIG ties the events together:

- Workday became the new county financial/HCM system in June 2022;
- PRiSM and Workday did not integrate correctly;
- the MBE Unit lost its prior data-transfer workaround;
- by spring 2023 it was sending monthly Excel spreadsheets by email and manually reviewing compliance;
- the integration problem persisted into 2024–2025;
- PRiSM payments continued, including six-figure annual payments after the Workday transition;
- the OIG still found MBE compliance incomplete.

That proves the causal chain can exist in public buyer evidence.

It still does **not** prove white space. The paid layer is PRiSM itself — the existing off-the-shelf MBE-compliance incumbent. The County’s own recommendation is to make PRiSM work with the financial system or transition to a more compatible software system.

## Cross-scan conclusion

Scan 077 changes the discriminating variable again:

```text
LIVE REPLACEMENT
+ BUYER-AUTHORED SAME-OUTCOME FAILURE
+ RECURRING MONEY
!=
UNOWNED RESIDUAL VALUE
```

The recurring money must land on a genuinely distinct residual provider or asset, not merely:

```text
THE SAME VERTICAL INCUMBENT
+ PLATFORM REMEDIATION
+ INTERNAL MANUAL LABOR
+ CONSULTING / INTEGRATION
+ PUBLIC AUTHORITY / EXPERT JUDGMENT
+ PHYSICAL LIFECYCLE SERVICE
```

St. Johns County also shows why budget intent is insufficient. A buyer can openly describe manual work after Workday and seek hundreds of thousands of dollars for additional technology without an executed recurring residual contract yet existing.

## Next evidence boundary

Scan 078 should require all of the following before retention:

- the replacement/new platform is demonstrably live;
- the buyer itself says the same target outcome remains unresolved;
- the buyer has **actually paid**, not merely budgeted, for a recurring residual provider or asset;
- that residual provider/asset is not the exact incumbent, standard platform complement, AMS/OEM support, integrator, consultant or BPO category;
- the residual is digitally/machine delegatable rather than authority-only, expert-only or physical-only work;
- founder-independence, data/action-rights, normalized-margin and generic-agent-substitutability preflight still apply;
- Scan 060–077 formations and primary signals remain excluded.

## Drift audit

- Internal manual work and lost productivity were not converted into fictitious external revenue.
- Budget requests were not treated as executed payments.
