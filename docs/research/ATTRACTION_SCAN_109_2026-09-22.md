## Purpose

Scan 109 keeps the Scan 107-108 normalized-cashflow floor unchanged and changes only evidence retrieval priority. The pass prefers direct operator interviews/records and refuses to complete a return model with another operator's capex, category averages or vendor ROI calculators.

```text
DIRECT OPERATOR RECORD / CROSS-CORROBORATED IDENTITY PACKET
+ SAME OPERATOR
+ SAME CONTROL POSITION
+ SAME TIME WINDOW
+ EXECUTED ACQUISITION / LICENSE / CONTRACT
+ CONTROL COST
+ RECEIVED EXTERNAL CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ COMPLETE DIRECT OPERATING COSTS
+ OWNER LABOR OR EXPLICIT REPLACEMENT COST
+ RIGHTS CLOSURE
+ REALIZED / BOUNDED EXIT VALUE
=> NORMALIZED NET CASHFLOW
```

## F1 — Qingdao franchise restaurant: direct records can prove a real failure without proving a founder-light asset

Bandao's September 2026 report interviews the same operator from entry through shutdown and also interviews the contractual counterparty.

Source:
- https://www.bandaoapp.com/detail?newsId=1295526456457433088&type=1

The operator reports more than RMB1.5 million total entry spending. The same store then produced RMB600,000-700,000 first-month sales but lost more than RMB200,000 after raw material, rent and labor. Second-month loss was RMB30,000-40,000. Roughly four months after opening, the operator could no longer pay wages or rent and closed.

This is materially stronger evidence than a franchise calculator because entry, received customer money, major cost categories, monthly loss and shutdown bind to one store/operator. The counterparty separately confirms a one-year guidance-service contract.

It still does not pass. Exact owner hours/replacement management cost and a complete cost ledger are unpublished. Final residual economics remain disputed: the operator says RMB50,000-60,000 returned inventory was not reimbursed, while the counterparty says some service/franchise fee may be refundable and disputes the claimed total loss. More importantly, recurring restaurant sourcing, staffing, sanitation, pricing and promotion remain human operations.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Guangzhou paid study room: machine-light access is not realized normalized cashflow

Guangzhou Daily interviews an operator who this year rented a 110㎡ second-floor commercial space, converted it into a paid study room with more than 40 seats, and pays RMB5,700 monthly rent.

Source:
- https://news.dayoo.com/finance/202607/24/171077_54983023.htm

The room sells seat time plus lockers, printing, tea/drinks and custodial services. The operator says returns are satisfactory but management takes more time and attention than expected. The article gives preliminary annualized-return calculations of 15%-22% for seats and 25%-32% with add-ons.

Those percentages are not a realized period P&L. Actual same-operator received revenue, complete direct costs, owner hours, exact initial capex and exit/transfer value are not published. A separate RMB70,000-80,000 fit-out example in the article belongs to another operator and is not imported into this case.

The operating layer is also highly standardized: booking/miniprogram access, furnishings and systems are readily available, while libraries, community study centers and cafes remain substitutes.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Chengdu independent liquor shop: founder absence exposes a person-specific acquisition asset

Daily Economic News' September 2026 field report visits Maodaxiong liquor shop and interviews its short-video-blogger owner.

Source:
- https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-13/4580138.html

The owner says business depends heavily on her followers and falls when she is absent. In a month when she was busy elsewhere, store revenue was below RMB10,000 while monthly rent was nearly RMB20,000. The store has operated for more than a year, but the owner says apparent busyness translates into limited profit.

This is unusually useful founder-dependence evidence because the source exposes an actual same-store counterfactual: remove the owner and revenue collapses. The commercially meaningful acquisition asset is therefore the owner's personal audience/presence, not a transferable store-owned rail.

Initial control cost, normal-month receipts, full product/labor/platform costs, owner shadow wage and exit value remain unclosed. The article separately reports a >RMB500,000 aggregate investment across two Waimai franchise stores; that multi-position number is not stitched into this independent store.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Excluded observations

- A two-store Waimai franchise packet reports >RMB500,000 aggregate entry investment and no profit after hard costs, but the figures span two control positions and cannot support one same-position return model.
- Current cloud-unattended convenience-store retrofit evidence is excluded as a primary signal because convenience retail was already a Scan 107 primary formation.
- Promotional/SEO ROI stories for self-service carwash, pet-wash and unmanned retail were excluded because they do not supply independently verifiable operator identity and realized cashflow closure.

## Learning

```text
DIRECT OPERATOR INTERVIEW
+ REAL ENTRY COST
+ REAL CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ REAL SHUTDOWN
!= FOUNDER-LIGHT NORMALIZED CONTROL CASHFLOW

MACHINE-LIGHT ACCESS
+ PRELIMINARY ROI ESTIMATE
!= REALIZED SAME-OPERATOR P&L

PERSONAL AUDIENCE
+ REAL CUSTOMER PULL
!= TRANSFERABLE FOUNDER-INDEPENDENT CONTROL
```

The direct-record method improves identity continuity, but public operator stories still tend to omit at least one of complete costs, owner shadow wage, transfer rights or final residual settlement.

The next pass should therefore keep every hard floor and prefer packets where a direct operator record can be paired with an independent closure, settlement, transfer or other outcome document for the **same identity and control position**. This is another evidence-method change, not a new vertical thesis.
