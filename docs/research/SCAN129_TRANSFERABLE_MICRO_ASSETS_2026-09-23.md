## Boundary

Scan 129 consumes the verified Scan 128 continuation and tests a different control structure:

```text
SMALL OPERATOR-ACQUIRABLE ASSET
+ EXISTING INBOUND
+ EXISTING PAYMENT
+ MACHINE-AUDITABLE DELIVERY
+ TRANSFERABLE CONTROL
+ LOW RECURRING HUMAN LABOR
-> POSSIBLE RUNNING VALUE FLOW AFTER ACQUISITION
```

This is not source-code shopping. A codebase with no users or revenue is build inventory, not an operating value flow. A traffic site whose revenue requires constant publishing or sales labor also fails the boundary.

## F1 — Small automated cashflowing web asset acquisition

A current A5 listing offers the live **Shaibar / Aether Tarot** site for **CNY 5,000** and reports **500+ IP/day** plus income. The independently reachable site exposes fixed-price AI-generated readings, so the delivery surface is plausibly automated rather than a disguised human service.

This is the first Scan 129 observation that combines:
- a bounded acquisition price;
- a self-revealing seller;
- an already-live product;
- seller-reported inbound and income;
- automated per-use delivery;
- a marketplace escrow/handover rail.

It is **not** transaction truth yet. The listing does not independently prove settled revenue, paid conversion, traffic durability, seller labor, full IP/control transfer or normalized margin. The current site also advertises crypto payment; for a China operator this cannot be used as the assumed compliant settlement path.

**Verdict:** retain for exact diligence only. No purchase and no commercial promotion.

The decisive evidence request is narrow:
1. last 90 days order count, gross revenue, refunds and settlement evidence;
2. traffic-source breakdown and direct/organic share;
3. seller weekly operating hours and distributor dependence;
4. exact domain/code/database/prompt/content/customer-data/IP handover scope;
5. hosting, model and payment costs;
6. a compliant non-crypto payment path for China operation.

## F2 — Transferable App Store micro-app

Apple provides a strong ownership-transfer primitive. A transferred app can remain available, preserve ratings/reviews and Bundle ID continuity, and route future sales/payment reporting to the recipient. Individual developer enrollment is low-cost.

The missing element is not the transfer rail. It is a current China-side asset that is simultaneously small-ticket, already cashflowing, low-maintenance and compliant. The fresh low-ticket A5 app examples observed in this scan did not prove current revenue.

**Verdict:** demote current formation; keep the transfer mechanism as evidence, not as a candidate.

## F3 — Cashflowing content-site acquisition

Current A5 listings show sites with real-looking traffic and seller-reported income, but the examples explicitly depend on manual publishing, content updates, membership operation or soft-article sales.

Buying traffic is not enough when the operator inherits recurring editorial/sales labor.

**Verdict:** demote.

## F4 — Transferable WeChat mini-program operating asset

Current supply exists and an official/platform-governed subject-change path exists, but the observed route requires organization identity, payment rebinding, refiling and manual approval. The observed asset also does not prove a current paid automated flow.

**Verdict:** demote.
