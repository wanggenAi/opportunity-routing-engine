## Boundary

Scan 129 tests whether a small operator can acquire an already-running digital asset whose demand, payment, delivery and useful control transfer with ownership.

```text
SMALL OPERATOR-ACQUIRABLE ASSET
+ INDEPENDENTLY BOUND EXISTING INBOUND
+ EXISTING PROCEEDS
+ MACHINE-AUDITABLE DELIVERY
+ TRANSFERABLE CONTROL
+ COMPLIANT PRODUCT FORM
+ LOW RECURRING HUMAN LABOR
-> POSSIBLE RUNNING VALUE FLOW AFTER ACQUISITION
```

Source code without demand is build inventory. Traffic without trustworthy paid conversion is not a value flow. A product whose current form fails a hard China compliance gate is not rescued by low purchase price or automation.

## F1 — Small automated cashflowing web asset acquisition

A5 currently lists **Shaibar / Aether Tarot** for **CNY 5,000** and displays **500+ IP/day** plus an income-present flag. The seller describes fixed-price AI-generated tarot, USDT settlement and a distributor system.

The first version of this scan incorrectly treated `shaibar.com` as an independently verified live product surface. Fresh verification shows why that is invalid:

- A5 hides the asset website address unless logged in.
- A5 explicitly says displayed traffic/weight metrics come from Aizhan and are for reference only.
- The current public `shaibar.com` surface is an AI API gateway, not a source-bound tarot asset.
- Therefore a title/product-name similarity cannot establish domain identity, live delivery, paid conversion or ownership scope.

A second, non-compensatory failure is current China content compliance. CAC cleanup materials in 2026 explicitly include AI fortune-telling among improper/violative AI functions targeted for cleanup. CAC's 2025 AI-abuse action targets AI fortune-telling/divination that misleads users or spreads superstition, and its 2023 life-service-platform action explicitly targets paid tarot, fortune and divination services as problematic superstition content.

The listing also says distributors are responsible for traffic acquisition. Even absent the compliance failure, that weakens the hypothesis that the buyer acquires self-propelling demand rather than product infrastructure that still needs ongoing distribution work.

**Verdict:** `DEMOTED_CURRENT_AI_TAROT_ASSET_FAILS_CHINA_CONTENT_COMPLIANCE_AND_LACKS_INDEPENDENT_PAID_FLOW_EVIDENCE`.

No seller outreach, purchase, deposit or private diligence is justified for this exact asset under the current China execution lens.

## F2 — Transferable App Store micro-app

Apple provides a real ownership-transfer primitive: transfer can preserve app availability, ratings/reviews, Bundle ID continuity and future proceeds to the recipient.

That mechanism is useful, but the current China-side examples observed did not produce a small-ticket asset that simultaneously proves existing cashflow, low recurring support and compliant operation.

**Verdict:** demoted for lack of a current matched asset, not because the transfer mechanism is false.

## F3 — Cashflowing content-site acquisition

## New hard lessons

1. **Asset identity must be source-bound.** A marketplace title or product name cannot be used to infer a hidden domain.
2. **Reference traffic is not transaction truth.** Seller income flags and third-party traffic summaries do not establish settled revenue.
3. **Ownership must transfer demand, not just product infrastructure.** Distributor-led acquisition can leave the buyer with the same recurring acquisition problem.
4. **Compliance is non-compensatory.** Automation, low price and theoretical margin cannot override a current hard content boundary.
5. **The mechanism still survives.** Transferable small digital assets remain worth searching, but Scan 130 must require independently bound asset identity and existing proceeds before deepening.

## Next boundary

Scan 130 should search current, small operator-acquirable assets whose identity and existing proceeds can be independently source-bound, whose product form is compatible with mainland execution, whose delivery is machine-auditable, and whose demand transfers with ownership rather than requiring recurring founder distribution.
