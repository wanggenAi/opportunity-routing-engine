and client delivery.

Current platforms already attach the strongest available license evidence to their own generation chain.

and authorization status in a project authorization proof.

Source:
- https://www.tapnow.ai/zh/generated-content-license

Other enterprise generation platforms bundle commercial authorization with paid plans.

Source:

contract rights.

Verdict: **demoted — native platform proof + fact-specific residual legal risk**.

## Main learning

The delivery prefilter worked: it removed obvious human-service noise.

It did **not** create white space.

```text
DIRECT PAYER
+ DIGITAL DELIVERY
!=
UNOWNED CONTROL EDGE
```

Most digital paid categories are easier for software companies to enter, which also means exact incumbent
density can be extremely high.

## Scan 036 process improvement

Do not invent a narrower opportunity shape.

Instead, change research order:

```text
PAYER / WORKAROUND SIGNAL
→ DELIVERY PREFILTER
→ EXACT INCUMBENT PREFLIGHT
→ ONLY THEN DEEP CAUSAL DESCENT
```

This is a research-efficiency rule, not a product ontology.
