# Discovery Cycle 001 — Broad Opportunity Pool

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: `CLEAN-SLATE POOL / 38 SEEDS`

Generated from current money-flow, psychology/behavior and case-mechanism evidence. None is a validated business. Previous experiments do not receive priority.

## Pool rules

Every seed must be traceable to at least one:
- money-flow divergence;
- observed behavior/workaround;
- recurring asset/channel/workflow;
- successful/failed mechanism analog.

Every seed is later judged on payer, transactionability, trust/safety, delegatability, orchestration value and regenerative demand.

# A. Community / property / stock-housing flows

## S01 — Property-anchored household-service orchestration backend
Property is the demand front door; external providers perform bounded low-risk home services; orchestrator manages catalog, SLA, acceptance, replacement, complaint and settlement.
Demand pump: 2,237 Xuzhou residential communities / property relationships.
Possible hook: zero-upfront value-added-service backend + revenue share / resident-paid orders.
Key risk: property can bypass and contract providers directly.

## S02 — Move-in / move-out home turnover bundle
Deep cleaning + appliance cleaning + minor non-regulated repair + evidence pack before handover.
Demand pump: rental/sale/turnover events aggregated by property/agency/landlord portfolios.
Payer: landlord/tenant/agency/property depending scope.
Risk: generic services have low moat.

## S03 — Stock-home repair orchestration
Route bounded repair tasks with fixed quote/evidence/replacement.
Demand pump: aging housing stock + 23,679 H1 new/used home transactions.
Risk: electrical/gas/structural work requires proper qualification; start low-risk.

## S04 — Property-led idle-home management support
Cleaning/checking/minor maintenance/inspection report for vacant homes; exclude unlicensed brokerage unless properly structured.
Demand pump: property-managed communities / absentee owners.
Risk: key/home access and agency regulation.

## S05 — Appliance cleaning/service route through property
Air-conditioner, washing-machine, range-hood cleaning routed through a property front door.
Demand pump: installed household appliance base + seasonal maintenance.
Risk: ticket is low and providers easy to bypass.

## S06 — Community convenience supplier backend
Water delivery, laundry pickup, recycling, household consumables or other recurring low-risk convenience services bundled for property.
Demand pump: daily household needs.
Risk: margin too thin unless orders are dense.

## S07 — Community idle-space activation operator
Match underused community space with low-risk service providers/activities under property authorization.
Demand pump: recurring space + resident base.
Risk: property/owner authorization and low utilization economics.

## S08 — Community merchant/service revenue-sharing layer
Property provides distribution; local service businesses provide offers; orchestrator manages qualification, settlement and performance.
Risk: resembles established community-commerce platforms; must be narrow and local.

# B. Engineering machinery / industrial installed-base flows

## S09 — Engineering-machinery urgent spare-parts routing
Route verified parts/source availability for time-sensitive equipment downtime.
Demand pump: installed machinery base.
Hook: speed + verified fit/availability.
Risk: technical mismatch can be expensive; expert qualification required.

## S10 — Aftermarket field-service dispatch orchestration
Route qualified maintenance/service resources to bounded non-safety-critical work orders.
Demand pump: installed equipment lifecycle.
Risk: high technical trust/liability and manufacturer/dealer incumbent networks.

## S11 — Used engineering-machinery evidence-pack orchestration
Coordinate equipment history, independent inspection, photo/video evidence and buyer-ready data.
Demand pump: second-hand transactions.
Hook: reduce information asymmetry before buyer travel/commitment.
Risk: qualified inspectors and liability.

## S12 — Used machinery cross-region logistics/inspection coordination
After buyer intent, orchestrate inspection, trial, transport and handover.
Risk: high transaction value and claim handling.

## S13 — Remanufacturing lead / capacity routing
Route old equipment/components to qualified remanufacturing capacity.
Demand pump: aging installed base.
Risk: strong existing dealer/manufacturer channels.

## S14 — Engineering-machinery overseas aftermarket micro-operations
Documentation, translation, parts inquiry triage, public-source market research, dealer support and other non-core tasks.
Demand pump: growing machinery exports.
Risk: buyer access and language/domain requirements.

## S15 — Industrial service-provider overflow task router
Previously explored candidate; now one seed only.
Demand pump: recurring implementation/service projects.
Risk: subcontracting restrictions and direct freelancer bypass.

## S16 — Industrial public-tender downstream capability router
For already-qualified contractors only: route bounded supporting capabilities after lawful subcontract permission.
Risk: procurement and subcontract compliance.

# Explicit non-promotion / caution

Do not promote as first core project without redesign:
- medical companion/health advice;
- childcare/child transport;
- unrestricted elder home-care;
- safety-critical industrial maintenance;
- labor dispatch without licensing/compliant structure;
- direct government prime contracting without qualifications;
- generic `AI agency`;
- generic freelancer marketplace;
- generic local-services marketplace;
- subsidy-only trade-in arbitrage.

# Governing pool rule

> The purpose of the pool is not to generate more ideas. It is to ensure the engine compares structurally different money flows before committing the operator's time.
