## Scan-level new learning

Scan 006 exposed a further distinction:

```text
VISIBLE JOIN != ACTIONABLE VALUE ROUTE
```

Even when:
- A is observable;
- B is observable;
- the match can be computed;
- the value jump is real;

the route is still weak for the current operator when the actual action gate is owned
by:
- a government platform;
- a regulated exchange;
- the original marketplace;
- a grid / infrastructure operator;
- a statutory administrator;
- another incumbent that already controls settlement/allocation.

The current target therefore needs **callable actionability**, not insight alone.

```text
DISCOVERABILITY
+ MATCH RESOLVABILITY
+ DIRECT ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE
+ ACCESSIBLE ACTION GATE
```

must converge.

## Data worlds inspected

### 1. Shortage drugs × approved producers / distributors

Attractive surface:
- shortage events are observable;
- drug identity is standardized;
- approved manufacturers/distributors are enumerable;
- the consequence of shortage is material.

Kill:
China already operates multi-source shortage monitoring, production/supply warning,
provincial reporting and supply-demand coordination. Provincial procurement systems can
directly publish shortage applications and distributor lists.

Representative sources:
- https://www.nhc.gov.cn/
- https://ybj.qinghai.gov.cn/

Verdict:
`INSTITUTIONAL_ACTION_GATE_ALREADY_OWNED`.

### 2. Solid waste / by-products × downstream users

Attractive surface:
- resource has low/negative value at the producer;
- another actor may value it as material/feedstock;
- quantities, categories and locations can be structured.

Kill:
municipal and market platforms already connect waste producers, transporters,
processors and buyers. Wuhan's 2026 "武废宝" is a concrete example with more than a
thousand enterprises and hundreds of matched transactions.

Representative source:
- https://sthjt.hubei.gov.cn/

Verdict:
`ROUTING_ALREADY_PLATFORMIZED`.

### 3. Freight demand × empty return capacity

Attractive surface:
- both sides move continuously;
- location/time/capacity are naturally machine-resolvable;
- empty return is direct waste.

Kill:
digital freight platforms already perform real-time/second-level cargo-truck matching,
route planning, tracking and settlement.

Verdict:
`MATURE_MARKETPLACE`.

### 4. Construction earth / building waste × fill / disposal demand

Attractive surface:
- producer, transporter and receiver are structurally complementary;
- location/time/quantity can be explicit;
- transport distance strongly affects value.

Kill:
Jiangsu's 2026 electronic-transfer system makes construction-waste producers,
transporters and receivers register in the same information infrastructure; existing
local rules explicitly support public discharge/demand information and online
adjustment.

Primary sources:
- https://sthjt.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/8/31/art_84025_11822887.html
- https://www.jsrd.gov.cn/qwfb/sjfg/202608/t20260811_1350832.shtml

Verdict:
`REGULATORY_PLATFORM_OWNS_THE_FLOW`.

### 5. Rooftop resources × grid-open capacity × photovoltaic investors

This was one of the strongest cross-domain near-misses.

Data world A:
public roof-resource investment /招商 projects.

Data world B:
power-grid distributed-generation open-capacity data, often published by transformer
and line in downloadable attachments.

Examples:
- https://www.dmgzjg.cn/info/11112.html
- https://gxq.cq.gov.cn/ggtz/202604/t20260410_15604506.html

Why the join is useful:
a roof opportunity that looks attractive in a property/招商 dataset can be filtered
against actual grid access capacity before capital is committed.

Why it was not retained:
- grid capacity is only one feasibility variable;
- roof load-bearing condition, electricity consumption, owner credit, lease/control,
  structural condition and final grid-connection approval remain material;
- current developers already treat high-quality roof sourcing as core development
  work;
- public roof projects often already solicit investors directly;
- the join improves investment screening but does not by itself control the project
  transaction.

Current public company evidence also states that roof selection requires roof
condition, owner electricity consumption, credit and operating condition, and that
resource development remains difficult.

Representative source:
- https://paper.cnstock.com/html/2026-08/26/content_2259434.htm

Verdict:
`USEFUL_DECISION_JOIN / NOT_A_NARROW_VALUE_ROUTE`.

### 6. Tradable environmental/resource rights

Examples inspected:
- water-budget surplus rights;
- water rights;
- pollution-discharge rights;
- carbon allowances / CCER.

These have exactly the economic shape the project seeks:
idle/surplus rights on one side and constrained expansion/compliance demand on the
other.

But the strongest examples are already institutionalized markets.

2026 Ningbo water-budget surplus rules explicitly direct transactions through the
water-budget platform and national water-rights trading system.

Sources:
- https://qf.ningbo.gov.cn/qfpt/fwbk/zcfw/rmzx/art/2026/art_8cbdcac74c854a2896812366d26216a0.html
- https://www.cwex.org.cn/publiccms/webfile/index.html

Shanghai's 2026 pollution-rights market provides an even clearer example:
a closing enterprise released NOx capacity and new/expanding projects bought it by
public auction.

Source:
- https://overview.cneeex.com/c/2026-07-16/497931.shtml

Carbon trading likewise has national registration/trading/service infrastructure.

Sources:
- https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk06/202602/t20260209_1143900.html
- https://www.yicai.com/news/103289132.html

Verdict:
`HIGH_VALUE_BUT_ACTION_GATE_IS_THE_EXCHANGE`.

### 7. Unclaimed bankruptcy distribution money × beneficiary identity

This was the strongest **direct-money** near-miss.

The national enterprise bankruptcy information network publishes current notices where:
- a named creditor already has money allocated;
- the exact amount may be stated;
- the claim window/deadline is explicit;
- the administrator provides a direct collection procedure.

Examples:
- a 2026 notice for RMB 79,053.91 in unclaimed distribution:
  https://pccz.court.gov.cn/pcgg/ggxq?id=26C783E91657E5BCDCBCA1C4224BFA3B
- a 2026 case where administrators exhausted contact methods and still could not reach
  some employee creditors:
  https://pccz.court.gov.cn/pcajxxw/pcgg/ggxq?id=2ac93a8ee2ef4e7f9a8c40bc5236d3be
- a 2026 case with an enterprise creditor's RMB 61,975.70 unreceived distribution:
  https://pccz.court.gov.cn/pcgg/ggxq?id=F61CBC8F6A99C67EEC89FA7481BC3A52

Why it looked unusually attractive:
- join can point to money that already exists;
- amount is concrete;
- beneficiary identity is explicit;
- deadline creates urgency;
- state change is direct: unreceived → received money.

Why it fails the current hard floor:
- the statutory administrator already has notification/distribution responsibility;
- many notices explicitly say phone/WeChat/contact attempts were already made;
- some beneficiaries are unreachable, have invalid accounts, dissolved entities, or
  explicitly abandon the money;
- the beneficiary is often **not already moving**, which violates bilateral voluntary
  motion;
- monetization would drift toward finder/legal/claims-recovery service.

Truth:
`DIRECT_MONEY / B_SIDE_MOTION_WEAK / ACTION_GATE_ALREADY_OWNED`.

Verdict:
`NOT_RETAINED`.

### 8. Global public procurement × Chinese export supply

Data world A:
UN procurement opportunities are continuously public and categorized.

Data world B:
Chinese/global B2B supplier platforms expose large supplier/product populations and
RFQ markets.

Sources:
- https://www.ungm.org/
- https://www.ungm.org/public/notice
- https://sourcing.alibaba.com/

Why it failed:
- UNGM already provides supplier registration and procurement opportunity access;
- Alibaba itself already operates a large RFQ market;
- external cross-matching mainly creates opportunity alerts / bid leads;
- supplier qualification, bid preparation, local rules, logistics and delivery widen
  the bridge;
- operator value tends toward tender/lead service rather than control of the
  transaction.

Verdict:
`DISCOVERABLE_BUT_LEAD_GENERATION_HEAVY`.

### 9. Data assets / data products

China's 2026 policy explicitly encourages data exchanges, data merchants and
data-for-data/order/service/model/scenario transactions.

Source:
- https://xxgk.jcgov.gov.cn/szfgzbm/jcsdsjyyj/fdzdgknr_31650/fgwj/202603/t20260318_2331297.shtml

This confirms that "data as an economic resource" is real.

But generic data-market matching is itself a policy-supported platform category.

Verdict:
`META_MARKET_ALREADY_FORMING / NO_CONCRETE_HIDDEN_JOIN`.

- A and B are visible;
- both are already moving;
- match is rule-resolvable;
