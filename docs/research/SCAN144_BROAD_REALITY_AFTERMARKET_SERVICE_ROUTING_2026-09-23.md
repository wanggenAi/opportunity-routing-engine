## Result

Only **ATTRACTION_SCAN_144-F1 — Chinese industrial-equipment exporters with thin overseas service coverage -> local independent field-service / commissioning capacity** clears the initial machine high-attraction floor. It is **research only**, not a commercial candidate.

Current industry evidence is unusually direct. China's 2025 construction-machinery exports reached USD 60.23B, up 13.9%, spanning more than 230 countries/regions. The same current analysis explicitly identifies lagging localized service construction, weak Africa/Latin America coverage, long parts cycles caused by shipping from China, and insufficient local technical talent for onsite high-end-equipment fault handling. Current robotics-export reporting independently says industrial products require local after-sales systems that consume substantial people and capital and that exporters are building local talent/cooperation channels.

The complementary supply side also exists independently of a single job: Automate America presents a worldwide direct marketplace connecting thousands of field-service and commissioning professionals with OEMs and equipment builders for installation, commissioning, repair, maintenance, warranty support and training.

This creates a falsifiable connection hypothesis: a standardized manufacturer-authorized service packet could bind equipment identity/model/error/manual/parts scope, technician skill/credential/geography, bounded work order, service report and close-out. The value would be orchestration and trust/coverage, not founder repair labor.
