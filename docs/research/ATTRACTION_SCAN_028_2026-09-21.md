## Why this scan was different

Scans 026–027 showed that repeatedly asking "where is money leaking?" had become another hidden mechanism
ontology.

Scan 028 therefore began from **new observed behavior** rather than from a preferred business shape:

```text
CURRENT BEHAVIOR FIRST
VALUE MECHANISM SECOND
BUSINESS THEORY LAST
```

The scan found several real 2026 behavior changes. None survives current commercial hard floors.

## Behavior 1 — AI service light entrepreneurship

Xianyu reported more than 1.5 million relatively persistent light entrepreneurs, with service orders growing
quickly. AI service orders grew 157% in the first half of 2026; AI programming and AI comic/video services
were among the fastest-growing categories.

Sources:
- https://www3.xinhuanet.com/tech/20260909/59924c17abd54a2682f6a3f7280a257d/c.html
- https://www.ithome.com/0/983/021.htm

This is a real new service/labor formation.

The tempting micro-edge was independent automated acceptance of AI-generated software/service deliverables,
because buyers increasingly cannot personally inspect the code or workflow they purchase.

But the edge fails:
- development marketplaces already define source-code/functionality acceptance and dispute rules;
- the transaction platform controls escrow/dispute money;
- AI/vibe-code QA products already perform automated functional/security/review checks;
- contract-driven QA for AI code is an active research/product direction.

Sources:
- https://rule.zbj.com/ruleshow-0?categoryId=278&pid=280
- https://vibeproz.ai/services/smart-qa-for-ai-built-products/
- https://vibecodexray.com/
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15691

Verdict: **new behavior real, independent acceptance layer not retained**.

## Behavior 2 — ordinary people collecting real-world robot data

A new flexible-employment role has appeared: people record household and work tasks so embodied-AI systems can
learn real-world manipulation.

Hunan's industry authority described "embodied-intelligence data collector" as an emerging job, including
people earning money by performing ordinary household tasks for capture.

Source:
- https://gxt.hunan.gov.cn/gxt/xxgk_71033/gzdt/rdjj/202609/t20260907_34057802.html

But the formation is already being industrialized:
- CMROBOT offers professional and iPhone crowdsourced collection plus QA/data operations;
- Norda manages task protocol, participant/scene recruitment, capture, labeling, QA and delivery;
- TOBI sells ready-to-buy embodied datasets and custom production;
- EgoHub and other operators package field collection, labeling and structured acceptance.

Sources:
- https://www.cmrobot.ai/
- https://norda.works/zh-cn
- https://www.tobi.cn/
- https://www.egohub.ai/zh-CN

Verdict: **new labor/resource flow, but exact platform/service incumbents already exist**.

## Behavior 3 — AI agents hiring humans

This is one of the clearest genuinely new actor relationships observed in the scan:

```text
AI AGENT
→ MACHINE-READABLE HUMAN TASK
→ VERIFIED HUMAN
→ PHYSICAL / SOCIAL / LEGALLY-HUMAN ACTION
→ STRUCTURED PROOF
→ SETTLEMENT
```

2026 has already produced multiple agent-native human-execution marketplaces:
- RentAHuman;
- Human in da Loop;
- Taskin;
- Mundane;
- HumanInLoop.

Sources:
- https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rentahuman
- https://www.humanindaloop.com/
- https://trytaskin.ai/
- https://mundane.market/
- https://www.humaninloop.org/

This proves the behavior is not speculative. It also kills the obvious business proposal. Entering would mean
building another two-sided marketplace with local identity, worker supply, trust/safety, payment, dispute and
insurance operations.

Verdict: **new economic relationship, no narrow unowned micro-edge established**.

## Behavior 4 — one-person AI-assisted cross-border businesses

At the 2026 Hunan cross-border e-commerce fair, industry participants described AI as enabling one person plus
one store to approach the productivity previously associated with a much larger operating team.

Source:
- https://swt.hunan.gov.cn/swt/hnswt/mt/202609/t20260907_378817139055152000.html

This organizational change is real, but platform-native agents are moving upstream rapidly. Amazon's Seller
Assistant and related agents already support analysis, expansion, advertising and autonomous seller actions.

Source:
- https://gs.amazon.cn/news-center/news-center-260301

Verdict: **behavior retained as a learning, not a business formation**.

## Behavior 5 — lightweight experience/service consumption

Current service consumption is shifting toward:
- low-cost trial experiences;
- self-service leisure;
- longer integrated experiences;
- emotion/interest-driven consumption;
- light entrepreneurship around personal skills.

Meituan reported strong growth in experience-course searches and supply, while Xianyu's service transactions
and light entrepreneurship continue to expand.

Sources:
- https://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0914/c1004-40798527.html
- https://www3.xinhuanet.com/tech/20260909/59924c17abd54a2682f6a3f7280a257d/c.html

But discovery, booking, payment and merchant operation are increasingly native local-life-platform functions.

Verdict: **new demand pattern, no independent control edge established**.

## Scan result

The scan validates the behavior-first methodology but also reveals another important reality:

```text
NEW BEHAVIOR
CAN PLATFORMIZE FASTER THAN A NEW ENTRANT CAN FORM A MOAT
```

Zero retention is therefore the correct result.

## Scan 029 boundary

Scan 029 should prioritize behavior **before** a dominant control surface has formed.

Look for current:
- self-organized groups;
- repeated manual coordination;
- cross-system exchange;
- informal task/asset sharing;
- people inventing a workaround because no single platform owns the flow;
- real money/resource exchange already occurring.

Do not assume the answer is a marketplace, router, recovery tool, AI product or compliance workflow.

```text
OBSERVE SELF-ORGANIZED EXCHANGE
→ VERIFY BOTH SIDES REALLY MOVE
→ IDENTIFY WHO CURRENTLY DOES THE BRIDGE
→ TEST WHETHER THE BRIDGE CAN BECOME NON-HUMAN AND REUSABLE
→ ONLY THEN DISCUSS BUSINESS
```
