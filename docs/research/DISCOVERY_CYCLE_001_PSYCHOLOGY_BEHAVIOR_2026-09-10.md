# Discovery Cycle 001 — Psychology & Behavior Snapshot

Date: 2026-09-10
Geography: China → lower-tier-city context → Xuzhou overlay
Status: `AGGREGATE SIGNALS / NOT POPULATION SHARE`

This snapshot combines official money/behavior data with public research signals. It does not create individual psychographic profiles.

## Measurement rule

For each psychology dimension distinguish:
- `signal_salience` — how visible the theme is in available research/content;
- `momentum` — rising/stable/falling;
- `behavior_corroboration` — actual use/search/purchase/visit/transaction evidence;
- `money_corroboration` — spending/revenue/price/order evidence;
- `confidence` — quality and independence of evidence.

No social-platform sample is treated as representative population share.

## 1. VALUE_FOR_MONEY — HIGH / RISING

Evidence:
- broad goods retail is weak while selected services and mid-price technology categories remain resilient;
- lower-tier consumers are described by QuestMobile as simultaneously seeking quality upgrades and remaining price sensitive;
- 1000–2999 RMB capability band remains a major lower-tier consumption band;
- trade-in/subsidy response in Xuzhou shows large purchases become much more elastic when value is made explicit.

Commercial implication:

> offers should make the economic exchange legible: fixed scope, transparent price, low-risk trial, no vague premium.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2074385925022957570/
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202606/t20260602_8576126.shtml

## 2. SPENDING_CAUTION — HIGH / STABLE

Evidence:
- household consumption expenditure grows slower than disposable income;
- broad retail is weak;
- durable/asset-heavy categories are mixed/weak;
- small manufacturers remain below the PMI expansion line.

But caution is selective, not universal. Service/experience, convenience, information and selected upgrading still grow.

Commercial implication:

> avoid high upfront commitment before proof. Prefer pay-per-use, accepted-output payment, trial, subscription only when recurring value is already visible.

Sources:
- National Bureau of Statistics 2026 H1 income/expenditure and Jan-Jul retail/investment releases.

## 3. EXPERIENCE_ORIENTATION — HIGH / RISING

Evidence:
- national service retail grows materially faster than goods;
- tourism consultation/rental and culture/sports/leisure are double-digit service-growth categories;
- QuestMobile lower-tier young users over-index on movies/performance and local-life experience consumption;
- Xuzhou H1 tourism visits and consumption both grew >12%.

Commercial implication:

> experiences are real money flows, but opportunity quality depends on demand aggregation, differentiation, repeat and merchant economics — not on event popularity alone.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2074385925022957570/
- https://www.zgjssw.gov.cn/shixianchuanzhen/xuzhou/202608/t20260803_8586728.shtml

## 4. CONVENIENCE_TIME_VALUE — HIGH / RISING

Evidence:
- instant/local retail is expanding and moving users from planned stocking toward immediate fulfilment;
- local-life platform usage remains massive;
- Xuzhou property cases show residents valuing front-door water delivery and other convenience services;
- public procurement repeatedly pays for outsourced operations/maintenance because response and continuity matter.

Commercial implication:

> response time, one-point acceptance, and avoiding multi-party coordination can be monetizable even where the underlying service is a commodity.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2084487578531254273/
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2056581155403087874/
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202608/10/content_53962.html

## 5. TRUST_RISK_AVERSION — HIGH / RISING IN HIGH-TOUCH SERVICES

Evidence:
- household/home-entry services require verification and responsibility allocation;
- used machinery platforms emphasize inspection and seller/device verification;
- vehicle after-sales satisfaction is highly sensitive to price/expectation accuracy;
- property is valuable partly because it is a pre-existing trusted local front door.

Commercial implication:

> trust can itself be an orchestration layer: verified provider, fixed quote, evidence of completion, replacement, complaint resolution and payment condition.

Sources:
- https://www.mechandlink.com/zh/about
- Xuzhou and national property-service case evidence.

## 6. QUALITY_UPGRADE_SELECTIVITY — HIGH

Evidence:
- lower-tier consumers show stronger mid/high consumption intent despite value sensitivity;
- mid-price smartphones gain share;
- Xuzhou housing market language has shifted toward improvement/quality rather than pure quantity;
- property residents express willingness to pay more when service quality is visibly higher.

Commercial implication:

> `cheap` alone is weak. Better pattern: transparent quality + bounded result + sensible price.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2074385925022957570/
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2097181173927231490/
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202608/10/content_53962.html

## 7. REPAIR_REUSE_RENT — HIGH / RISING STRUCTURALLY

Evidence:
- second-hand digital platforms remain large/growing;
- Xuzhou stock housing transactions recovered;
- property lifecycle businesses report strong repair growth;
- Xuzhou engineering machinery explicitly promotes remanufacturing and second-hand equipment as a second growth curve;
- trade-in programs create disposal/recycling/installation flows.

Commercial implication:

> value is migrating from `buy new` toward extending, restoring, transferring, verifying and reusing assets. This creates orchestration opportunities around fragmented service chains.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2084487578531254273/
- https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2026/7/21/art_33718_11808010.html
- https://www.xinhuanet.com/house/20260814/cccc6a5bbfad4c29827284b956f51b35/c.html

## 8. EMOTIONAL_VALUE_SELF_REWARD — MEDIUM-HIGH / ACTIVE

Evidence:
- QuestMobile reports emotion/experience/appearance/health as important search/decision themes;
- adult `童心经济`, hobby and participatory experiences show social/behavior signals;
- Xuzhou youth-space cases show participation and merchant revenue movement under externally aggregated programs.

Caution:
- high social salience can be trend amplification;
- Xuzhou youth-shop success involved official/public-program intervention.

Commercial implication:

> use as a product/experience design signal, never as independent payer proof.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2038888455543558145/
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2061652786781634561/

## 9. HEALTH_LONGEVITY — MEDIUM-HIGH / RISING WITH AGE STRUCTURE

Evidence:
- 46+ and 51+ cohorts are a structural digital-growth group in lower-tier markets;
- Xuzhou has more than 2.2998 million registered residents aged 60+;
- local policy/service systems are expanding aging and medical-assistance services.

Commercial implication:

> large demand exists, but vulnerable-person/home-access/medical boundary makes this poor as a first unrestricted orchestration wedge. Prefer low-risk, non-medical services or institutional channels.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2074385925022957570/
- https://szb.cnxz.com.cn/xzrb/pad/con/202606/28/content_51967.html

## 10. SOCIAL_CONNECTION_BELONGING — MEDIUM

Evidence:
- youth/night-school, hobby experience, night-economy and participatory consumption remain visible;
- Xuzhou public youth-space experiments generated participation and merchant effects.

Caution:
- public subsidies/traffic and event novelty can inflate observed success;
- willingness to join does not imply willingness to pay a private organizer.

Commercial implication:

> treat as a feature of experience/service design, not as a standalone business thesis until real payment/repeat exists.

## 11. OUTCOME_CERTAINTY — HIGH

Observed across different markets:
- users value clear repair quotes and expected outcomes;
- B2B buyers pay for maintenance/uptime and accepted work;
- property/household services benefit from one-front-door resolution;
- used machinery transactions add inspection/verification because raw listings are not enough.

Commercial implication:

> `we introduce someone` is weak. `we define result + verification + replacement + resolution` is stronger and is aligned with the orchestration kernel.

## 12. AI_NORMALIZATION — HIGH / RAPIDLY RISING

Evidence:
- QuestMobile reports very rapid AIGC adoption in lower-tier markets;
- lower-tier young adults use AI as everyday work/study/content tools;
- older lower-tier users are also increasing AI use.

Commercial implication:

> AI itself is not the opportunity. It lowers the cost of research, specification, content, coordination and QA, allowing a small orchestrator to run a larger capability network.

Sources:
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2074385925022957570/
- https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2084487578531254273/

## Governing interpretation

Current psychology is best summarized as:

> **not `people refuse to spend`, but `people demand clearer value, lower commitment risk, stronger convenience/trust, and selectively move money toward services, experience, reuse/repair, quality upgrade and immediate outcomes`.**

Opportunity generation must still prove an exact payer and recurring transaction loop.
