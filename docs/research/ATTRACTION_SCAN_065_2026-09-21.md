## Search question

Scan 064 proved that parallel double spend is a strong willingness-to-pay signal but often buys human accountability, strategy, implementation, relationship labor or liability transfer.

Scan 065 therefore kept the payer signal and required the second paid unit itself to be primarily:

- deterministic;
- repetitive;
- machine-executable or tightly delegatable;
- objectively machine-verifiable;
- low in required human judgment and liability.

The scan remained formation-diverse and did not inherit a vertical or required product shape.

## Result

All six cases satisfy the stronger execution-shape floor. None survives exact incumbent/native-control and category-saturation preflight.

The common pattern is:

```text
PAID INCUMBENT
+ RECURRING EXTERNAL SPEND
+ DETERMINISTIC REPEATABLE OUTPUT
+ OBJECTIVE ACCEPTANCE
=
STRONG DELIVERY SHAPE

BUT OFTEN ALSO

NATIVE FEATURE / APP MARKET / SPECIALIST SAAS ALREADY OWNS THE LOOP
```

The cleanest machine units are often the easiest units for software markets to productize early.

## Examined formations

### F1 — Amazon Seller Central + third-party repricer

Amazon provides free Automate Pricing for Professional sellers. Repricer.com sells automated repricing at $99/$299/$499 per month with real-time rule execution, multichannel pricing and measurable Buy Box / pricing outcomes.

**Closure:** deterministic recurring spend is proven, but the exact loop is already both platform-native and a mature external software category.

### F2 — Salesforce + Cloudingo deduplication

Salesforce has native matching rules, duplicate rules and duplicate jobs. Cloudingo remains a paid AppExchange product starting at $2,500/company/year with scheduled and real-time merge automation.

**Closure:** the machine-verifiable unit exists, but it is already a long-running mature control surface.

### F3 — Shopify + inventory-sync apps

Shopify's current App Store exposes hundreds of inventory-sync applications with recurring paid tiers and real-time/scheduled synchronization.

**Closure:** objective synchronization is routable, but the market is densely productized with many substitutes and low switching barriers.

### F4 — Stripe + external chargeback automation

Chargeflow sells fully automated evidence collection/submission for 25% of recovered chargebacks. Stripe now ships Smart Disputes and broader built-in dispute automation.

**Closure:** objective settlement outcomes are attractive, but the platform is actively absorbing the same control loop while a mature specialist category already exists.

### F5 — Microsoft 365 + Veeam Data Cloud backup

Veeam sells Microsoft 365 backup on recurring per-user plans. Microsoft itself now sells Microsoft 365 Backup at $0.15/GB/month.

**Closure:** backup/restore is one of the cleanest objective machine units in the sample, and precisely because of that it is already a mature category with native platform entry.

### F6 — QuickBooks Online + Dext document extraction

Dext sells recurring receipt/invoice extraction and publishing. QuickBooks Online itself captures receipts, extracts transaction data and proposes matches.

**Closure:** document extraction is deterministic and repeatable, but the core unit is already productized natively and externally.

## Learning

Scan 065 falsifies the idea that stronger machine-executability alone is enough to expose new operator white space.

A stronger next floor is required:

> Find recurring buyer-paid deterministic units **before** they have become a named mature app/SaaS/managed-service category.

Scan 066 must therefore require at least two independent buyer-side spend signals for the same bounded deterministic unit, while exact native capability, app marketplace, specialist SaaS and roadmap preflight all remain fail-closed.
