## Search rule

Scan 039 looked only where the prior hard gate was satisfied:

- a real structural control/data/action gap exists;
- the customer can authorize an independent operator to access the relevant contracts, invoices, logs, reports or platform account;
- the operator can in principle execute the recovery/dispute loop.

That combination is materially better than mere manual-work evidence, but it still does not prove white space.

## Results

Six unrelated operator-accessible recovery formations were tested.

- **SaaS/cloud SLA credits:** providers still require evidence and timely claims, but Complaya already monitors 190+ providers and automates claims.
- **SaaS contract/invoice overbilling:** customer-controlled contracts and invoices make the loop executable, but multiple contingency audit/recovery products already sell it.
- **Parcel invoice refunds:** carrier-account access makes claims executable, but the category is commoditized across multiple contingency audit providers.
- **Commercial lease CAM overcharges:** lease audit rights and tenant documents are accessible, but deterministic CAM-audit products and specialist recovery services already exist.
- **CPG retail deductions:** supplier evidence and retailer portal rights are accessible, but AI deduction classification, dispute and recovery is now a dense product category.
- **Amazon FBA reimbursement:** seller account/report rights are accessible, but Amazon has expanded proactive reimbursement and specialist seller recovery products cover remaining manual windows.

## Main learning

```text
STRUCTURAL GAP
+
CUSTOMER-AUTHORIZED INPUTS / ACTION RIGHTS
+
DIRECT RECOVERY ECONOMICS
!=
WHITE SPACE
```

This pattern frequently converges into a mature contingency-recovery category.

The next useful timing edge is earlier:

```text
NEW / RECENTLY EXPANDED ENTITLEMENT OR RULE
→ MONEY NOW RECOVERABLE / CLAIMABLE
→ PUBLIC RULES + CUSTOMER-AUTHORIZED DATA
→ NO EXACT END-TO-END RECOVERY INCUMBENT YET
```

## Scan 040

Search broadly for recently created or materially expanded monetary entitlements, refund rights, credits, rebates, claims, reimbursements or policy windows. Reject old categories merely rediscovered with AI.
