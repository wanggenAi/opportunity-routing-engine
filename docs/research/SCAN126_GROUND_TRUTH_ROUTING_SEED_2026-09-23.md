## Formation, not product

The current evidence does **not** justify “start a sourcing agency.”

It does justify a broader formation hypothesis:

```text
REMOTE OVERSEAS PRINCIPAL
+ TIME-SENSITIVE CHINA DECISION
+ DIGITAL INFORMATION IS INSUFFICIENT
+ PHYSICAL LOCAL ACTION / OBSERVATION IS REQUIRED
+ REPLACEABLE LOCAL EXECUTOR SUPPLY EXISTS
+ EVIDENCE CAN BE SCOPED AND ACCEPTED
+ BUYER CAN PAY BEFORE LOCAL COST IS INCURRED
→ POSSIBLE CHINA GROUND-TRUTH EXECUTION ROUTER
```

Factory visits are one manifestation, not the ontology.

Current public demand also shows:
- production-progress verification;
- showroom/product video collection;
- shop/shelf surveys;
- test-center site audits;
- fair/factory visit support;
- sample/QC/consolidation support.

## Why this is materially different from generic freelancing

The operator should not sell personal labor.

The intended control plane is:

```text
SELF-REVEALED REMOTE INTENT
→ SCOPE NORMALIZATION
→ PREPAYMENT / PLATFORM ESCROW
→ CITY + TASK + DEADLINE MATCH
→ REPLACEABLE LOCAL EXECUTOR
→ CHECKLIST / LIVE VIDEO / PHOTO / DOCUMENT EVIDENCE
→ QA / ACCEPTANCE
→ BUYER DECISION
→ SETTLEMENT
→ EXECUTOR + TASK OUTCOME HISTORY
```

The reusable asset is the dispatch/evidence/acceptance layer and its outcome history,
not “I personally know factories.”

## The bootstrap correction

The repository previously risked conflating:

```text
FIRST FEW MANUAL TRANSACTIONS
```

with:

```text
PERMANENT RECURRING FOUNDER DEPENDENCE
```

They are not equivalent.

A maximum three-transaction / fourteen-day manual bootstrap is allowed only when:
- external demand is already observed;
- external payment willingness is observed;
- buyer money or platform escrow is secured before local execution cost;
- the task and acceptance evidence are standardized;
- replaceable local executor supply is evidenced;
- founder free labor is excluded from normalized economics;
- the target steady state does not require founder search or delivery;
- the task is not specialist certification masquerading as generic inspection.

Manual bootstrap may prove transaction truth. It **cannot** prove founder independence.

## Current commercial interpretation

This formation has substantially stronger attraction than a generic “build a SaaS and
see whether anyone wants it” experiment because actor motion and paid tasks are
already visible.

But it is not yet a commercial promotion.

The decisive unknown is whether the operator can turn fragmented public demand and
fragmented local executor supply into a repeatable prepaid dispatch/evidence flow
without becoming the permanent salesperson, inspector or sourcing expert.

## First bounded transaction shape

Prefer the narrowest lawful evidence task:
- buyer already has a target site/supplier;
- buyer states exactly what decision the visit informs;
- access is permitted;
- no technical certification is promised;
- deliverables are a fixed checklist, timestamp/location evidence where lawful,
  photos/video where permitted, unresolved-item list and written observations;
- buyer prepays or funds platform escrow;
- a local executor performs the visit;
- the operator handles scope, dispatch, QA and delivery.

Do not begin with end-to-end sourcing, negotiation, logistics or customs. Those
introduce expertise, custody and recurring service burden before the narrow bridge is
proven.
