## Purpose

Scans 103–104 separated scarcity from fresh-operator acquirability and transferability from verified economics. Scan 105 raises the evidence floor one step:

```text
ACTUAL CONTROL CHANGE
+ FRESH OPERATOR RECEIVES / LICENSES / CONTRACTS THE POSITION
+ EXTERNAL MONEY CONTINUES AFTER THE CHANGE
+ ORIGINAL FOUNDER IS NOT THE RECURRING DELIVERY ENGINE
+ RIGHTS / NORMALIZED ECONOMICS ARE EVIDENCE-BOUND
```

This is an evidence property, not an acquisition-market or asset-trading ontology.

## Result

| Formation | What is actually proven | Decisive failure |
|---|---|---|
| Gel Blaster brand acquisition | 2024 executed acquisition, 100% Chinese ownership, post-acquisition profit and USD 70m 2024 sales | Not a fresh small-operator position; original founder stayed CEO and the system requires factories, global teams, compliance, stores and continuous product operations |
| Existing parcel station bought by ordinary operator | ~RMB 60k actual takeover, 800+ parcels/day continued, ~RMB 6k+ monthly post-transfer earnings | Buyer personally worked roughly 9am–10pm seven days/week and later resold for a little over RMB 20k; cashflow is a purchased physical job, not founder-light margin |
| Independent developer sells source code | USD 1,200 executed source-code payment | Seller transaction revenue is not buyer post-transfer customer cashflow; exclusive copyright transfer and exact rights package are unproven, and the function is generic-agent reproducible |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — Chinese manufacturer acquires Gel Blaster

Amazon Global Selling's current China seller case documents a 2024 acquisition of the former US customer brand Gel Blaster. The case states that the brand became 100% Chinese-owned while the original founder stayed CEO to preserve operating continuity.

Official source:
- https://globalselling.amazon.com/sellerstory/home/gel-blaster

The same case reports that the acquired brand had reached USD 50 million sales in 2022; after the acquisition and dual-brand restructuring, the Chinese operator's enterprise reached USD 70 million in 2024 sales and returned to profit. By July 2025 it reported 54 US experience stores and 500,000 users accumulated in six months.

This is exceptionally strong proof that a non-commodity position can actually change hands and keep producing external value. But it fails the project boundary in exactly the opposite direction from Scan 104: the control package is too organization-heavy. The acquirer already controlled manufacturing capability, had acquired a licensed factory, operated separate China/US teams, and the original founder remained CEO. Physical compliance, stores, product design, marketing and 3–4 product releases per year remain material.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Ordinary buyer takes over an existing parcel station

China News Weekly reported in January 2026 that Cheng Si bought a parcel station in September 2025 for nearly RMB 60,000. It already handled more than 800 parcels per day. After takeover, the flow continued and she reported about RMB 6,000-plus per month after rent, technology-service and utility costs.

Participant/current case:
- https://finance.sina.cn/stock/jdts/2026-01-02/detail-inhewuuv4694417.d.html

This is the cleanest small-operator transfer in the pass. The buyer reviewed prior parcel-flow data, investigated the neighborhood and then actually took control.

But the money was attached to a full-time physical job. The operator described repeated scanning, labeling, shelving, customer pickup/search/verification and late calls, with a roughly 9am–10pm seven-day schedule and occasional help from retired parents. She started planning to leave after just over a month and ultimately resold for a little over RMB 20,000.

The Xuzhou postal regulator's January 2026 risk notice independently confirms the cost and control structure: station economics depend on rent, labor, equipment, parcel compensation, parcel volume, competition and service quality; operator/responsible-person changes require timely filing.

Official local corroboration:
- https://js.spb.gov.cn/jssyzglj/c102434/c102436/202601/152cdbcb846a4b0a90e61a27fe94ac7c.shtml

Once owner shadow wage and roughly RMB 40,000 capital loss are recognized, the reported monthly cashflow does not establish founder-light normalized return.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Independent developer sells source code for USD 1,200

A China independent developer reported on 2026-07-10 that a content-marketing agency bought the source code of a Markdown-to-Twitter-thread script for USD 1,200.

Participant source:
- https://www.w2solo.com/topics/7712

This proves a real small software-asset transaction. It does **not** prove the Scan 105 condition that the buyer continued to receive external customer payments after the transfer. The post does not identify the buyer's downstream product, customer revenue, retention or margin.

It also matters that "bought the source code" is not automatically the same as receiving exclusive software copyright. China's current Copyright Law treats computer software as a protected work, permits economic rights to be licensed/transferred, and requires a written contract specifying the transferred rights, territorial scope and price. Rights not expressly transferred do not silently move.

Official legal source:
- https://www.ncac.gov.cn/xxfb/flfg/flfg_532/202103/t20210309_50530.html

No such contract is public for this transaction. The underlying text transformation is also readily reproduced by generic agents.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

Scan 105 isolates three separate false positives:

```text
EXECUTED ACQUISITION + POST-TRANSFER REVENUE
BUT LARGE ORGANIZATION / LEGACY MANAGEMENT REQUIRED
!= FRESH SMALL-OPERATOR EDGE

EXECUTED SMALL-BUSINESS TRANSFER + POST-TRANSFER CASHFLOW
BUT BUYER MUST PERSONALLY WORK FULL-TIME
!= FOUNDER-LIGHT ASSET

SELLER RECEIVES TRANSFER PRICE
WITHOUT BUYER POST-TRANSFER CUSTOMER CASHFLOW
!= ECONOMIC CONTINUITY
```

The next pass should **repeat** the executed-transfer + post-transfer-continuity floor on fresh formations rather than turn one sample into a new acquisition ontology. It makes one existing guardrail explicit: recurring full-time labor by the new operator is also a founder-independence failure, not merely recurring labor by the old founder.
