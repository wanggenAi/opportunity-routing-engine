## What is now evidenced

The field is real. Current buyers are paying for bounded China-local observation/evidence tasks, including retail shelf surveys, predefined site audits, showroom capture and factory verification. Upwork fixed-price milestones can be funded before work begins, so a legitimate buyer-money-before-local-cost path exists.

China-side replaceable supply is also real. The prior Scan 126 seed already persisted a field-execution network with standardized task briefs, location/time constraints, evidence QA and public worker payout bands. WeiChaiShi independently demonstrates nationwide crowdsourced retail audit/data collection with QA.

That is enough to stop treating demand and executor existence as the main unknowns.

## What is not evidenced

Raw price spread is not normalized margin.

Using the current research FX snapshot (USD/CNY ~6.701) and the conservative Upwork freelancer-fee ceiling of 15%:

- a USD100 predefined site-audit budget leaves ~RMB569.6 after platform fee; comparing it with the public RMB80-200 short-video/multi-point band shows screening headroom, but the audit includes staff interaction and an English predefined report, so that band is **not yet a same-scope executable cost quote**;
- a USD25 accepted retail-shop visit leaves ~RMB142.4 after fee; against RMB30-80 basic-photo payout, raw headroom is ~RMB62.4 at the high payout bound;
- a USD4 GPS-photo task leaves only ~RMB22.8 after fee, below the RMB30 public basic-photo payout floor before travel, so it is rejected.

Travel, QA, rework, failed-visit and city-specific executor costs are not yet bounded. Therefore the USD25 and USD100 examples are **positive-headroom signals, not normalized margin proof**.

## Scope boundary

Potential first bootstrap class, if incumbent preflight survives:

1. retail shelf / shop photo evidence.

Conditional pending same-scope executor quotes:
- predefined nontechnical commercial-site observation;
- showroom photo/video capture where staff questions remain bounded.

Excluded from the generic bootstrap:
- factory production/QC judgment;
- sample-quality inspection;
- licensed or professional audit;
- end-to-end sourcing/negotiation;
- custody-heavy logistics;
- private/sensitive surveillance.

The USD200 factory-verification job cannot borrow the generic RMB80-200 crowd payout as its cost basis. Its factory access, production judgment and management interaction are materially different work.

## Payment and delegation boundary

Upwork can provide pre-funded fixed-price milestones. However, an individual freelancer cannot silently subcontract work: client consent is required. A transparent agency arrangement is another legitimate multi-person path.

Therefore:

```text
FUNDED MILESTONE
!= AUTHORITY TO HIDE A LOCAL SUBCONTRACTOR
```

Any bootstrap that dispatches a replaceable executor must have an explicit authorized delegation path.

## Exact incumbent threat

The broad formation is not white space by default.

- ChinaLocalAgent already sells defined China-local execution to overseas clients, including site visits, permitted photo/video evidence, checklists and reporting.
- Upwork already owns buyer discovery, funded fixed-price contracts and direct matching to China-based workers.
- WeiChaiShi already owns a nationwide crowd + retail-audit/data-collection + QA control surface.
- UU Paotui now exposes machine-callable physical errand primitives through MCP/CLI/Agent Skill/OpenAPI, although evidence-specific capture/acceptance is not proven on that rail.

The only remaining plausible independent edge is narrower:

```text
MACHINE-NATIVE CROSS-BORDER SCOPE NORMALIZATION
+ LAWFUL REPLACEABLE LOCAL DISPATCH
+ STANDARDIZED EVIDENCE ACCEPTANCE
+ POSITIVE NORMALIZED ECONOMICS
```

That edge is **not proven**.
