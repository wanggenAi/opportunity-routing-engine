# Attraction Scan 054 — 2026-09-21

## Boundary

Scan 054 started from six narrow exception classes where the remediation looked fixed, the economic settlement was directly observable, and a callable action path appeared plausible. It excluded broad operations roles, regulated signatures, Scan 053 vertical inheritance, and any formation whose decisive outcome depended on discretionary platform approval.

## Result

**Commercial promotions: 0. Retained research formations: 0. FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW: NOT_PROVEN.**

The scan tested six formation-diverse fixed-remediation money loops:

1. **Pre-chargeback alert auto-refund** — a crisp network event and API action exist, but Chargeflow, Kount and Justt already own the alert→refund/outcome loop.
2. **Stripe duplicate-charge refund** — Stripe exposes refunds and idempotency; Macha already executes the exact support workflow end to end, while idempotency removes the root exception upstream.
3. **Supplier duplicate-payment recovery** — direct recoveries and success-fee economics are real, but Recoup and mature recovery firms already automate detection/claims, and final cash recovery still depends on the supplier or an existing offset right.
4. **Amazon FBA residual reimbursement** — Amazon proactively reimburses most standard loss/damage cases; residual manual cases remain Amazon-approved and are already served by dense reimbursement tooling.
5. **Google Ads invalid-traffic credits** — Google automatically filters/credits much of the category and owns the investigation/credit decision for residual claims; third-party automated claim products already exist.
6. **Cloud SLA service credits** — outage thresholds and credit schedules are machine-readable, but provider confirmation is still required and Next Signal already sells success-fee automation for the exact recovery loop.

## Structural finding

```text
SINGLE EXCEPTION
+
FIXED REMEDIATION
+
CALLABLE API
+
DIRECT ECONOMIC SETTLEMENT
!=
UNOWNED OPERATOR CONTROL
```

The tighter the remediation and settlement become, the faster the category tends to converge on one of three endpoints: an exact control product, native platform automation, or a platform/counterparty approval boundary. In preventable payment exceptions, the value may collapse further upstream into idempotency or native control logic.
