# ATTRACTION_SCAN_120 — Current Low-FTE Positive-Profit Positive-OCF First, Then Reverse-Check Control

Date: 2026-09-23  
Status: COMPLETE — zero retained formations / zero commercial promotions

## Why this scan exists

Scan 119 showed that acquisition-first research wastes effort on many low-headcount targets that immediately fail current profit, operating cashflow, execution or control-window gates. Scan 120 therefore changes **retrieval order only**:

```text
CURRENT SAME-ENTITY REPORT
+ CURRENT / LATEST DIRECTLY DISCLOSED LOW FTE
+ POSITIVE EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ UNAMBIGUOUS POSITIVE OCF
THEN:
  COMPLETED FRESH-OPERATOR-REPRODUCIBLE CONTROL BEFORE REPORTING PERIOD
  + EXISTING STANDARDIZED REPEATABLE NON-PROJECT REVENUE
  + BOUNDED CONTROL / OPERATING / WORKING CAPITAL
  + NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE VERIFIED FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

The commercial constitution did not change.

## F1 — Tianlu Huaxin (831951): the closest Scan 120 packet

This formation survives substantially farther than the Scan 119 cases.

- Current 2026 H1 employee count: **10**.
- Current revenue: **RMB3,045,201.52**.
- Current attributable net profit: **RMB2,318,577.59**.
- Current operating cashflow: **RMB3,886,851.84**.
- Current total assets: about **RMB15.525m**.
- Wen Xianguang entered from zero ownership in 2024. A 2024-02-06 agreement covered **6,058,500 shares / 35%** for **RMB1.1511m**.
- The 2024 annual report identifies Wen Xianguang as controlling shareholder and actual controller with 35%, so the transfer was completed no later than 2024 year-end and cleanly predates 2026 H1.

### Why it still fails

The current operating model is not a founder-light control rail. The disclosed business is business promotion, enterprise-management consulting and commercial services, combining one-time project fees with annual consulting arrangements. Current strategy also explicitly uses controlling-shareholder/controller resources, while newer agricultural supply-chain activity introduces physical procurement and working-capital requirements.

**DEMOTED:** strong current economics + fresh control are real, but project consulting, relationship acquisition, controller-resource dependence and physical supply-chain execution remain direct operating inputs.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The important result is that the Scan 120 retrieval inversion **worked operationally**:

1. It found Tianlu Huaxin, which passed current low-FTE, positive-revenue, positive-profit, positive-OCF and completed pre-period fresh-control gates together.
2. It found Huajin Media, which passed current economics but immediately failed the fresh-operator-control test.
3. It rejected Jiazhi 3 before control research because positive OCF was not backed by current customer revenue or profit.

The remaining bottleneck is not simply finding low-headcount cash-generative entities. It is finding one where the control position itself is freshly reproducible **and** the operating cashflow is not dependent on project consulting, founder/controller relationships, media-resource procurement, physical supply-chain work, or another human/contract-heavy delivery engine.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 121

Repeat the **same current-economics-first retrieval order on fresh formations** before narrowing the boundary again.

This is deliberate. One near-survivor is useful evidence, but not enough to conclude that a new product/vertical ontology should be created.

Scan 121 therefore preserves:

- current same-entity report;
- directly disclosed intrinsically low current/latest FTE;
- positive external revenue;
- positive net profit;
- unambiguous positive OCF;
- only then reverse-check completed fresh-operator-reproducible control;
- standardized repeatable non-project revenue;
- bounded control, operating and working capital;
- noncommodity machine-operable control;
- fail closed on project consulting, outsourcing, field work, media buying, manufacturing, after-sales and pending injections.
