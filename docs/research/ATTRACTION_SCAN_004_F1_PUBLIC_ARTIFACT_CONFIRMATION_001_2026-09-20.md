## Real current China-side case

A July 2026 procurement notice from Shaanxi Yanchang China Coal Yulin records a
specific old-equipment repair situation.

The procurement note states:
- the D-type filter inlet electric valve has internal mechanical wear and cannot be
  repaired;
- the original valve is an old complete-set mechanical electric valve;
- the spare part has been discontinued;
- the site buys IB4 gearboxes for a 72-rpm IQ12 electric actuator;
- the site explicitly says it uses **electric actuator heads dismantled from other
  places** and combines them with the purchased gearbox for repair/reuse;
- internal stock is zero.

Source:
https://www.bidcenter.com.cn/news-430389654-1.html

This is direct behavioral evidence that the hypothesized donor-organ pattern already
occurs inside a Chinese industrial operator:

```text
DISCONTINUED ORIGINAL SPARE
+ CURRENT FAILED ASSET
+ DONOR ACTUATOR HEAD REMOVED FROM ELSEWHERE
+ NEW / FABRICATED GEARBOX
→ REPAIR / REUSE
```
