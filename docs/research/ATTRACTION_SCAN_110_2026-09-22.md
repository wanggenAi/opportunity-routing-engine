## F1 — Chengdu tea franchise

A 2026 court-supplied case binds one operator to a one-year brand service contract, RMB120,000 service fee, RMB10,000 deposit, store opening and closure, roughly RMB400,000 cumulative input, and a judicially bounded residual recovery.

Source:
- https://www.peopleweekly.cn/html/2026/fazhi_0429/269402.html

The court ordered RMB84,000 service-fee return, full RMB10,000 deposit return, and more than RMB63,000 compensation for equipment/renovation loss. That closes contract termination and part of exit value unusually well.

It still does not publish received customer revenue, a complete period cost ledger or owner hours/replacement labor. Court-awarded residuals therefore cannot be converted into normalized operating cashflow.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Chengdu restaurant IP-guidance liability

A July 2026 People's Court Daily report describes one operator paying RMB39,800 to join a restaurant project. The franchisor repeatedly directed signage/fit-out that infringed another party's trademark. After a second infringement judgment, the operator paid more than RMB330,000 in an execution settlement.

Source:
- https://fazhi.yunnan.cn/system/2026/07/18/034088225.shtml

The operator then recovered against the franchisor; the first-instance judgment was affirmed on appeal. This closes a large realized legal cash outflow and who ultimately bears it.

But it supplies no customer receipts, ordinary food/rent/payroll/tax costs, owner labor or store resale/closure value. The licensed control surface is also upstream-owned and legally defective.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Xiangyang beer sales incentive contract

A 2026 arbitration report binds one restaurant to a two-year beer contract with a 2,500-case target and RMB40,000 asserted incentive package. The restaurant closed after 15 months with only 1,300 cases purchased.

Source:
- https://www.hj.cn/content/2026-08/13/content_20194124.html

The arbitrator ordered return of the RMB16,000 signage incentive. The supplier's RMB24,000 claim for 600 cases of drinks failed because delivery was not sufficiently proved.

This is useful outcome closure, but the 1,300 cases are upstream purchases, not received end-customer revenue. Selling prices, total receipts, rent, labor, other costs, owner time and residual store value remain unknown.

**Verdict:** demoted.
