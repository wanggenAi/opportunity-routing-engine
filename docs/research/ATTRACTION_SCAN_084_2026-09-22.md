## Boundary

Scan 084 is the required independent confirmation pass for the bilateral-partial-flow evidence object introduced in Scan 083.

A formation must begin with repeated observed exchange between replenishing A/B actor populations and recurring connection pressure. Product categories, jobs, gigs, RFQs, procurement feeds and inherited mechanisms remain downstream or excluded.

## Result

The second sample confirms the same pattern across three unrelated domains: real bilateral flow sharply improves reality grounding, but highly visible matching pressure tends to select domains where the control surface is already owned.

| Formation | Repeated bilateral flow | Closure |
|---|---|---|
| Available truck capacity → shipper freight/backhaul | Hundreds of millions of load/truck posts and millions of shipments; every delivery recreates the next-load problem | DAT, Convoy Platform and Uber Freight already own load discovery, recommendations, pricing, booking, tracking and payment. |
| Industrial residuals → secondary-material buyers | Live offers/demands, priced tonnes and large participant networks repeatedly trade waste/by-products as inputs | Cyrkl and peers already combine marketplace matching with expert waste/compliance execution. |
| Cancelled NHS slots → waiting patients | Cancellations release scarce capacity repeatedly; providers offer earlier appointments to eligible waiting patients | NHS/provider scheduling systems own patient eligibility, release rules, notifications and allocation rights. |

## F1 — truck capacity to backhaul freight

This is an exceptionally strong A↔B flow. DAT says more than 291 million loads and trucks are posted annually across its load-board network. Uber Freight reports more than 18 million shipments annually. In June 2026 DAT expanded the Convoy Platform so carriers can see reload options near their destination before booking the first leg, explicitly to reduce costly empty miles.

The connection is repeated, paid and machine-executable. That is precisely why the current control plane is dense: live load inventory, carrier credentials, lane/rate data, instant booking, tracking and payment are already platform assets.

There is no evidence here for a distinct unowned operator layer.

## F2 — industrial residuals to secondary-material buyers

Cyrkl's current marketplace visibly contains supply, demand, quantities and prices for recycled materials. It describes a network trusted by more than 20,000 companies. A 2026 Interreg circular-transition guide uses the platform as an example for converting production residues and clean by-products into cross-industry resource flows.

The connection pressure is real: one side wants to avoid waste cost or monetize residuals while the other wants usable secondary inputs.

But the residual after simple matching is not empty. Cyrkl also sells Circular Waste Scans, green sourcing, compliance/logistics support and comprehensive waste management. The difficult tail is material characterization, treatment and regulation — expert/physical work already bundled around the marketplace.

## F3 — cancelled NHS slots to waiting patients

NHS England reported 16 million missed GP appointments in 2025 and explicitly asks patients to cancel or rearrange so slots can be used by someone else. By April 2026 all acute trusts were connected to NHS App appointment/referral visibility, and current hospital guidance shows earlier slots may be offered to waiting patients when cancellations occur.

The capacity value is large and the matching logic can be automated, but the decisive rights are not open. Providers control clinical priority, eligibility, specialty constraints and final allocation. The NHS App and provider portals already sit on the privileged scheduling rail.

This is a valuable bilateral flow without an independently ownable operator right.

## Learning

Across Scans 083 and 084:

```text
REPEATED A↔B FLOW
+ CONNECTION PRESSURE
=> BETTER REALITY GROUNDING
but often
=> OBVIOUS MATCHING VALUE
=> EXISTING CONTROL SURFACE
```

Two independent passes are enough to change the evidence object without inventing a product mechanism.

Scan 085 should start one level deeper: **participants must already be completing the same bilateral transfer manually or off-platform despite an available exact control surface, and the evidence must state why that surface is bypassed or incomplete.**

That asks reality to reveal the residual instead of inferring one from market structure.
