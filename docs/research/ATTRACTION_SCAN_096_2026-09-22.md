## F1 — Shanghai bulky furniture disposal

The participant signal is not a policy abstraction. Residents describe an actual failed disposal chain: no accepted community drop point, unclear property/neighborhood guidance, failed free self-pickup on a second-hand platform, and inconsistent pricing.

Shanghai's 2026 rule now makes the economic structure explicit. Residents may pay a market-priced door-to-drop short-haul fee, while district authorities contract clearing units and encourage reuse, donation and refurbishment.

That means external value flow is real. It does **not** mean there is a new software business. The scarce work is carrying, vehicle capacity, disassembly, local access and endpoint acceptance. District mini-programs and existing recycling/second-hand channels already handle discovery and booking.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Cross-hospital imaging and test-result portability

A current Xi'an participant account describes the old workaround precisely: carry physical image bags across hospitals, worry about missing or degraded films, and sometimes repeat CT examinations. Current imaging-cloud infrastructure removes much of that state-transfer burden.

The underlying data task is machine-executable, but the control surface is not open. National policy assigns the rail to health-information infrastructure, while current Jiangsu/Suzhou systems already perform retrieval and mutual recognition. Whether a result is clinically reusable still depends on quality, time, disease evolution and physician judgment.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Jiangsu agricultural machinery scheduling

This formation has the strongest Jiangsu/Xuzhou fit in the scan.

During the 2026 wheat harvest, a Liuhe farm operator with 1,200 mu could not find a harvester because the usual operator was away on cross-region work. The local agriculture authority coordinated four machines. Separately, a cross-region machine operator in Jiangsu said finding work previously depended on luck.

The complementarity is real: mobile machines and time-critical crop demand already connect across geography. But the exact matching layer is not unowned. Current county platforms and cooperatives dispatch machines, and Maliduo explicitly offers algorithmic farmer-to-machine matching from its base in Xuzhou's Yunlong District.

**Verdict:** demoted. A new generic matcher would enter an already formed category while still depending on distributed physical operators.

## Two-pass learning

Scans 095 and 096 now provide two independent China participant-authored samples.

The source shift succeeded at one thing: it gets closer to real behavior and real money than starting from rules or vendor documents.

But six fresh China formations show a second pattern: high-friction participant stories often terminate at one of these surfaces:

1. physical execution and site rights;
2. regulated/licensed human authority;
3. public/government digital rails;
4. an already exact end-to-end matcher/platform.

That is enough to change the **next evidence priority**, not enough to invent a product.

Scan 097 should prefer participant-authored current friction where the decisive completion actions are already inside **participant-authorized digital systems**. It should deprioritize chains dominated by physical delivery, licensed clinical/authority judgment, public-asset governance, or a mature exact end-to-end platform.

This is a control-surface evidence filter, not a vertical or product mechanism.
