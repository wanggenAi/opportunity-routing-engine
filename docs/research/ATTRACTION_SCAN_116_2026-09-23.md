# ATTRACTION_SCAN_116 — Standalone Target Ledger / Current Labor Closure

Date: 2026-09-23  
Status: COMPLETE — zero retained formations / zero commercial promotions

## Why this scan exists

Scan 115 proved that explicit target-level positive operating cashflow can be found, but it did not close the same-target combination of current labor burden and bounded total usable capital. Scan 116 therefore prioritizes acquired targets that continue to publish standalone or equivalent target-level financial statements.

The hard packet is:

```text
COMPLETED CONTROL CHANGE
+ CLEAN POST-CONTROL TARGET PERIOD
+ SAME TARGET POSITIVE REVENUE
+ SAME TARGET POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ SAME TARGET EXPLICIT POSITIVE OCF
+ CURRENT TARGET FTE OR EXPLICIT REPLACEMENT-SERVICE COST
+ BOUNDED CONTROL / OPERATING / WORKING-CAPITAL FOOTPRINT
+ LOW RECURRING EXPERT / FIELD / MANUFACTURING / AFTER-SALES DELIVERY
=> POSSIBLE FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

No mixed reporting period is promoted to post-control fact. A nominal control transaction price is not promoted to total usable capital.

## F1 — Jiangsu Jiuheng New-data Intelligent Technology / former Jinrui Technology (430585)

This is the closest formation in the scan.

- Xuzhou Jiuheng Investment acquired the 8,376,000-share / 49.86% control block by judicial auction on 2024-11-15 and became the largest shareholder/controller.
- The 2026 H1 period is fully post-control.
- Same-entity 2026 H1 revenue: RMB6.3883m.
- Same-entity 2026 H1 net profit: RMB0.7527m.
- Same-entity explicit operating cashflow: **positive RMB0.2264m**.
- 2026-06-30 total assets: RMB5.2142m.
- Current staff: **37**, including **28 technical**.
- Current business model remains project-based direct sales, software technical development and solution delivery; increased project acceptance is explicitly tied to revenue realization.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

Scan 116 finally closes the financial-ledger problem on a low-current-asset target, but the direct current labor evidence is fatal. Positive OCF and a small asset base do not become founder-light control cashflow when 28/37 employees are technical and project delivery/acceptance remains a core value-production mechanism.

## F2 — Shenzhen Huacheng Industrial Control (838214)

- On 2025-07-15 Huayancheng bought 35,000 shares (0.12%); the same day the existing shareholder group signed a concert-party agreement under Tang Yong's controlling opinion.
- The concert party held 40.52%, and Tang Yong became actual controller.
- This is a real control change, but the tiny incremental share trade is **not** a reproducible fresh-operator control cost: it only works because the decisive insider holdings already existed.
- The 2026 H1 period is fully post-control.
- Attributable net profit: **negative RMB3.4446m**.
- Explicit OCF: **negative RMB13.2925m**.
- Total assets: RMB182.0546m.
- Current staff: **212** — 66 technical, 71 sales, 51 production, 18 management, 6 finance.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

The formation fails the clean post-control profit/OCF gate, fresh-operator control-capital gate and current low-labor gate simultaneously.

## F3 — Shanghai Feitian Technology (430427)

- On 2025-06-30 Shanghai Puw Technology agreed to acquire 100% of Shanghai Pengyuan Technology for RMB2.0m; the ownership chain changed Feitian from no actual controller to Chen Sihui.
- The transaction was subsequently completed.
- Feitian's 2025 year-end total assets were RMB40.5331m.
- 2025 full-year revenue was RMB40.1912m.
- 2025 full-year net loss was RMB4.8816m.
- 2025 full-year explicit OCF was negative RMB0.3734m.

### Time-window boundary

The 2025 annual reporting period straddles the 2025-06-30 controller change. Therefore the full-year revenue/loss/OCF is **not** relabeled as clean post-control economics.

The RMB2.0m holding-company transaction is also not promoted to the target's total usable capital. A clean later target period with current FTE was not bound in this Scan 116 packet, so those fields remain UNKNOWN.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

This formation is retained only as a falsifier for two common accounting errors: low indirect control transaction price != low operating capital, and mixed pre/post-control annual performance != post-control continuity.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The scan changes the research boundary materially:

1. **Ledger visibility is no longer the main missing capability.** Jiuheng proves that current revenue, profit, explicit OCF, FTE and a low asset base can all be bound to one post-control target.
2. **Current replacement labor is now the decisive floor.** A business with 28 technical employees out of 37 and project delivery cannot be called founder-light merely because its assets and OCF look attractive.
3. **Control price must be reproducible for a fresh operator.** Huacheng's 35,000-share trigger only has control meaning because an existing insider coalition already owns the decisive position.
4. **Time windows stay exact.** Feitian's mixed 2025 period is not converted into post-control data.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 117

Prioritize fresh formations with all of the following bound at once:

- completed controlling transfer;
- clean post-control standalone target period;
- positive external revenue;
- positive net profit;
- explicit positive operating cashflow;
- current target FTE disclosed and intrinsically low;
- no project implementation / field / manufacturing / after-sales organization as the recurring delivery engine;
- a control surface that a fresh independent operator can actually acquire rather than one that depends on a pre-existing insider coalition;
- bounded control consideration plus current operating-asset / working-capital footprint;
- noncommodity machine-operable control.

Do not derive a vertical or product from the zero-retention result.
