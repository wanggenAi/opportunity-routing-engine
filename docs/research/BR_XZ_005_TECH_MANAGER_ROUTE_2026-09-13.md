# BR-XZ-005 — Technology-Manager Participation Path

Status: `ENTRY PATH EVIDENCED / NO ENDORSEMENT CLAIMED`

Public evidence shows that China University of Mining and Technology National University Science Park was recognized as a Jiangsu technology-manager office. The park stated that it would absorb and coordinate technology managers in technology-transfer services.

In April 2026 the park co-organized an initial technology-manager training class in Xuzhou. Participants included people from universities, research institutes, technology companies, technology parks and technology-service organizations.

Xuzhou Daily reported that nearly 1,000 certified technology managers are active across laboratories, enterprises and technology markets in Xuzhou. It also reported that the city has a recurring enterprise technology-demand discovery mechanism involving Jiangsu Huaihai Technology Property Rights Exchange Center and the CUMT Science Park technology-manager office.

This establishes a real professional-role pathway. It does not establish project sponsorship, enterprise access or automatic participation after training.

Questions still requiring direct verification:
- current participation requirements;
- whether a training certificate is required, preferred or insufficient by itself;
- whether externally discovered enterprise technical problems can enter the office workflow;
- confidentiality, IP and case-attribution rules;
- how successful outcomes are measured;
- whether new participants can learn alongside experienced technology managers on real projects.
