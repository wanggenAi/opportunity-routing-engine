# Scan 133 — Durable buyer receipt coverage + receipt economics

Date: 2026-09-23  
Primary domain: China  
Result: **0 retained / 0 commercial promotions / FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW = NOT_PROVEN**

## Admission tightening

Scan 133 did not merely require a nominal lease or machine asset. It front-loaded:

- an exact entry/transfer price;
- a receipt amount attributable to the exact acquired asset;
- an **unprepaid** future buyer receipt window;
- explicit post-transfer contract continuity;
- counterparty identity;
- durable coverage;
- low recurring human delivery;
- receipt-to-entry economics.

A long contract with old-owner prepayment is not buyer cashflow. A pooled rent formula without the unit's actual amount is not source-bound economics. An asset class known to generate money is not enough without the exact asset's receipt ledger.

## F1 — Nanchong RMB 128,004 pooled-rent shops

The official court sale is active through 2026-10-23. Three shops are sold together for RMB 128,004 and are part of a combined amusement-center lease through 2029-12-19. The shopping-center owners committee leases the combined space, collects rent and distributes it to shop owners once per year according to area.

This is structurally strong: very low entry price, multi-year occupancy, central collection and little recurring owner labor. But the official notice does **not** publish how many RMB per year these three units actually receive. The receipt is pooled and the allocation amount is unbound.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_ACTIVE_RMB128004_POOLED_RENT_ASSET_HAS_LONG_LEASE_AND_CENTRAL_COLLECTION_BUT_NO_ASSET_ATTRIBUTABLE_RECEIPT_QUANTUM_OR_EXACT_FIRST_BUYER_PAYMENT`

## F2 — Dongyang long lease, zero future tenant payment window

The current Tongle Street 85-9 sale starts at RMB 1.204m. The protected tenant lease runs through 2030-03-28 at RMB 50,000/year.

The decisive field is payment status: rent is already paid through **2030-03-28**, the lease end. No public source states that this prepaid rent is apportioned to a buyer at transfer. Therefore the visible RMB 50,000 annual rent is not a future buyer receipt stream.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_CURRENT_LONG_LEASE_EXPLICIT_RMB50000_ANNUAL_RENT_IS_FULLY_PREPAID_TO_OLD_OWNER_THROUGH_2030_SO_BUYER_HAS_NO_SOURCE_BOUND_FUTURE_RECEIPT_WINDOW`

## F4 — active Yulin property with China Tower roof lease

The active Yulin sale starts at RMB 1.9212m. China Tower Yulin leases 85 m² of roof space from 2025-09-01 through 2030-08-31 for RMB 24,000/year, paid annually.

The court discloses a decisive contract-continuity failure: the lease was signed after the property's mortgage and seizure. After auction, continued use requires the new owner and China Tower to sign a **new lease**. If they do not, China Tower will remove its equipment. So the existing enterprise rent cannot be treated as inherited cashflow.

Verdict: `DEMOTED_ACTIVE_CHINA_TOWER_RMB24000_ANNUAL_ROOFTOP_RENT_HAS_LONG_TERM_ENTERPRISE_PAYER_BUT_POST_TRANSFER_RECEIPTS_REQUIRE_NEW_LEASE_SO_EXISTING_CONTRACT_CASHFLOW_IS_NOT_INHERITED`
