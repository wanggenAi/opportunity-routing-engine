## Search rule

Scan 036 changed research order, not the required opportunity shape:

```text
DIRECT EXISTING PAYMENT / REPEATED WORKAROUND
→ DIGITAL OR DELEGATABLE DELIVERY PREFILTER
→ EXACT CURRENT INCUMBENT PREFLIGHT
→ ONLY THEN DEEP CAUSAL DESCENT
```

The purpose was to stop spending deep-research effort on categories whose exact control loop is already sold.

## Result

All six broad, unrelated formations were killed at exact-incumbent preflight. No deep causal promotion was justified.

### 1. Enterprise AI usage-based software spend / credit control

Current vendor billing is becoming more usage- and credit-based. GitHub and Atlassian expose native budgets/limits/usage controls, while Tropic and MacroSpend already sell cross-vendor AI spend visibility, committed-versus-consumed tracking, overage forecasting, utilization, renewals and negotiation workflows.

Sources:
- https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/manage-and-track-spending/manage-company-spending

Verdict: **demoted — native vendor controls + exact procurement/FinOps products**.

## Main learning

The Scan 036 process change worked.

```text
EARLY EXACT-INCUMBENT PREFLIGHT
→ SIX FAST KILLS
→ ZERO UNNECESSARY DEEP CAUSAL DESCENTS
```

This does **not** mean “incumbent exists → reject.” The relevant rule remains:

```text
INCUMBENT PRESENCE != FLOW SOLVED
```

These six were killed because current products resolve the same operational control loop, not merely because a company exists nearby.

The next search should therefore remain broad but require evidence of **residual leakage, workaround, exclusion, delay or an unowned action edge after exact-incumbent preflight** before paying the cost of deep causal descent.
