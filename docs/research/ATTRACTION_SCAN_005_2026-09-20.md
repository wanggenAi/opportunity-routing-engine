## Strongest near-miss — Regulatory Delta Cross-Registry Obligation Gap

Working structure:

minus
ALREADY-COMPLIANT POPULATION
equals

The strongest example was China RoHS.

The 2026 catalogue added 23 product categories with new conformity-assessment obligations effective 2027-08-01. MIIT reported that as of 2026-07-31 only 11 enterprises and 28 products from those newly added categories had uploaded conformity data.

The official China RoHS platform supports public query by certificate number, self-declaration number, product name, product model and producer.

### Why it was not retained

1. The immediate output is mainly a qualified prospect list. It does not itself reserve capacity, complete compliance, change product state, release payment, allocate an asset/right or execute a transaction.
2. SGS, Intertek, CQC and other providers are already upstream: they publish rule changes, train manufacturers and provide direct testing/certification channels.
3. Compliance software already performs product/component regulatory mapping, change tracking, documentation and supplier outreach.
4. Public market pricing does not establish extraordinary route economics; common RoHS testing offers are often hundreds to low thousands of RMB for simple products/reports.
5. The new-category mandatory date is 2027-08-01, reducing immediate urgency.

Verdict: RESEARCH_NEAR_MISS / NOT_RETAINED.

## Other demotions

### CCC transition / certifier matching
Rules and provider scopes are visible, but certifiers already provide conversion workflows, education and application channels. Public bulk enumeration of all affected enterprises/products was not established. External operator control is weak.

### Online industrial-product compliance
The 2026 online-sales rules make obligations machine-readable, but platforms themselves are required to verify licenses/certificates/reports and can block listings. The platform owns the strongest visibility and action gate.

### Environmental permit renewal/change
Permit events are visible, but provider suitability is not equivalently rule-resolvable and delivery remains project/consulting labor. The result is mainly a sales lead.

### Public asset leasing × brand expansion
Both sides are observable, but mature commercial-real-estate products already maintain structured brand expansion, project and matching databases.

### Failed tender / rebid intelligence
Observable and timely, but existing tender-intelligence products already mine rebids, qualifications, supplier sourcing and pre-tender signals.

### Bid consortium / qualification complement
Qualifications can be parsed, but trust, legal responsibility and joint delivery widen the bridge; partner boards and joint-bid services already exist.

### Bid award → finance / guarantee
Public award events are economically meaningful, but electronic guarantee and government-procurement financing platforms already automate much of this path.

### Trademark / patent status → agency service
Official IP data is highly discoverable and downloadable, but the exact pattern is already productized as renewal/change prospect mining and agency CRM automation.

### Bankruptcy / liquidation assets
Supply is highly visible through national bankruptcy information and auction platforms, but specific buyer demand is not comparably visible; condition often requires inspection and the route degrades into inventory picking/arbitrage/due diligence.

### Medical-device recall × hospital procurement
UDI/recall identities are structured, but the manufacturer/registrant legally owns the recall action and distribution/use units are already inside the traceability chain.

## Scan-level learning

Discoverability is necessary but not sufficient.

A common failure topology is:

PUBLIC STATE CHANGE -> PERFECTLY IDENTIFIABLE PROSPECT -> STANDARD SERVICE PROVIDER

This often becomes lead generation rather than high-leverage orchestration.

Visible prospect != routable value.

The stronger target is:

DATA WORLD A
+ INDEPENDENT DATA WORLD B
+ NON-OBVIOUS JOIN
+ JOIN DIRECTLY CHANGES ALLOCATION / TRANSACTION / ACCESS / TIMING
+ NO SINGLE INCUMBENT ALREADY OWNS BOTH WORLDS
+ MATCH IS MACHINE/RULE RESOLVABLE
+ ROUTER CAN CAPTURE PART OF THE VALUE FLOW

## Source-topology learning

Future scans should preferentially search cross-domain joins between independently maintained data universes. Same-domain platforms usually already own the obvious matching layer, while regulation-to-standard-service relationships often collapse into lead generation.

Desired evidence topology:
- one side exposed by an official full/incremental dataset or recurring public stream;
- the other exposed by a different platform/registry/event stream;
- a stable join key exists;
- no incumbent controls both data universes;
- the join immediately changes an economic decision.
