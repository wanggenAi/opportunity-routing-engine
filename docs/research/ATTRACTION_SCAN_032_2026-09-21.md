## 1. Agricultural production outsourcing / 托管

2026 agricultural-socialized-service programs continue to move smallholder production toward professional
service entities.

Current programs explicitly:
- prioritize small farmers;
- aggregate demand through collectives / 托管员;
- use qualified service organizations;
- cover plowing, sowing, crop protection and harvesting;
- use contracts, telemetry and service-platform supervision.

Sources:
- https://www.szss.gov.cn/sstbhzq/ssw/jrss/ggtz/content/post_12982149.html
- https://www.qingcheng.gov.cn/xxgk/tzgg2/content/post_2173108.html
- https://www.zd.gov.cn/columns/6441d165-2f34-4739-93cd-899069759437/202608/10/ab604835-23b8-47a6-8c11-51d66f88d666.html

The flow is real. But the value delivered is still physical field service, equipment and local fulfillment,
while public/sector platforms already coordinate qualified suppliers.

Verdict: **demoted — recurring field delivery + institutional service network**.

## 2. Rehabilitation-assistive-device rental

This was one of the clearest current frictions in the reset.

Shanghai users report:
- not knowing which smart assistive devices are actually rentable;
- some manufacturers refusing rental because the economics do not work;
- cross-district subsidy restrictions;
- limited home-trial capability;
- weak coordination between device makers and care users.

Source:
- https://sh.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0722/c176737-41646787.html

At the same time, national and local policy is explicitly building:
- integrated display/evaluation/rental/sales centers;
- center + station service networks;
- community rental;
- shared rental;
- online + offline service.

Sources:
- https://hunan.gov.cn/zqt/zcsd/202607/t20260714_34025856.html
- https://mzj.beijing.gov.cn/art/2026/5/15/art_10834_691794.html

The residual pain is valuable, but it is not a thin matching problem. It requires fitting, home trial,
delivery, use instruction, maintenance, disinfection and recovery.

Verdict: **demoted — local physical service and fitting remain core**.

## 3. High-end laboratory / scientific-instrument sharing

Suzhou is opening a 5.9-billion-yuan-class laboratory instrumentation platform to enterprises, with more than
100 high-end analytical instruments planned. Hefei is likewise using shared professional laboratory capacity
to reduce SME R&D barriers.

Sources:
- https://www.suzhou.gov.cn/szsrmzf/bmdt/202609/1753a7cd15624ff7bbc9df8ebb86a4da.shtml
- https://ah.people.com.cn/n2/2026/0913/c358428-41694576.html

This proves real resource opening.

But the bridge is not simply "find an idle machine." Sample preparation, method selection, instrument
configuration and interpretation remain expert work; the host facility owns scheduling, acceptance and the
physical asset.

Verdict: **demoted — public/open platform + recurring expert testing dependency**.

## 4. Pet insurance moving into lifecycle management

Pet insurance is becoming a larger mainstream spend category. Xinhua Finance reported:
- ZhongAn H1 2026 pet-insurance premium +22.7% to RMB 691m and >1.61m pet owners served;
- Ping An H1 premium RMB 247m, +113.2%.

Source:
- https://www.cnfin.com/yw-lb/detail/20260908/4466735_1.html

The behavioral signal is strong: insurance is expanding from one-time reimbursement toward broader lifecycle
health services.

But the strongest state asset belongs to insurers and provider networks—the claims history, hospital outcomes
and payment rails—while clinical diagnosis/treatment remains professional veterinary work.

Verdict: **demoted — insurer data/control + human medical delivery**.

## 5. Automotive aftermarket expansion

China's 2026 policy push explicitly targets six aftermarket areas:
- modification;
- RV/camping;
- classic cars;
- repair/insurance;
- motorsport;
- vehicle rental.

Source:
- https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/syxwfb/art/2026/art_34458c6d83354316b6bb19d2e08f0b10.html

This is useful reality evidence of growing service consumption, but the broad market itself is not an
opportunity. The actual action loops remain owned by repair networks, insurers, rental firms, event operators
and physical-service providers.

Verdict: **demoted — broad growth signal without a narrow unowned control edge**.

## 6. Industrial circular economy / remanufacturing

Current industrial policy and company behavior are expanding remanufacturing into newer equipment categories,
while circular-economy firms are reporting strong activity.

Sources:
- https://epaper.tianjinwe.com/tjrb/h5/html5/2026-09/14/content_143088_3836149.htm
- https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2026-09-10/4578493.html

But collection, inspection, repair, certification, storage and resale are physical/capital-intensive.

Verdict: **demoted — industrial asset operations, not a lightweight control edge**.
