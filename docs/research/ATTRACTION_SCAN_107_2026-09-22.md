## Purpose

Scan 106 showed that executed transfer plus continuing economic motion is still too weak: a transferred position can remain organization-heavy, capital-heavy or economically opaque.

Scan 107 therefore raises the evidence floor to one normalized small-operator control position:

```text
EXECUTED SMALL-OPERATOR CONTROL CHANGE
+ SAME OPERATOR / SAME CONTROL POSITION
+ RECEIVED CUSTOMER REVENUE
+ DIRECT OPERATING COSTS
+ OWNER LABOR OR REPLACEMENT COST
+ RIGHTS CLOSURE
+ REALIZED / BOUNDED EXIT VALUE
=> NORMALIZED NET CASHFLOW ESTIMATE
```

The pass is deliberately formation-diverse and does not start from a business-for-sale marketplace, franchise category or prior vertical.

## Result

| Formation | What is actually proven | Decisive failure |
|---|---|---|
| Shenzhen convenience-store entrant | Fresh individual entry at low-six-figure RMB scale; post-entry turnover; reported monthly accounting remainder; reported capital loss and exit | Accounting profit excludes a closed owner shadow wage/full cost ledger; recurring physical labor remains decisive; durable transfer/control rights are not closed |
| Three-room Dali homestay transfer | Actual RMB450k turnkey transfer; seller reports buyer recovered >50% of investment after one year | Buyer receipts, exact direct costs, owner labor and rights are not disclosed; economics from seller's different seven-room property cannot be stitched into the buyer case |
| Xi'an lottery station takeover | Named operator reports RMB160k takeover and current annual gross turnover >RMB4m | Gross ticket sales are not operator income; official agency qualification is regulated/non-saleable; exact approved rights path, costs and owner labor economics are unclosed and value remains person-specific |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — Shenzhen convenience-store entry: accounting profit can still be a purchased job

A current Shenzhen operator report documents Xu Ming entering a convenience-store franchise after taking a premises previously used as a steamed-bun shop. It reports an initial outlay above RMB100,000, including roughly RMB30,000 franchise fee, RMB30,000 custom shelving, RMB20,000 inventory and about RMB20,000 decoration/deposit.

Source:
- https://www.huxiu.com/article/4892717.html

During the opening period, daily turnover reportedly reached roughly RMB1,200-1,900. After the first loss-making month, the books showed around RMB7,000 per month remaining after costs.

That still does not close normalized cashflow. After about six months, Xu planned to transfer the store for around RMB50,000 and stated that roughly RMB60,000 of initial principal had been lost. He described the period as hard work for no gain and said his body could no longer sustain the operation.

The same current report separately gives a different operator's 15-hour workday and roughly RMB8,000 monthly income. That is useful evidence that owner shadow wage is material in this operating class, but those hours are **not** assigned to Xu. Same-operator binding means the model must leave Xu's exact owner hours unknown.

Current category pressure is independently corroborated by CCFA's 2026 H1 survey: 71.7% of sampled convenience-store firms reported declining customer counts and 60% reported declining net profit.

Official/industry source:
- https://www.ccfa.org.cn/portal/cn/xiangxi.jsp?id=447307&type=10003

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Three-room Dali homestay: executed transfer, but buyer economics remain second-hand

A Dali operator interview reports that the seller transferred his first three-room guesthouse in 2023 for RMB450,000 as a turnkey operation. The seller says the buyer operated it for a year and had recovered more than half of the investment.

Source:
- https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30426596

That is materially stronger than an asking-price listing, but the buyer's own OTA/customer receipts, rent, cleaning, utilities, consumables, owner time and cash distributions are not published.

The article also contains a detailed revenue/cost account for the seller's **different seven-room property**. Those numbers are intentionally excluded from the buyer's return model. Cross-property stitching would manufacture evidence that does not exist.

Exact lease assignment, landlord consent, accommodation-registration continuity, channel/reputation transfer and realized exit value for the three-room buyer are also not independently closed.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Xi'an lottery station: turnover is real, but gross sales and private takeover language do not close economics or rights

A May 2026 local report identifies Guo Qi as the operator of station 61011816. It states that he investigated an existing station and spent RMB160,000 to take it over in 2013. The same report says current annual turnover exceeds RMB4 million.

Current source:
- https://www.gjnews.cn/2026/0517/623351.html

The station has a real noncommodity asset: a long-lived local trust graph and a high-trust group-purchase community. But that value is strongly person-specific. The owner still personally serves customers and maintains the community.

Gross lottery turnover is not the operator's received income. Station-specific commission receipts, rent, staffing, utilities, taxes, owner hours and exit value are not disclosed.

Rights are also decisive. Ministry of Finance implementation rules require a lottery agency contract and agency certificate; the certificate is the legal qualification and may not be lent, rented or sold.

Official source:
- https://jdjc.mof.gov.cn/fgzd/202204/t20220422_3805017.htm

The current article does not document how the 2013 reported takeover corresponded to an institution-approved contract/certificate change. Therefore exact rights closure is **unknown**, not assumed valid or invalid.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Learning

Scan 107 adds a stricter evidence invariant:

```text
ONE OPERATOR'S HOURS
+ ANOTHER OPERATOR'S PROFIT
!= NORMALIZED CASHFLOW

SELLER'S OTHER PROPERTY COSTS
+ BUYER'S ACQUISITION PRICE
!= BUYER ROI

GROSS TRANSACTION VOLUME
!= OPERATOR RECEIVED REVENUE

PRIVATE "TAKEOVER"
!= REGULATED RIGHTS CLOSURE
```

The important failure is not that small businesses never make money. It is that public evidence frequently binds only part of the economic identity, and cross-entity stitching can make an incomplete position look complete.
