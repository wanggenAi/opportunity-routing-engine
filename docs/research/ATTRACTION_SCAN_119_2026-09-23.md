# ATTRACTION_SCAN_119 — Current Post-Control Low-FTE / Unambiguous Positive-OCF Early-Gate Pass

Date: 2026-09-23  
Status: COMPLETE — zero retained formations / zero commercial promotions

## Why this scan exists

Scan 118 showed that low headcount, completed control and positive accounting signals can each be real while the combined founder-light packet still fails. Scan 119 therefore moves the current-report, clean control-window, current FTE, positive-net-profit and unambiguous-positive-OCF checks forward as early gates before spending deep research effort on recurring revenue or control scarcity.

```text
COMPLETED FRESH-OPERATOR-REPRODUCIBLE CONTROL BEFORE REPORTING PERIOD
+ CURRENT POST-CONTROL REPORT AVAILABLE
+ CURRENT / LATEST DIRECTLY DISCLOSED FTE INTRINSICALLY LOW
+ SAME-ENTITY POSITIVE EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ UNAMBIGUOUS POSITIVE OCF
+ EXISTING STANDARDIZED REPEATABLE NON-PROJECT REVENUE
+ BOUNDED CONTROL / OPERATING / WORKING CAPITAL
+ NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE VERIFIED FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

The scan does **not** loosen any gate merely to retain a case.

## F1 — Jintian Yuanfeng (830874): completed pre-period control + 8 FTE, but current profit and OCF are negative

- Gu Jiayu acquired 100% of Shengdi Banglun for **RMB840,000** under a 2024-09-03 agreement.
- Registration completed on **2024-10-18**. Through Shengdi Banglun he indirectly held **54.42%** of Jintian Yuanfeng and became actual controller.
- The 2026 H1 report directly discloses **8 employees**.
- 2026 H1 revenue: **RMB765,709.75**.
- 2026 H1 attributable net profit: **-RMB604,400.80**.
- 2026 H1 operating cashflow: **-RMB2,546,914.06**.
- Current total assets are about **RMB3.04m**, while equity is negative.
- The current business is physical intelligent-vehicle-control-system components, supply-chain coordination and technical support.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

This is a legitimate small-headcount completed-control packet and therefore a useful hard test. It still fails before any deep recurring-control analysis: current profit is negative, OCF is deeply negative, equity is negative and value delivery remains physical component procurement/sales/support.

## F2 — Longjoy Technology (831322): 8 FTE, but the 81.26% acquisition was terminated unpaid

- Wang Qinglei and Li Hongwei signed an agreement on 2025-10-23 for **17,756,000 shares / 81.26%**.
- On **2026-06-09** the parties signed a termination agreement.
- The company disclosed that the acquirer **had not paid the acquisition consideration**.
- Li Hongwei remained controlling shareholder and actual controller.
- Current employee count is **8**.
- 2026 H1 revenue: **RMB351,299.25**.
- 2026 H1 attributable net profit: **-RMB71,596.35**.
- 2026 H1 OCF: **-RMB96,495.30**.
- Current delivery includes multimedia equipment, software/hardware systems integration, installation, commissioning, training and technical services.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

A signed acquisition report is not an executed control transfer. The early execution gate prevents the system from assigning current economics to a hypothetical new controller who never acquired the company. Current loss, negative OCF and integration/service delivery independently fail the economic and machine-delivery floors.

## F3 — Yudu Media (873155): control completed inside H1, current loss and field-project revenue

- The 2026 H1 report states that Zhongtai Jiye Asset Management completed the acquisition on **2026-02-09** and Pan Qiao became actual controller.
- After completion the acquirer held **31.09%** economically and controlled **94.01%** of voting rights through direct/indirect holdings plus voting-right delegation.
- Because completion occurred inside the 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-30 reporting period, H1 is **not a clean full-period post-control ledger**.
- Current employees increased from 6 to **15**.
- 2026 H1 revenue: **RMB1,053,459.11**.
- 2026 H1 attributable net profit: **-RMB433,179.59**.
- Revenue includes **RMB943,396.23 of field-promotion projects** and RMB110,062.88 of advertising services.

### Verdict

**DEMOTED.**

A real completed control event still fails the clean-period gate when it happens inside the reporting period. Negative profit and field-project-dominated revenue make deeper OCF reconciliation or scarcity analysis unnecessary for promotion purposes.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The important result is methodological rather than categorical:

1. **Early gates are working.** They reject weak packets before expensive recurring-revenue/control-scarcity analysis.
2. **Low FTE is not rare enough to be the starting ontology.** Jintian Yuanfeng and Longjoy both show single-digit teams while failing current economics or executed control.
3. **Signed acquisition documents are not execution evidence.** Longjoy is an explicit terminated/unpaid negative control.
4. **Control timing must be clean.** Yudu Media's February completion makes H1 a mixed pre/post-control period.
5. Searching acquisition announcements first remains inefficient because many targets die immediately on current profit/OCF or execution.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 120

Invert **retrieval order only**, not the commercial constitution or product ontology.

Before deep control-transfer research, first require:

- a current same-entity report;
- directly disclosed current/latest intrinsically low FTE;
- positive external revenue;
- positive net profit;
- unambiguous positive operating cashflow.

Only then reverse-check:

- completed fresh-operator-reproducible control before the reporting period;
- existing standardized repeatable non-project revenue;
- no custom implementation, outsourcing, field work, manufacturing, after-sales or pending injection as the delivery engine;
- bounded control consideration, operating assets and working capital;
- noncommodity machine-operable control.

This change reduces research waste without weakening any promotion gate.
