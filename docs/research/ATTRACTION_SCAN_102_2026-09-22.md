## Purpose

Scan 101 showed that requiring an independently acquirable control surface fixes the upstream-gatekeeper bias from Scans 099–100, but its three samples all collapsed into commodity infrastructure.

Scan 102 therefore repeats the same entrance once more on a fresh formation-diverse China sample before changing the evidence priority:

```text
CURRENT / RECENT CHINA PARTICIPANT-RECORDED REALITY
+ INDEPENDENT OBJECTIVE CORROBORATION
+ CURRENT EXTERNAL PAYMENT / SPEND / DIRECT MARGIN
+ DECISIVE ASSET / RIGHT / ROUTING POSITION
  ACQUIRABLE BY AN INDEPENDENT SMALL OPERATOR
+ EXACT INCUMBENT / OPEN-SOURCE / GENERIC-AGENT /
  FOUNDER-INDEPENDENCE / MARGIN / RIGHTS PREFLIGHT
→ RETAIN ONLY IF THE ACQUIRABLE CONTROL IS ALSO A DURABLE OPERATOR EDGE
```

## Result

| Formation | Acquirable control + money motion | Why it still fails |
|---|---|---|
| Self-service car wash | Leasable site + purchasable equipment + reported >RMB 20k monthly revenue / >RMB 10k monthly profit | Real value is site/capital/physical uptime; recurring calls, consumables and maintenance remain |
| Community / small-site EV charging | Ownable/contractable station + current public-charging spend + standardized operator partnership | Edge reduces to site/grid/capex inside mature charging networks |
| Group-expense mini-program ads | Owned software + registered mini-program + organic usage + ~RMB 10/day ads | Tiny normalized economics; settlement primitive has mature free/open-source substitutes |

No formation survives all floors.

## F1 — Self-service car wash

A January 2026 China operator reported two self-service car-wash stores while retaining a full-time job. The first store was above RMB 20,000 monthly revenue, the second exceeded RMB 10,000 during its first half-month, and single-store monthly profit was reported above RMB 10,000 on roughly RMB 200,000 investment.

Participant source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/amp/t/1184755

The current V2EX side-hustle index also shows a September 2026 follow-up titled “自助洗车店副业更新（第 3 家开业）”. This is continuity evidence only; no unsupported new revenue figure is inferred from it.

Continuity source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/go/sidehustle
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1221989

The control stack is plainly acquirable. A small operator can lease a site, buy wash hardware, and contract store-control software. Current commercial wash software exposes QR session start, pricing/payment, stall state, operating data and remote equipment switching, while current open-source car-service systems already expose store/member/order/payment primitives.

Objective/control-surface sources:
- https://www.jikecar.cn/
- https://github.com/fushengqian/fuintCar

The operator's own account names the remaining recurring work: calls, chemical replenishment and equipment maintenance. The strong reported store profit is therefore a return on a local physical asset and operations stack, not evidence of a reusable machine-executable orchestration layer.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — Community / small-site EV charging

A 2026 China participant reported having built a charging station in 2019 and described the still-operating station as a materially successful fallback business.

Operator source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1186511

A separate January 2026 participant described a concrete small-operator path: use an owned or rented parking space, install a charger, make it shared, and collect charging payments from other residents.

Participant source:
- https://www.v2ex.com/t/1184715

External spend is independently visible. One China EV owner reported 51 charging sessions in a year at a community Xiaoju fast-charging site, 976.915 kWh consumed and RMB 1,035.6 paid, averaging roughly RMB 1.06/kWh.

Spend source:
- https://v2ex.com/t/1167817

The operator right is not hypothetical. StarCharge's current partner materials explicitly package site selection, station construction, operations and maintenance and accept partners with the relevant business scope plus funds or site resources.

Objective source:
- https://www.starcharge.com/partner/?tp=3

That same maturity is the problem. A new operator can acquire a station, but the differentiating asset is the physical site/power position. Discovery, payment and operating control are already wrapped by established networks. No current evidence proves superior normalized small-operator margin after site, grid, capex and maintenance.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Group-expense mini-program advertising

In September 2026 a China developer reported that a group-expense mini-program originally built for personal use had accumulated more than 900 group ledgers with little active promotion. After enabling two ad placements, the operator reported roughly RMB 10/day of ad revenue.

Participant/operator source:
- https://jp.v2ex.com/t/1242626

This is unusually strong evidence for founder-light digital operation: usage appeared organically, the runtime is software-executable, and external money already moves.

The right is also standardized rather than imaginary. Tencent Cloud documentation describes the standard mini-program traffic-owner flow: enable monetization, create ad units, obtain ad-unit IDs and integrate them into the published mini-program.

Objective source:
- https://cloud.tencent.com.cn/document/product/1301/103788

But the control is not scarce. Current web tools already implement multi-member split rules, shared rooms and minimum-transfer settlement; a current open-source WeChat mini-program template implements the same core bill-splitting and settlement suggestion logic.

Substitute sources:
- https://toolv.com/zh-CN/app/aa-ji-zhang
- https://github.com/yanqr213/bill-split-miniprogram-kit
- https://www.splitwithdivvy.com/best-splitwise-alternatives

The operator itself framed the current advertising revenue mainly as server-cost coverage. With mature substitutes and approximately RMB 10/day revenue, the formation fails normalized economics and generic-substitutability pressure.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Two-pass learning

Scans 101 and 102 together now provide two independent samples of the same entrance. The repeated failure pattern is stable enough to strengthen the next evidence priority:

```text
OPERATOR-ACQUIRABLE CONTROL
+ REAL EXTERNAL MONEY
≠ DURABLE OPERATOR EDGE

NEXT:
require evidence that the useful control is not available to
an equivalent competent entrant on materially similar terms,
and that the advantage is operator-specific or compounds
through distribution, data, contract, supply, trust or another
evidence-backed mechanism.
```

This is an **evidence property**, not a product category. Scan 103 must still reopen broad China reality and must not start from a preselected “data moat”, “exclusive contract”, “network effect” or other fashionable mechanism.
