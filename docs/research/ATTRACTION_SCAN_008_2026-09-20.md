## Direction 1 — Flight / crew disruption × hotel / transport booking

The mechanism is real: disruption or crew-schedule events can create immediate travel booking demand.

But current travel technology already turns crew schedule / IROPS state into policy-aware hotel, air and ground booking workflows.

Representative source:
https://routespring.com/crew-travel-api/

Verdict: `EXTERNAL_SIGNAL_ALREADY_PRODUCTIZED_INTO_ACTION_RAIL`.

## Direction 2 — Events / weather / local demand × hotel inventory and pricing

Hotel revenue-management systems already consume external signals such as local events, weather, competitor pricing, booking lead time and demand patterns to change inventory/pricing decisions.

Representative sources:
https://www.chinahotel.org.cn/
https://www.6renyou.com/

Verdict: `MATURE_REVENUE_MANAGEMENT_JOIN`.

## Direction 3 — Compute/load signal × spot/open cloud capacity

Cloud spot and auto-scaling rails are highly actionable, but the provider already integrates price/capacity and load-aware allocation logic.

Representative sources:
https://help.aliyun.com/zh/ecs/user-guide/spot-instances
https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/ec2-fleet-allocation-strategy.html

Verdict: `RAIL_OWNER_ALREADY_INTEGRATES_THE_SIGNAL`.

## Direction 4 — Judicial auction assets × valuation / market-liquidity signal

Judicial auction assets have an open bidding rail and strong public data.

But 2026 market products and data services already monitor large auction inventories and provide pricing, discount, liquidity and AI/data analysis. The residual would mainly be investment decision support and capital deployment.

Verdict: `ANALYTICS / CAPITAL_RISK / NOT_ROUTING_CONTROL`.

## Direction 5 — Cross-border ecommerce trend / demand signal × procurement / listing rail

Large ecommerce platforms are already moving opportunity detection, product selection, compliance and supply-chain planning into AI-driven workflows.

Representative source:
https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/small-business/amazon-seller-ai-tools-2026

Verdict: `PLATFORM_ALREADY_MOVING_UPSTREAM_INTO_SIGNAL_DISCOVERY`.

## Direction 6 — Existing booking price drop × cancel/rebook rail

Monitoring an existing reservation for a lower price and rebooking is a valid independent-signal × action-rail mechanism.

But the mechanism is already productized by services such as Pruvo/Rebookly and has matured into B2B travel infrastructure.

Representative sources:
https://www.pruvo.com/
https://www.webbeds.com/

Verdict: `MECHANISM_VALID / ALREADY_PRODUCTIZED`.

## Why native commission still does not create attraction

Without a self-revealing high-intent demand source, the operator still has to:
- acquire traffic;
- create content;
- cold-message users;
- run ads;
- build an audience;
- or otherwise manufacture demand access.

That is ordinary affiliate/distribution marketing.

```text
OPEN TRANSACTION RAIL
+ NATIVE COMMISSION
+ NO SELF-REVEALING INTENT
= DISTRIBUTION / MARKETING, NOT HIGH-ATTRACTION ROUTING
```

## Strongest intent surface tested — B2B RFQ

Alibaba International RFQ is a strong self-revealing intent surface:
- buyers actively publish what they need;
- the platform states roughly 20,000 new RFQs per day;
- RFQ search/detail/recommendation and supplier quotation APIs exist;
- the purchase window is open by definition.

Sources:
https://supplier.alibaba.com/supplier/rfq.html
https://developer.alibaba.com/docs/api.htm?apiId=42604
https://activity.alibaba.com/supplier/94d0ef4e.html

But the economic actor model is wrong for the current goal:
- the expected responder is a supplier/merchant;
- the supplier must quote and negotiate;
- the platform does not establish a native third-party router take-rate for matching an RFQ to someone else's supply;
- rules treat the RFQ information as merchant-accessed sourcing information;
- becoming the responder would make the operator supplier/sales execution.

Truth:
`SELF_REVEALING_INTENT_CONFIRMED / ROUTER_NATIVE_TAKE_RATE_UNPROVEN`.

Global mechanisms show the combination is possible. Some RFQ/referral platforms compensate ambassadors or earn supplier-side success fees after buyer selection.

Examples:
https://zeozgig.com/Home/PartnerwithZeozGig
https://faktorist.com/referral
https://globalb2bgroup.com/trust-center

These are mechanism analogues only, not China demand proof.
