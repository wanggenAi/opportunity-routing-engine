## Retained research beacon

### ATTRACTION_SCAN_021-F1 — SME_OVERDUE_PAYMENT_EVIDENCE_COMPILER
Chinese: **中小企业逾期回款证据编译器**

This is deliberately **not** a lawyer, debt-collection agency, or generic accounts-receivable SaaS.

The proposed machine role is narrower:

1. ingest the SME's contract, delivery/acceptance, reconciliation, settlement and payment records;
2. extract the bounded payment-trigger facts and dates that are actually present;
3. test basic eligibility for the official SME overdue-payment complaint rail;
4. identify missing materials before submission;
5. compile a structured evidence index, factual chronology, requested-remedy draft and submission checklist;
6. route the user to the correct official national/provincial entry;
7. fail closed when facts are disputed or the matter requires professional legal judgment.

The user remains the complainant and performs official submission. No recovery result is promised.

## Why it survived the first hard-floor pass

### 1. The pain is direct cash, not abstract compliance

The September 2026 State Council payment-recovery policy explicitly targets SME cash-flow harm caused by
large-enterprise payment delays and requires industries to clarify four payment elements: starting point,
payment method/process, inspection/acceptance standard and deadline, and maximum payment term.

Official source:
- State Council / policy text mirrored by MOFCOM:
  https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=106834
- MIIT policy briefing:
  https://jxca.miit.gov.cn/xwdt/bwyw/art/2026/art_b6457889b27343d990dde21aa8a61599.html

### 2. The execution rail is real

The national complaint platform is live and separates central-government/central-SOE complaints from
provincial complaints involving local agencies, large SOEs and large private enterprises.

Source:
- https://sme-dj.miit.gov.cn/
- https://ythzxfw.miit.gov.cn/bssx/ssts/art/2025/art_d2bf0d80a983451cb37e98e47210c712.html

### 3. The draft complaint workflow is unusually machine-structurable

MIIT's 2026-09-14 draft complaint rule requires a bounded set of submission materials: complainant identity,
respondent identity, specific request plus supporting facts/evidence (including contracts, acceptance records,
reconciliation and settlement records), and a signed/stamped undertaking that the same matter is not already
being handled by court, arbitration, another authority or mediation institution. Incomplete material is
returned for supplementation.

The same draft provides a 10-working-day form review, ordinarily 30 days for handling, at most 90 days for a
complex case, and a review mechanism after the result. For disputed debts, the clear and undisputed portion
should be pushed toward payment while materially disputed portions may be directed to litigation/arbitration.

Source:
- MIIT notice:
  https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/qyj/gzdt/art/2026/art_f958f4ccac2349aba2b5958f9be67813.html
- Draft PDF:
  https://www.miit.gov.cn/cms_files/filemanager/1226211233/attach/20269/8616c177497846e9888a7a3596955a43.pdf

### 4. The rail has demonstrated realized value

This is stronger than policy intent alone.

- Hunan reported that from 2025 through April 2026 it processed 4,042 SME complaint leads and helped
  enterprises recover RMB 566 million.
  https://fgw.hunan.gov.cn/fgw/tayabl22/202608/t20260826_34051402.html
- Chongqing reported 734 complaint leads in Jan-Apr 2026, 436 resolved, involving RMB 324 million.
  https://jjxxw.cq.gov.cn/zwgk_213/jytafhgk/202606/t20260618_15764079.html
- MIIT previously reported 48,000 complaints processed in 2024, showing the underlying complaint-intent class
  is not tiny.
  https://cqca.miit.gov.cn/xwdt/bsxx/art/2025/art_a3be0adf09d24de585481b8345edfb26.html

These figures prove that the official rail can matter economically. They **do not** prove that SMEs will pay
a third party for evidence compilation.

## Hard boundary: legal-service risk

The product must not silently become an unlicensed law firm.

The Lawyers Law reserves litigation representation and other lawyer work to appropriately licensed practice,
and current judicial-administration guidance distinguishes legal-consulting companies from law firms and
warns that non-law-firm entities may not perform lawyer/litigation representation.

Sources:
- Lawyers Law:
  https://xzfy.moj.gov.cn/c/2021-01-06/487877.shtml
- 2026 judicial-administration public guidance:
  https://www.huaihua.gov.cn/sfj/c112120/202606/15621bf6d2b045b4b38d304d02bb0408.shtml

Therefore F1 is retained only for **legal-boundary falsification**. The safe product hypothesis is factual
document extraction, completeness checks, official-rule mapping and user-controlled packet generation.
Genuinely disputed matters, legal strategy, representation, negotiation and litigation/arbitration work must
fail closed or be referred to qualified professionals.

## Other mechanisms tested

### A. Shared manufacturing / idle-capacity activation — real value, generic opportunity demoted

This mechanism is real. In Hebei, a current shared-manufacturing platform decomposes urgent orders into
process-level suborders and matches them across SMEs; one reported participant lifted daily capacity 37% and
cut key-equipment idle rate from 35% to 8%.

Source:
- https://hebfb.hebccw.cn/system/2026/07/15/102203791.shtml

Jiangsu's SME public-service platform also exposes real, long-lived manufacturing demand such as heat
treatment and shaft rough-machining work:
- https://www.smejs.cn/require_show.aspx?requireid=af60b92bccbe49c794876f4f1a83115e

But the generic "build a capacity-matching platform" formation is already occupied by current platforms with
AI quoting, capacity listing, real-capacity data, matching and workflow collaboration:
- https://www.gyysdata.com/
- https://aicorgi.cn/
- https://www.linkmes.com.cn/index.html

Verdict: **mechanism validated, generic entrant demoted**.

### B. Unified privacy compliance for malls/parks — one-to-many structure, payer pull unproven

The 2026 simplified personal-information rule allows a commercial-property / park manager to formulate one
unified rule for multiple same-type small offline processors in its managed area. This creates a genuine
one-to-many software shape.

Source:
- https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-07/24/c_1786638889576451.htm

But exact mall/park willingness to buy a lightweight software layer was not established. Generic privacy
compliance spend by large enterprises does not substitute for this exact payer proof.

Verdict: **demoted pending payer evidence**.

### C. Electronic trade documents — transition real, control surface already occupied

The standards/policy transition is real, but current carriers, banks, document-system operators and
enterprise platforms already sit on the execution surface. A generic entrant would mostly become another
integration or consulting layer.

Verdict: **demoted**.

### D. Small-enterprise safety standard tooling — no mandatory pull established

A simplified standard can create useful checklists and workflows, but this scan did not establish that the
standard itself creates mandatory paid demand or a current self-revealing high-intent buyer pool.

Verdict: **demoted**.
