## Selection rule for the two required controls

Before the commercial ask, a control must pass:

```text
CONTROL_QUALIFIED =
  current storefront found
  AND suitable public low-risk space
  AND observable / admitted weak period or acquisition objective
  AND owner / manager / authorized decision route reachable
  AND not counted solely because it is part of the same youth-night-school success story as the anchor
```

Prefer two controls with different conditions:

1. one small independently operated cafe / youth-facing merchant;
2. one more established or mall-adjacent cafe/merchant as a negative-control contrast.

This makes the first field result more informative than interviewing three near-identical stores.

## Exact control question

After recent-behavior and acquisition-cost questions:

> `如果我在你们固定的空档时段，组织8到10个符合你目标客群的年轻新客来参加一场60到90分钟的小活动；达到约定到场人数后，你们愿不愿意支付200元组织费？如果200元不成立，具体是因为这批客不值200、你们从不为获客付费、还是需要先证明消费/复购？`

Record the mechanism of refusal. `NO` is useful only when we know **why**.
