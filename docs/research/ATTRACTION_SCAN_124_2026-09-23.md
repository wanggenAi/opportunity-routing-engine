## Interpretation

Scan 124 validates the retrieval change but not a new commercial formation. Positive-profit and positive-OCF search signals efficiently surface financially real operators, yet those signals alone skew toward organizations whose current FTE is well above the thin-operator boundary. Conversely, genuinely low-FTE packets remain easy to find but still frequently fail profit or cash conversion.

This does **not** justify relaxing FTE, profit, OCF or external-revenue requirements. It also does not produce the second admitted survivor needed to promote completed fresh-operator control into the entry gate.

Scan 125 should therefore preserve the constitution and tighten retrieval again: seek **joint low-FTE + positive-profit + positive-OCF** current-report signals before spending deeper research effort. External revenue remains a required independent gate.
