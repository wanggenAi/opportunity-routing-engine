## F1 — France B2B e-invoicing migration

France's electronic-invoicing reform is now a live operating change, not a future compliance story. The Ministry of Economy says more than 10 million economic actors are affected. From 1 September 2026, all businesses in scope must be able to receive electronic invoices, and large and mid-sized firms must also issue through the new regime.

The objective endowment already exists inside firms: ERP/accounting systems, supplier and customer master data, invoice archives, payment records and internal approval workflows. The new regime forces those assets through a standardized external rail, creating migration, mapping, reconciliation and exception work.

The missing-edge hypothesis fails at the control-surface preflight. French tax authorities require invoice exchange and reporting through an approved platform. The official list is maintained by the administration after regulatory, security and interoperability qualification. A generic agent can help with field mapping, validation, reconciliation and connector logic, but it does not acquire approved-platform status or independent transmission rights.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F2 — U.S. prior authorization burden + FHIR transition

This is the strongest participant-authored signal in the scan.

The AMA's 2025 physician survey, published in 2026, reports about 40 prior authorizations per physician per week and about 13 hours of physician/staff time spent each week on the process. It also reports that only 24% of physicians say their EHR supports electronic prior authorization for prescription drugs and that phone remains the most common method for medical-service prior authorizations.

CMS-0057-F has already tightened operational requirements, while major API implementation requirements generally begin in 2027. The same transaction is therefore visible in two states at once: repeated manual work today and standardized FHIR-based exchange being built underneath it.

The partial-flow hypothesis is real, but the operator-rights test fails. CMS assigns the Prior Authorization API to impacted payers. Payers retain authoritative approval, denial and request-more-information decisions. Availity already documents an end-to-end CRD/DTR/PAS workflow that connects EHR-initiated discovery, documentation and authorization submission through a payer-routing FHIR gateway.

A general agent may reduce documentation or navigation burden, but without a distinct network position or payer rights that layer is substitutable and sits on top of infrastructure already being standardized.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## F3 — Stranded office reuse and conversion

The asset mismatch is also real.

CBRE's 2026 occupier survey reports that 25% of non-headquarters locations are being vacated rather than reimagined. JLL reports that conversions and redevelopments are removing office inventory, while office-to-apartment conversion activity is at record levels in 2026. JLL also announced a $113 million financing/equity package on 21 September 2026 for a 320-unit Chicago office-to-residential conversion already under construction.

The objective endowment is substantial: existing structures, locations, utility connections and embodied materials. But activation depends on whether each building can actually support another use.

Gensler's conversion work shows the boundary clearly. It has screened more than 1,300 buildings and reports that only roughly one quarter to one third are suitable. Its tooling can standardize early evaluation of context, form, floor plate, envelope and servicing, but the downstream value remains site-specific.

The decisive rights are property ownership, lender capital, municipal entitlements and construction authority. The decisive work is architecture, engineering, finance and physical redevelopment. That is not a founder-independent, machine-routable recurring unit.

**Verdict:** demoted.

## Cross-pass learning

Scans 093 and 094 together are enough to change evidence-source priority without inventing a new product mechanism.

Across six broad-current-reality formations, two recurring traps appear:

1. highly indexable rule/vendor transitions often have a preassigned control surface; and
