# Attraction Scan 052 — 2026-09-21

## Boundary

Scan 052 restarted from current recurring paid units with deterministic or machine-checkable acceptance and replaceable execution. It explicitly excluded audio annotation and the other Scan 051 verticals, and ran exact incumbent/control-surface preflight before deep research.

## Result

**Commercial promotions: 0. Retained research formations: 0. FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW: NOT_PROVEN.**

The scan tested six formation-diverse paid units:

1. **Dental ERA/EOB claim posting** — current buyers pay per claim for recurring Open Dental posting, but Open Dental already auto-downloads, matches, posts and can fully finalize standard ERAs. The residual work is exception handling.
2. **AWS funding document-set assembly** — current recurring per-document spend exists, but AWS Partner Central agents now recommend funding programs and create fund requests; remaining template assembly is generic-agent work.
3. **Google review removal** — success-fee economics are crisp, but Google owns the policy, appeal path and final removal decision.
4. **FOIA/bodycam completed case packages** — current per-case demand exists, but filing/tracking is already productized by MuckRock and the residual value remains subjective case selection, fact-checking and agency follow-up.
5. **Rare-collectible buyer-intent lists** — current buyers pay per verified public-web record, but Clay already productizes agentic web research, intent signals and structured enrichment at scale; observed unit economics are extremely compressed.
6. **UK document certification** — acceptance is crisp, but professional attestation and signature are the required value, so machine execution cannot replace the core unit.

## Structural finding

```text
RECURRING PER-UNIT PAYMENT
+
MACHINE-CHECKABLE OUTPUT
!=
UNOWNED EXECUTION CONTROL
```

The failures split into four mechanisms:

- native workflow software already executes the standard case;
- an external platform owns the decisive approval/removal state;
- a general agent already performs the public-web/template transformation;
- the human credential or subjective judgment is itself the product.

## Why nothing is retained

The strongest-looking units either collapse into native automation (Open Dental, AWS), platform-owned decision rights (Google), recurring judgment/external response dependence (FOIA), generic-agent substitution plus poor per-record economics (collectible intent), or regulated/trusted human attestation (UK certification).

No formation survives all hard floors for operator control, founder-independent execution, distinct compounding asset, evidenced economics and incumbent absorption pressure.
