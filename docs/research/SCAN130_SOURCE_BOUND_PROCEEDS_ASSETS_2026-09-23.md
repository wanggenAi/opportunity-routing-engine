# Scan 130 — Source-bound proceeds asset search (2026-09-23)

## Question

Can a small operator in the primary China execution domain acquire a transferable asset whose **existing proceeds are independently source-bound**, whose demand/payment survive transfer, and whose recurring delivery does not recreate a founder job?

## Result

No current formation cleared all gates. This is not a claim that the asset classes are economically worthless. It is a narrower repository conclusion: the current public evidence does not justify commercial promotion under the Attraction Field constitution.

### F1 — domestic domain assets

Alibaba Cloud and Juming prove current mainland trading, native payment and transferable control. Juming's live rules also make the decisive limitation explicit: third-party analytics and listing descriptions are reference material and buyers bear verification risk. Transfer rail is proven; existing operating proceeds are not.

**Verdict:** `DEMOTED_DOMAIN_TRANSFER_AND_NATIVE_SETTLEMENT_EXIST_BUT_NO_INDEPENDENTLY_SOURCE_BOUND_EXISTING_PROCEEDS_OR_TRANSFERABLE_DEMAND`.

### F2 — verified music royalty income rights

Royalty Exchange is the strongest current mechanism-level match: historical income is verified before listing and financial data are provided for diligence. MCSC proves that music-use royalties are a real, repeated China cashflow rail. But the current directly purchasable verified-income market found is foreign, and this scan did not establish a compliant mainland-small-operator acquisition/settlement path or a complete transfer of all relevant distribution shares.

**Verdict:** `DEMOTED_VERIFIED_ROYALTY_CASHFLOW_EXISTS_BUT_NO_PROVEN_SMALL_OPERATOR_MAINLAND_ACQUISITION_SETTLEMENT_AND_COMPLETE_RIGHTS_TRANSFER_PATH`.

### F3 — China patent license income

CNIPA proves formal transfer, public open-license price terms and a large real licensing market. What is missing is asset-level binding: aggregate contract statistics and an open-license declaration do not establish a current small purchasable patent with settled recurring proceeds and low recurring technical/legal work.

**Verdict:** `DEMOTED_DOMESTIC_TRANSFER_AND_LICENSE_INFRASTRUCTURE_EXISTS_BUT_NO_SMALL_ASSET_SPECIFIC_VERIFIED_EXISTING_PROCEEDS_OR_LOW_LABOR_COMMERCIALIZATION`.

### F4 — domestic film copyright rights

Shenzhen Cultural Property Exchange proves a current formal mainland copyright transaction venue. Its public surface does not expose a small current asset with verified historical distribution proceeds, automatic future settlement and low recurring rights/sales work. Current exchange guidance also rejects public share-like securitization shortcuts.

**Verdict:** `DEMOTED_FORMAL_MAINLAND_COPYRIGHT_TRANSFER_EXISTS_BUT_NO_PUBLIC_SMALL_ASSET_SPECIFIC_VERIFIED_PROCEEDS_OR_FOUNDER_LIGHT_POST_TRANSFER_FLOW`.
