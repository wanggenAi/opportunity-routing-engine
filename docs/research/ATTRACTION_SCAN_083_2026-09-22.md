## Boundary

Scan 083 changes the evidence object after two broad actor/state-change passes. A formation is not examined merely because one actor has pain. It must begin with **repeated observable A↔B partial flow** between replenishing actor populations before any product category or mechanism is named.

No job, gig, RFQ or procurement feed is used as the discovery ontology. No router, marketplace, integration or other mechanism is inherited.

## Result

The bilateral-flow lens materially improved evidence quality: all three formations have real recurring cross-actor transfer or booking behavior. None survives exact current control-surface preflight.

| Formation | Observed A↔B flow | Why it fails promotion |
|---|---|---|
| Surplus food donors → hunger-relief agencies | Billions of pounds of food are repeatedly rescued from businesses to food-bank networks; pickup timing and logistics are operationally real | MealConnect already owns national donor/agency matching at no cost, Too Good To Go spans commercial surplus management, and the residual is physical logistics/cold chain. |
| Surplus-soil construction sites → fill-demand sites | One project pays to remove material while another pays to acquire it; current platforms expose live HAVE/NEED positions | Soil Connect and Earth Exchange already own map/location matching, quotes, reporting, paid subscriptions and managed compliance/haul support. |
| Private charger hosts → drivers without home charging | Co Charger supports recurring weekly bookings; JustPark pays hosts monthly and pairs fleet drivers with nearby private charging | Existing networks already own host density, booking, installation, access and settlement. |

## F1 — surplus food donor to hunger-relief pickup

This is a high-volume bilateral flow rather than a hypothetical market. Feeding America reported in April 2026 that MealConnect had facilitated more than seven billion pounds of food rescue since launch. Its Food Rescue Fund says more than two billion pounds came through retail donations in the prior year while another 800 million pounds at retailers remained available to rescue. Starbucks FoodShare operates daily donation flows and now folds collection into replenishment routes.

The connection pressure is clear: edible surplus expires, agencies have varying pickup/storage capacity, and logistics must be coordinated quickly.

But the digital missing edge is already occupied. MealConnect performs donor-to-agency matching nationally at no cost; Too Good To Go offers an enterprise platform that spans expiry management, markdowns, consumer resale and charity redistribution. What remains difficult is largely physical pickup, cold chain, storage and local network capacity rather than a distinct software-only operator asset.

## F2 — construction surplus soil to fill-demand sites

This is the cleanest economic imbalance in Scan 083. Soil Connect describes the condition directly: one construction project pays to remove excess soil while another pays to bring soil in. The value of connection is distance-sensitive, and a successful match can avoid both disposal and procurement/haul costs.

The flow is already operationalized. Soil Connect exposes location-based HAVE/NEED posts, matching, reporting, hauling support and 2026 quote/benchmark tooling; it states average dirt/haul savings of 34%. Earth Exchange operates the same surplus/requirement model in the UK and lists paid regional subscriptions plus managed Materials Management Plan support.

The formation therefore proves the bilateral signal but not white space. The exact matching/control layer is already a named current category with live networks, pricing and execution support.

## F3 — private charger host to driver without home charging

Here the bilateral flow includes direct recurring payments. Co Charger supports one-off and weekly recurring bookings between EV drivers and nearby hosts. JustPark FleetCharge pairs fleet drivers with driveway owners, installs charging hardware, manages access and reports guaranteed monthly host earnings around £1,000 per year plus energy reimbursement.

The connection pressure is attractive: proximity, availability, charger compatibility, trusted property access and settlement all matter, and most of the coordination layer is machine-executable.

But the exact control plane is already present. Co Charger handles neighbor discovery/booking/payment; JustPark goes further into installation, reserved access, enterprise fleet management and settlement. A new operator would need a genuinely different control right or network asset, not another matcher.

## Learning

The bilateral-flow lens is better than single-actor pain because it requires evidence of both sides and the attempted connection:

```text
REPEATED A↔B FLOW
+ REPLENISHING POPULATIONS
+ CONNECTION PRESSURE
!=
UNOWNED CONNECTION EDGE
```

One pass is not enough to reject the evidence object or derive a new mechanism. Scan 084 will run a second independent formation-diverse bilateral-partial-flow pass before any stronger boundary is inferred.
