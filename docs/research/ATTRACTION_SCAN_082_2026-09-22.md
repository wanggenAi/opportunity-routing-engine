## F1 — U.S. unpaid family caregiver coordination

NORC's 2026 research shows a large, replenishing actor population: more than 63 million Americans provide unpaid care. In its December 2025 probability-panel survey, 48% of unpaid caregivers used at least one technology support, 27% used medication-management tools and 19% used care-coordination platforms. More intensive caregivers used technology more often.

This is real coordination behavior, not an invented need. Caregivers manage appointments, medication, transportation, household tasks and communication across people and systems. But the bottleneck does not collapse to one machine-owned edge. NORC reports broad adoption barriers; CareMobi users value centralization but still ask for stronger team coordination; and current platforms such as Cariloop and Wellthy already monetize employer-sponsored coordination and concierge support.

The non-substitutable residual is largely the part a generic agent cannot safely own: consent/proxy authority, provider navigation, family context and exception handling. That is important value, but it is not yet a narrow founder-independent operator asset.

## F2 — California wildfire hardening to insurance eligibility

This formation starts from a household asset-state transition. On 16 September 2026, State Farm General said it filed guidelines that would allow qualifying IBHS Wildfire Prepared homes to be considered for new policies in California. IBHS had already expanded the designation program to 14 states in April 2026, with third-party verification of mitigation actions. California regulators also reported broader insurer expansion and mitigation-linked discounts during 2026.

The state-dependent value jump is strong: physical hardening can improve insurability and terms. But the operator surface is not unowned. IBHS controls the designation standard and verification framework; insurers retain underwriting and pricing authority; and the decisive interventions are physical construction, vegetation management, inspection and ongoing maintenance.

Digital guidance, document handling and scheduling are assistive layers, not ownership of the value transition.

## F3 — Used EV battery health to resale trust and value

Cox Automotive's 2026 market monitoring shows growing used-EV availability as off-lease returns and trade-ins increase. Battery condition is a high-value hidden state because it affects usable range, buyer confidence and residual value.

The behavior is already visible: Autotrader introduced VIN-level battery-health scores on listings; Recurrent lets owners create battery reports from connected-vehicle data; AVILOO offers independent battery certificates, dealer workflows and API integration into exchanges and remarketing systems.

This is highly machine-delegatable and economically meaningful, but that is precisely why the control surface is already productized. A new operator would need a distinct data right, acceptance rail or settlement advantage not established by the current evidence.
