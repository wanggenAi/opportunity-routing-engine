# Attraction Scan 060 — 2026-09-21

## Boundary

Scan 060 changes the evidence source rather than the opportunity mechanism. Job postings cannot be the sole payer signal. This pass starts from direct non-role buying behavior: monthly subscriptions, per-episode purchases, flat-rate projects and managed outcome services across unrelated categories.

## Result

**Commercial promotions: 0. Retained research formations: 0. FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW: NOT_PROVEN.**

Six formation-diverse productized purchases were tested:

1. **WordPress monthly care** — WP Buffs sells $89–$359/month per-site plans and white-label recurring care at scale. Standard backup/update/uptime/security work is already automatable; the managed-service residual is human edits, emergencies and compatibility/custom-code exceptions.
2. **General virtual assistant subscription** — Wing sells dedicated assistant capacity at $699/month for 80 hours and $999/month for 160 hours. This proves direct recurring spend but the purchased unit is explicitly human time and contextual execution.
3. **Unlimited design subscription** — ManyPixels sells $699/$1,199/$1,399 monthly plans with unlimited requests/revisions and designer/project-manager capacity. Canva AI already generates designs and presentation drafts; paid residual value is taste, brand interpretation and revision judgment.
4. **Podcast postproduction** — We Edit Podcasts sells $180/$320/$550+ per episode with month-to-month plans. Descript now automates standard cleanup, filler/retake removal, sound enhancement, chaptering, show notes and clips; premium residual work is storytelling and editorial judgment.
5. **Managed QA as a Service** — BetterQA starts at €1,400/month per engineer, while QA Wolf already combines AI agents with dedicated engineers and guarantees 80%+ E2E coverage. This is an exact mature managed-outcome category.
6. **On-demand CAD/design** — Cad Crowd sells flat-rate and prepaid-hour CAD outcomes and reports 33,000+ designs delivered. Autodesk Fusion automates 2D drawing generation and generative design; remaining high-value work carries project-specific engineering judgment and liability.

## Structural finding

```text
DIRECT PRODUCTIZED-SERVICE PURCHASE
PROVES MONEY MOTION
BUT OFTEN OBSERVES AN ALREADY-FORMED CATEGORY
```

Scan 060 improves payer evidence quality compared with job-posting scans, but exposes a different bias: if discovery starts from a seller that has already packaged and priced the outcome, the search is often starting **inside an incumbent category**.

Across the six cases, one of two patterns dominates:

- the productized service is essentially managed human capacity with procurement friction removed; or
- native AI/software has absorbed the standard execution layer, while the service monetizes exceptions, taste, domain judgment or accountability.

Neither pattern creates a distinct founder-independent operator layer merely because customers demonstrably pay.
