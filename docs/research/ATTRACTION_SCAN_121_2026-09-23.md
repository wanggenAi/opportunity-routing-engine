# ATTRACTION_SCAN_121 — Economics-First Durability Repeat

Date: 2026-09-23  
Status: COMPLETE — zero retained formations / zero commercial promotions

## Why this scan exists

Scan 120 showed that current economics-first retrieval is more efficient than acquisition-first research and produced one much deeper near-survivor. Scan 121 repeats that order independently on fresh formations before changing the boundary:

```text
CURRENT SAME-ENTITY REPORT
+ CURRENT / LATEST DIRECTLY DISCLOSED LOW FTE
+ POSITIVE EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ UNAMBIGUOUS POSITIVE OCF
THEN:
  COMPLETED FRESH-OPERATOR-REPRODUCIBLE CONTROL BEFORE REPORTING PERIOD
  + EXISTING STANDARDIZED REPEATABLE NON-PROJECT REVENUE
  + BOUNDED CONTROL / OPERATING / WORKING CAPITAL
  + NONCOMMODITY MACHINE-OPERABLE CONTROL
=> POSSIBLE VERIFIED FOUNDER-LIGHT CONTROL CASHFLOW
```

No product, vertical or acquisition ontology is inherited.

- Current 2026 H1 revenue: **RMB141,509.43**.

- Current OCF: **-RMB116,902.77**.

## Scan-level conclusion

Zero formations are retained and zero commercial candidates are promoted.

The durability result is stronger than simply observing three weak companies:

1. **Very low headcount is independent from economic quality.** Six employees can still coexist with loss and cash burn.
2. **Positive OCF is independent from positive profit.** Xinrui Yingcheng's cashflow improvement is driven by contract advances while the entity remains loss-making.
3. **Positive accounting profit is independent from positive operating cash conversion.** Jinante passes revenue and profit but fails consolidated OCF.
4. **Early gates prevent wasted control research.** None of the three deserves acquisition/control-history deepening because each already fails the same-period economic conjunction.

Scan 120 and Scan 121 together therefore support an efficiency refinement without changing the commercial constitution.

## Next evidence boundary — Scan 122

Use the full current economic conjunction as a **primary-formation admission gate**:

```text
CURRENT SAME-ENTITY REPORT
+ INTRINSICALLY LOW CURRENT/LATEST FTE
+ POSITIVE EXTERNAL REVENUE
+ POSITIVE NET PROFIT
+ UNAMBIGUOUS POSITIVE OCF
=> PRIMARY FORMATION MAY BE OPENED
ELSE
=> RECORD AS EXCLUDED OBSERVATION AND STOP
```

Only admitted survivors should receive reverse-checks for:

- completed fresh-operator-reproducible control before the reporting period;
- standardized repeatable non-project revenue;
- bounded acquisition/control, operating and working capital;
- noncommodity machine-operable control;
- no recurring founder acquisition, expert delivery or physical/project execution.

This is a retrieval-efficiency change, not a product or vertical ontology.
