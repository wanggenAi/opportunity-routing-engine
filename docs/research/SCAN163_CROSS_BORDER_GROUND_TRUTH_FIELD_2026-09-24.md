- Freelancer already owns marketplace matching for Local Jobs.
- ChinaLocalAgent sells scoped local execution and factory/site visits.
- QIMA/CIS and specialist inspectors own higher-trust professional inspection tiers.
- 现场核验云 already owns a domestic task-dispatch surface.

Therefore the missing edge is **not** “someone can visit a factory.”

The remaining hypothesis is narrower:

> Can a recurring overseas verification Demand Pump contract one China-wide normalized execution layer instead of repeatedly recruiting one worker per city, while the China layer routes replaceable local executors and owns evidence acceptance?

That is the decisive unknown.
