# Discovery Cycle 001 — Case Mechanism Library

Date: 2026-09-10
Status: `MECHANISM MINING / SUCCESS + FAILURE CONTROLS`

Do not copy industries. Extract hooks, resource structures, payer logic, repeat loops and failure modes.

## M01 — Property as trusted demand pump → lifecycle services

Case: 万物云 / community and housing asset services.

Observed mechanism:
- existing property relationship controls a high-frequency resident touchpoint;
- the same installed community base routes leads into brokerage, renovation and repair;
- repair products are increasingly standardized;
- 2026 H1 repair revenue grew strongly and a large share of renovation leads came from property channels.

First-class insight:

> **A trusted installed-base channel can be more valuable than owning the delivery labor.**

Hook class: `TRUSTED_DISTRIBUTION + EXISTING_DEMAND_BASE`.
Demand pump: managed communities / housing lifecycle events.
Resources: property front door + specialist service delivery.
Risk: large incumbents prove mechanism but also prove competition; a new entrant must operate a narrow backend or underserved property segment, not build a generic consumer platform.

Sources:
- https://www.xinhuanet.com/house/20260814/cccc6a5bbfad4c29827284b956f51b35/c.html
- https://www.onewo.com/about/overview/index.html

## M02 — Property front end + multi-provider backend dispatch

Case: Shanghai Changning 2026 `物业+养老` service package.

Observed mechanism:
- property is the single front door;
- backend service resources are coordinated centrally;
- packages are priced monthly at RMB399 / RMB990;
- 50 service items are available across home service and support categories.

Replicable mechanism:

> **Do not force residents to find each provider. Use a trusted local front end and orchestrate specialist delivery behind it.**

Hook class: `ONE_FRONT_DOOR + BUNDLED_CAPABILITY`.
Risk: elderly/home/medical-related work has elevated legal/trust/safety requirements. A new model should start with low-risk non-medical household services.

Source:
- https://www.shcn.gov.cn/col7343/20260428/1308931.html

## M04 — Event traffic → merchant spend (`票根经济`)

Case: Xuzhou sports/tourism event linkage.

Observed mechanism:
- event creates concentrated high-intent traffic;
- ticket/stub acts as identity/proof token;
- participating merchants offer benefit;
- public promotion links audience to consumption.

Hook class: `PRE-AGGREGATED DEMAND`.
Demand pump: recurring events / visitor flows.
Failure/control:
- public promotion, coupons and event novelty materially contaminate private payer economics;
- merchant participation does not prove merchant willingness to pay an independent orchestrator.

## M05 — Youth-space activation through externally aggregated demand

Case: Xuzhou `青年夜校+青年小店`.

Observed mechanism:
- local program finds already-interesting youth-facing spaces;
- curated activities fill idle/off-peak capacity;
- one cafe was reported to see material revenue uplift;
- activity participation creates both traffic and social experience.

Hook class: `IDLE_CAPACITY + AGGREGATED PARTICIPANTS`.
Failure/control:
- official-program traffic and promotion are contaminating interventions;
- do not infer independent merchant payment or sustainable private acquisition economics.

## M06 — Installed equipment base → recurring aftermarket economy

Case: Xuzhou engineering machinery strategy / XCMG lifecycle service.

Observed mechanism:
- equipment sale creates an installed base;
- installed base continuously generates parts, maintenance, repair, remanufacturing, financing, resale and O&M events;
- Xuzhou is explicitly building global spare-parts, remanufacturing, second-hand and digital O&M infrastructure.

Replicable mechanism:

> **The best Demand Pump can be an installed asset base, not a marketing channel.**

Hook classes: `UPTIME`, `SPEED`, `VERIFIED_PART/PROVIDER`, `LIFECYCLE_COST`.
Risk: high technical trust, liability, access rights and expert requirements.

## M07 — Verification layer creates value in used-equipment trade

Cases: XCMG Used / MechLink.

Observed mechanism:
- raw listing is insufficient for high-value machinery;
- trusted seller/device verification and third-party inspection reduce information asymmetry;
- logistics/customs/transaction support can be bundled after matching.

Hook class: `VERIFIED INFORMATION + TRUST + TRANSACTION COMPLETION`.
Orchestration value: inspection routing, evidence pack, logistics, acceptance, replacement/claim process.
Risk: incumbents already exist and expert inspection cannot be faked; local niche or non-core layer is required.

Sources:
- https://www.xcmgused.com/
- https://www.mechandlink.com/zh/about

## M08 — Flexible-work routing can materially reduce enterprise acquisition cost

Case: Suzhou `零工巧匠` public employment platform.

Observed mechanism:
- manufacturing temporary work is a high-frequency demand class;
- platform reportedly reduced job-search time and enterprise temporary-recruitment cost and improved fill speed;
- matching rules are updated using performance/settlement data.

Hook class: `READY SUPPLY + FAST FILL + MEASURABLE COST SAVING`.
Failure/control:
- employment matching/labor dispatch has significant legal/compliance boundaries;
- public/free employment services can crowd out a private fee model.

## M09 — Government/park acts as trust bridge for micro-enterprise demand

Case: Xuzhou OPC communities / entrepreneurship parks.

Observed mechanism:
- individual technical founders often have capability but no brand/cases/customer access;
- park/community matching events provide a trusted introduction channel;
- one public story describes a technical founder moving from rejected free trials to signed business projects after matching support.

Hook class: `TRUSTED DEMAND ACCESS`.
Failure/control:
- the public institution may already provide matching for free;
- direct payer for an independent orchestration layer remains unknown.

## M10 — Pet sitting: highly fragmented supply becomes transact-able through standardization

Case: `TA岛宠物` public reporting.

Observed mechanism:
- founder first observed holiday pet-care demand personally;
- initial bookings validated the demand;
- sitter supply was standardized, with evidence/live reporting and platform trust controls;
- service expanded across multiple cities and high holiday volumes.

Hook class: `LOCAL FLEXIBLE SUPPLY + TRUST STANDARDIZATION`.
Demand pump: pet owners' travel/absence cycles.
Failure/control:
- home access, pet safety, liability and holiday seasonality are material;
- not a good first model without mature trust controls.

## M11 — Public procurement feed as a continuous institutional-demand sensor

Case: Xuzhou public resource trading platform.

## M12 — Trade-in: public subsidy as temporary demand accelerator

Case: Xuzhou consumer trade-in 2026.

Observed mechanism:
- subsidy lowers the decision threshold for durable replacement;
- market flow creates secondary work in delivery, old-item removal, recycling, installation and after-sales.

Hook class: `POLICY-LOWERED PRICE`.

> **Policy-created demand is not automatically regenerative demand.**

## M15 — Property/housing demand loop is reinforced by stock-market turnover

Observed mechanism:
- every sale, lease, vacancy, move-in/out and aging property produces cleaning, repair, refresh, furnishing, inspection and handover tasks;
- a property/community/agency with many units can aggregate these events.

Hook class: `PORTFOLIO DEMAND + BOUNDED SERVICE BUNDLE`.
Risk: generic cleaning/repair is easy to bypass; orchestration must own service standard, acceptance, replacement, complaint resolution or recurring channel economics.

# Failure / anti-pattern library

1. broad marketplace before transaction density;
2. high social heat with no payer commitment;
3. founder personally performing every job;
4. low-ticket service with high acquisition/support cost;
5. public subsidy mistaken for independent willingness to pay;
6. free public alternative ignored;
7. contact introduction with no continuing governance value;
8. regulated/high-trust work entered before trust controls;
9. supply pool recruited before one exact demand exists;
10. large TAM mistaken for accessible demand.

# Cycle 001 transfer rule

A new opportunity gets extra attention when it combines at least three mechanisms:

```text
recurring demand pump
+ optioned/borrowed resources
+ bounded acceptance
+ trust/replacement value
+ low upfront capital
```

It still requires a real payer test.
