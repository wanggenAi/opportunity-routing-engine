# ATTRACTION_SCAN_076 — Post-replacement recurring residual-spend causal test

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Commercial promotions:** 0  
**Retained research formations:** 0  
**FIRST_EXTERNAL_VALUE_FLOW:** NOT_PROVEN

## Boundary

Scan 076 tests the stronger payer signal left by Scan 075:

> after a replacement or new platform is already live, does the buyer separately keep paying recurring money for the **same unresolved outcome**?

The scan does not treat ordinary support, maintenance, monitoring, implementation, training, transition, expert escalation or physical service as white space merely because it is recurring. A qualifying seed needs a buyer-side causal chain that connects the post-live residual payment to the same outcome the replacement failed to resolve.

No mechanism shape is inherited.

## Result

Six formation-diverse post-live residual-spend patterns were pressure-tested. None survives the commercial floor.

| Formation | What is actually paid after go-live | Why it closes |
|---|---|---|
| Olathe Workday AMS | Three more years of specialist production support after an initial three-year AMS term | Buyer describes integration builds, maintenance, deployments and advanced troubleshooting; no same-unresolved-outcome replacement causal chain, and Workday AMS is already a mature specialist category |
| MPI ArcGIS managed service | Monitoring, maintenance and support for a new ArcGIS-on-Azure geospatial platform | Clear new-platform plus recurring spend, but the notice shows ordinary platform lifecycle operations, not a business outcome left unresolved by the replacement |
| Humberside Salesforce/MuleSoft estate | L1-L3 support, triage, monitoring, maintenance, code changes and service management | Mature application managed service with platform-specific operational accountability; no post-replacement failure causality |
| Saskatoon SAP S/4HANA AMS | Troubleshooting, updates, monitoring and performance optimization | The buyer explicitly buys specialized SAP management; recurring spend is real but is standard AMS rather than an unowned operator layer |
| DLA ChargePoint EV charging | OEM maintenance plans, network services, remote monitoring, repair dispatch and cybersecurity compliance | OEM-bound and technician/physical/compliance dependent; no replacement-left-outcome-unresolved chain |
| Perth & Kinross BMS | Upgrade, improvement, servicing and maintenance under the same lifecycle contract | Fails the separate-residual gate and remains site-specific physical building-controls work |

## Cross-scan conclusion

Scan 076 changes the evidence standard again:

```text
NEW / REPLACEMENT PLATFORM IS LIVE
→ RECURRING THIRD-PARTY OR OEM SPEND CONTINUES
!=
SAME BUSINESS OUTCOME REMAINS UNRESOLVED
```

The observed recurring money is real, but it mostly buys one of four things:

```text
PLATFORM-SPECIFIC EXPERT ESCALATION
+ LIFECYCLE OPERATIONS / MAINTENANCE
+ OEM / PARTNER SUPPORT
+ PHYSICAL / SITE-SPECIFIC SERVICE
```

Those are established service categories. None of the examined evidence shows a buyer saying, in effect:

> the new platform/provider is already live, the original outcome still fails, and we therefore keep a **separate recurring paid contract** specifically to close that same unresolved outcome.

That buyer-authored causal chain is the next discriminating signal.

## Next evidence boundary

Scan 077 should require all of the following before deeper retention:

- the new/replacement platform or provider is demonstrably live;
- the buyer explicitly documents that the **same target outcome remains unresolved** after go-live;
- the buyer separately pays a recurring residual contract because of that remaining outcome;
- the residual is not ordinary OEM/partner AMS, maintenance, monitoring, enhancement, training or transition;
- the residual is not authority-only, expert-judgment-only or physical-only lifecycle work;
- exact incumbent, founder-independence, data/action-rights, normalized-margin and generic-agent-substitutability preflight still apply;
- Scan 060–076 formations and primary signals remain excluded.

## Drift audit

- The scan did not infer white space from contract size, duration or the existence of a second provider.
- Platform support and physical maintenance were treated as downstream money-motion evidence, not as a new discovery ontology.
- Samples span enterprise back office, geospatial operations, police application estates, municipal ERP, EV charging infrastructure and building controls.
- No candidate was promoted merely because the buyer had already spent money twice.
- The next boundary strengthens causal evidence; it does not prescribe routing, agents, marketplaces or a software mechanism.
