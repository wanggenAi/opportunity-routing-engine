# EXP-006 Xuzhou Field Pack — First Payer-Commitment Run

Status: `READY TO EXECUTE / NO-CODE`

Date: 2026-09-10

Canonical experiment: `docs/EXPERIMENT_006_XUZHOU_YOUTH_MICRO_EXPERIENCE_PAYER.md`

## 1. Single purpose of this field run

Do **not** go out to ask whether young people like social activities.

Resolve one commercial unknown:

> For one exact, low-risk youth micro-experience, will a participant place a real deposit, will a venue commit real economic value for qualified foot traffic, or will neither side pay?

The field run is successful even if the answer is `FAIL`, as long as the payer uncertainty is materially reduced.

## 2. Fixed first format

To avoid mixing payer evidence with multiple activity concepts, use one format only for the first run:

> **10-person beginner coffee-flavor micro-experience + guided low-pressure interaction**

Provisional structure:
- public cafe / suitable coffee venue;
- 60–90 minutes;
- maximum 10 participants;
- venue/barista provides the coffee-specific capability if the venue agrees;
- organizer defines group size, booking, check-in, interaction structure and feedback;
- no dating/matchmaking promise;
- no alcohol;
- no sensitive personal-profile collection;
- no paid advertising before payer proof.

If no suitable venue can deliver this format, record `CAPABILITY FAIL` for this format. Do **not** silently switch to crafts, board games or photography inside the same test. A different format requires a new test record.

## 3. Price / commitment design

### Participant arm

Offer:
- test price: `RMB 49 / person`;
- reservation method: real refundable deposit / booking payment;
- event runs only after the defined minimum is reached;
- if the event cannot be produced, deposit is returned.

Participant payer PASS:
> >=6 qualified participants place real deposits for the same event/date/venue structure.

A verbal `49 yuan is acceptable` is Level 2, not PASS.

### Venue arm

Ask for one of:
- >=`RMB 200` cash sponsorship / organizer fee;
- >=`RMB 200` minimum guarantee;
- a revenue-share structure with >=`RMB 200` guaranteed economic contribution.

Venue payer PASS:
> >=1 qualified venue makes a real written/approved monetary commitment.

Free space, discounts or free coffee are useful `RESOURCE / CAPABILITY` evidence but do **not** pass the payer gate by themselves.

### Cost discipline

Before Level 3 payer evidence:
- organizer cash spend cap: `RMB 100`;
- no app/site build;
- no bulk materials;
- no paid influencer;
- no prepaid venue rental unless covered by committed payer economics.

## 4. Hypothesis

```text
Young adults already spend on small leisure / interest experiences
+
Some local venues have underused time / space and value qualified youth traffic
+
A bounded hosted experience may create value for both sides
↓
At least one side should make a real economic commitment before production
```

## 5. Critical UNKNOWN

`G1 PAYER CLARITY`:
- participant?
- venue?
- blended?
- neither?

Secondary unknowns:
- does the venue actually have an off-peak capacity problem?
- is RMB49 above or below real participant willingness for this exact format?
- does the venue value traffic enough to pay cash rather than only contribute unused space?
- do public/free youth activities make the private experience unnecessary?

## 6. Field clusters — where to go

These are **public-report anchors / search areas**, not guaranteed current storefront addresses. Verify the current storefront in a map app before departure.

### Cluster A — Gulou central commercial / Huanglou area

Public Xuzhou/Jiangsu reporting has described `空山半闲咖啡` as a youth-night-school-linked cafe in the Gulou/Huanglou central commercial area, with older reporting placing it near the `1818美食广场` area.

Why this cluster matters:
- directly connected to the local `night school + youth shop` pattern;
- public reporting attributes measurable revenue/customer outcomes to this kind of venue reuse;
- surrounding cafes / youth shops provide comparison interviews even if the named venue is unavailable.

Field target:
- 3–4 cafe / youth-shop owners or managers with authority to discuss weak hours and event economics.

### Cluster B — Yunlong / Pengcheng Qili demonstration area

Public reporting places `徐州小鲁班` in the `彭城七里示范段` and describes it as a youth-shop / craft-space participant in youth-night-school activity.

Why this cluster matters:
- evidence that nontraditional retail/service space can be converted into youth experience capacity;
- useful comparison against cafes even though the first test format remains coffee.

Field target:
- 1–2 operators to learn how activities changed traffic, spend, repeat behavior and what they would actually pay an external organizer for.

Do not change the first event into a craft event during this test; use these conversations as payer-mechanism evidence.

### Cluster C — one university-adjacent youth activity area

Do not wander across campuses broadly. Select one convenient university-adjacent commercial cluster after the venue/date hypothesis is concrete.

Field target:
- 10–15 participants aged roughly 20–35 who can name a recent paid leisure/interest activity or comparable attendance behavior.

The participant sample should not consist only of friends who want to support the operator.

## 7. Venue interview — 7 questions

Ask the decision-maker, not only a front-line employee when payment authority matters.

1. `你们一周里哪几个固定时段最空？最近四周都是这样吗？`
2. `你们现在为了拉新客，真实花钱做过什么？团购、达人、平台折扣、活动，最近一次花了多少？`
3. `以前办过小活动吗？来了多少人？当场客单、后续复购有没有记录？`
4. `如果我能组织 8–10 个符合你目标客群的第一次到店用户，这批客人对你值多少钱？为什么？`
5. `如果做一场 60–90 分钟、10 人以内的咖啡风味体验，你愿意提供什么：场地、咖啡师、折扣、现金？`
6. `如果需要你先承诺 200 元活动支持费 / 最低保底，你现在能不能决定？不能的话，谁能决定，什么数据才会让他同意？`
7. `什么结果出现以后，你下个月愿意再付一次？新客消费额、复购、加微信、会员、还是别的？`

Record exact refusal reason if the answer is no.

## 8. Participant interview — 7 questions

Do not start by pitching the event.

1. `你上一次自己花钱参加线下休闲/兴趣活动是什么？什么时候，多少钱？`
2. `最近一个周末或下班后，你本来想出去但最后没去的活动是什么？为什么没去？`
3. `你参加这种小活动最怕什么：尴尬、没人一起、内容水、陌生人、安全、价格、时间，还是别的？`
4. `10 人左右、60–90 分钟，对你来说合适还是太小/太长？`
5. `如果是这一场“入门咖啡风味体验 + 有主持的轻互动”，你真正想得到的是咖啡知识、放松、认识人、还是单纯新鲜感？`
6. `同一场活动 29 / 49 / 79 元，你会在哪个价格开始拒绝？你最近类似消费能对照吗？`
7. `如果时间地点确定、49 元、满 6 人成行，现在你愿不愿意付一个可退订金占位？如果不愿意，具体卡在哪里？`

The last question is the commercial test. Do not replace it with `有兴趣加群吗`.

## 9. Qualification rules

### Qualified participant
At least one is true:
- paid for a leisure / class / workshop / board-game / city-walk / sports / similar experience in the last 90 days;
- actively searched/bookmarked/planned a comparable activity recently;
- repeatedly uses paid cafes/venues for the same after-work/weekend outcome.

### Qualified venue
Must have:
- a real public venue suitable for a low-risk 8–10 person activity;
- recurring underused capacity or a concrete customer-acquisition objective;
- access to owner/manager/payment decision-maker or a clear route to one.

A venue that is already full at the target time has little `TIME_GAP` and should not be forced into the sample.

## 10. What counts as PASS / FAIL

### PASS — participant payer
- >=6 qualified participants;
- same defined event;
- same price;
- real deposit/payment;
- no fake/friendly bookings counted.

### PASS — venue payer
- >=1 qualified venue;
- >=RMB200 real cash / minimum-guaranteed economic commitment;
- authority confirmed.

### PARTIAL
Examples:
- participants pay RMB29 but reject RMB49;
- venue contributes real variable cost/space but zero cash;
- venue will pay only after one event proves conversion;
- participants want the event but date/time blocks commitment.

Record as partial and redesign one variable only.

### FAIL
Current form fails if:
- 15 qualified participant conversations produce zero deposits at the lowest viable tested price; AND
- 8 qualified venue conversations produce zero monetary commitment;
- or free/public alternatives solve the same outcome sufficiently;
- or venue acquisition + participant acquisition + coordination cost cannot plausibly fit the event gross contribution.

## 12. First field-run stopping rule

Do not continue interviewing merely to increase sample count after the decisive evidence appears.

Stop and update the experiment when one of these occurs:
- participant Level 3 threshold passes;
- venue Level 3 threshold passes;
- both arms hit their fail sample;
- a new hard safety/legal/transaction constraint invalidates the format.

If one arm passes, proceed to **one** real event, not a platform build.
