# Discovery Cycle 002 — Preliminary Xuzhou Research Ranking

Date: 2026-09-12
Status: `PRELIMINARY / RESEARCH PRIORITY ONLY`
Source: `docs/research/DISCOVERY_CYCLE_002_XUZHOU_LATENT_VALUE_2026-09-12.md`

This ranking does **not** declare a business winner. It ranks where the engine should spend its next unit of evidence-acquisition effort.

## Current research order

### 1. Manufacturing Digital Continuity / Exception Routing

Why first:
- strongest intersection of current Xuzhou structural change and operator capability;
- city-scale transformation wave already moved into implementation/acceptance;
- potentially repeatable small technical exceptions rather than one large software sale;
- execution can plausibly be decomposed into bounded CapabilityUnits;
- operator can own architecture/QA/routing rather than permanent coding.

What would kill it:
- approved service providers have no paid overflow;
- all work is contractually internal/non-delegable;
- enterprises have no recurring post-go-live friction;
- task volumes are too sparse;
- economics require founder-heavy execution.

Next evidence: A1 + A2.

### 2. Engineering-Machinery Lifecycle Digital Service Orchestration

Why second:
- directly aligned with Xuzhou's strongest industrial cluster;
- structural move from manufacturing-only toward lifecycle services;
- local and international service networks create potentially fragmented tasks;
- operator's systems, database, integration and cross-context experience may be unusually useful in designing the route.

What would kill it:
- value is already fully internalized by large OEM/service networks;
- external actor access is unavailable;
- no repeatable small capability slots exist;
- confidentiality/trust costs dominate economics.

Next evidence: B1.

### 3. AI Problem Translation / Measurable Micro-Automation

Why third:
- strong policy and industrial direction;
- software/IT services are locally growing quickly;
- operator has enough technical depth to distinguish real automation value from AI theater.

Why not first:
- likely a capability layer inside #1/#2 rather than a standalone wedge;
- payer/data/access evidence is currently weak.

Next evidence: observe actual operational friction first; only introduce AI when it measurably improves the route.

### 4. Experience-Consumption / Merchant Utilization Orchestration

Why retained:
- Xuzhou retail and tourism/service consumption are objectively strong;
- external visitor flow, night economy and event consumption create real demand circulation.

Why below industrial fields:
- operator advantage is less differentiated;
- high risk of collapsing into manual merchant BD, local sales or generic platform building.

Promote only if a hidden data/routing/trust problem is found that can be systematized and delegated.

### 5. SME Digital-Commerce Capability Activation

Retained because online retail is growing and many local firms may have underused product/domain assets.

Downgrade if it becomes generic agency work, content production or founder-led account operations.

### 6. Silver-Life Service Coordination

Large, recurring, socially important demand field, but current trust/regulatory/payer complexity is high and the operator's unique technical-system advantage is less clear.

Keep scanning; do not force entry.

## Evidence acquisition order

```text
A1 service-provider overflow
A2 enterprise post-go-live friction
B1 engineering-machinery lifecycle micro-friction
C1 AI/data task only after real workflow friction exists
D1 consumer/merchant routing only if a non-sales structural blocker appears
```

## Decision rule

A candidate can move from `LATENT_VALUE_HYPOTHESIS` toward canonical Need/Resource/Blocker evidence only when reality supplies:

```text
repeated observed behavior/friction
+ exact actor
+ measurable economic consequence
+ identifiable payer or payer hypothesis with evidence
+ compatible resource/capability
+ reason the exchange is not already happening
```

No operator-fit score can override missing market truth.
