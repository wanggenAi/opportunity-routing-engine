## 1. What changed in this batch

This batch is the first post-reset attempt to use the repository doctrine in reality rather than merely describe it.

The search did **not** begin with:
- an RFQ;
- an outsourcing request;
- a supplier catalogue;
- the operator's IT skills;
- a procurement project;
- an existing software product.

It began with the locked sequence:

```text
MACRO / MONEY FLOW
→ PSYCHOLOGY / BEHAVIOR
→ INDUSTRY STRUCTURE
→ ACTOR STATE / CHANGE
→ HIDDEN OR UNDERRECOGNIZED VALUE
→ COMPLEMENTARY ACTOR STATE
→ TRANSFORMATION MECHANISM
→ WHY VALUE IS STRANDED
→ CHEAPEST DECISIVE VALIDATION
```

A second engineering correction was also made in this batch:

> `NARRATIVE COMPLETENESS != EVIDENCE COMPLETENESS`.

A candidate cannot become `VALIDATION_READY` merely because all descriptive fields are filled.  Evidence must separately support:
1. the origin actor/state;
2. the complementary actor/state;
3. the stranding/blocker structure.

This prevents the engine from manufacturing opportunity maturity through fluent explanation.

## 2. Current candidate pool

### LV-XZ-001 — Person-bound industrial diagnostic knowledge

**Doctrine fit: VERY HIGH**

**Current state: `COMPLEMENTARITY_HYPOTHESIS`**

Xuzhou has an unusually dense skilled-worker base.  Public 2026 evidence shows 1,535 newly qualified technician-or-above skilled workers in the first four months, master-studio transmission and veteran-worker transition into intelligent-manufacturing roles.  At the same time the engineering-machinery cluster is moving toward full-lifecycle service, digital O&M, global service and industrial AI/data infrastructure.

Potential hidden asset:

```text
senior technician experience
→ fault signatures / diagnosis sequence / tuning heuristics
→ currently person-bound
→ potentially extractable into rights-cleared reusable knowledge units
```

This candidate is deliberately **not** validation-ready because the most important evidence is still missing:

> Is the knowledge actually stranded/underrecognized outside internal training, and can its rights be cleared without exposing employer trade secrets?

Until that is directly observed, the engine must not call this a resource.

Why this candidate remains especially important:
- it is value that may not currently be treated as a market object;
- value may survive beyond the person's own hours if abstraction works;
- it fits the engine's desired role: discover → structure → verify → connect;
- it does not require the operator to become the permanent technician.

Cheapest decisive test: one narrow non-confidential fault domain, 3-5 authorized experts/employers, 3-5 potential users, no software build.

### LV-XZ-002 — Distributed service/maintenance traces as a governed dataset

**Doctrine fit: HIGH**

**Current state: `OBSERVED_PATTERN`**

Engineering-machinery leaders are proving that high-quality operating/failure data can support predictive maintenance and AI.  The open question is whether smaller service stations, rental fleets, dealers or equipment owners possess fragmented maintenance traces that are currently operational waste rather than governed data assets.

No public evidence yet proves that such a reusable pool exists among the target smaller actors.  Therefore this remains a search target, not a promoted opportunity.

Cheapest next action: inspect actual record types, rights and retention formats at 5-10 independent actors before copying or aggregating any data.

### LV-XZ-003 — University technology capability bundles

**Doctrine fit: MEDIUM-HIGH**

**Current state: `VALIDATION_READY`**

China University of Mining and Technology reports 8,619 valid domestic invention patents and operates technology-transfer, technology-manager and concept-validation mechanisms.  Recent public transfer notices also confirm real patent/technology transactions.

The hidden-value thesis is **not** `universities have patents`.  The university already knows that.

The hypothesized hidden value is the combination:

```text
related patents
+ inventor tacit know-how
+ concept-validation capability
+ test/implementation boundary
+ one industrial outcome
→ optionable technology capability bundle
```

Public evidence also shows that experts visiting Xuzhou firms are still discovering concrete problems onsite and recommending a dynamic technology-demand ledger and more precise expert matching.  This is evidence that translation and combination friction persists even though the individual assets are known.

Cheapest decisive test: choose one narrow industrial outcome, compose one authorized 3-5-asset bundle with an inventor/technology manager, and test it against 10 firms for a real pilot/LOI/commitment rather than generic interest.

### LV-XZ-004 — Fragmented technical deficits as an aggregated demand asset

**Doctrine fit: HIGH**

**Current state: `VALIDATION_READY`**

This candidate treats a deficit itself as a latent resource.

Individual SMEs may experience small, irregular technical problems that are uneconomic for strong experts/labs to acquire one by one.  But when repeated problem classes are normalized and aggregated, the group may become:
- purchasing power;
- a reusable test benchmark;
- a training signal;
- a market map;
- a reason for an expert/lab to create a bounded solution batch.

The key transformation is:

```text
many small local problems
→ confidentially abstracted problem archetype
→ minimum viable cohort
→ one expert/test/solution batch
→ lower search/acquisition cost on both sides
```

This is materially different from simple demand matching because the original small problems are not individually valuable market objects; aggregation creates the resource.

Cheapest decisive test: take 10-20 authorized problem summaries in one narrow domain and determine whether at least three can share a real abstractable problem class and whether cohort economics improve over separate engagements.

### LV-XZ-005 — Off-peak merchant/venue capacity as time-slot inventory

**Doctrine fit: MEDIUM-HIGH**

**Current state: `COMPLEMENTARITY_HYPOTHESIS`**

Xuzhou has a dense first-store, event and second-venue network.  Public evidence shows that sport/culture events are deliberately used to activate daytime and nighttime commercial traffic.

The potential hidden resource is not the merchant itself.  It is:

> genuinely idle two-hour capacity that the merchant currently treats as ordinary quiet time rather than programmable inventory.

Public evidence does **not** yet prove actual off-peak unused capacity or merchant willingness to option it, so this cannot be promoted further.

Cheapest decisive test: measure real utilization by time block for 20 candidates, option only 3-5 truly idle slots, then route one qualified group and compare incremental gross margin against baseline.

These rankings answer different questions.  `validation-ready` does not mean `best business`, and `high doctrine fit` does not mean `commercially true`.

## 4. What the operator should judge

The system can collect evidence and expose structure, but several judgments should remain explicit rather than hidden inside a score:

1. **Is the resource genuinely latent?**
   - Would the origin actor describe it as an asset before we frame it?
   - Or are we merely renaming an already-known product/service?

2. **Does value emerge from a new relationship?**
   - Does the complementary actor make the origin asset more valuable than it is alone?

3. **Does transformation create value rather than just transfer it?**
   - packaging;
   - aggregation;
   - rights clarification;
   - verification;
   - standardization;
   - time/space conversion;
   - trust creation.

4. **Can the mechanism survive without the operator's permanent labor?**

5. **Can the origin actor gain more than it gives up?**
   - If the system simply extracts cheap value from an uninformed actor, it is not regenerative orchestration.

6. **Is there enough economic surplus after rights, trust, QA, failure and acquisition costs?**

## 5. Immediate next validation sequence

### V1 — LV-XZ-001 rights/stranding probe

Goal: answer only whether person-bound industrial know-how can legally and economically become an independent asset.

Do not ask experts to disclose trade secrets.

Capture:
- what knowledge is personal/general skill versus employer-owned confidential know-how;
- how it is currently transferred;
- whether any useful part can be externalized with authorization;
- whether the expert/employer currently treats it as an asset;
- what proof/compensation/usage limit would be required.

### V2 — LV-XZ-003 technology-bundle pilot

Goal: determine whether bundling creates incremental value beyond existing technology-transfer channels.

Pass only if an authorized bundle produces a concrete enterprise commitment that the isolated-asset route did not already capture.

### V3 — LV-XZ-004 cohort economics test

Goal: prove that three or more firms share one sufficiently abstractable problem class and that aggregation materially improves expert/lab economics or solution quality.

## 7. Current conclusion

The first batch does **not** prove a business.

It does prove that the corrected architecture can produce candidates that are materially different from `who has work -> who can do it`.

The strongest philosophical candidate is currently person-bound industrial tacit knowledge.  The strongest immediately testable structures are university technology-capability bundling and aggregated SME technical-problem cohorts.

The next production milestone is not more broad ideation.  It is to obtain the missing stranding/rights evidence for LV-XZ-001 while independently testing whether LV-XZ-003 or LV-XZ-004 creates incremental value that existing transfer/matching channels do not already create.
