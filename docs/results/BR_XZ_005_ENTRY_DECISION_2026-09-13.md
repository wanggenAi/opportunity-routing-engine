The same rule states that offices organize or coordinate technology managers to enter enterprises, identify technical needs, connect university/research teams and results, integrate resources and promote incubation, commercialization and transactions.

Source:
- https://jszy.njtech.edu.cn/info/1020/3585.htm

```text

TRAINING != FIRST PROVEN GATE
```
